## Asset recipe

Every Reel contains exactly three layers:

1. **Human tension:** a question someone would actually type or say.
2. **Mechanism:** one causal chain, shown through distinct scene changes.
3. **Memory check:** a non-trading check viewers can repeat.

Every carousel contains five slides: confusion → moving parts → dated evidence →
common mistake → repeatable check. BhaavBrief appears only as the observation
layer after the explanation, never as the first or final sales message.

## Non-negotiable quality gate (Claude-refined)

Before rendering, the producer must record in the manifest:

1. A **human opening**: the first two seconds make a viewer feel recognised,
   curious, defensive or validated — never merely informed.
2. One plain-language **mechanism** that can be said in a single sentence.
3. One named **primary source**: MCX/SEBI/PPAC/EIA/RBI/World Gold Council, or
   another original publisher. Secondary reporting can only add context.
4. One **save frame** with a term, number, or repeatable check — never a generic
   “follow/comment/share” request.
5. A warm, female **voice tone**: a capable friend explaining a frustrating
   real-life moment, not a lecture or a trading call.

Reject a draft if it opens on a chart, price tile, or text-only title; if the
mechanism arrives after 20 seconds; if the evidence is only secondary; or if the
boundary against trading direction is missing from the spoken script.
