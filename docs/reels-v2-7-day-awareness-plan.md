# BhaavBrief — 7-day market-awareness run

**Run:** 11–17 September 2026  
**Job:** Make people feel more fluent in Indian commodity markets. BhaavBrief earns attention by explaining the gap between a headline and an Indian price, not by asking viewers to buy a product.

## Editorial mix

- 5 market-trend / market-mechanism posts
- 2 high-friction learning posts
- Female narrator only; captions on every Reel; music always below the voice
- One product reference maximum, after the insight has been earned
- No price targets, trade calls, or implied certainty

## Daily story companion

1. **Observation:** one sourced market fact, time-stamped.
2. **Audience prompt:** a precise poll, such as “What confuses you more: MCX gold or USD/INR?”
3. **Resolution:** the one-sentence explanation plus “tomorrow we unpack it.”

Avoid “download now.” The productive CTA is **save this before the next session** or a question that invites a real reply.

## Release checklist

- The hook describes a real confusion a viewer could have, not a generic market update.
- All figures share a timestamp and source; delete a number if it cannot be verified.
- On-screen copy and narration say the same thing.
- V2 artwork leads; data is a consequence layer, never the opening visual.
- Use `Educational data, not investment advice.` where a post includes data.
- Review first frame, caption legibility, audio mix and the female-only voice lock.

## Decisions at 24 hours

Track reach, 3-second plays, average watch time, 50% completion, saves, shares, profile visits and replies. Keep the series with the strongest saves + shares per reach; replace the first-frame hook if 3-second retention is weak. Do not judge the week on follower count alone.
