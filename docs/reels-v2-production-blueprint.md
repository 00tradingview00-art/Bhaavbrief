# BhaavBrief Reels V2 — Production Blueprint

## The mandate

Make commodity intelligence feel physical, useful, and unmistakably BhaavBrief.
V2 is not a prettier chart renderer. It is a short visual story where the first
frame carries an everyday consequence and the market mechanism earns attention.

## Creative system

### Price You Feel — 7–10 seconds

One current market fact, translated into a lived consequence. Gold is a wedding
budget, crude is a fuel-cost pressure, and USD/INR is an importer invoice. These
are high-frequency discovery videos, not a daily market bulletin.

### Why It Moved — 12–16 seconds

One force, one causal chain, one visual metaphor. Use this only when there is a
verifiable reason rather than a weak explanation invented to fill a posting slot.

### Market Myth — 10–14 seconds

Evergreen, save-worthy education: MCX versus jewellery gold, margin versus
capital, or why a contract is not a local retail quote. No live price is needed.

### Desk Note — up to 18 seconds

Reserved for an event with genuine relevance: RBI, FOMC, OPEC+, supply shock,
or a clear MCX-specific data release. This is never the default daily format.

## Visual language

- A physical Indian object or environment opens every reel: bangle, petrol
  nozzle, shipping container, invoice, workshop, or warehouse.
- Data appears as a consequence layer, never as the first visual.
- Use generated imagery as original art direction, then animate with editorial
  camera motion, parallax, light, and sparse typographic overlays.
- No synthetic human host, fake terminal, generic trader, or unlicensed news
  footage.
- Text stays inside a 160px top / 260px bottom safe zone at 1080×1920.

## First launch concepts

1. **Gold changed your wedding budget overnight.** Gold bangle + price-pressure
   visual. Explains global gold → USD/INR → Indian quote.
2. **Your petrol bill starts in a shipping lane.** Nozzle + container route.
   Explains crude, freight, and domestic price lag without a prediction.
3. **A weaker rupee can move gold without gold moving.** A gold bar caught in
   a currency exchange beam. Explains import parity.
4. **Margin is not your capital.** A small entry token above a much deeper safety
   buffer. Evergreen risk education, no market call.
5. **MCX gold and a jewellery quote are different prices.** Split-screen gold
   object and invoice. Save-first evergreen explainer.
6. **The calendar event behind a market move.** Event card enters a physical
   commodity scene. Use only sourced, current event data.

## Required release contract

Every V2 reel requires a JSON manifest, `validate-reel-v2.mjs` pass, and a human
approval state of `approved`. A live-data reel must include the exact disclaimer
`Educational data, not investment advice.` in both the visual/voice script. The
V2 scripts are review-only until a human explicitly authorizes the new Instagram
release workflow.

### Narration

V2 uses a **female narrator only**. Every manifest must declare
`"voice_profile": "female"`; the release gate rejects any other value. The final
production voice is calm, direct, and Indian-English first, with a female Hinglish
variant available when the editorial script calls for it. Music always ducks below
the narration, and captions are timed to the final voice track.

## Measurement and decisions

Use Trial Reels for format tests. At 24 hours, record reach, plays, average watch
time, shares, saves, follows, and the first-frame variant. Compare formats only
within a similar duration band. The first four weeks optimize for (1) early watch
time, (2) shares/saves per reach, then (3) qualified profile visits—not raw volume.
