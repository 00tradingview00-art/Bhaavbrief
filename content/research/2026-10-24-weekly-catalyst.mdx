## The catalyst

The dominant catalyst into next week is not a data release — it is the **gap between crude's war-risk premium and the physical flow evidence**.

MCX Crude closed the week at **₹9784/bbl, +3.16%**, with WTI at **$103.44, +3.99%**. That move was built on a specific news chain: Rosneft supply pressure (Oct 21), a tanker fire in the strait (Oct 22), Gulf export threats, and an extension of the Iran conflict (Oct 23). Against that, one verified input cuts the other way: **"Gulf exports more oil than it did before the Iran war despite surge in Hormuz attacks" (Oct 21)**.

That is the whole thesis. The market is currently paying for *disruption risk*, not *realised disruption*. Risk premia built on headlines have a decay function — they bleed out unless renewed by fresh incidents. We already saw the mechanism live this week: the Oct 22 "Iran strike denial" headline visibly capped the rally before the conflict-extension story re-ignited it.
