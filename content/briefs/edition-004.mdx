*Monday, 19 May 2026 | Edition 4*

---

**The macro picture this morning is defined by one dominant force: a firming US Dollar.** USDINR at **₹96.38** signals rupee softness that will mechanically support INR-denominated commodity prices even where global benchmarks lack conviction. Traders must separate genuine demand-side moves from currency-driven noise today.

---

## Crude Oil

[WATCH] MCX Crude holds at **₹9,995** with no directional catalyst strong enough to break range. The absence of headline risk cuts both ways — no fresh geopolitical premium, but equally no demand destruction narrative. The near-term structure is consolidative. Key support sits at **₹9,820–9,850**; a close below that opens a move toward **₹9,650**. Resistance is layered at **₹10,150** and then **₹10,400**. With global refinery margins under pressure entering the northern hemisphere summer, any rally toward **₹10,150** is a short opportunity unless accompanied by a supply disruption. Rupee weakness at **96.38** adds approximately **₹180–200** of cushion to the downside versus dollar-equivalent levels — a key buffer for domestic traders holding long positions.

---

## Metals

[BULLISH] Gold continues to hold elevated ground at **₹1,59,541** per 10g. The structural bid remains intact — central bank accumulation, residual safe-haven demand, and a weak rupee all point the same direction. Immediate support is **₹1,57,800**; a pullback to that level is a buy. Resistance at **₹1,61,500** is the next meaningful target before **₹1,65,000** comes into view over the coming fortnight. Do not fight this trend intraday without a clear reversal signal.

Silver at **₹2,73,334** per kg is holding its breakout structure. Industrial demand linkage makes silver sensitive to any global growth surprise. Support at **₹2,68,000**; breach targets **₹2,61,500**. Above **₹2,78,000** and the next leg higher accelerates.

Copper at **₹1,333** per kg sits in a tight range. No fresh demand signal from China's manufacturing complex means Copper stays rangebound **₹1,305–₹1,365** this week. Directional trades require a break of either extreme.

Natural Gas at **₹291** reflects a quiet shoulder-season demand picture. Range: **₹278–₹305**. No edge here without a weather catalyst.

---

## Agri / Mandi

[NEUTRAL] No significant agri-commodity headlines to trade off this morning. With kharif sowing still weeks out, market participants should monitor pre-monsoon forecast updates closely — the IMD's seasonal guidance will be the dominant agri price driver through June. Spices and oilseeds remain underpinned by general INR softness; however, without a fresh fundamental trigger, chasing levels in this segment carries poor risk-reward today.

---

## What to Watch

- **USDINR trajectory** — any move above **₹97.00** accelerates bullish repricing across all MCX contracts mechanically; a reversal below **₹95.80** takes that tailwind off the table
- **Gold's ability to hold ₹1,57,800** on the first meaningful correction — that level defines whether the bull trend is structurally intact or extended and vulnerable
- **Crude inventory data mid-week** — US EIA figures will either validate the current consolidation range or force a directional break through **₹9,820** support
