## Crude Oil

[WATCH] MCX Crude holds at **₹9,917**, printing a flat open with zero directional momentum. This is consolidation, not indecision — price has compressed into a tight band after last week's moves, and a breakout is building. **Key support sits at ₹9,780**; a breach there opens the door to **₹9,600–9,620**. On the upside, **₹10,100** is the level bulls need to reclaim to signal any meaningful trend continuation. Brent's global narrative remains tethered to OPEC+ compliance and US inventory data due mid-week. No fresh geopolitical flare-up overnight means the risk premium stays thin. Bias is **range-bound with a mild downside lean** until **₹10,100** is taken out on volume. Intraday traders: fade the extremes, respect the band.

## Metals

[BULLISH] Gold at **₹1,40,922** is trading at elevated levels and holding its ground. The rupee's weakness versus pre-2025 levels continues to inflate domestic gold prices structurally — every 50 paise move in USDINR is roughly **₹250–300** on MCX Gold. **Immediate support: ₹1,39,500**. A close below that invites profit-booking toward **₹1,37,800**. Resistance cluster at **₹1,42,500–₹1,43,000** — a zone where short-term longs should tighten stops.

Silver at **₹2,37,679** is the more volatile play. Industrial demand signals from China remain the swing factor. **Key range: ₹2,33,000–₹2,42,000** this week. A breakout above **₹2,42,000** on volume confirms momentum; below **₹2,33,000** turns the chart negative.

Copper at **₹1,328** sits at a technically sensitive zone. **₹1,310** is hard support; bulls want **₹1,355** reclaimed. Global manufacturing PMI data later this week will be the real driver here — watch that number closely.
