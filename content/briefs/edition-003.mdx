---

The macro picture this week is shaped by one dominant force: **dollar softness**. USDINR holding at **₹96.32** represents a structurally weaker rupee environment that mechanically lifts landed costs across every imported commodity on MCX. With no major headline catalysts overnight, price discovery today will be driven by global cues from Friday's US session close and early Asian session positioning. Traders should treat the currency level as the primary lever until fresh fundamental data arrives mid-week.

---

## Crude Oil

[WATCH]

MCX Crude prints at **₹9,895**, flat on the session. The absence of a directional move overnight is itself a signal — markets are digesting last week's OPEC+ compliance chatter and waiting on demand confirmation from China's industrial output data due Wednesday. Brent spot is hovering in a consolidation band that translates to **₹9,820–₹10,050** on MCX in the near term.

Bias is **cautiously bullish** on dips. The **₹9,820** level is immediate support — a clean hourly close below that opens **₹9,700**. On the upside, **₹10,050** is the resistance that bulls need to clear to confirm a trend leg. Play the range until that break materialises. Intraday traders: fade extremes. Swing traders: watch for a **₹9,820** entry with a stop below **₹9,680**.

## Metals

[BULLISH]

MCX Gold at **₹1,40,977** continues to hold above the critical **₹1,39,500** floor that has acted as support for the past fortnight. The rupee's softness is providing a natural bid — every 50-paise move in USDINR adds roughly **₹700–750** to MCX Gold's floor value. The directional call remains long on dips toward **₹1,39,800**, with a medium-term target of **₹1,43,500** if dollar weakness extends.

MCX Silver at **₹2,37,775** is lagging gold on the Gold/Silver ratio — watch for a catch-up trade. Key support is **₹2,34,000**; resistance at **₹2,42,000**. Silver tends to move with industrial sentiment, so Wednesday's China data is the trigger to watch here too.

MCX Copper at **₹1,328** is range-bound. Support at **₹1,305**, resistance at **₹1,352**. No fresh directional bias until Chinese PMI or US manufacturing data shifts the narrative.

MCX Natural Gas at **₹291** — neutral to slightly bearish. Pre-monsoon demand suppression is a seasonal headwind. **₹278** is downside target; **₹305** caps the upside.
