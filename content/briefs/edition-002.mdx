The dominant macro signal this morning is **USD weakness**. With USDINR at **₹96.33**, the rupee is holding firm — a headwind for INR-denominated commodity bulls. A stronger rupee compresses MCX upside even when dollar-denominated benchmarks inch higher. Watch this cross closely; a break below **₹95.80** on USDINR would cap MCX commodity gains across the board regardless of global cues.

## Crude Oil

[WATCH] MCX Crude is flat at **₹9,887**, mirroring a range-bound Brent that has found neither a fresh demand catalyst nor a supply shock to break its consolidation. The near-term structure is neutral-to-cautious. Key support sits at **₹9,720–₹9,750**; a close below that zone opens a slide toward **₹9,580**. Resistance is firm at **₹10,050–₹10,120** — that band has capped every rally attempt over the past fortnight. OPEC+ compliance data due later this week is the swing factor. Any signal of production quota slippage by Iraq or Kazakhstan would re-test the lower support. Until that data lands, **₹9,800–₹10,050** is the operative range. Avoid aggressive longs above **₹9,980**.

## Metals

[NEUTRAL] Gold at **₹1,41,262** is coiled. The metal is holding above its short-term pivot at **₹1,40,000** but has failed to mount a sustained push toward **₹1,43,500** — the level that would confirm the next leg higher. Dollar index direction this week is the gating factor. For MCX traders, **₹1,39,400** is the line in the sand; a daily close below that signals a pullback to **₹1,37,000–₹1,37,500**. Stay positioned but keep stops tight.

Silver at **₹2,38,472** continues to shadow gold but with amplified beta. Industrial demand signals from China's manufacturing PMI revision (due this week) could inject volatility. Support at **₹2,32,000**; resistance at **₹2,44,000**. A breakout either side will be sharp — Silver moves fast in thin mornings.

Copper at **₹1,332.5** is the most policy-sensitive metal on the board right now. Any fresh stimulus headline out of Beijing lifts this immediately. Watch **₹1,310** as support and **₹1,355** as the breakout trigger for a run toward **₹1,380**.
