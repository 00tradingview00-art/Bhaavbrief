*Monday, 19 May 2026 | Edition 5*

---

The dominant macro signal this week is dollar fragility. USDINR has crossed **₹96.38**, a level that mechanically inflates every rupee-denominated MCX contract. When the dollar softens globally but the rupee also weakens against it, commodity bulls get a structural tailwind regardless of underlying demand. That asymmetry is the lens through which every trade this week must be viewed.

---

## Crude Oil

[WATCH] MCX Crude sits at **₹9,940**, holding a tight range with no directional catalyst declared in today's session. The absence of fresh geopolitical escalation in the Middle East removes the fear premium that had been creeping in, but OPEC+ compliance chatter and a seasonally strong US driving demand season cap the downside. Directional bias: **sideways-to-mildly bullish**. Support is firm at **₹9,820–₹9,850**; a breach of **₹10,080** on the upside confirms a momentum leg toward **₹10,250**. Traders should avoid chasing — wait for either a clean break above **₹10,080** or a dip-and-hold at **₹9,830** before entering. The USDINR tailwind adds roughly ₹80–100 of artificial floor to this contract at current exchange levels.

---

## Metals

[BULLISH] Gold at **₹1,60,001** is not a coincidence — it is a magnet level where institutional positioning clusters. The psychological barrier is now support, not resistance. The next meaningful resistance sits at **₹1,62,500**, and any intraday dip toward **₹1,58,800** is a structural buy for swing traders with a 5–7 day horizon. Global central bank accumulation continues to underpin the floor.

Silver at **₹2,75,342** is the more aggressive trade this week. The gold-silver ratio remains elevated, meaning silver has catch-up potential. Key support: **₹2,70,000**. A close above **₹2,78,500** opens a run toward **₹2,85,000–₹2,88,000** within the week. Industrial demand from solar and EVs gives this move fundamental backing, not just momentum.

Copper at **₹1,335** is consolidating after its recent rally. Bias is **neutral**. Range trade between **₹1,310–₹1,355** until a China demand signal breaks the deadlock.

Natural Gas at **₹291** remains range-bound. Pre-monsoon power demand provides a soft floor near **₹282**. No aggressive positioning warranted.

---

## Agri / Mandi

[NEUTRAL] No major agri headline is disrupting the desk today. However, traders with Chana, Castor, or Crude Palm Oil exposure should note that the pre-monsoon forecast window opens this week — IMD's first substantive seasonal update will move these contracts sharply. Position sizing should be trimmed ahead of that announcement. Hold existing longs in CPO with a stop below the 5-day low.

---

## What to Watch

- **USDINR trajectory**: A move above **₹96.80** would provide an additional ₹40–60 boost across all MCX contracts mechanically — watch the RBI intervention threshold closely.
- **Crude inventory data (Wednesday)**: US EIA weekly report will either validate the ₹10,080 resistance break or push crude back toward ₹9,820. This is the week's single most important data point.
- **IMD pre-monsoon update**: Any deviation from normal monsoon forecasts triggers volatility in agri futures — particularly Chana and Soybean — and could dominate Thursday's session.
