## The Big Picture

US-Iran peace talks have collapsed, and the market has made its verdict: risk premium is back, and it is not leaving quietly. Crude leads the charge with a **3%+** surge, pulling energy complex higher while a concurrent import duty hike on precious metals has lit a separate fire under gold and silver. The rupee holding at **₹96.38** against the dollar amplifies every dollar-denominated move on MCX. Today's tape is unambiguously **risk-on for commodities, risk-off for everything else** — geopolitical disruption plus policy-driven import cost pressures are the twin engines. Trade with the trend, not against it.
