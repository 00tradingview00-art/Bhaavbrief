## Macro Pulse

[WATCH] Markets open the week in a data vacuum — no major commodity catalysts have crossed the wire overnight, which makes price action today a clean read on positioning rather than news. The USDINR at **₹96.33** is the number to anchor all cross-commodity thinking. A rupee holding above 96 compresses import-parity support for crude and metals simultaneously. Watch whether the RBI allows further appreciation or defends current levels — that decision alone will set the tone for INR-denominated commodity ranges through the week.

## Crude Oil

[NEUTRAL] MCX Crude is parked at **₹9,834** with zero overnight movement, signalling that traders are waiting for a catalyst rather than chasing direction. The flat open after the weekend is not complacency — it is compression. These setups resolve sharply. Immediate support sits at **₹9,740–9,760**; a break below that zone opens a move toward **₹9,620**. On the upside, **₹9,950** is the first meaningful resistance, with **₹10,080** as the level bulls need to reclaim to shift the weekly structure. OPEC+ supply trajectory and US inventory data mid-week are the triggers. Until then, range-trade the **₹9,760–9,950** band with tight stops. Directional bias: mildly bearish on rupee strength and subdued Asian demand signals.

## Metals
