Welcome to BhaavBrief — India's first daily commodity intelligence brief built for MCX traders and merchants. Every weekday at 7 AM, we cut through the noise and tell you what matters, why it matters, and what to do about it.

Edition 1. Let's go.

## [WATCH] Crude Oil — Hormuz shadow returns

The Strait of Hormuz is back in focus. Iran's IRGC announced naval exercises Monday through Tuesday, with satellite imagery showing elevated tanker clustering near the Oman coast. This is the same pattern we saw in late April before WTI's 12% correction.

The difference today: the market is less complacent. OVX (crude implied volatility) is elevated at 38+, which means options premiums are rich. **MCX June crude range: ₹6,600–7,100** for the session. A confirmed disruption signal could push it through ₹7,200.

For covered call writers: roll up your short strikes by at least ₹150 before the open. Do not get caught with tight strikes in a volatile tape.

**Key levels:** Support ₹6,580 · Resistance ₹7,080 · Stop for longs ₹6,450

## [BULLISH] Gold — DXY weakness is the gift that keeps giving

The Dollar Index has slipped to 100.2 following a weaker-than-expected US retail sales print. This is a structural tailwind for gold, not a one-day move. **COMEX gold is holding above $3,240/oz** — a level that has been defended three times in the past two weeks.

MCX parity translates to a **₹72,200–72,600 range this week**. The FOMC minutes on Wednesday are the key event risk — if they reveal a more dovish tone than expected, we could see a push toward ₹73,500.

No aggressive shorts here. The trend is your friend until it isn't.

## [BEARISH] Agri — Monsoon early, soya under pressure

IMD upgraded its southwest monsoon onset probability to **78% by June 1**, tracking five days ahead of the normal schedule. This is unambiguously bearish for kharif crop futures — markets will begin pricing in a good production season.

**NCDEX Soya May:** Watch the ₹4,580 support level. A break below opens the way to ₹4,420. Chana is similarly soft at ₹5,890. If you're long agri from higher levels, this is the week to reassess.

## What to Watch

- **Tuesday:** US CPI data — a hot print puts gold under pressure; a soft print accelerates the DXY decline
- **Wednesday:** FOMC minutes — the single most important event for metals this week
- **Thursday:** IMD weekly monsoon bulletin — will determine whether the agri selloff accelerates
