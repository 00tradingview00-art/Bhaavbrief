## The Big Picture

The UAE's formal exit from OPEC is the dominant macro event reshaping commodity markets this week. With Abu Dhabi signalling independent production policy, the cartel's cohesion fractures at precisely the moment US-Iran ceasefire talks inject further supply-side ambiguity into crude. Simultaneously, India's import duty hike on precious metals has triggered a sharp repricing of gold and silver on MCX, driving domestic premia higher even as the dollar firms on global haven demand. Every commodity on the MCX board is moving on geopolitical supply shocks today — not demand data.
