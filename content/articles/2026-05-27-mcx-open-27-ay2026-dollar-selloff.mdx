## MCX Open Brief — Wednesday, 27 May 2026

### 1. Overnight Summary

Dollar strength delivered a broad commodity selloff overnight. COMEX Gold fell 1.47% to $4,475.51/oz, COMEX Silver dropped 3.18% to $74.91/oz, and WTI Crude shed 3.07% to $90.63/bbl — its sharpest single-session decline in recent weeks. COMEX Copper eased 0.82% to $6.37/lb. Henry Hub Natural Gas was the lone outlier, gaining 2.46% to $3.08/mmBtu. The dominant theme: a firming US dollar repricing the entire commodity complex lower.
