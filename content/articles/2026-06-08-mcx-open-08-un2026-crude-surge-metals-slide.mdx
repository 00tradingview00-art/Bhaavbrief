### 3. Cross-Asset Connections

USD/INR is at ₹94.95. A weaker rupee means Indian importers pay more rupees for every dollar of commodity they buy — this mechanically lifts MCX prices in rupee terms, even when COMEX prices are flat. Today that effect amplifies the crude jump. The gold-silver ratio (how many silver kilograms one gold unit buys) has widened this morning — silver is falling faster than gold, which in past episodes has reflected sharper risk-off positioning in silver rather than a gold-specific move.

### 4. Import Parity Check — Crude Oil (Biggest Overnight Move)

Crude oil had the largest overnight move at +3.80%. A rough import parity calculation:

- COMEX WTI crude near **$67.50/barrel** (implied from MCX open and current USD/INR)
- At USD/INR of ₹94.95: $67.50 × 94.95 = **₹6,409/barrel** (base)
- Adding customs duty (~10%) and other levies (freight, insurance, cess — approximately 35–40% total landed cost uplift): theoretical fair value lands near **₹8,900–₹8,960/barrel**
- MCX actual open: **₹8,941** — broadly in line with import parity, confirming the move is fundamentally driven, not a pricing anomaly.
