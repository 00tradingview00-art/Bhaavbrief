**The mechanism:** Silver carries a dual identity — part monetary metal, part industrial input. With WTI crude at $95.44 (session: -0.32%) and Henry Hub natural gas at $3.24 (session: -0.25%), energy softness is signalling that industrial demand expectations are being scaled back. Silver, which derives roughly 55–60% of its demand from industrial uses including solar panels and electronics, historically tracks this sentiment closely. MCX Gold's 2.37% gain to ₹158,469/10g reflects demand for gold as a safe harbour amid this cross-asset uncertainty, with COMEX Gold at $4,478/oz.
