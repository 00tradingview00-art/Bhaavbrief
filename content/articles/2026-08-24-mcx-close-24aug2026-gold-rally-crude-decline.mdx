## TODAY'S SESSION RECAP

MCX traded in defensive mode as gold rallied while crude collapsed. Crude fell **2.62%** to **₹8140/bbl**, tracking a sharp **2.68%** decline in WTI to **$84.73/bbl** overnight. The crude weakness mirrored demand concerns from slowing global growth signals.
