## TODAY'S SESSION RECAP

MCX crude dominated Friday's session, closing at **₹9722/bbl** with a **+6.85%** gain on geopolitical supply concerns and overnight OPEC+ production signals. The commodity traded a **₹710** range (high **₹9774,** low **₹9064**), reflecting volatility across the morning. Metals moved inverse: copper fell **-3.49%** to **₹1374.85/kg**, pressured by demand weakness from China's PMI miss this week. Gold retreated **-1.02%** to **₹152200/10g** despite safe-haven flows, as USD strength at **₹95.53** weighed on rupee-denominated prices. NatGas edged **+0.41%** to **₹270.8/mmBtu** on steady demand. Silver barely moved, **-0.11%** to **₹233850/kg**.

## HOW MCX CLOSED VS THE DAY'S RANGE

Crude closed at the top of its range—**₹9722** near the session high of **₹9774**—signaling bullish structure into close. Buyers held the upper band through the final 30 minutes, rejecting the **₹9064** support established earlier. Copper, by contrast, faded sharply from its **₹1430.9** intraday high, closing near the bottom of its **₹61**-point range at **₹1374.85**. This retrace from highs indicates profit-taking and weakening conviction in metals. Gold similarly pulled back from **₹154450** to **₹152200,** retracing **1.5%** of its intraday swing.

## WHAT COMEX IS DOING NOW

COMEX is diverging from MCX crude's bullish close. WTI trades at **$102.82/bbl** (**+7.05%** on the day) but is consolidating—not extending higher into the US evening. COMEX gold at **$4380.20/oz** and copper are both fading, down **-0.81%** and **-5.18%** respectively. This divergence—MCX crude holding strength while COMEX retreats—suggests profit-taking into US equity hours and caution ahead of tomorrow's Asian open.
