## TODAY'S SESSION RECAP

Energy dominated MCX today as crude and natural gas both rallied on what appears to be supply tightness signals. MCX Crude surged **+3.04%** to close at **₹9787/bbl**, the day's top mover, driven by seasonal refinery maintenance factors or resilient demand across Asian refineries. MCX Natural Gas followed with a **+1.46%** gain to **₹292.6/mmBtu**. Precious metals diverged sharply: MCX Silver fell **-1.15%** to **₹194450/kg** while MCX Gold retreated **-0.37%** to **₹143600/10g**. Copper remained flat at **+0.20%** to **₹1400.2/kg**.
