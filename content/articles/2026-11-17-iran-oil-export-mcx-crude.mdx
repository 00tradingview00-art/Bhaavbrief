## Introduction

Understanding how Iran oil export restrictions affect MCX crude India prices requires tracing a chain that runs from Persian Gulf tanker routes through NYMEX futures to your MCX terminal screen. When Iranian barrels are removed from global supply — through sanctions or geopolitical pressure — the displacement ripples directly into the ₹-per-barrel price Indian traders and jewellers watch every session.

## The Mechanism

Iran holds approximately 9% of global proven oil reserves and, at peak output, exports around 2–2.5 million barrels per day (mbpd). When US-led sanctions tighten — as they did sharply in 2018 and again in 2024 — that volume must either disappear from global supply or be replaced by other producers.

The transmission works in steps:

1. **Supply removal:** Iranian export restrictions reduce global available supply by 0.5–1.5 mbpd depending on enforcement severity.
2. **WTI repricing:** Reduced supply lifts WTI on NYMEX. Historically, each 1 mbpd production cut lifts WTI by ₹5–10 per barrel in dollar terms.
3. **MCX conversion:** MCX Crude tracks this move via the import parity formula — **MCX Crude (₹/barrel) = WTI ($/bbl) × USD/INR × 1.025** — where the 1.025 factor embeds India's import duty differential.
4. **Rupee amplification:** If sanctions simultaneously pressure the INR — through India's current account widening on higher oil import costs — the USD/INR rate rises, further amplifying the MCX price in rupee terms. A ₹1 depreciation in the rupee adds roughly ₹60–75 per MCX lot at prevailing WTI levels.
5. **Sentiment layer:** Strait of Hormuz tension, through which approximately 20% of global oil transits, adds a geopolitical risk premium on top of the fundamental supply gap.

## India-Specific Context

India imported Iranian crude substantially before 2018 sanctions, benefiting from concessional pricing and extended credit terms. Post-sanctions, Indian refiners — particularly IOC and BPCL — shifted to Middle Eastern and US grades, paying market rates and absorbing freight premiums.

On MCX, the contract is denominated in ₹ per barrel with a lot size of 100 barrels, meaning a ₹100/barrel move equals ₹10,000 per lot. SEBI-mandated circuit limits cap intraday moves, which can cause MCX to open with a gap on Monday when weekend geopolitical events have already moved NYMEX significantly. GST applies to MCX commodity derivatives, adding a transactional cost layer that global benchmarks do not carry. RBI's management of the USD/INR rate through intervention can partially cushion or widen the impact depending on its policy stance at any given time.

## Historical Episodes

**2018 US sanctions reimposition:** When the Trump administration withdrew from the JCPOA and reimposed sanctions in May 2018, Iranian exports fell from approximately 2.5 mbpd to under 0.5 mbpd within months. WTI climbed from roughly $65 to near $76/bbl between May and October 2018, with MCX crude moving correspondingly higher in rupee terms, amplified by the rupee depreciating past ₹74/USD that period.

**2019–2020 Strait of Hormuz incidents:** Iranian seizure of tankers in the Strait of Hormuz in mid-2019 caused WTI to spike approximately 5% in a single session, with MCX reacting at the Monday open due to weekend timing.

**2024 sanctions tightening:** Renewed enforcement pressure on Iranian oil flows in early 2024 coincided with OPEC+ cuts, contributing to Brent trading above $90/bbl for extended periods — MCX crude contracts touched multi-year highs in that cycle in rupee terms.

## What to Watch

- **US EIA inventory data:** Released every Wednesday at approximately 8:00 PM IST — a draw amplifies any Iran-driven supply concern
- **OPEC+ meeting calendar:** Scheduled and emergency meetings; Iran's quota status is always on the agenda
- **US State Department sanctions announcements:** Typically after Washington market hours, creating MCX gap risk
- **USD/INR daily fixing:** RBI reference rate published around 1:30 PM IST
- **China PMI release:** Monthly; weak readings historically cap crude despite supply shocks
- **MCX circuit filter levels:** Check before taking large positions ahead of weekends with active Middle East tensions
