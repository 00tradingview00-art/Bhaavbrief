## TODAY'S SESSION RECAP

NatGas led MCX higher with a **+1.93%** gain to **₹300.8/mmBtu**, while gold and silver followed defensive demand with **+0.55%** and **+0.57%** gains respectively to **₹150,130/10g** and **₹227,365/kg**. NatGas benefited from seasonal volatility and overnight US inventory data expectations ahead of tomorrow's EIA release.
