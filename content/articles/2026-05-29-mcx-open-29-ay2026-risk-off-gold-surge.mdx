## 1. Overnight Summary

COMEX Gold gained +0.94% to $4,569.76/oz as risk-off sentiment dominated overnight flows. WTI Crude fell -0.65% to $88.11/bbl while COMEX Silver slipped -0.75% to $75.42/oz despite gold's advance — a divergence that widened the gold-silver ratio. COMEX Copper shed -0.58% to $6.38/lb. Henry Hub Natural Gas was the sole energy gainer, rising +1.57% to $3.36/mmBtu. Safe-haven demand into precious metals characterized the session.
