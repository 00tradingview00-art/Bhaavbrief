## Introduction

Understanding what is MCX VWAP and how to use it in India is essential for any trader who wants to decode where institutional money is positioned on any given session. VWAP — Volume Weighted Average Price — is the single price benchmark that separates informed institutional execution from retail noise on MCX.

## The Mechanism

VWAP is calculated continuously throughout the MCX trading session using this formula:

**VWAP = Σ (Price × Volume) ÷ Σ Volume**

At every executed trade, the system multiplies the transaction price by the number of lots transacted, sums all such values from session open, and divides by cumulative volume traded. The result is a running average that weights high-volume price zones more heavily than low-volume ones.

Institutions — proprietary desks, large bullion banks, hedging entities — use VWAP as an execution benchmark. A large gold import house covering its physical exposure on MCX Gold will typically execute in tranches, comparing each fill against VWAP. If the execution price is below VWAP for a long position, the desk has performed well relative to the market consensus for that session.

VWAP also functions as a dynamic support and resistance reference. Historically, price tends to revert toward VWAP after momentum-driven deviations, making it a mean-reversion anchor. When MCX Gold or Silver trades persistently above VWAP with expanding volume, institutional participation is broadly confirming that price level rather than fading it.

## India-Specific Context

MCX VWAP behaves differently from COMEX Gold VWAP for several structural reasons. MCX prices are denominated in INR per 10 grams for Gold and INR per kilogram for Silver, meaning USD/INR spot rate movements directly alter the VWAP calculation independent of any change in COMEX futures. A one-rupee depreciation in the currency adds roughly ₹50–60 to the MCX Gold price, shifting the entire VWAP baseline.

Beyond currency, India's import duty structure — currently 15% effective duty inclusive of Agriculture Infrastructure Development Cess — creates a persistent premium between COMEX-derived import parity and MCX prices. MCX circuit limits (typically ±4% for gold) also mechanically cap intraday VWAP range, a constraint absent on COMEX. SEBI's position limits on MCX further restrict the lot sizes institutions can deploy, which compresses volume concentration and affects how quickly VWAP responds to large-order flow.

## Historical Episodes

In 2020, during the peak COVID-19 safe-haven rally, MCX Gold VWAP on single sessions stretched as much as 2–3% above the morning open price as relentless institutional buying through the session kept cumulative volume concentrated at higher prices — the contract touched ₹56,200 per 10 grams in August 2020, with intraday VWAP tracking within ₹300 of the session high.

In 2022, when the Russia-Ukraine conflict triggered a sharp spike in COMEX Gold, MCX Gold VWAP on the February 24 session reflected a ₹1,800 intraday range compressed into the first two hours, after which VWAP acted as a ceiling for the remainder of the session as hedgers used it to lock in favourable rates.

In 2023, a surprise RBI intervention to defend the rupee near ₹83.5/USD caused MCX Silver VWAP to diverge from LME Silver VWAP by nearly 1.8% intraday, illustrating how currency policy can disconnect Indian VWAP from global benchmarks in real time.

## What to Watch

Monitor these specific triggers for VWAP distortion events on MCX:

- **RBI MPC policy decisions** — announced on scheduled dates; rupee volatility immediately reprices MCX VWAP
- **US CPI and Fed FOMC statements** — shift COMEX Gold, feeding into MCX open the following session
- **MCX session open volume** — a surge in first-30-minute volume skews VWAP toward the opening price range for the rest of the day
- **MCX circuit filter alerts** — when price hits the ±4% limit, VWAP calculation freezes in effective range
- **India customs duty revision notifications** — Union Budget or mid-year duty changes reset the import parity baseline that anchors MCX VWAP structurally

Knowing what is MCX VWAP and how to use it in India means tracking these inputs before the session, not after the move.
