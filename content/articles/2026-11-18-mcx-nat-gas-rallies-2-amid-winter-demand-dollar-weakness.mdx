**WHAT HAPPENED**

The disconnect reveals seasonal heating demand is overriding the dollar-strength headwind that is crushing precious metals and industrial demand indicators. This is classic seasonal transmission — Northern Hemisphere winter heating demand is pushing US domestic gas tighter, transmitting to MCX via NYMEX parity despite a weakening global growth narrative.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
At **₹307/mmBtu**, MCX Natural Gas has broken above the **20-day simple moving average of ₹292** and is trading near the month's high of **₹319**, signaling withdrawal-season tightness. Import parity logic is straightforward: Henry Hub **$3.26** × USD/INR **93.48** = approximately **₹305** before freight and regasification costs, explaining why MCX is near fair value. The key mechanism is the EIA storage report released Thursdays at ~8:30 PM IST — if this week's withdrawal exceeds the 5-year seasonal average by **80+ Bcf**, the next session will see reinforcement of this rally. Conversely, if storage draws prove lighter than seasonal norms, downside pressure to **₹300**–**₹295** follows.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Petronet LNG, which manages spot and term LNG procurement at Dahej and Kochi terminals, faces a margin compression decision. At **₹307/mmBtu** (approximately **$3.28** landed cost post-regasification), cost-plus pass-through to city gas distributors (IGL in Delhi, MGL in Mumbai) triggers within 30–60 days; if winter stays cold, spot LNG demand from Europe will ease, allowing Petronet to source cheaper term cargoes. Conversely, fertilizer producers reliant on gas feedstock — particularly urea plants — now face input cost pressure heading into winter; any spike past **₹311** will force a decision to either invoke government subsidy mechanisms or curtail production.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Winter heating demand, not demand-destruction, is driving this move — the only commodity rallying into the North American withdrawal season while others tank on rate-cut reality. This is seasonally normal, but its persistence despite broader commodity malaise signals storage drawdown is running tight to or above seasonal norms.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
