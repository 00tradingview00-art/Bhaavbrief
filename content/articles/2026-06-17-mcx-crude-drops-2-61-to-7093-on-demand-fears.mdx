MCX Crude has fallen **2.61% to ₹7093/bbl** this session, with WTI at **$74.25** and Brent at **$77.90**, making it the clear session laggard across the complex. The trigger is a combination of weaker-than-expected US retail sales data released Tuesday and a fresh OPEC+ production increase signal, which together compressed demand expectations globally. The twist: crude is trading just **₹51 above its 20-day range floor of ₹7042** — in prior episodes where WTI broke below $74, the MCX contract found buyers near this level as Asian physical demand stepped in. The **20-SMA sits at ₹8410**, nearly **₹1317 above current price**, confirming the contract is in deep technical distress; at USD/INR **₹94.56**, Indian OMCs importing at this exchange rate see a partial cost cushion versus earlier $80+ levels. Indian Oil Corporation, which processes roughly 1.5 million barrels daily, sees notional import cost fall by an estimated **₹190–230 crore per day** for every $1/bbl decline in WTI at current rupee levels.

**Watch:** ₹**7042** — the 20-day range floor and session low; a close below this level would mark a fresh multi-week breakdown.
