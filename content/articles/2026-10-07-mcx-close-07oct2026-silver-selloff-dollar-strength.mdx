## TODAY'S SESSION RECAP

Silver led a broad selloff across MCX, closing **-1.71%** at **₹223,353/kg** after testing **₹226,208** intraday. Crude Oil retreated **-0.76%** to **₹8,562/bbl** despite an intraday high of **₹8,824**. Gold declined **-0.67%** to **₹149,110/10g**. Dollar strength (**₹97.08** USD/INR) and persistent demand concerns—particularly from China's PMI data and global growth fears—pressured precious metals throughout the session. Copper bucked the trend with a modest **+0.44%** gain to **₹1,419.7/kg**, while Natural Gas held flat at **₹309.7/mmBtu** despite range volatility of **₹13**.

## HOW MCX CLOSED VS THE DAY'S RANGE

Silver closed 2,855 paise below its high of **₹226,208,** retreating sharply from intraday strength into the final hour. The close near the session low of **₹221,373** signals a fading structure—buyers capitulated into close. Crude Oil similarly reversed, closing **₹262** below its **₹8,824** high, though it held above the **₹8,533** low, indicating a less severe retrace but still a mid-range close lacking conviction.

## WHAT COMEX IS DOING NOW
