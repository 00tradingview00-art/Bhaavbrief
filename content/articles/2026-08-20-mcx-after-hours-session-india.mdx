## Introduction

Understanding what MCX after hours session timings India traders operate within is essential for anyone holding open commodity positions overnight. MCX runs an evening session from 17:00 IST to 23:30 IST on most non-agri commodities, extended to 23:55 IST for internationally linked contracts like gold, silver, crude oil, and base metals — a window deliberately aligned with active London and New York market hours.

## The Mechanism

The transmission from global benchmark to MCX price follows a specific chain. During the MCX after hours session, prices on COMEX (gold, silver, copper), NYMEX (crude oil), and LME (base metals) continue moving as European and American trading desks remain active.

The MCX settlement price for these contracts uses an import parity calculation:

**MCX Price (INR/unit) = International Benchmark Price (USD/unit) × Conversion Factor × USD/INR Rate**

For gold: COMEX spot price in USD per troy ounce is converted to INR per 10 grams using the RBI reference rate published at approximately 13:30 IST. However, during the evening session, the live USD/INR rate on the NDS-OM or interbank market — not the fixed RBI rate — drives real-time MCX price movement.

If COMEX gold rises 1% overnight while the rupee simultaneously weakens 0.5% against the dollar, the MCX price compounds both moves — a 1.5% effective move in INR terms is entirely possible from a single global session. Conversely, rupee appreciation can partially absorb a COMEX rally, capping MCX gains. This currency amplification or dampening effect means MCX price movement is rarely a clean translation of the global benchmark move.

## India-Specific Context

Several structural factors make MCX evening session behavior distinct from raw global price movement. First, India's import duty on gold currently sits at 15% (including agriculture infrastructure cess), meaning the domestic price floor is structurally elevated above COMEX-equivalent levels. A sharp COMEX fall does not immediately erase this duty premium from MCX quotes.

Second, GST at 3% on gold and 18% on industrial metals creates a layered cost structure that affects physical arbitrage thresholds. Third, SEBI-mandated daily circuit limits — typically ±6% for gold and silver, ±4% for crude oil on MCX — can halt trading before a full global move is absorbed. RBI's monetary policy stance directly influences the USD/INR rate, making MPC meeting outcomes a live variable for overnight MCX positioning. MCX contract sizes and lot structures also mean that margin calls during after hours moves can force position liquidation before the Indian morning session even opens.

## Historical Episodes

In March 2020, when crude oil prices collapsed following the Saudi-Russia price war breakdown, MCX crude hit its lower circuit limit of ₹1,520 per barrel within minutes of the evening session opening, representing a single-session fall of approximately 25% — a move that left many short-margin traders facing automatic square-offs.

In August 2020, COMEX gold crossed $2,000 per troy ounce for the first time; MCX gold futures simultaneously crossed ₹56,000 per 10 grams during the evening session, a roughly 35% gain within that calendar year, amplified by simultaneous rupee weakness.

In 2022, following the Russia-Ukraine conflict escalation, MCX natural gas futures recorded back-to-back upper circuit hits across multiple evening sessions, with cumulative moves exceeding 40% over two weeks as European energy markets repriced risk.

## What to Watch

Specific data releases that historically trigger sharp MCX evening session moves:

- **EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report** — released every Wednesday at 21:00 IST; primary crude oil volatility trigger
- **US CPI data** — released on a monthly schedule around 18:30-19:00 IST; moves gold and silver sharply
- **China Caixin Manufacturing PMI** — first business day of each month; moves copper and aluminum
- **FOMC meeting dates** — Fed statements release around 02:00 IST following the decision day
- **RBI MPC bi-monthly policy dates** — affects USD/INR and therefore all MCX metal contracts
- **MCX circuit limit levels** — check pre-session on the MCX website; proximity to limits signals compression risk
