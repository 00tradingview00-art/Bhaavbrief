## Introduction

The question of weekly vs monthly MCX options which to trade India comes down to one core trade-off: time decay speed versus premium cost. Weekly options bleed theta faster and suit short-horizon hedges, while monthly options carry more premium but give price moves room to develop.

## The Mechanism

MCX options are European-style, meaning they can only be exercised at expiry — not before. This single structural fact shapes everything about how weekly and monthly contracts behave differently.

**Weekly options** expire every Friday (for gold and silver, typically the last Friday of the near week). With 5–7 calendar days remaining, theta — the daily erosion of time value — is at its steepest. A ₹1,000 premium on a weekly gold option may lose ₹150–₹200 of value in a single session if the underlying MCX Gold price remains flat.

**Monthly options** expire on the last day of the contract month. With 20–30 days remaining at purchase, the same ₹1,000 premium loses perhaps ₹30–₹50 per day in flat markets early in the cycle, accelerating sharply in the final week.

**The pricing pathway:**

1. COMEX Gold spot moves (denominated in USD/troy oz)
2. Multiply by 31.1035 (grams per troy oz) → USD/gram
3. Multiply by prevailing USD/INR rate (RBI reference or spot)
4. Multiply by 10 (MCX Gold contract is 10 grams) → INR/unit
5. Add import duty + GST to arrive at domestic parity
6. MCX futures price converges toward this import parity; options are priced off MCX futures via Black-76 model

The implied volatility (IV) used in Black-76 is typically higher for weekly options because short-dated contracts price in event risk per day more aggressively. A 20% annualised IV on a monthly option translates to a much higher effective daily IV on a weekly option covering the same event window.

## India-Specific Context

India-specific factors create persistent wedges between COMEX/LME benchmarks and MCX prices. The basic customs duty on gold is currently 15%, GST is 3%, and the Agriculture Infrastructure Development Cess (AIDC) adds another 5% — making the landed cost roughly 23–24% above international price. This duty structure means MCX Gold rarely falls below import parity for sustained periods; physical demand acts as a floor.

For MCX options specifically, SEBI mandates physical delivery settlement for in-the-money commodity options at expiry, unlike cash-settled equity index options. This creates genuine delivery risk in the final days of a monthly contract that weekly options generally avoid by rolling before that window. MCX circuit limits (typically ±6% for gold) can also suspend options trading mid-session, trapping positions.

## Historical Episodes

**2020 COVID rally:** Between March and August 2020, MCX Gold futures surged approximately 40%, from around ₹40,000 to ₹56,000 per 10 grams. Weekly call options with 2–3 days to expiry saw premium multiples of 8–10x within single sessions during peak volatility.

**2022 Russia-Ukraine shock:** In the week of February 28, 2022, MCX Gold moved roughly ₹3,500 (about 6%) in three sessions. Weekly options expired massively in-the-money; monthly options had sufficient time cushion to manage positions.

**2023 rupee depreciation episode:** When USD/INR crossed 83.5 in mid-2023, MCX Silver gained approximately 4–5% in a fortnight even as LME Silver was relatively stable — illustrating how currency moves alone can determine MCX options outcomes independently of global commodity direction.

## What to Watch

- **FOMC rate decision dates** (USD/INR sensitivity — check Fed calendar)
- **RBI MPC meeting dates** (published quarterly; rupee volatility spikes around decisions)
- **MCX expiry calendar** (available on MCX website; note Friday weekly expiries vs. month-end)
- **MCX daily circuit limit breaches** (reported in MCX circulars; signals potential options trading halt)
- **COMEX options expiry dates** (global IV spike transmits to MCX IV within hours)
- **India import duty revision cycles** (Union Budget and mid-year notifications via CBIC)

Understanding weekly vs monthly MCX options which to trade India ultimately requires matching your time horizon to the contract's theta profile — a mechanical question before it is anything else.
