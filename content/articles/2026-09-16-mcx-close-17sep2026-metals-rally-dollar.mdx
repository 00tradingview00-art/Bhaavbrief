## TODAY'S SESSION RECAP

MCX metals complex closed decisively higher on Thursday, led by gold and silver. **MCX Gold rallied ₹1.42% to close ₹152,950/10g**, while **MCX Silver advanced ₹1.36% to ₹235,275/kg**. Both precious metals extended gains from overnight dollar weakness — USD/INR held at **₹95.95** throughout the session, supporting rupee-denominated commodity prices. Copper followed with a **+0.75%** close at **₹1,375.8/kg**. Energy lagged: crude edged **-0.04%** to **₹9,815/bbl** and natural gas fell **-1.00%** to **₹277.4/mmBtu** on global supply ease and receding inflation narrative.

## HOW MCX CLOSED VS RANGE

Gold closed at the **upper end of its ₹1,500 range** (L: **₹151,450** / H: **₹152,950**), capturing the day's high and holding the bullish structure into close. Silver similarly demonstrated strength, settling **₹825/kg** below its **₹236,100** intraday peak but well above the **₹233,600** low — a controlled retreat rather than capitulation. Both precious metals preserved session momentum into the final 30 minutes, signaling conviction among buyers.

## WHAT COMEX IS DOING NOW

COMEX is **diverging and fading**. Gold sits at 4,312.70 $ (−**0.46%** on the day), reversing MCX's bullish close — a bearish signal for tomorrow's MCX open. WTI crude has collapsed −**3.35%** to 102.29 $ on inventory concerns, while COMEX silver paradoxically remains **+0.87%**, creating mixed overnight directional bias. US equity weakness is driving safe-haven demand away from commodities into bonds.
