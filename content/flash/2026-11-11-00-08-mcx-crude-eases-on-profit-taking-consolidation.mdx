**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude slipped to 8080 INR/bbl at 12:08 am IST, posting a −0.20% decline as traders booked gains after yesterday's volatile swings. The contract has retreated from intraday peaks near 9113 INR/bbl recorded at 11:36 pm IST yesterday, signalling shallow profit-taking. USD/INR held steady at 94.03 INR (±0.00%), removing near-term rupee tailwinds that supported the recent bounce to 8570 INR/bbl at 02:15 am IST.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, this consolidation reflects exhausted momentum after a 2.46% rally earlier. Gold's −1.76% slide and silver's −2.53% drop to 182387 INR/kg indicate broad risk-off sentiment is re-engaging. The rupee's stability at 94.03 INR removes the currency hedge that had cushioned recent strength, leaving crude vulnerable to global demand concerns.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8000 INR/bbl support level is critical; a breach below it would confirm the profit-taking thesis. Watch for USD/INR to breach 94.50 INR—any rupee weakness could reignite crude bids near 8200 INR/bbl.
