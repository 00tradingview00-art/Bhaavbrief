**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rallied to 222836 INR/kg at 21:23 IST, posting a +1.58% gain from the previous close. The advance reverses the profit-taking that sent silver to 218306 INR/kg at 11:36 IST this morning. The recovery mirrors renewed safe-haven interest, with MCX Gold climbing +0.35% to 143324 INR/10g while crude oil collapsed -5.35% to 8541 INR/bbl, signaling risk-off sentiment and capital rotation into precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, this rebound reestablishes the intraday support zone near 222800 INR/kg after a deep pullback. The cross-asset snapshot shows crude's sharp decline is driving commodity rotation into gold and silver despite rupee stability at 96.55 INR/USD. Traders should note the recovery stalls below the 227902 INR/kg peak from 20:03 IST yesterday, suggesting resistance remains intact at that level.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor whether silver sustains above 223000 INR/kg or rolls back toward 218306 INR/kg support. A close above 225000 INR/kg would confirm upside extension; a break below 220000 INR/kg reopens the profit-taking scenario.
