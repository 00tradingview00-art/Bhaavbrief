**WHAT HAPPENED**
Houthi forces have seized strategic islands in the Red Sea, threatening the Bab el-Mandeb strait — a chokepoint through which approximately **12% of global seaborne oil** transits toward Europe and Asia.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks importing **Brent-linked crude** face immediate route diversification costs as tankers reroute via the Cape of Good Hope, adding **10-14 days transit time** and higher marine insurance premiums on every consignment. Refiners locked into fixed-price contracts now absorb extended carrying charges, while spot buyers see **WTI and Brent spreads widen**, forcing rupee-denominated cost-per-barrel calculations upward at each futures roll. Silver and copper — historically safe-haven commodities during supply-chain disruptions — benefit from risk-off positioning in **MCX Silver** and **MCX Copper** contracts as traders hedge against broader inflation from energy cost pass-through.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Lubricant blenders and fuel retailers tied to refinery gate prices face delayed product replenishment windows, compressing working-capital cycles and forcing inventory decisions forward by 2-3 weeks. Petrochemical processors downstream — producing polymers, adhesives, and packaging resins — see feedstock acquisition timelines stretch, creating margin pressure on quarterly earnings if input costs lock higher while output pricing remains contract-bound. Households purchasing diesel-dependent transportation, fertiliser-intensive agricultural inputs, and plastic consumer goods absorb cumulative cost pressures; fertiliser distribution networks already operating on thin margins may ration supplies to dealers, delaying seasonal stocking ahead of crop cycles.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Refinery margins in India compress as crude acquisition costs rise while refined product pricing remains sticky to spot market benchmarks for 1-2 weeks. **MCX Crude Oil** breaks above key resistance; traders monitor **WTI spread acceleration** relative to Brent as the primary signal of sustained rerouting demand. Diesel prices at Indian retail pumps face upward pressure within 7-10 days if crude premiums persist, directly widening household transport costs.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **OPEC+ emergency production signals** and next **US Energy Information Administration weekly crude inventory report** for confirmation of demand destruction or inventory builds. Breach of **$85/bbl WTI** would signal sustained geopolitical premium embedding into MCX contract curves.
