**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper rose to 1395.65 INR/kg at 09:36 am IST, gaining +0.52% from the previous close. The move reflects renewed support across the base metals complex, with Silver climbing +0.50% to 245337 INR/kg alongside the copper advance. USD/INR weakness at 95.4 (−0.34%) provided marginal tailwind to rupee-denominated metals, offsetting broader macro headwinds seen in energy markets where Crude fell −2.17% to 7667 INR/bbl.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's recovery to near-1396 marks a consolidation after yesterday's late-session pullback to 1371.65 INR/kg at 20:18 IST. The intraday volatility pattern—peaking at 1393.05 IST yesterday morning, then falling −0.84% by evening—suggests traders are testing support ahead of key resistance. Base metal upside is restrained by crude weakness and risk-off sentiment, keeping positioning cautious.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1400 INR/kg round level remains the immediate resistance target. A break above 1400 would signal sustained recovery momentum; a slide back toward 1380 would confirm the pullback trend continues.
