**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold retreated to 143424 INR/10g at 18:24 IST, declining 1.55% from the previous close. The pullback reverses earlier momentum that had pushed the contract to 145706 INR/10g at 20:20 IST yesterday. Silver fell harder at 220364 INR/kg (-2.92%), while Crude surged +5.07% to 8836 INR/bbl. The rupee strengthened marginally to 96.57 INR (+0.24%), weighing on precious metals priced in foreign currency.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's retreat signals profit-taking after a two-day rally that breached 145000 INR/10g. The divergence with Crude's strength suggests a flight from safe-haven metals into energy plays. For MCX traders, the weakness reflects rupee appreciation and potential dollar strength, both headwinds for INR-denominated gold futures.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 142500 support level—near the recent 142883 low from 15:29 IST—will confirm whether this pullback is corrective or trend-breaking.
