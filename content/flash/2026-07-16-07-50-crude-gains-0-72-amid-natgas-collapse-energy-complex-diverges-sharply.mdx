Silver and gold both declined—silver by **0.82%** to **₹218805/kg** and gold by **0.47%** to **₹141181/10g**—signaling broad-based weakness in risk-off trades.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

The crude uptick occurs against a backdrop of safe-haven liquidation (gold and silver falling), suggesting demand-side buoyancy rather than geopolitical premium accumulation. Natural gas's steeper decline points to supply adequacy and seasonal cooling demand in Northern Hemisphere summer, a structural tailwind unrelated to crude's trajectory. Import parity for crude tracks Brent × USD/INR exchange rate (**₹96.26** today); the **₹7668** level reflects stable global pricing without rupee volatility as a confounding driver, meaning the MCX gain mirrors external fundamentals.
