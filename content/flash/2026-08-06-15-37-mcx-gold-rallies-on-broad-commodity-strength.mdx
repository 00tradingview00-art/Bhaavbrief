**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 149760 INR/10g at 03:37 pm IST, gaining +0.85% from the previous close. The rally extends recovery momentum across the commodity complex: MCX Crude climbed +0.94% to 7176 INR/bbl, while MCX Copper advanced +1.34% to 1392.2 INR/kg. Silver lagged slightly at 227230 INR/kg (−0.16%), and USD/INR edged higher to 95.24 INR (+0.15%), signaling modest rupee weakness supporting commodity import parity.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's sustained advance from 141250 INR/10g at 22:41 IST yesterday reflects renewed appetite for safe-haven metals amid broad-based commodity recovery. The +8510 INR gain in less than five hours signals strong intraday momentum. For MCX traders, the persistence of this rally despite rupee softening suggests external (dollar-denominated) price strength is overriding currency headwinds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 150000 INR round level offers immediate resistance. A close above this psychological barrier would confirm the recovery impulse; failure to sustain it would test the 149000 support zone established earlier in the session.
