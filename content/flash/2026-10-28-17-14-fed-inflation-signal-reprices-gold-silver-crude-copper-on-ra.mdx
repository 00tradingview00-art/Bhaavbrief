**WHAT HAPPENED**
The Federal Reserve is convening an October policy meeting to assess **elevated inflation** against midterm election timing, with markets pricing probability of further rate hikes or a pause depending on incoming CPI data.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
If the Fed signals **persistent rate hikes ahead**, real US yields will rise, increasing the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver** for central banks, ETF funds, and domestic jewellers hedging inventory. Simultaneously, a firmer dollar — the transmission channel — will make rupee-denominated import costs for gold and silver refiners and bullion dealers at Mumbai's wholesale markets more expensive, compressing their procurement margins. Higher US rates also dampen growth expectations, reducing industrial demand; **MCX Copper** and **MCX Crude** will reprice lower as traders anticipate slower Chinese and global manufacturing activity. If the Fed pauses, the reverse occurs: real yields compress, gold and silver attract safe-haven inflows, while crude and copper recover on reflation expectations.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Gold and silver fabricators importing refined bullion for jewellery manufacturing face immediate input cost repricing based on dollar strength, squeezing already-thin 2–3% processing margins. Retail jewellery chains setting festival season catalogue prices today must decide whether to lock in hedges or absorb potential cost increases. Copper wire manufacturers and cable producers serving infrastructure projects see procurement costs flex, forcing logistics operators to recalculate delivery economics. Households purchasing gold ornaments and solar panels ahead of Diwali absorb final price moves at retail.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Bullion importers and jewellery refiners operating on spot-plus-markup models will see working capital requirements shift within 24–48 hours if rate guidance hardens. **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver** will likely trade lower on hawkish Fed signals, while **MCX Crude** and **MCX Copper** reprice downward on growth-dampening expectations. Retail gold buyers purchasing ahead of major festivals face potential price volatility based on Fed communications clarity.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The next **US CPI print** (mid-November) and any Fed Chair commentary on terminal rate levels will confirm or reverse this commodity repricing. Chinese **manufacturing PMI data** in early November will signal whether global copper demand assumptions hold.
