**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver surged ₹13,584/kg on MCX while gold posted concurrent gains, driven by escalating geopolitical tensions pushing safe-haven demand across precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (near-month contract) and MCX Gold (near-month contract) are the primary contracts affected, with both contracts registering sharp upside momentum. Silver on MCX is currently trading around ₹268,026/kg, directly reflecting the COMEX spot price of $79.19/oz translated at USD/INR ₹95.70, plus applicable customs duty and import levies that structurally keep MCX at a premium to landed COMEX equivalent. Gold MCX mirrors COMEX at $4,581/oz, converting to approximately ₹162,083/10g — Indian traders must account for the 15% basic customs duty plus GST on gold imports, which widens the domestic premium during demand spikes and compresses arbitrage windows. The geopolitical risk premium currently embedded in COMEX prices transmits directly into MCX settlement, meaning any de-escalation event triggers amplified downside on MCX contracts due to the unwinding of both the dollar move and the risk premium simultaneously.

**WATCH**
Monitor the next COMEX silver settlement for a close above $80/oz, which would technically confirm continuation and push MCX Silver toward ₹271,000/kg. Any geopolitical de-escalation headline or USD/INR softening below ₹95.00 negates the current bullish structure.
