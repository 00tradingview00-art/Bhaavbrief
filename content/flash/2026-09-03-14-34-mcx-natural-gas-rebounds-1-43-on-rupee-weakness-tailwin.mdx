**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas rallied to 283 INR/mmBtu at 14:34 IST, posting a +1.43% gain from the previous close. The bounce reverses overnight weakness (275.9 INR/mmBtu at 21:00 IST) and recovers within striking distance of the 19:35 IST high of 284.2 INR/mmBtu. USD/INR weakened 0.55% to 94.52 INR, providing tailwind to rupee-denominated commodities. Gold (+1.06%) and silver (+0.96%) also posted gains, signaling broad-based strength across the precious metals complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Natural gas traders are navigating 8–9 INR/mmBtu ranges within a single session, reflecting elevated intraday volatility. The morning strength to 273.8 INR/mmBtu and subsequent recovery signals mean-reversion trading around the 280 level. The rupee's 0.55% depreciation is acting as a price support; any further INR weakness could extend the rally, while a stronger rupee could cap upside near 284 resistance.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 284.2 INR/mmBtu intraday high (set at 19:35 IST) remains the immediate resistance. A close above this level would confirm breakout intent; failure to reclaim it signals consolidation into 280–283 range.
