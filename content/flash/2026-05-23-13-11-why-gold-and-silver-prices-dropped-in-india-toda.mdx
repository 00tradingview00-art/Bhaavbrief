**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices in India declined in today's session, tracking a pullback in international COMEX prices from recent highs, with gold at $4,523/oz and silver at $76.20/oz translating to MCX reference levels of approximately ₹1,60,047/10g and ₹2,57,896/kg respectively.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) face direct downward pressure as COMEX spot weakness transmits through the USD/INR conversion at ₹95.70. The rupee holding near ₹95.70 provides limited cushion — a stronger dollar environment compresses the import parity benefit Indian traders typically rely on during COMEX dips. At current COMEX gold levels, the landed cost including 15% customs duty and GST keeps MCX gold structurally elevated above ₹1,55,000/10g, meaning any COMEX correction below $4,400/oz pushes MCX toward that critical import-cost floor. Silver's high import sensitivity means the ₹2,57,896/kg MCX level tracks COMEX moves with amplified rupee-adjusted volatility, leaving domestic spreads tight with little arbitrage buffer.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve commentary and weekly COMEX COT (Commitments of Traders) positioning data will confirm whether this pullback is a technical correction or the start of sustained selling pressure. A breach of $4,450/oz on COMEX gold is the key level MCX traders monitor for follow-through downside.
