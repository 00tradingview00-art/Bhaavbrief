**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver crashed **1.67%** to ₹223,255/kg in the evening session, leading MCX's broad selloff as renewed US-Iran conflict rhetoric triggered a rotation out of safe-haven metals. Gold held firmer at ₹141,985/10g despite the geopolitical noise, while crude slipped **0.63%** to ₹6,575/bbl—suggesting markets are parsing geopolitical premium separately from demand fundamentals. Natural gas fell **1.12%** to ₹308.50/mmBtu, indicating no Middle East contagion into energy markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's 1.67% decline reflects liquidation of its safe-haven bid rather than industrial-demand weakness, as the conflict narrative typically strengthens gold's inflation-hedge status while silver loses the safe-haven premium it commands during geopolitical stress. The divergence between silver's sharp decline and gold's stability suggests traders are specifically unwinding silver's geopolitical positioning, not responding to semiconductor or solar manufacturing demand signals. Crude's modest **0.63%** drop signals markets are pricing conflict-risk as contained to regional logistics rather than broad supply disruption—a geopolitical premium exists, but remains shallow relative to underlying demand trends.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
A solar panel manufacturer hedging silver exposure faces lower input costs if this liquidation sustains, though the move reflects positioning unwind rather than structural oversupply. An oil marketing company managing crude inventory hedge sees margin compression limited to **0.63%** because markets are not pricing broad supply shock—allowing normal procurement cycles to proceed. A jewellery exporter benefits from gold's stability above ₹141,985/10g, as silver's collapse creates unfavorable alloy ratios for near-term production scheduling.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Silver's **1.67%** underperformance versus gold reveals the move is safe-haven positioning unwind, not industrial demand destruction—natural gas's isolation at **1.12%** down confirms geopolitical risk is not transmitting into energy fundamentals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
USD/INR movement above ₹95.50 and silver's hold above ₹220,000/kg into close; any escalation language from official sources that re-ignites the safe-haven bid.
