**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR fell to 95.9 INR at 01:30 pm IST, marking a -1.01% decline from the previous close. The rupee's appreciation reflects continued foreign inflow sentiment and softer dollar demand in domestic markets. MCX precious metals responded positively: gold traded at 143855 INR/10g (+0.52%), silver at 223950 INR/kg (+0.82%). Energy contracts diverged sharply—crude at 8132 INR/bbl (-5.49%) and natural gas at 262.9 INR/mmBtu (-5.19%)—suggesting WTI weakness offset rupee tailwinds.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A weaker dollar structurally supports MCX gold and silver valuations in rupee terms, reducing imported inflation costs for Indian refiners. However, energy futures traders face cross-pressure: rupee strength typically helps dollar-priced crude competitiveness, but today's -5.49% crude crash and -5.19% natgas plunge indicate global oversupply dominated the rupee effect. The 95.9 level signals sustained capital inflows.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 95.5 support level and 96.0 psychological round number will determine whether rupee strength accelerates. A hold above 95.5 confirms inflow momentum; a break below signals deeper depreciation risk despite today's gains.
