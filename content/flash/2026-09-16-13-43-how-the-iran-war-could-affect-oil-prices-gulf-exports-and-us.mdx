**TITLE:** Iran Tensions Push MCX Crude, Gold Higher; Inflation Risk Looms

**WHAT HAPPENED**
Escalating geopolitical conflict involving Iran threatens to disrupt oil supply from the Persian Gulf, the world's largest crude export corridor, raising immediate concerns about global energy costs and inflationary spillover into India's import basket.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refiners importing **Arabian crude** face sudden repricing risk on forward contracts if **Brent crude** breaches $85–90/bbl; every $5/bbl move translates to ₹350–400 crore cumulative hedging losses across PSU and private refinery procurement desks. **Gold bullion dealers** importing doré bars see rupee-denominated acquisition costs rise if **spot gold** climbs on safe-haven demand, while **natural gas** consumers tied to **Henry Hub-indexed LNG contracts** experience higher feedstock bills as oil-linked gas pricing kicks in.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Petroleum product retailers holding inventory at pump stations face delayed margin recovery as crude oil input costs spike faster than retail price decontrol mechanisms can absorb. Aviation fuel (Jet-A1) procurement for airlines, already squeezed on narrow margins, tightens further as kerosene blending costs jump in tandem with crude. Downstream: **fertiliser manufacturers** using natural gas as feedstock renegotiate ammonia plant operating costs, delaying urea price stabilisation. **Power distribution utilities** sourcing imported **LNG** for thermal backup face higher per-MMBTU rates within weeks. End consumers feel the ripple through petrol/diesel pump prices (delayed but inevitable), **cooking gas cylinders**, and fertiliser costs that eventually reflect in food inflation through the agricultural input chain.

**BOTTOM LINE**
**Indian refineries** booking **Brent-indexed crudes** will absorb ₹800–1,200 crore in higher import costs if tensions sustain for 4+ weeks, compressing downstream margin guidance. Watch **MCX Crude Oil September futures** breach ₹6,800/bbl — a technical level that signals sustained supply-risk premium. **Household LPG cylinder prices** will rise within 15–20 days of sustained crude elevation above $90/bbl, cascading into kitchen inflation for retail consumers.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
**OPEC+ emergency meeting announcements** or **tanker transit disruptions through the Strait of Hormuz** in the next 48–72 hours will confirm whether this is tactical tension or structural supply loss. Watch **Iranian oil export data** released via tanker tracking platforms — any sustained drop below **2.5 million barrels/day** locks in premium pricing.
