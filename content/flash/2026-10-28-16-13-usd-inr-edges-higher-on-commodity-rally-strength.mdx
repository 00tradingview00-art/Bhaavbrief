**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR moved to 95.55 INR at 04:13 pm IST, marking a +0.07% gain from the previous close. The rupee's mild weakness follows renewed dollar strength driven by a broad-based commodity rally across MCX. Crude surged +1.39% to 9630 INR/bbl and natural gas climbed +1.53% to 292.8 INR/mmBtu, signaling dollar-denominated commodity demand, while gold retreated -0.13% to 143957 INR/10g and silver fell -0.86% to 195016 INR/kg.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The pair's consolidation near 95.55 INR reflects competing flows: energy commodity strength typically supports dollar bids, while precious metal weakness suggests profit-taking in safe-haven trades. MCX traders face a cross-current environment where energy hedging demand may sustain rupee pressure, but the modest +0.07% move signals consolidation rather than conviction directionally.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Track the 95.50–95.60 INR band. A sustained close above 95.60 INR would confirm fresh dollar momentum; a dip below 95.40 INR would signal renewed rupee strength aligned with precious metal stabilization.
