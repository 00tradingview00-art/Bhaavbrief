**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX (Multi Commodity Exchange) executed a stock split, causing its share price to decline approximately 80% in nominal terms — a mechanical reduction, not a value destruction event.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX equity stock split carries zero direct impact on MCX commodity futures contracts — Gold, Silver, Crude Oil, and Copper derivatives continue trading on MCX's platform entirely independent of the exchange's own share price movement. Gold MCX remains anchored near ₹160,802/10g, reflecting COMEX at $4,520/oz translated at USD/INR ₹96.21, plus applicable customs duty and transaction charges levied by MCX. The exchange's fee structure, margin requirements, and contract specifications remain unchanged post-split; Indian traders importing price discovery from COMEX still absorb the full currency conversion spread at ₹96.21. MCX's operational capacity to settle Crude Oil contracts near ₹9,666/bbl and Copper contracts derived from COMEX's $6.3498/lb pricing is unaffected by its equity restructuring.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange circular for any post-split changes to transaction fees or margin frameworks — any revision there directly alters cost of carry for commodity positions. The next MCX board disclosure or SEBI filing confirming unchanged fee schedules will be the definitive confirmation for traders.
