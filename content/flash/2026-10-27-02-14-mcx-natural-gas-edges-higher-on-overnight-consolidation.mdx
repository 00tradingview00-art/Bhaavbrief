**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas rose to 248.8 INR/mmBtu at 02:14 am IST, posting a +0.16% gain from the previous close. The modest overnight advance follows recent volatility, with the contract consolidating after sharp swings that saw it trade between 238.8 and 264.5 INR/mmBtu over the past sessions. USD/INR strengthened 0.29% to 95.2 INR, while MCX Crude declined -0.87% to 9715 INR/bbl, signaling divergent energy sector momentum.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The +0.16% bounce reflects stabilization rather than directional conviction in natural gas. Traders holding positions near recent lows face resistance from the rupee's strength and crude oil's retreat, both headwinds for INR-denominated energy futures. The price remains well below the 264.5 level touched at 03:06 am IST, keeping the near-term bias cautious.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 250 round-level hold through the Asian morning session. A break above 252 INR/mmBtu would signal stronger consolidation; failure below 247 INR/mmBtu resets the downside momentum.
