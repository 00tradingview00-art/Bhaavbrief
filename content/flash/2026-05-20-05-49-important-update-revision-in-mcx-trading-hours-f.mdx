MCX has revised its commodity derivatives trading hours effective November 3, 2025, through March 6, 2026, aligning the exchange's evening session close with the winter schedule adjustment.

This change directly affects all actively traded MCX contracts — including crude oil, natural gas, gold, silver, and base metals such as copper and aluminium. Evening session timing shifts compress the window for price discovery aligned with international markets, particularly London Metal Exchange and NYMEX. Traders in energy and bullion segments carrying overnight positions must recalibrate order placement and stop-loss triggers to the revised closing time to avoid execution gaps during the four-month winter period.
