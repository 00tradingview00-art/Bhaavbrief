**WHAT HAPPENED**
Ukraine suspended attacks on Russian oil refineries for three days while Russia intensified strikes on Ukrainian energy infrastructure, according to President Zelensky's statement.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The tactical pause reduces immediate supply-shock risk in global crude markets, easing pressure on **MCX Crude at ₹9787/bbl**, but Russian energy infrastructure damage remains unresolved — keeping upside risk intact for importers hedging geopolitical supply disruption. Indian refinery procurement desks benefit from near-term price stability on spot bids, though the continuation of Russian strikes on power grids signals sustained energy sector volatility, locking in hedging costs for refiners managing three-to-six-month forward positions. Simultaneously, safe-haven demand lifts **MCX Gold to ₹143,600/10g**, pushing rupee-denominated bullion costs higher for jewellers and accumulating households even as the USD/INR spot rate at **₹95.52** partially offsets dollar-linked import price rises.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Domestic crude-consuming refineries and power generation utilities face reduced immediate procurement urgency on the three-day pause, but must still price in tail-risk hedges for renewed supply disruption after the ceasefire window closes. Aviation fuel distributors and road transport operators managing diesel inventory costs see temporary breathing room on fuel surcharge passthrough, though forward contracting for Q4 still demands premium placement. Gold jewellery fabricators and organized retail chains holding unhedged finished-goods stock experience margin compression as working-metal costs rise in rupee terms, while festive-season retail jewellery buyers encounter higher unit pricing on branded ornament purchases — a direct cost transmission to households preparing for Diwali and wedding season purchases.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Refinery margins on Russian crude intake stabilize near-term but require elevated hedging premium for post-pause volatility exposure. **MCX Crude signals support above ₹9600/bbl if strikes resume; breach below signals demand destruction from refined-product cutbacks.** Retail jewellery buyers face sticky upside on ornament pricing until gold retraces below **₹141,000/10g**.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch for Russian energy infrastructure repair timelines and any extension or resumption of Ukrainian strikes post-ceasefire window. OPEC+ production guidance in early November will confirm whether crude supply tightness persists or demand fears overtake geopolitical risk.
