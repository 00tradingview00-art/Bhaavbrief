**WHAT HAPPENED**
Houthi forces have expanded shipping restrictions in the Red Sea beyond the Strait of Hormuz chokepoint, blocking Israeli-linked vessels and disrupting crude oil export routes to Europe and Asia, forcing tanker rerouting around the Cape of Good Hope.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The extended blockade increases voyage duration by 10-14 days and raises insurance premiums, forcing **IOCL, BPCL, and Reliance's crude procurement teams** to pay **WTI-linked premiums** on Gulf cargoes now competing for longer-haul tanker capacity. This mechanism directly widens the **India crude import basket cost** against dated Brent benchmarks, while **MCX natural gas traders** face upstream pressure as LNG shipments from Middle Eastern suppliers experience delayed scheduling and higher freight components.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
**IOCL and BPCL's refinery scheduling desks** encounter higher crude acquisition costs per barrel when sourcing from traditional OPEC+ suppliers, while **Reliance Industries' downstream divisions** absorb extended working capital cycles on in-transit inventory. **Air India, IndiGo, and petrol pump operators** on India's west coast experience cascading increases in aviation turbine fuel and retail petrol costs, as refinery gate prices reprice against landed crude expenses.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Integrated refiners like **Reliance** face **₹2,000-3,500 per tonne margin compression** on crude conversion if crude import premiums persist beyond 60 days. **MCX Crude Oil (Feb-Mar contracts)** should breach **₹6,800/bbl resistance** if Cape-rerouted tanker bookings confirm sustained supply tightness. Retail **petrol prices in Mumbai and Kochi** will inch upward by **₹1.50-2.00 per litre** within 2-3 weeks if refinery crude costs remain elevated.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **OPEC+ production adjustment signals** at next scheduled meeting and track **MCX Crude daily closing above ₹6,850/bbl** confirming sustained supply-side stress. Watch **tanker spot rate strength** in the next **Clarkson weekly report** for Cape-rerouted voyage confirmation.
