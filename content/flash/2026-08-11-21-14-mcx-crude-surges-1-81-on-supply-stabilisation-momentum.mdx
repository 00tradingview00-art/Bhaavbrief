**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 7944 INR/bbl at 21:14 IST, posting a +1.81% gain from the previous close. The advance sustains the recovery trajectory from the 7134 INR/bbl trough logged at 22:29 IST yesterday. Supply-side stabilisation sentiment remains the primary driver, supported by a firmer USD/INR at 95.43 INR. Gold and copper also posted modest gains, signalling broader commodity resilience.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sustained move above 7900 INR/bbl reinforces the recovery narrative established since yesterday's lows. For MCX crude traders, this consolidation in the 7900–7950 band signals institutional accumulation amid supply concern relief. The correlation with a stronger rupee (+0.23% on USD/INR) underscores the currency headwind traders must monitor; any rupee weakness could amplify upside momentum.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 7954 INR/bbl high from 12:10 pm IST today is the immediate resistance. A close above this level would confirm the recovery trend; a dip toward 7800 INR/bbl would test near-term support.
