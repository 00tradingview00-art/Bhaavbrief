**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 9927 INR/bbl at 02:06 pm IST, posting a +2.16% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 96 INR (+0.38%). The move extends the multi-leg recovery that began at 7918 INR/bbl on 21:26 IST yesterday, building on successive peaks at 9083 INR/bbl (19:19 IST), 9264 INR/bbl (14:33 IST), 9560 INR/bbl (18:23 IST), and 9722 INR/bbl (12:10 am IST). Rupee weakness is amplifying the dollar-denominated crude rally.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
MCX Crude has reclaimed ground after reversing sharply lower to 9509 INR/bbl at 02:31 pm IST yesterday (−2.19%). The current 9927 level sits within a fresh recovery phase supported by rupee depreciation. Cross-asset weakness in gold (−0.52%) and silver (−0.97%) reflects broad risk appetite, while natural gas (278.3 INR/mmBtu, −0.04%) shows stability. Traders are now positioned for potential resistance testing.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9722 INR/bbl peak from 12:10 am IST will define immediate resistance. A break above that level targets the psychological 10,000 INR/bbl round number. Failure to hold 9927 would signal exhaustion and risk a retest of 9509 INR/bbl support.
