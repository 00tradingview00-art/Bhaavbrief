**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude climbed to 7250 INR/bbl at 04:41 pm IST, posting a +0.50% gain from the previous close. The advance continues the recovery trajectory established from 7545 INR/bbl lows logged at 10:17 pm IST on 24 January, as supply-side sentiment stabilizes. USD/INR softened 0.22% to 95.12 INR, while precious metals—gold at 144416 INR/10g (+1.28%) and silver at 225996 INR/kg (+1.98%)—rally alongside crude's consolidation phase.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude traders are holding mid-consolidation levels after the +6.67% bounce off January lows. The rupee weakness (INR depreciation supports commodity INR pricing) and broad risk-on tone in gold and silver suggest underlying demand appetite. This is a stabilization move, not a breakout—positioning remains cautious between the 7545 support and prior 8961 peak.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
7300 INR/bbl resistance and the 7500 zone will confirm whether consolidation can sustain into fresh highs, or if crude retreats into support.
