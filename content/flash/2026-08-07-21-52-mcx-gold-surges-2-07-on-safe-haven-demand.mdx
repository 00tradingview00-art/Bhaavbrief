**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold accelerated to 151935 INR/10g at 21:52 IST, gaining +2.07% from the previous close. The sharp rally extends the recovery momentum that began at 11:23 IST when the contract touched 143814 INR/10g (+0.86%). Silver tracked higher at 231689 INR/kg (+2.59%), while Crude advanced +1.26% to 7470 INR/bbl. USD/INR held steady at 95.2 INR (+0.13%), supporting rupee-denominated gold strength.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's +2.07% move signals renewed safe-haven positioning as equities face headwinds. The broader commodity complex is mixed—silver and crude follow gold higher, but copper retreats -0.69% to 1366.55 INR/kg, reflecting divergent risk appetite. For MCX futures traders, this breakout tests conviction above the 150000-151000 zone established since 03:37 IST.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 152500 INR/10g psychological resistance level. A close and hold above it signals sustained safe-haven demand; failure to sustain would indicate profit-taking into the evening session.
