**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude inched up to 10215 INR/bbl at 12:40 am IST, posting a +0.06% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 95.94 INR (+0.33%). The move extends the recovery sequence that began at 7918 INR/bbl on 21:26 IST yesterday, though momentum remains subdued. Copper outperformed at +0.45%, while precious metals retreated—gold at 150723 INR/10g (−0.34%) and silver at 232036 INR/kg (−0.28%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude's flat tone masks underlying rupee depreciation support; every paisa of INR weakness bolsters import parity. After the sharp −2.19% reversal to 9509 INR/bbl at 02:31 pm IST yesterday, stabilization near 10215 INR/bbl suggests consolidation around the multi-leg rally's mid-point. Traders should monitor whether USD/INR momentum sustains or reverses.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 10200 INR/bbl round level now acts as minor support. Break above 10300 INR/bbl—or a fresh USD/INR spike above 96.00—would signal continuation of the broader recovery trajectory.
