**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 95.06 INR at 02:04 pm IST, marking a -0.19% fall from the previous close. The rupee's appreciation reflects continued foreign inflow strength in domestic markets. MCX precious metals moved lower in sympathy: gold at 152425 INR/10g (-1.32%), silver at 230438 INR/kg (-1.46%), while crude rebounded to 8303 INR/bbl (+1.89%), signaling divergent risk appetite across commodities.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's steady strength against dollar weakness narrows hedging spreads for MCX importers and weakens rupee-denominated commodity valuations. Gold and silver's sharper declines suggest foreign flows are rotating away from safe-haven metals into risk assets. Crude's counter-move signals energy demand resilience, but the broader INR weakness theme remains the dominant cross-asset driver for commodity positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 95.00 round number holds psychological support for USD/INR. A break below would confirm continued rupee momentum; a rebound above 95.30 would signal dollar demand returning to domestic markets.
