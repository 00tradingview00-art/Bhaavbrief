**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude crept to 8381 INR/bbl at 02:58 am IST, posting a +0.20% gain as USD/INR consolidated at 94.79 INR (−0.16% vs prev close). The rupee's marginal stability has provided shallow support after yesterday's volatile swings that saw the contract swing from intraday peaks near 9893 INR/bbl down to 9113 INR/bbl. Overnight consolidation reflects cautious positioning ahead of Asian session opens.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude's muted overnight move masks the intense volatility of the prior session—traders remain caught between rupee depreciation tailwinds and sharp intraday profit-taking. The 512 INR swing from peak to trough signals positioning risk. Gold's +2.75% outperformance and natural gas's +2.62% surge suggest capital rotation away from crude into safer commodities and energy hedges.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Hold 8350 INR/bbl support to confirm overnight consolidation. A break below signals resumption of yesterday's downtrend toward 9000 INR/bbl. Conversely, recovery toward 8450 INR/bbl would test whether fresh rupee weakness can reignite upside momentum.
