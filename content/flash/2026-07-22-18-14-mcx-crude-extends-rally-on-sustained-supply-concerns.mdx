**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude climbed to 8392 INR/bbl at 06:14 pm IST, marking a +2.97% gain from the previous close. The move extends the day's bullish momentum, reinforcing geopolitical supply risk narratives that have underpinned the rally since 10:23 pm IST yesterday. Across the complex, natural gas surged +2.32% to 281.7 INR/mmBtu, while gold and silver posted modest gains of +1.45% and +0.81% respectively. The rupee held steady at 96.56 INR.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, the price action confirms sustained risk-on positioning in oil despite profit-taking from earlier highs near 8570 INR/bbl. The consolidation above 8350 INR/bbl signals belief in the supply narrative. Cross-asset resilience in gold and natural gas reinforces a broader risk-premium environment where energy remains the primary outperformer.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8450 INR/bbl round number and yesterday's intra-day high of 8570 INR/bbl are critical resistance points. A decisive break above 8450 would confirm continuation; a dip below 8300 INR/bbl would signal consolidation reversal.
