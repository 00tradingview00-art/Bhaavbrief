**WHAT HAPPENED**
Ukraine's President Zelensky has proposed three conditions for an energy sector ceasefire with Russia while explicitly rejecting any sanctions relief ahead of formal negotiations, signalling prolonged geopolitical friction over Eastern European energy flows.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude oil importers and refinery procurement teams face sustained supply-chain uncertainty, keeping **MCX Crude at ₹9613/bbl** anchored above pre-escalation levels as Russian export flows remain at risk. Simultaneously, bullion dealers and institutional safe-haven accumulators are rotating into **MCX Gold at ₹142920/10g** — a classic flight-to-quality play — because prolonged sanctions regimes reduce global liquidity and boost precious metals' defensive appeal relative to energy assets tied to geopolitical disruption.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Petroleum refineries and fuel retailers operating on thin margins face inventory repricing pressure if crude supply tightens further, while their downstream fuel-pump pricing remains constrained by domestic regulation and consumer resistance. Power utilities and industrial units consuming natural gas for operations or feedstock face forward-curve volatility that forces them to either lock in hedges now or absorb future spot premium shocks — a choice that reshapes capex and maintenance scheduling. Jewellery retailers and gold coin dealers see finished-goods valuations rise in rupee terms, compressing retail margins on catalogue SKUs, while households deferring gold purchases weigh immediate affordability against longer holding periods if sanctions-driven safe-haven demand sustains the rally.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian petrochemical exporters operating on dollar-indexed feedstock costs will see gross margins compress if crude premiums persist while rupee strength against the dollar remains elusive at ₹95.21. **MCX Natural Gas contracts will remain elevated** as long as Eastern European pipeline negotiations stall, creating procurement headwinds for fertilizer and ammonia producers. Indian retail gold buyers will face costlier sovereign gold bond issuances and higher jewellery making charges if the metal sustains its upward pressure.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor next OPEC+ production decision and any formal multilateral sanctions announcements from Western governments; a breakdown in Ukraine–Russia energy talks will confirm crude's upside bias, while a ceasefire agreement could reverse safe-haven inflows into gold within days.
