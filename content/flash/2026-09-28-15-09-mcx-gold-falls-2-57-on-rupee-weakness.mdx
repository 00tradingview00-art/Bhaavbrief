**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 146996 INR/10g at 03:09 pm IST, posting a -2.57% loss from the previous close. The sharp pullback extends the weakness that began at 19:22 IST when gold fell to 150924 INR/10g. USD/INR edged up to 95.95 INR (+0.19%), eroding rupee support for bullion. Silver tracked lower at 226885 INR/kg (-3.33%), signaling broad precious metals pressure.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

Rupee depreciation against the dollar is the primary headwind, offsetting any safe-haven demand. Cross-asset weakness in silver and natural gas (-2.99%) suggests risk-off positioning, yet crude's +3.94% rally creates mixed macro signals for commodity traders.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
