**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver crashed to 188943 INR/kg at 03:27 am IST, declining 2.77% from the previous close. The sharp breakdown extends weakness across the precious metals complex—MCX Gold fell 0.94% to 142351 INR/10g, while MCX Copper slipped 0.34% to 1394.8 INR/kg. USD/INR held steady at 95.52, offering no support to rupee-denominated bullion. The move represents a continuation of multi-leg losses logged overnight.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's breach below the 190000 INR/kg zone signals renewed profit-taking after yesterday's 210936 INR/kg spike. For MCX futures traders, this 2.77% move reflects risk-off sentiment rippling through precious metals. The synchronized weakness in Gold (−0.94%) and Copper (−0.34%) suggests macro headwinds rather than Silver-specific flows, complicating hedging calculus for portfolio managers.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 185000 INR/kg support level—a breach would target the recent 188943 print as resistance and confirm deeper correction momentum through the Asian morning session.
