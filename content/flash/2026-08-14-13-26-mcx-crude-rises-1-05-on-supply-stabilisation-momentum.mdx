**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 7903 INR/bbl at 01:26 pm IST, posting a +1.05% gain from the previous close. The rally resumes the recovery trajectory that had pushed prices to 8001 INR/bbl at 10:39 am IST earlier in the session. Supply-side stabilisation sentiment continues to underpin strength, with natural gas also gaining +0.96% to 263.1 INR/mmBtu. Precious metals remain under pressure—gold at 153050 INR/10g and silver at 234589 INR/kg both declining.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The breach back above 7900 INR/bbl signals renewed conviction in the recovery from yesterday's 7754 INR/bbl low. For MCX futures traders, this consolidation within the 7754–8001 range indicates supply narrative is holding near-term bid. Rupee weakness at 95.42 INR provides marginal tailwind; however, the absence of gold-silver participation warns of broader risk-off undertones beneath commodity strength.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8000 INR/bbl psychological level is critical resistance. A sustained break above it would confirm fresh upside conviction; failure to hold would target the 7878 INR/bbl support zone logged earlier in the session.
