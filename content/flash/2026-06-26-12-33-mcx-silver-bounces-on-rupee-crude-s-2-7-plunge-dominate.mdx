**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rallied **+0.76% to ₹221,351/kg** in this afternoon session despite gold and silver futures softening in global markets. **WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's domestic uptick against weakening global prices points to rupee strength (USD/INR at **₹94.39**) providing a structural floor for INR-denominated MCX contracts. Import parity on silver: global spot (~$26.5/oz) × 94.39 × import duty (~5%) lands near **₹220,500/kg** — MCX trading at premium suggests short covering or domestic physical demand. Crude's sharp decline reflects macro headwinds: either demand destruction signals from growth-sensitive economies or unexpected supply builds in US inventory data.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Crude's -2.70% collapse dominates structural risk; silver's rupee-cushioned bounce masks global weakness. Traders should treat crude weakness as session-defining until ₹6,550 support holds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
