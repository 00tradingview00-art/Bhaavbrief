**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold climbed to 152950 INR/10g at 07:17 pm IST, posting a +0.31% gain from the previous close. The modest recovery follows the earlier decline to 150924 INR/10g at 19:22 IST. USD/INR holding steady at 95.93 INR (−0.03%) is providing steady rupee support for bullion. Silver outpaced gold with a +1.32% jump to 237876 INR/kg, while copper added +1.44%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold has stabilized after morning weakness, with the rupee anchor preventing deeper losses. The +0.31% move sits well below the +1.79% intraday rally peak seen at 18:17 IST, signaling consolidation rather than conviction. Cross-asset strength in silver and copper suggests risk-on tone, though gold's lag indicates caution among traders positioned for further dollar strength.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch 152400–152500 INR/10g support. A break below invalidates the evening stabilization; a hold above 153000 INR/10g would signal a genuine retest of the 155132 INR/10g peak from 18:17 IST.
