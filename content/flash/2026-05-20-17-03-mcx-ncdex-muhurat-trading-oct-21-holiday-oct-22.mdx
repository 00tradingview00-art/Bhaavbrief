MCX and NCDEX will conduct a special Muhurat trading session on October 21, followed by a full exchange holiday on October 22 on account of Diwali.

The compressed trading window on October 21 will affect all actively traded MCX commodities — gold, silver, crude oil, and base metals. Volumes typically thin sharply during Muhurat sessions as institutional participants step back, widening bid-ask spreads across contracts. The subsequent full holiday on October 22 means any open positions carried from the Muhurat session will remain unhedged for an additional day, elevating overnight risk exposure for traders holding leveraged positions in volatile commodities like crude oil and natural gas.
