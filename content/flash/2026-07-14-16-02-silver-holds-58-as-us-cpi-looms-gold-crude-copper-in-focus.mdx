**WHAT HAPPENED**
**Silver** edged higher near **$58/oz** as markets await **US CPI data** release, a key inflation gauge that will influence Federal Reserve policy expectations and dollar strength.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Softer-than-expected US inflation would lower real yields, reducing the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding precious metals for global funds and central banks including the RBI, while simultaneously weakening the dollar and lowering rupee-denominated import costs for jewellers and refiners importing bullion. This transmission mechanism directly reprices **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver** upward, as lower real rates make precious metals more attractive relative to USD bonds. A hotter CPI print reverses this dynamic, strengthening the dollar and pressuring both precious metals while lifting **MCX Crude** as growth expectations firm, though **MCX Copper** would face conflicting signals—stronger growth supporting demand from Chinese fabricators against a firmer dollar raising hedging costs for Asian copper importers.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Bullion refiners and jewellery exporters in Zaveri Bazaar and Surat see their dollar-denominated raw material costs fluctuate with rupee strength, while corporate treasury desks hedging USD payables and crude importers recalibrate their MCX futures positions in real time. Retail gold buyers timing Diwali season purchases and petrol pump operators passing through crude costs to consumers absorb the direct price impact within days.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Bullion refiners' margin compression or expansion hinges directly on whether US CPI confirms or breaks the disinflation narrative. **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver** will reprice higher if CPI undershoots expectations, signalling the next pivot point near **₹76,500 and ₹95,000** respectively. Retail gold jewellery prices and pump petrol rates will track the dollar move within 48 hours.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
US **CPI release** (expected within 48 hours) and any **Fed speaker commentary** on rate cuts will extend or reverse this trend. Chinese **manufacturing PMI** data next week will clarify copper demand signals.
