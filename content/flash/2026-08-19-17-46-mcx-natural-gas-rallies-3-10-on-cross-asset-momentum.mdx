**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas surged to 273 INR/mmBtu at 05:46 pm IST, gaining +3.10% from the previous close. The rally extends a volatile session marked by earlier strength at 263.6 INR/mmBtu (+3.13%) at 03:14 pm IST. Crude Oil climbed +0.47% to 8126 INR/bbl, while Gold (+0.24%) and Silver (-0.62%) showed mixed momentum. The rupee held steady at 95.74 INR (+0.05%), limiting currency headwinds.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The +3.10% move signals renewed bullish positioning after overnight liquidation wiped 4.62% on 01:27 am IST. Natural gas is testing recovery momentum off the 264.5 INR/mmBtu collapse level. Traders face a critical test: whether this rebound holds or reverts to the volatility pattern seen across the prior 48 hours. Crude's modest +0.47% gain provides mild tailwind but lacks conviction.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 282.6 INR/mmBtu resistance level from 10:25 pm IST overnight session. If Natural Gas reclaims that level, the rally has structural legs. A break below 270 INR/mmBtu would signal rejection of the current bounce.
