**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold futures surged 6.46% to ₹1,63,360/10g and MCX Silver jumped 7.25% to approximately ₹3,00,000/kg on May 13, driven by the broader COMEX rally with spot gold at $4,520/oz and silver at $76.34/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold June and Silver July contracts are the primary instruments affected by this move. The domestic gold print of ₹1,63,360/10g trades at a premium relative to the live COMEX-derived benchmark of ₹1,59,935/10g, reflecting the embedded customs duty component (currently 6% basic + AIDC) and INR conversion at ₹95.70. This spread of approximately ₹3,425/10g represents the import parity cushion that Indian traders must account for when hedging against COMEX positions. For silver, the MCX price near ₹3,00,000/kg compares against the COMEX-derived ₹2,58,366/kg, a spread of roughly ₹41,634/kg — again pricing in duty and freight, but any sharp INR depreciation beyond ₹95.70 widens this spread further and amplifies domestic price gains independent of COMEX direction.

**WATCH**
The US CPI print (May 13, US session) is the immediate catalyst that will either sustain or reverse this COMEX-driven rally; a softer-than-expected reading reinforces gold above $4,500 and keeps MCX elevated, while a hawkish surprise compresses the move.
