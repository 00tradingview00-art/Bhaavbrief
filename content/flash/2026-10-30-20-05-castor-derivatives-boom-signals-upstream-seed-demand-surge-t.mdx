**WHAT HAPPENED**
India's **castor oil derivatives market** is projected to expand significantly through 2035, signalling sustained downstream demand for processed castor products across industrial and consumer applications.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crushers and oil refiners processing **castor seed** into oil and derivatives face a multi-year demand visibility that will anchor their forward procurement strategies against NCDEX spot price volatility. This structural growth in derivatives — used in pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, lubricants, and bio-based polymers — means seed millers in Gujarat and Rajasthan will increasingly lock in castor seed supplies through forward contracts rather than spot-only purchases, effectively pulling up the **Castor Seed NCDEX contract basis** as derivative manufacturers secure their raw material pipelines ahead of production commitments.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Castor farmers across Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, and Rajasthan will see procurement agents from oil refineries competing for inventory earlier in the season, tightening spot mandi prices in Mehsana and Siddipet as crushers front-load their sourcing to meet medium-term derivatives contracts. Pharmaceutical manufacturers and cosmetic brands relying on **castor-derived actives** — castor wax, ricinoleic acid, sebacic acid — will face more stable but potentially elevated input costs as refiners pass through the premium from longer-term seed procurement commitments into their downstream pricing. Retail consumers purchasing castor oil-based personal care products and households buying industrial lubricants will absorb incremental cost where brands cannot absorb margin compression.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Mehsana mandi traders will see seed arrivals concentrated in forward-contracted lots rather than spot offerings, compressing their working capital float. The **NCDEX Castor Seed contract** will likely price in a forward premium reflecting derivative sector hedging demand. Consumers purchasing castor oil supplements and industrial lubricant users will face steady or moderately elevated pricing through the forecast window.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Track **NCDEX castor seed open interest growth** in forward contracts (November 2025 onwards) and watch for Gujarat government procurement data indicating crusher stockpiling patterns.
