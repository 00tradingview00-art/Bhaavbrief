**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper declined to 1371.5 INR/kg at 12:17 am IST, falling −1.31% from the previous close. The pullback follows intraday volatility that saw copper peak at 1395.65 INR/kg at 09:36 am IST. Precious metals extended losses—Gold at 151699 INR/10g (−1.79%), Silver at 228993 INR/kg (−2.07%)—signaling broad-based risk-off momentum. USD/INR stability at 95.07 (−0.18%) offers modest support, but base metals weakness persists across the complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The overnight retreat erases morning gains and signals fragility in copper's recovery pattern. For MCX futures traders, the break below intraday highs suggests profit-taking after the +1.07% advance at 09:36 am IST. Precious metals' steeper declines indicate demand destruction concerns outweighing currency tailwinds. Risk positioning has shifted defensively overnight.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 1365 INR/kg support level. A close below this overnight range would confirm weakness; a bounce back toward 1385 INR/kg would test the morning's resilience.
