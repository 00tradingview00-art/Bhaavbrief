**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas fell to 287.6 INR/mmBtu at 20:48 IST, extending a -1.51% decline from the previous close. The pullback follows volatile swings across the energy complex, with MCX Crude down -2.04% to 9525 INR/bbl. USD/INR softened marginally by -0.15% to 95.05 INR, offering mild support to rupee-denominated commodities. Precious metals bucked the trend, with MCX Gold and Silver both posting modest gains of +0.67% and +0.61% respectively.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The renewed softness in natural gas signals continued energy sector pressure despite intraday volatility. Traders holding positions above 290 INR/mmBtu face margin pressure as the contract tests lower consolidation levels. The divergence between energy weakness and precious metals strength underscores flight-to-safety positioning in the broader MCX complex.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 280 INR/mmBtu support level. A break below signals potential acceleration toward the recent 238.8 INR/mmBtu low set at 15:52 IST on 08-Jan.
