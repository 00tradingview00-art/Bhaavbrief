**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 9493 INR/bbl at 12:51 am IST, posting a +3.10% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 94.87 INR (+0.30% vs prev close). The rupee's depreciation has restored export competitiveness for domestic crude producers after overnight consolidation. Natural gas climbed to 268.6 INR/mmBtu (+1.47%), while gold retreated to 143303 INR/10g (–1.90%), signaling risk-on sentiment.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, the rupee depreciation has reignited the export margin tailwind that crude producers depend on. The +3.10% move breaks the recent oscillation pattern between 9275–9463 INR/bbl. Cross-asset weakness in gold and copper suggests broad dollar strength is driving the energy rebound, not crude-specific fundamentals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 9500 INR/bbl round level—a break above signals conviction in rupee-driven strength. A retest of the 9567 INR/bbl high from 11:02 pm IST would confirm the rally's legs.
