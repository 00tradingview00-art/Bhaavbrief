**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas rallied to 263.5 INR/mmBtu at 11:44 pm IST, gaining 0.96% from the previous close. The recovery follows the sharp 4.62% collapse at 01:27 am IST to 264.5 INR/mmBtu that had wiped out overnight gains. The rebound suggests stabilization after profit-taking, with the broader energy complex showing modest strength: MCX Crude at 8120 INR/bbl (+0.07%), while rupee weakness at 95.64 INR (-0.14%) provides marginal support.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The bounce signals recovery demand after capitulation, but volume remains thin near resistance. Traders are testing whether 263.5 holds as a floor or if volatility resumes toward the overnight high of 282.6 INR/mmBtu. Cross-asset correlation with crude and precious metals (Gold +0.30%, Silver +0.79%) remains intact, sustaining conviction in the upside bias despite recent intraday whipsaws.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break and hold above 265 INR/mmBtu will signal sustained recovery toward 275–280 range. Failure to close above 263 risks revisit of 256.6 lows set at 12:21 am IST.
