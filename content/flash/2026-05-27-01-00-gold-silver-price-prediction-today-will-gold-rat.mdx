**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on MCX is trading near ₹1,60,440/10g, sustaining above the psychologically critical ₹1,00,000/10g threshold that domestic markets breached earlier this year, as COMEX spot holds firm at $4,535/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold June and August futures are the primary contracts in focus, with spot COMEX at $4,535/oz translating directly to ₹1,60,440/10g at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹95.69 — after applying India's 6% customs duty plus GST on import value. The COMEX-to-MCX conversion mechanics mean every ₹1 move in USD/INR adds approximately ₹320-330/10g to domestic gold prices, creating a currency amplification effect that Indian traders must price independently of the dollar move. MCX Silver is simultaneously tracking at ₹2,60,590/kg against COMEX silver at $77.00/oz, with import duty maintaining a structural premium over international parity for Indian spot buyers. The spread between MCX futures and landed import cost remains the key arbitrage variable keeping large-lot institutional hedgers active on both contracts.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve meeting minutes release and any revision to U.S. CPI data will either reinforce or pressure the $4,500/oz COMEX support level — a break below that floor pulls MCX Gold toward ₹1,55,800/10g at current USD/INR. Simultaneously, any RBI intervention that shifts USD/INR below ₹94.50 compresses MCX prices independent of COMEX direction.
