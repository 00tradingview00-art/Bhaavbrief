**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver surged to a record high on COMEX at $76.20/oz while MCX rates topped ₹3.19 lakh/kg, breaching the live MCX reference level of ₹2,57,896/kg by approximately 23.7%.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver contract (SILVERM and SILVER kg contracts) is directly exposed to this COMEX breakout, with the current live MCX translation at ₹2,57,896/kg already reflecting the USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. The gap between the reported ₹3.19 lakh MCX print and the COMEX-derived ₹2,57,896/kg level signals either a premium for near-month delivery, domestic supply tightness, or a data discrepancy requiring verification against the active contract expiry. Indian importers face elevated landed costs as silver import duties (typically 15% basic customs duty plus GST) compound the COMEX surge, widening the effective cost basis well above the raw COMEX-to-INR conversion. Traders holding short positions on MCX Silver face margin pressure as the contract tracks the COMEX record; the import parity ceiling now functions as the structural price floor.

**WATCH**
The $100/oz psychological resistance level on COMEX is the immediate technical trigger — a confirmed close above $80/oz on COMEX will determine whether the MCX contract sustains above ₹3 lakh/kg. U.S. CPI data and Fed commentary remain the macro catalyst to confirm or arrest this momentum.
