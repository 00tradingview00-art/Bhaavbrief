**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold advanced to 141412 INR/10g at 04:41 pm IST, gaining +0.63% from the previous close. The move reflects broad-based strength across the precious metals complex—MCX Silver surged +2.45% to 187050 INR/kg and MCX Copper rose +0.51% to 1344.15 INR/kg. USD/INR weakened 0.14% to 93.9, easing rupee headwinds and supporting INR-denominated precious metals valuations.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold traders face a multi-driver rally: rupee depreciation reduces the local currency cost of dollar-priced bullion, while synchronized strength in silver and copper signals risk-on sentiment and broad commodity demand. The move extends the uptrend from 138447 INR/10g established earlier, though volatility around 142000–144045 INR/10g suggests consolidation pressure.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 143729 INR/10g resistance level (04:52 pm IST high) remains the near-term breakout target. A close above 143500 would confirm continuation; failure to hold 141000 would signal reversal.
