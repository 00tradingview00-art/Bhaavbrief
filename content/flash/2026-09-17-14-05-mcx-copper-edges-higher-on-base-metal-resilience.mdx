**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper climbed to 1383 INR/kg at 02:05 pm IST, advancing +0.63% from the previous close. The move reflects modest momentum in the base metals complex even as precious metals retreat—Gold at 151639 INR/10g (−0.55%) and Silver at 233471 INR/kg (−0.56%). USD/INR stability at 95.95 (−0.01%) provided a neutral backdrop, while Crude weakness at 9719 INR/bbl (−1.02%) underscores selective commodity demand.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's outperformance versus the precious metals complex signals tactical strength in industrial demand expectations. The 1383 level holds above the earlier 1355.5 INR/kg pullback at 02:17 pm IST, suggesting traders are defending near-term support. This divergence—base metals rallying while gold and silver fade—hints at rotational positioning ahead of broader macroeconomic signals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break above 1410.55 INR/kg (the 10:29 pm IST high) would confirm the recovery trajectory. Failure to hold 1380 INR/kg risks reverting to the 1355–1371 INR/kg support band established overnight.
