**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices declined on MCX on September 24 due to profit booking by traders following a recent rally.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (current reference: ₹162,115/10g) and MCX Silver (current reference: ₹263,979/kg) are the directly affected contracts. Profit booking after a sustained upward move creates short-term selling pressure, pulling spot prices lower on the exchange. For Indian traders, the MCX price already embeds the USD/INR rate of ₹95.24 plus applicable customs duty (currently 6% on gold imports), meaning any rupee depreciation acts as a floor that limits the downside even when COMEX softens. The spread between COMEX Gold at $4,604/oz and MCX levels reflects both the duty-paid import cost and currency conversion, so domestic prices do not fall in lockstep with any global correction.

**WATCH**
Track the next US PCE inflation print and any RBI commentary on the rupee, as a move in USD/INR beyond ₹95.50 will mechanically support MCX gold even if COMEX gold retreats further. A breach below ₹160,000/10g on MCX Gold would signal that profit booking has deepened beyond a routine pullback.
