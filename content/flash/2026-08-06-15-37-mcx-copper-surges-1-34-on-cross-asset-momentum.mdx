**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper climbed to 1392.2 INR/kg at 03:37 pm IST, rising +1.34% from the previous close. The move reflects renewed strength across the MCX complex: Gold gained +0.85% to 149760 INR/10g, Crude advanced +0.94% to 7176 INR/bbl, while Silver edged lower −0.16% to 227230 INR/kg. The rupee strengthened +0.15% to 95.24 INR, providing a mild headwind offset by broader commodity demand signals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's sustained climb above 1361.5 INR/kg (from 11:32 am) signals durable risk appetite in the industrial metals complex. For MCX futures traders, this extended rally into afternoon trade suggests the morning momentum has fresh legs, driven by cross-asset inflows rather than standalone rupee weakness. The selective strength—Gold and Crude joining Copper—hints at macro liquidity flows.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Track the 1400 INR/kg round level. A close and hold above it signals acceleration; pullback below 1370 INR/kg would suggest the momentum lacked conviction.
