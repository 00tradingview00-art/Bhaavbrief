**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold and Silver contracts recovered on Diwali, with Gold trading near ₹1,62,115/10g and Silver near ₹2,63,979/kg, aligned with COMEX Gold at $4,604/oz and Silver at $78.37/oz at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹95.24.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold October and December contracts are the primary instruments affected by this festive-session recovery. The COMEX-to-MCX conversion at ₹95.24 produces a base import parity of approximately ₹1,62,115/10g; any premium above this level on MCX reflects India's 15% customs duty plus GST loading embedded in domestic spot prices. Silver's MCX equivalent of ₹2,63,979/kg tracks COMEX at $78.37/oz, and Indian traders monitor the MCX-COMEX spread closely — a widening spread signals elevated domestic physical demand, which Diwali buying seasonally amplifies. For Indian traders, rupee depreciation risk is already priced into current levels; any INR weakness beyond ₹95.50 mechanically lifts MCX floor prices without any change in COMEX.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the US PCE inflation print and Federal Reserve commentary due this week — a hawkish surprise strengthens the dollar, pressuring COMEX Gold below $4,580/oz and pulling MCX contracts lower despite festive demand support. Monitor MCX Gold December open interest post-Diwali session close for confirmation of fresh longs or profit-booking.
