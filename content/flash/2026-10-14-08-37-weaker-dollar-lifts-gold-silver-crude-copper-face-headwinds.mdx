**WHAT HAPPENED**
The **US dollar index** fell as markets repriced **Federal Reserve rate-cut probabilities** upward ahead of the next **CPI print and Fed policy meeting**, reducing the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding commodities.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A weaker dollar directly raises the rupee-denominated cost of importing **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver** into India, but simultaneously reduces the global dollar price of these metals, creating a net tailwind for Indian buyers on international benchmarks. Lower US real yields — implied by cut expectations — reduce the opportunity cost of holding gold for **RBI reserves, institutional investors, and domestic ETF holders**, supporting **MCX Gold** futures. Conversely, **MCX Crude** and **MCX Copper** face demand headwinds because a weaker dollar typically signals growth concerns, reducing fuel consumption and industrial metal demand from **Chinese manufacturers and construction firms** who price in dollars. The transmission flows through **MCX Copper** as fabricators in India (wiring, electronics, plumbing) face input volatility but also lower global spot costs competing with futures pricing.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Bullion importers and refinery operators hedging dollar exposure see their **rupee liability shrink on forward contracts**, while jewellery retailers at **Zaveri Bazaar and tier-2 gold markets** reset finished-goods pricing downward, compressing working capital. Copper wire-drawing mills and electrical component makers absorb lower global input costs but face delayed procurement decisions as they wait for **MCX Copper** to stabilize. Retail gold buyers and festival jewellery purchasers benefit from lower ornament prices, while diesel consumers and truck operators experience temporary relief if **MCX Crude** weakness feeds into fuel pricing.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Jewellery retailers face margin compression as **MCX Gold** reprices to global weakness while rupee strength dampens import premiums. **MCX Gold** is likely to find support if **US real yields** decline further, while **MCX Copper** remains vulnerable if Chinese PMI data disappoints this week. Retail gold ornament buyers will see price relief at showrooms within 2–3 trading sessions.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the **US CPI release date** and the **FOMC decision announcement** for confirmation of rate-cut timing. Watch **Chinese manufacturing PMI data** this week — a print below **50** would extend weakness in **MCX Copper** and reinforce dollar-driven commodity repricing.
