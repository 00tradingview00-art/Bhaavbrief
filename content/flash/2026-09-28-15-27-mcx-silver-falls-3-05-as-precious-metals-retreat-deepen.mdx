**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver crashed to 227548 INR/kg at 03:27 pm IST, posting a −3.05% loss from the previous close. The sharp decline reverses the +1.99% recovery logged at 02:36 pm IST and extends into fresh weakness territory. Gold mirrors the slide at −2.36% to 147316 INR/10g. USD/INR strength (+0.28% to 96.39) amplifies pressure on rupee-denominated bullion amid broad risk-off sentiment.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 6.36% swing from the 02:36 pm IST peak (242950 INR/kg) to current levels signals intraday volatility and weakening conviction in precious metals. The 227548 level breaks below the 229803 INR/kg trough from prior session, testing trader risk tolerance. Copper weakness (−1.16%) and natgas decline (−3.06%) reinforce deflationary bias despite crude's +4.29% bounce.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support holds at 225000 INR/kg psychological level. A close below 227000 signals continuation toward 220000. Resistance rebuilds at 230000 INR/kg — breach there re-engages bulls.
