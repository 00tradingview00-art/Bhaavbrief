**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 146482 INR/10g at 12:57 am IST, posting a −0.53% loss from the previous close. The pullback extends intraday volatility following yesterday's sharp recovery to 148354 INR/10g at 07:36 pm IST. USD/INR remained flat at 95.05, offering no tailwind for gold. Silver outperformed with +0.80% to 211500 INR/kg, while crude and copper posted modest gains.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold traders face conflicting signals: the rupee's stability removes a supportive factor, yet silver's relative strength suggests selective precious metal demand persists. The current level sits below yesterday's recovery high, signaling continued consolidation rather than fresh directional conviction in the 146400–148350 range.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 146000 INR/10g support and 148000 INR/10g resistance will define whether this consolidation holds or yields a breakout in either direction.
