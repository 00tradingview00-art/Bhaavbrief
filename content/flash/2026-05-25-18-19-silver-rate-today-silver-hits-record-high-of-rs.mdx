**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on MCX hit a fresh all-time high of ₹2,54,174/kg while gold surpassed ₹1,40,444/10g, with both metals registering record domestic peaks in the same session.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver continuous contract and MCX Gold continuous contract are both directly affected. Silver's MCX record of ₹2,54,174/kg sits below the live COMEX-derived import parity of ₹2,64,499/kg, indicating a discount of approximately ₹10,325/kg on MCX versus theoretical landed cost — this spread reflects customs duty absorption and near-term demand-supply dynamics at the exchange level. Gold's MCX print of ₹1,40,444/10g compares against the live COMEX-derived level of ₹1,62,826/10g, a gap that includes India's 15% customs duty plus GST on gold imports, meaning the domestic price is not yet pricing in full COMEX parity. For Indian traders, the wide silver spread signals that domestic physical demand and import logistics are acting as a price buffer against full COMEX transmission at USD/INR ₹95.70.
