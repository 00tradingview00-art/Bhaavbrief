**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude slipped to 7794 INR/bbl at 06:36 pm IST, extending losses with a -2.05% decline from the previous close. The retreat marks a continuation of the pullback from the 8961 INR/bbl peak posted at 10:25 pm IST yesterday, as initial supply concerns moderate. USD/INR softened -0.75% to 95.84 INR, while natural gas weakened -1.36% to 260.9 INR/mmBtu across the complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The consistent downtrend from yesterday's spike signals traders are unwinding geopolitical risk premiums that had driven crude sharply higher. At 7794 INR/bbl, the market is testing whether the initial supply shock narrative has lost conviction. Rupee strength (USD/INR down) is adding headwind to INR-denominated crude, compounding the fundamental weakness.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 7700 INR/bbl round level—a breakdown here would confirm the unwind has accelerated; a hold would suggest support is forming ahead of overnight WTI action.
