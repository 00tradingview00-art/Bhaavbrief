**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 141534 INR/10g at 03:42 pm IST, posting a −0.74% loss from the previous close. The pullback mirrors broader precious metals weakness, with MCX Silver falling 2.72% to 184300 INR/kg. USD/INR edged 0.25% lower to 95.27, removing near-term currency support. Energy strength—MCX Crude +1.90% to 9893 INR/bbl—signals risk-on rotation away from safe-haven metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's break below intraday support extends volatility witnessed since the 07:17 pm IST dip to 142502 on 03:42 pm IST. Cross-asset flows favoring crude over precious metals suggest appetite for cyclical exposure. Silver's sharper 2.72% loss underscores de-risking momentum, pressuring gold despite USD weakness. Traders face renewed downside risk if sentiment remains tilted toward energy.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
