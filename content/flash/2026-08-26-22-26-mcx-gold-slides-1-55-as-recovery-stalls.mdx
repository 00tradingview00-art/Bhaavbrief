**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold collapsed to 160362 INR/10g at 22:26 IST, posting a -1.55% loss and erasing the prior session's +1.98% rally that had held above 162000 INR/10g. Silver mirrored weakness at 240407 INR/kg (-1.52%). The pullback occurred as crude recovered +1.03% to 7918 INR/bbl and natural gas surged +3.22% to 279 INR/mmBtu, signaling a shift in risk appetite away from safe-haven metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The break below 162000 INR/10g signals exhaustion of the multi-session recovery arc established since earlier in the evening. For MCX futures traders, the loss of this support level reopens downside exposure toward 160000 INR/10g. The synchronized weakness in both gold and silver, coupled with strength in energy, indicates a broader risk-on rotation that pressures precious metals positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch whether 160000 INR/10g holds as the next support floor at 22:26 IST levels. A breach below that round number will confirm the recovery's failure and extend losses toward 158000 INR/10g.
