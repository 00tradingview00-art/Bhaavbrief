**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 8977 INR/bbl at 05:01 pm IST, posting a +4.97% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 97.13 INR (+0.06% vs prev close). The rupee's renewed weakness has rekindled export competitiveness for domestic crude, reversing the overnight losses logged at 09:39 pm IST when the pair had retreated to 96.27 INR. Natural gas tracked the crude strength, rising +1.48% to 314.5 INR/mmBtu, while gold and silver remained subdued.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee depreciation narrative has reasserted control over crude price discovery on MCX. For futures traders, the 8977 level represents a critical recovery point—nearly 350 bucks above the 09:39 pm reversal low—signalling renewed conviction in the export-tailwind trade. Cross-asset weakness in silver (-1.32%) and copper flatness suggests selective crude bid rather than broad commodity strength.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9000 INR/bbl round number and the prior intraday peak of 9197 INR/bbl logged at 03:09 pm IST. A break above 9000 will confirm the rupee-driven rally has legs; failure there invites another reversal.
