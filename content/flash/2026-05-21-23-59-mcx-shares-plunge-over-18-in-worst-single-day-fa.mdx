**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX shares crashed over 18% in their worst single-day decline since the Covid crash, triggering lower circuits simultaneously across gold, silver, and copper futures contracts on the exchange.

**MCX IMPACT**
The lower circuit halt directly froze price discovery on MCX Gold (active contract ~₹161,878/10g) and MCX Silver (~₹263,074/kg), severing the live arbitrage link between Indian futures and COMEX benchmarks during the circuit period. When circuits are triggered, Indian traders cannot execute hedges or exit positions, meaning basis risk against COMEX gold ($4,551/oz) and COMEX copper ($6.3545/lb) widens uncontrollably — open positions accumulate unhedged mark-to-market losses with no exit. For import-linked participants, the frozen MCX copper price disconnects from the COMEX-plus-customs-duty import cost structure, creating artificial spreads that persist until trading resumes. The exchange's stock collapse also raises margin and counterparty confidence concerns among clearing members, which mechanically tightens available liquidity across all MCX commodity segments.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange circular on circuit relaxation timings and SEBI's response statement — resumption of normal trading and restoration of COMEX-MCX price convergence within normal customs duty spreads will signal market function has stabilized.
