**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude jumped to 8570 INR/bbl at 02:15 am IST, posting a +2.46% gain as USD/INR weakened to 94.53 INR (−0.31% vs prev close). The rupee's sharp appreciation has offset some gains for domestic crude traders. Gold climbed +0.51% to 143970 INR/10g, signalling renewed risk-off sentiment, while copper sold off −1.12% to 1352.75 INR/kg, reflecting mixed commodity demand signals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 2.46% rally marks a meaningful reversal after yesterday's −4.78% drop to 9462 INR/bbl at 06:14 pm IST. Traders are pricing in fresh supply concerns offsetting the rupee headwind. Cross-asset weakness in copper combined with gold strength suggests a bifurcated risk narrative—defensive flows supporting crude via geopolitical premium rather than growth-led demand.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8600 INR/bbl round number is the immediate resistance. A break above signals conviction in the supply tightness thesis; a pullback below 8500 INR/bbl would confirm the rally as tactical profit-taking.
