**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 95.37 INR at 08:46 pm IST, registering a -0.07% fall from the previous close. The rupee's mild appreciation reflects sustained foreign inflow momentum persisting through the evening session. MCX precious metals showed mixed signals: gold at 158468 INR/10g (-0.33%), silver at 242243 INR/kg (+0.66%), while crude at 7957 INR/bbl (-0.09%) and copper at 1384.4 INR/kg (+0.36%) remained relatively flat.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's incremental strength continues to weigh on dollar demand in domestic markets, though the -0.07% move suggests consolidation rather than directional conviction. For MCX traders, this cross-asset backdrop—gold weakness offset by silver resilience and copper gains—signals competing macro narratives between foreign flows and commodity-specific technicals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 95.30 support level will determine if rupee strength sustains. A break below signals continued appreciation momentum; a rebound to 95.50 would indicate dollar demand recovery.
