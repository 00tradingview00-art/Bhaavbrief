**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper climbed to 1337.5 INR/kg at 11:51 am IST, up 0.49% from the previous close. The move reflects selective strength in the MCX complex while precious metals and energy retreat: Gold traded 142645 INR/10g (−0.41%), Silver at 219246 INR/kg (−0.33%), Crude at 7843 INR/bbl (−2.41%), and Natural Gas at 266 INR/mmBtu (+0.53%). The rupee weakened to 95.37 INR (−0.38%), providing modest tailwind to rupee-denominated metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's outperformance amid broad MCX weakness signals divergent demand signals—likely reflecting manufacturing activity or infrastructure sentiment distinct from energy and precious metals. Traders should note this intra-complex rotation: industrial metals gaining while crude and gold slip. The move remains modest at +0.49%, suggesting consolidation rather than conviction.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
1340 INR/kg resistance level. A break above signals momentum continuation; failure to hold suggests mean reversion toward 1330 support where recent intraday trades clustered.
