**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX suffered a technology outage on the second day of its newly launched bullion index options, triggering a trading halt across contracts and sending MCX Ltd shares down 1% on NSE.

**MCX IMPACT**
The halt directly disrupted live Gold and Silver futures and options contracts on MCX, where Gold is currently priced at ₹161,878/10g and Silver at ₹263,074/kg. During any exchange-level technical failure, open positions cannot be hedged or exited, leaving traders exposed to full mark-to-market risk on both sides of the book. The timing is particularly damaging given elevated COMEX Gold at $4,551/oz and Silver at $77.32/oz — any price movement during the halt window creates unhedged spread risk between COMEX and MCX rates at the prevailing USD/INR of ₹96.21. Indian importers and jewellers using MCX as a hedge against customs duty exposure on physical gold face unquantified gap risk for the duration of the outage, as import cost benchmarks remain frozen while international spot continues to move.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange notice confirming system restoration and whether SEBI initiates a formal inquiry into the outage — a regulatory review triggers mandatory exchange-level disclosures that affect margin frameworks for bullion contracts. The next MCX trading session open will confirm whether price discovery resumes at parity with COMEX-derived INR rates or opens with a dislocation gap.
