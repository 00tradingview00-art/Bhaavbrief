**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold and MCX Silver are declining in tandem with a sharp pullback on COMEX, with Gold trading near ₹159,935/10g and Silver around ₹2,58,366/kg on MCX.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are both under selling pressure as COMEX Gold at $4,520/oz and COMEX Silver at $76.34/oz reflect a broad precious metals correction. The rupee at ₹95.70 against the dollar provides partial cushion to the downside — a weaker rupee inflates import costs and supports MCX prices relative to COMEX, but the current rate is not weak enough to offset the magnitude of the COMEX selloff. At the prevailing USD/INR rate, the landed import cost for gold inclusive of India's 6% customs duty and GST keeps MCX floor support elevated, meaning the COMEX-to-MCX spread remains structurally wide. Indian traders holding long positions in near-month contracts face mark-to-market pressure, and the basis between spot and futures on MCX is likely to compress as hedgers reduce exposure.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve commentary or CPI data release is the critical trigger — a sustained breach of $4,480/oz on COMEX Gold will extend losses to the ₹1,57,500/10g zone on MCX. Monitor whether MCX Silver holds the ₹2,50,000/kg support level intraday.
