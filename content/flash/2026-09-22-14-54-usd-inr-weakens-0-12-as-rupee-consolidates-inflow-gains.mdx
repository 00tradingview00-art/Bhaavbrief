**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 95.71 INR at 02:54 pm IST, falling -0.12% from the previous close. The rupee's consolidation follows sustained foreign portfolio inflows into domestic equities and bonds documented since 01:34 pm IST. MCX precious metals remain under pressure: gold at 152225 INR/10g (-0.57%), silver at 237442 INR/kg (-0.78%), while crude retreated to 8746 INR/bbl (-1.02%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's steady appreciation narrows USD/INR trading ranges, lifting hedging costs for MCX commodity traders. Weaker dollar-priced commodities (crude, precious metals) face dual headwinds from rupee strength and demand softness. Copper's resilience at 1416.35 INR/kg (+0.28%) suggests selective resilience despite broader currency-driven weakness in the commodity complex.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break below 95.50 INR would signal continued rupee strength; hold above 96.00 INR would indicate consolidation bounds. This level will determine whether FPI inflows sustain further currency appreciation or stabilize into a trading band.
