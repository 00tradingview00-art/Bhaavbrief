**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR rose to 95.67 INR at 09:24 am IST, marking a +0.22% move from the previous close. The rupee's modest depreciation against the dollar reflects easing foreign inflow sentiment and renewed dollar demand in domestic markets. MCX precious metals declined in tandem: gold fell to 155053 INR/10g (-0.57%), silver to 235680 INR/kg (-0.99%), while crude oil strengthened to 8149 INR/bbl (+1.07%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's depreciation reverses the three-session appreciation streak noted in recent flashes. For MCX traders, the inverse relationship between USD/INR and precious metals pricing remains intact—gold and silver weakness aligns with dollar strength. Crude's concurrent strength suggests oil demand resilience is offsetting the rupee headwind, keeping energy complex support intact.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 96.0 round-number level. A decisive close above it would signal sustained dollar demand revival; a pullback below 95.50 would reconfirm the foreign inflow-driven rupee appreciation bias established in prior sessions.
