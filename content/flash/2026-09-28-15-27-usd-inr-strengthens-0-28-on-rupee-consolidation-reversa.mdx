**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR rebounded to 96.39 INR at 03:27 pm IST, advancing +0.28% from the previous close. The rupee's intraday reversal follows a consolidation phase that saw the pair touch 95.71 INR at 02:54 pm IST. MCX precious metals remain under sustained pressure: gold at 147316 INR/10g (-2.36%), silver at 227548 INR/kg (-3.05%), while crude oil surged +4.29% to 9228 INR/bbl.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The USD/INR recovery signals weakening of the foreign portfolio inflow momentum documented since 01:34 pm IST. For MCX traders, the renewed dollar strength is amplifying metal depreciation across gold, silver, and copper (-1.16%), while crude's offsetting rally suggests energy markets are diverging from currency-driven commodity pressure. Cross-asset volatility remains elevated.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 96.50 INR level—a near-term resistance—will confirm whether this rebound holds conviction or reverses back into the 95.70–96.00 INR consolidation band established earlier today.
