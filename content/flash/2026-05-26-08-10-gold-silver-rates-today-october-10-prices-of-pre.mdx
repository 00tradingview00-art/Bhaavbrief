**MCX IMPACT**

At current COMEX gold of $4,571/oz, the landed import cost for Indian refiners, inclusive of India's 15% customs duty and GST, pushes effective acquisition costs well above the MCX spot print, keeping the COMEX-to-MCX spread structurally elevated. For silver, the ₹259,506/kg MCX rate reflects the ₹95.24 exchange rate fully priced in; any rupee depreciation beyond ₹96 tightens trader margins on imported physical stock. Indian traders holding overnight positions face gap-risk driven by after-hours COMEX moves that MCX opening prices must absorb.
