**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on MCX dropped ₹10,153 per 10g and silver crashed approximately 6% in a single session, aligning with COMEX gold at $4,581/oz translating to ₹1,62,083/10g and silver at $79.19/oz translating to ₹2,68,026/kg.

**MCX IMPACT**
The Gold June and August futures contracts on MCX are directly repricing in line with the COMEX-to-INR conversion at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹95.70, with the ₹10,153 correction reflecting the sharp unwinding of risk premium built into recent sessions. Silver MCX contracts bear the steeper damage — a 6% single-session move on a contract already priced at ₹2,68,026/kg represents approximately ₹16,082/kg in absolute loss exposure per lot. Indian traders holding silver carry additional import cost sensitivity: India's 15% customs duty on silver means landed cost remains structurally elevated above COMEX-derived prices, compressing the incentive for fresh physical arbitrage even at these corrected levels. The COMEX-MCX spread on gold remains within normal conversion bounds at current USD/INR, confirming this is a global sell-off rather than a currency-driven dislocation.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the US Federal Reserve's upcoming policy commentary and the Friday COMEX options expiry — a close below $4,500/oz on COMEX will confirm continued pressure on MCX gold toward the ₹1,55,000/10g zone.
