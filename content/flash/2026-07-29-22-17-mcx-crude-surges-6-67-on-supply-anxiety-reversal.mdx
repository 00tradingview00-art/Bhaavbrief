**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 8110 INR/bbl at 10:17 pm IST, posting a +6.67% gain from the previous close. The sharp recovery reverses a sustained pullback from the 8961 INR/bbl peak logged at 10:25 pm IST on 24 January. USD/INR eased to 95.64 INR (-0.14%), while natural gas edged up +0.69% to 262.8 INR/mmBtu, signalling renewed risk appetite in energy markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 567 INR rally within 48 hours signals shifting sentiment on crude supply concerns. For MCX futures traders, the price has retraced nearly 90% of the prior decline, now testing intermediate resistance. Cross-asset softness in gold (-0.43%) and silver (-0.23%) contrasts with crude's strength, suggesting commodity rotation rather than broad risk-off positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
8150 INR/bbl marks the next barrier toward yesterday's 8961 peak. A close above 8150 would signal conviction; failure there risks renewed pullback toward 7959 support.
