**WHAT HAPPENED**
Houthi forces have escalated attacks on shipping and infrastructure as Saudi Arabia raises oil export volumes to near-wartime levels in response to regional supply disruption threats.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks and crude import managers face a dual pressure: while elevated Saudi export volumes suggest adequate global supply, the underlying attack risk creates price volatility that bleeds directly into rupee-denominated **MCX Crude** settlement costs. Refiners locking in barrels at higher WTI-linked pricing see their per-barrel acquisition cost spike, compressing gross refining margins on every tonne of diesel, petrol, and fuel oil they produce for domestic sale.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Downstream fuel retailers and logistics operators sourcing diesel for commercial fleets face immediate pressure on procurement costs — every truck operator's per-litre fuel surcharge calculation shifts upward as terminal gate prices track crude higher. Fertiliser manufacturers dependent on crude-derived feedstock (naphtha) and thermal power plants burning fuel oil for generation see input costs reprice within days, forcing either margin compression or cost pass-through into farmer-paid urea prices and grid tariffs. Household consumers filling petrol and diesel tanks absorb the ripple as pump prices adjust to wholesale cost floors; aviation fuel surcharges on domestic airline tickets rise in lockstep, affecting business and leisure travel segments.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Integrated oil refiners operating on merchant crude procurement see gross margins compress by 50–150 basis points for every $5/bbl crude spike absorbed before output repricing. **MCX Crude Oil** at **₹9862/bbl** signals rising geopolitical premium; breach above recent swing highs confirms sustained supply anxiety. Commuter households buying petrol and diesel see per-litre pump prices drift upward within 7–14 days of crude settlement moves.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
OPEC+ production guidance in the coming fortnight and any damage assessments from new Houthi strikes will confirm whether this export surge is sustainable or temporary. MCX Crude holding above **₹9900/bbl** would signal market confidence in supply adequacy; breakdown below **₹9750/bbl** would indicate fade in geopolitical risk premium.
