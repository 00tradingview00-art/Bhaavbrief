**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rebounded during the session while MCX Silver surged ₹6,367/kg as easing crude oil prices shifted risk sentiment across the commodity complex.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (current reference: ₹1,60,047/10g) and MCX Silver (current reference: ₹2,57,896/kg) are the primary contracts in focus. The silver spike of ₹6,367/kg reflects amplified momentum relative to gold, consistent with silver's higher beta behavior during precious metals rallies. With COMEX Gold at $4,523/oz and USD/INR at ₹95.70, the landed import cost including India's 15% customs duty on gold pushes effective acquisition cost well above spot MCX levels, keeping the COMEX-to-MCX spread structurally elevated and limiting sharp downside corrections for domestic contracts. For Indian traders, any further rupee depreciation against the dollar directly inflates MCX prices independent of COMEX direction, creating a dual-variable risk on open positions.
