**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude firmed to 7424 INR/bbl at 01:20 pm IST, extending a +0.64% gain from the previous close. The move sustains the recovery established from the 7134 INR/bbl trough logged at 22:29 IST as supply-side stabilisation sentiment persists. USD/INR strengthened 0.18% to 95.25 INR, while precious metals rallied—gold +0.94% to 150250 INR/10g and silver +2.89% to 232361 INR/kg.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude holds within the 7134–7296 INR/bbl recovery range established overnight. The modest +0.64% advance signals consolidation rather than fresh momentum; traders remain positioned for supply-side rebalancing. Cross-asset rally in gold and silver suggests underlying risk-on tone supporting energy demand expectations amid a firmer rupee backdrop.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 7450 INR/bbl resistance level—the intraday high resistance from the overnight surge to 7296 INR/bbl—will signal whether crude can sustain northward momentum or retreat back toward 7350 INR/bbl support.
