**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rallied to 157178 INR/10g at 18:45 IST, gaining +1.89% from the previous close. The advance extends the metal's recovery trajectory that broke through 155000 INR/10g earlier in the session and pushed past 155450 INR/10g at 13:06 IST. MCX Silver tracked higher at 234904 INR/kg (+1.07%), while natural gas spiked +2.95% to 272.6 INR/mmBtu. USD/INR held steady at 95.74 INR, providing a stable currency backdrop for rupee-denominated bullion.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold has carved a fresh intraday high, signaling sustained safe-haven demand despite morning profit-taking around 153425 INR/10g. The cross-asset strength in both precious metals and energy suggests risk-off positioning. For MCX futures traders, the 157000+ level now acts as a newly established resistance anchor; breach above 157500 would confirm the recovery's conviction.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 157500 INR/10g round level for sustained upside follow-through. A failure to hold above 156500 INR/10g would signal mean reversion toward the 155000 zone.
