**WHAT HAPPENED**
Multi Commodity Exchange of India (MCX) has announced a 1:5 stock split effective January 2, 2026, reducing the face value of each MCX share by a factor of five.

**MCX IMPACT**
This is a corporate action on MCX Ltd. (NSE/BSE listed exchange operator) — not a change to any MCX commodity futures contract specification. MCX Gold (February), MCX Silver (March), MCX Crude Oil (January), and MCX Copper contracts remain structurally unchanged in lot size, margin requirements, and settlement mechanics. The split increases the number of MCX shares outstanding fivefold, lowering the per-share price proportionally, which historically improves retail liquidity in the exchange's own equity stock. For commodity traders, no adjustment to MCX contract multipliers, delivery norms, or the current MCX Gold reference level of approximately ₹161,025/10g is triggered by this event.
