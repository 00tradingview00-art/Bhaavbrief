**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX (Multi Commodity Exchange of India) shares hit a record high as a broad-based rally across commodity markets drove exchange volumes and revenue expectations sharply higher.

**MCX IMPACT**
The record on MCX stock is a direct reflection of elevated trading volumes across MCX Gold, Silver, Crude Oil, and Copper contracts — all of which are currently trading at elevated absolute levels. MCX Gold is priced at approximately ₹160,960/10g and MCX Silver at ₹260,662/kg, levels that widen the rupee-denominated notional value of each lot, increasing per-trade transaction fee revenue for the exchange mechanically. MCX Crude Oil at ₹9,566/bbl similarly inflates lot-value-based turnover figures. For Indian traders, the USD/INR rate of ₹96.21 continues to amplify import cost benchmarks across all dollar-denominated commodities, sustaining wide absolute spreads versus COMEX — keeping hedging demand and speculative interest structurally elevated on the exchange.

**WATCH**
Track MCX monthly volume and turnover data for April–May 2025 when disclosed, as any contraction in commodity price volatility or a rupee strengthening move below ₹95.00 will directly compress notional turnover and reverse the revenue-expansion thesis driving the stock.
