**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rallied to 162579 INR/10g at 22:31 IST, gaining +1.98% as the metal extended its multi-session recovery arc that has now pierced through 160000 INR/10g and held above 162000 INR/10g. Silver tracked higher at 247301 INR/kg (+1.67%), while copper gained +1.01% and natural gas +1.38%, signalling broad-based risk-off appetite. The rupee weakened 0.20% to 95.69 INR, providing tailwinds for rupee-denominated commodity prices.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 162579 level represents a structural breakout for gold traders — the metal has now cleared three consecutive resistance bands (155000 → 160000 → 162000) in a single session arc. Cross-asset correlation remains intact: weakness in the rupee and synchronized strength across copper and natgas suggest macro risk-off is driving commodity safe-haven demand, not isolated gold factors. Momentum positioning has likely extended.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The round 163000 INR/10g level will be the critical test. A decisive close above it signals the recovery extends toward 164000; a reversal below 162000 before European open would indicate profit-taking into overnight strength.
