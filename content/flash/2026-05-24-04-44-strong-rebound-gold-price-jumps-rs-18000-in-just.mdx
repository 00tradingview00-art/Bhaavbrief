**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged ₹18,000 and MCX Silver jumped ₹37,500 in two trading sessions, with Gold reaching approximately ₹160,047/10g and Silver touching ₹257,896/kg.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary instruments absorbing this move. The rally is a direct mechanical transmission from COMEX Gold at $4,523/oz and COMEX Silver at $76.20/oz, amplified by USD/INR at ₹95.70 — a weaker rupee structurally inflates INR-denominated import costs and widens the landed price. At current COMEX levels, the theoretical MCX Gold parity sits near ₹160,047/10g after accounting for India's 15% customs duty plus GST and other levies; any MCX premium above this level signals domestic demand absorption rather than pure arbitrage. For Indian traders, the import cost barrier at these COMEX prices reduces the likelihood of physical arbitrage pressure pulling MCX back toward COMEX parity, keeping the domestic spread supported.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the US Federal Reserve's upcoming policy commentary and US CPI data — a softer print extends gold's safe-haven bid and holds Silver above ₹255,000/kg on MCX, while a hawkish surprise compresses the spread back toward COMEX parity levels.
