**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX gold fell $80/oz and silver dropped $4.70/oz in a single session as U.S. Federal Reserve rate-cut expectations were repriced sharply lower by markets.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (current reference: ₹160,047/10g) faces direct downward pressure as the COMEX spot decline of roughly 1.7% transmits into rupee-denominated contracts, with the magnitude partially cushioned or amplified by the USD/INR rate at ₹95.70. MCX Silver (reference: ₹257,896/kg) absorbs a steeper percentage hit from the $4.70/oz COMEX drop, equivalent to approximately ₹14,300/kg at current exchange rates, making it the more volatile contract to monitor. Indian importers face reduced landed costs in dollar terms, but at ₹95.70 per dollar, import parity and customs duty (currently 15% on gold) remain significant buffers that prevent MCX from crashing proportionally with COMEX. The COMEX-MCX spread mechanics mean Indian traders holding long positions see erosion on both the international price and any carry premium built into near-month contracts.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve meeting minutes release and any fresh U.S. CPI or jobs data will either reinforce the rate-cut-off narrative and extend this selloff, or trigger a reversal if data softens. On price, COMEX gold holding above $4,440/oz is the structural level to monitor for stabilisation signals.
