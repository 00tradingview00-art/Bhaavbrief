**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 8375 INR/bbl at 12:37 pm IST, extending a +2.76% gain from the previous close. The sharp move reflects renewed supply risk concerns in global oil markets, with geopolitical tensions continuing to support prices. Gold climbed +1.10% to 144450 INR/10g and natural gas gained +1.05% to 278.2 INR/mmBtu, signaling broad risk-on momentum across the complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, crude has broken above the 8300 level with conviction, suggesting bullish momentum is holding despite earlier consolidation. The correlation with gold and natural gas indicates risk premium is pricing in sustained supply disruption fears. Copper's mild weakness (-0.33%) suggests selective strength in energy versus broad risk-on.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 8400 INR/bbl resistance level. A close above this round number would confirm the geopolitical premium is extending; failure to hold would signal consolidation ahead.
