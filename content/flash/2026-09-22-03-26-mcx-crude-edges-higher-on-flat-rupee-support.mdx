**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rose to 8840 INR/bbl at 03:26 am IST, posting a +0.05% gain as traders held positions ahead of the Asian session open. USD/INR remained steady at 95.81 INR (−0.17%), offering consistent rupee support. The move follows yesterday's profit-taking exhaustion at 9000 INR/bbl, with crude now consolidating in a narrow band as gold retreated −0.85% and copper gained +0.57%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The +0.05% micro-move reflects thin overnight liquidity and cautious positioning after recent sharp reversals. At 8840 INR/bbl, crude sits well below yesterday's intraday peak of 10215 INR/bbl, signaling that bulls remain hesitant to chase. Cross-asset weakness in precious metals suggests risk-off sentiment is limiting upside momentum in energy contracts.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 8850 INR/bbl round number for a breakout signal. Sustained holds above 8850 could test 8900; a slip below 8800 would confirm continued consolidation near support levels established post-exhaustion.
