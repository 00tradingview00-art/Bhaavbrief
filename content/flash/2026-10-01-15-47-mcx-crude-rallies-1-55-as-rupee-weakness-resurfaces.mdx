**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rose to 8869 INR/bbl at 03:47 pm IST, posting a +1.55% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 96.51 INR (+0.40% vs prev close). The rupee depreciation has rekindled domestic export competitiveness for crude, reversing the three-hour pullback from the 9197 INR/bbl peak logged at 03:09 pm IST. Gold and silver tracked modest strength (+0.57% and +0.58%), while natural gas contracted -1.55%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
MCX Crude is oscillating between rupee-driven carry trades and intraday profit-taking. The 8869 level sits 27 points below the 9197 intraday high, signaling trader caution despite renewed USD strength. Cross-asset softness in natural gas (-1.55%) and copper (-0.40%) suggests energy demand concerns persist beneath the headline crude rally.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 8900 INR/bbl round number and the 9000 level for resistance. A close above 8900 would confirm the rupee weakness trade; failure to hold 8869 signals mean-reversion back toward 8700–8750 support.
