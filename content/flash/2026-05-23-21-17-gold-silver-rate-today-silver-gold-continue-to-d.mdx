**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices on MCX declined in today's session, driven by profit booking across both precious metals contracts.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are facing selling pressure as traders lock in gains after the recent run-up to current levels of ₹160,047/10g and ₹257,896/kg respectively. Profit booking in domestic markets mirrors the broader corrective tone, though COMEX Gold holding at $4,523/oz and Silver at $76.20/oz set a firm floor for the MCX decline — any sharp rupee depreciation against the dollar at ₹95.70 mechanically raises import costs and compresses the downside. India's 15% customs duty on gold imports means the landed cost remains elevated, keeping MCX prices structurally above raw COMEX parity and limiting the depth of any correction for import-dependent physical demand. Traders monitoring the MCX-COMEX spread should note that significant divergence from theoretical import parity typically attracts arbitrage activity that stabilises the spread.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the US Federal Reserve's upcoming statements on interest rate trajectory — a hawkish tone strengthens the dollar, pressuring COMEX gold and pulling MCX lower despite rupee offset. A sustained breach below ₹158,500/10g on MCX Gold would confirm the corrective phase is extending beyond routine profit booking.
