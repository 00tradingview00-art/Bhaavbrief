**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper slipped to 1352.75 INR/kg at 03:02 am IST, extending a −1.12% decline from the previous close. The retreat continues profit-taking momentum that has driven prices lower since the 1411 INR/kg peak logged at 01:37 am IST. USD/INR weakened to 94.53 (−0.31%), while crude oil surged +2.46% to 8570 INR/bbl, indicating divergent cross-asset positioning in the base metals complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's continued downside reflects risk-off sentiment in base metals despite crude's rally. The 1352.75 level marks deeper retracement from intraday highs, signalling that support is being tested. For MCX futures traders, the rupee's softening (USD/INR down) typically provides a headwind to INR-denominated commodity prices, even as global crude strength offers partial counterweight.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 1350 INR/kg round level. A break below would confirm continuation toward the −1.90% trough at 1364 INR/kg logged at 12:56 am IST; hold above signals consolidation.
