**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rose to 199917 INR/kg at 12:51 am IST, posting a +0.16% gain from the previous close. The modest rally contrasts sharply with weakness across the broader precious metals complex—MCX Gold fell −1.90% to 143303 INR/10g, while MCX Copper declined −1.90% to 1364 INR/kg. USD/INR strengthened to 94.87 (+0.30%), typically headwind for rupee-denominated commodities, yet silver managed resilience.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's outperformance signals selective demand despite rupee depreciation and gold's sharp retreat. For MCX futures traders, this divergence suggests rotation from gold into silver as a relative value play. The modest +0.16% move keeps silver in a consolidation band—far below the 210936 INR/kg peak logged at 02:46 pm IST, signaling trader caution ahead of key support levels.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 207990 INR/kg floor tested at 12:02 am IST. A breach below this level would confirm weakness; holding above it suggests accumulation into the 195000–200000 INR/kg range.
