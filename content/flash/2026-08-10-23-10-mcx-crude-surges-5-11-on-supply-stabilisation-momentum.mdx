**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude accelerated to 7803 INR/bbl at 23:10 IST, posting a +5.11% gain from the previous close. The advance extends the recovery trajectory from the 7134 INR/bbl trough logged at 22:29 IST yesterday, as supply-side stabilisation sentiment strengthens. Natural gas followed with a +4.30% move to 266.6 INR/mmBtu, while precious metals gained modestly—gold at 153152 INR/10g (+0.88%) and silver at 236969 INR/kg (+2.38%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude has reclaimed 609 INR/bbl from yesterday's low, signalling sustained recovery momentum despite muted USD/INR at 95.29 INR. The cross-commodity uptick—especially natural gas tracking crude higher—suggests broad energy risk appetite. For MCX futures traders, this move tests whether supply stabilisation narrative can sustain above 7800 resistance or if profit-taking emerges.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 7800 round number—now at price—becomes the critical pivot. A break above 7850 INR/bbl would confirm momentum; a dip below 7700 INR/bbl would signal the recovery lacks conviction.
