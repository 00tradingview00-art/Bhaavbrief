**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 9771 INR/bbl at 11:28 pm IST, posting a +3.03% gain as USD/INR weakened to 94.92 INR (+0.07% vs prev close). The rupee's continued depreciation has restored export competitiveness for domestic crude producers. Copper climbed +1.10% to 1377.65 INR/kg, while natural gas fell sharply to 252.3 INR/mmBtu (−5.89%), signalling divergent energy sector momentum.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's softness is sustaining crude's upside after consolidation around 9463 INR/bbl earlier at 10:34 pm IST. For MCX futures traders, this price action signals sustained FX-driven demand for domestic crude exports despite margin compression in natural gas. Cross-asset weakness in silver (−1.89%) and gold (−0.79%) suggests selective strength in energy.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9800 INR/bbl round level and prior resistance at 9493 INR/bbl (from 12:51 am IST) will determine if this +3% move extends or consolidates into the close.
