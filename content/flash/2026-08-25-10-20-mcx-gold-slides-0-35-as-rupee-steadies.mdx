**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 162664 INR/10g at 10:20 am IST, posting a -0.35% loss from the previous close. The pullback interrupts the metal's multi-session recovery arc that had held above 162000 INR/10g through overnight trade. Silver extended weakness to 241949 INR/kg (-0.93%), while USD/INR remained anchored at 95.72 (+0.02%), offering no fresh depreciation tailwind to support precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The modest retreat from recent highs signals consolidation rather than trend reversal. Gold remains well-positioned above the 162000 INR/10g pivot that has proven sticky support. Cross-asset weakness—silver down 0.93%, natgas -1.50%—suggests broad-based risk-off sentiment, but the rupee's stability at 95.72 removes the currency depreciation prop that would amplify gold's appeal to rupee traders.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 162000 INR/10g support level is critical. A hold above it maintains the recovery structure; a breakdown below would test the 160000 INR/10g round number breached at 12:33 pm IST on the prior session.
