**WHAT HAPPENED**
Escalating military tensions between Iran, Israel, and US forces have triggered supply disruption concerns in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil transit.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks face immediate rupee-cost pressures as **MCX Crude at ₹9273/bbl** reprices upward on geopolitical risk premium, while dollar-denominated WTI exposure translates into elevated landed-cost coefficients per barrel cleared through spot and forward contracts. Simultaneously, bullion dealers and institutional safe-haven buyers are accumulating **MCX Gold at ₹138185/10g**, compressing near-term physical fabrication margins as spot premiums widen on dollar-denominated international price floors.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Refinery throughput scheduling and import-parity diesel pricing algorithms now embed Strait-closure risk, forcing margin recalibration at fuel pump terminals and logistics operators sourcing bunker fuel on monthly tenders. Diesel-dependent transportation fleets — trucking operators, cold-chain logistics networks, and mid-mile couriers — face widening freight rate pressure as fuel surcharges reset on contract renegotiation cycles, cascading into FMCG distribution and e-commerce last-mile economics. Retail fuel buyers and agricultural diesel consumers absorb the pump-price impact within weeks, while jewellery retailers and silver-backed industrial electroplating units holding finished inventory see working-capital stress as gold and precious metal input costs lock in at elevated levels.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian oil refiners operating on cost-plus margin structures will see both crude input costs and rupee forex conversion rates move against procurement budgets simultaneously. **MCX Natural Gas contracts** should signal demand-destruction and LNG import repricing, tracking downside pressure if Hormuz transit fears persist beyond the immediate shock window. Retail petrol and diesel consumers will absorb incremental per-litre costs within the statutory pricing adjustment window.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
