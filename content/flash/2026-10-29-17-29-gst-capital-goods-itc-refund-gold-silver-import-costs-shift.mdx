**WHAT HAPPENED**
The Government of India announced a new GST mechanism permitting Input Tax Credit (ITC) refunds on capital goods purchases to be claimed over 60 months instead of upfront, effective immediately through a GST Council circular notification.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold and silver importers — refineries, bullion dealers, and fabrication units sourcing capital equipment (melting furnaces, casting machinery, assay labs) — now face a working capital squeeze as their GST outlay on plant and machinery gets amortised across five years rather than recovered in the import quarter. This delays cash recovery by up to 4–5 years, raising the effective financing cost of setting up or upgrading precious metals processing infrastructure. **MCX gold futures holders** exposed to refinery hedges will see import-parity pricing shift upward as refiners embed the time-cost of deferred tax recovery into their bid prices.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Bullion importers and precious metals refiners holding **capital-intensive operations** — hallmarking labs, electrolytic refining units, casting facilities — now carry deferred tax benefits on their balance sheets, forcing them to either tap working capital lines at the **RBI repo rate of 5.5%** or defer equipment expansion. Mid-chain jewellery manufacturers buying refined **gold at ₹142,864/10g** and **silver at ₹190,060/kg** spot prices face slightly higher per-gram landed costs as refiners price in the cost of financing deferred ITC claims, narrowing their procurement margins on bulk orders. Retail jewellers, bridal collections, and households purchasing gold ornaments ahead of wedding seasons absorb this cost incrementally through higher jewellery prices set by manufacturers absorbing refiners' margin compression.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Precious metals refiners will see their equipment financing costs rise materially over the next 60 months, forcing them to pass through price hikes to downstream fabricators. **MCX gold futures** should trade at a slight premium to import parity as refiner hedges reflect deferred tax recovery timelines. Retail gold jewellery prices will edge higher as manufacturers recoup the margin loss created by refiners' higher working capital burden.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the GST Council's next clarification on capital goods ITC amortisation rules — any accelerated recovery clause or sector exemption could reverse this cost shock. Track **MCX gold and silver refinery forward curves** for widening backwardation as near-term premiums absorb financing costs.
