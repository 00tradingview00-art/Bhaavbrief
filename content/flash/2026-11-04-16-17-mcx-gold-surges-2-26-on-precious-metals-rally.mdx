**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rallied to 142600 INR/10g at 04:17 pm IST, posting a +2.26% gain from the previous close. The move extends the precious metals recovery, with MCX Silver climbing +3.90% to 186706 INR/kg and MCX Copper advancing +0.37% to 1370.45 INR/kg. USD/INR edged 0.11% lower to 94.83, sustaining rupee strength that underpins gold's rupee-denominated appeal.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's break above 142600 signals sustained safe-haven demand amid broader precious metals strength. For MCX futures traders, this signals conviction in the upside—the move follows earlier consolidation near 138447 INR/10g at 04:55 pm IST yesterday and underscores renewed appetite for inflation hedges. Cross-asset weakness in USD/INR amplifies positioning sensitivity.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 144100 INR/10g resistance level—last posted during yesterday's volatile range—will confirm whether this +2.26% rally has structural legs or remains corrective within the 138447–144100 trading band.
