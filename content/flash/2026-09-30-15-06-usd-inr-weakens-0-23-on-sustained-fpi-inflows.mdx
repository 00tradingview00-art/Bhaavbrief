**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 96.05 INR at 03:06 pm IST, falling -0.23% from the previous close. The rupee's continued appreciation reflects sustained foreign portfolio inflows documented since 01:34 pm IST. MCX precious metals remain under pressure: gold at 147400 INR/10g (+0.80%), silver at 226098 INR/kg (+0.29%). Crude oil dropped -0.47% to 8637 INR/bbl, while natural gas fell -0.85% to 291.2 INR/mmBtu.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's strength creates headwinds for commodity exporters and extends the rally in gold and silver despite currency appreciation. FPI inflows into domestic assets have anchored rupee momentum through the afternoon session. Crude and natural gas weakness suggests demand concerns may be offsetting any benefit from rupee depreciation pressure for energy traders.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
96.00 INR is the technical floor. Break below signals deeper rupee strength and further pressure on dollar-denominated commodity prices. A retest of 95.71 INR would confirm the intraday consolidation range holding.
