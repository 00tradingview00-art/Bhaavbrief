**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rebounded and MCX Silver surged approximately ₹6,000/kg in the domestic session, coinciding with a drop in crude oil prices, as COMEX Gold trades at $4,523/oz and COMEX Silver at $76.20/oz with USD/INR at ₹95.70.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) tracks the live COMEX-to-INR conversion at approximately ₹1,60,047/10g, making the rebound consistent with global spot strength amplified by rupee translation. MCX Silver's ₹6,000/kg jump aligns with COMEX Silver at $76.20/oz, which converts to approximately ₹2,57,896/kg — any spread widening beyond 0.5% signals domestic demand premium building. Indian traders carry an additional cost layer through the 15% customs duty plus GST on gold imports, which structurally keeps MCX prices at a premium to raw COMEX conversion and amplifies upside moves. With WTI Crude at $96.60/bbl (MCX ~₹9,430/bbl), the crude drop reduces energy-linked industrial input costs, which historically compresses the gold-silver ratio as silver benefits from dual monetary and industrial demand.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve commentary and weekly COMEX Commitment of Traders (COT) report will confirm whether speculative long positioning is driving this rebound or if physical demand is the primary catalyst. A sustained hold above ₹1,60,000/10g on MCX Gold is the critical structural level to monitor.
