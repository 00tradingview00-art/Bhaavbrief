**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on MCX hit a record high of ₹1,43,340 per 10g while Silver surged ₹12,803 in a single session, tracking COMEX spot gold at $4,523/oz and silver at $76.20/oz against a USD/INR rate of ₹95.70.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are both directly affected by this move. The record print on MCX Gold at ₹1,43,340 sits approximately ₹16,707 above the current live COMEX-derived import parity of ₹1,60,047/10g — that gap reflects the standard 15% customs duty plus GST and handling costs embedded in domestic pricing. Silver's surge to levels implied near ₹2,57,896/kg on import parity means the landed cost for physical silver importers has widened sharply, compressing refiner and jeweller margins. For Indian traders, the rupee at ₹95.70 is amplifying every dollar move on COMEX — a $10 rise in COMEX gold translates directly to approximately ₹954 on MCX before duty, making currency risk a live variable in any position sizing calculation.

**WATCH**
The next COMEX session open and any Federal Reserve commentary on rate trajectory will either extend this breakout or trigger a technical pullback from these record levels. Monitor USD/INR at the ₹96.00 resistance — a breach accelerates MCX upside independently of COMEX movement.
