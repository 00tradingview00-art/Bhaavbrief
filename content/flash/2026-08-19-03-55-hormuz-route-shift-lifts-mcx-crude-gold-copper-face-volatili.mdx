**WHAT HAPPENED**
China's largest oil shipping operators have rerouted vessels away from the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab, signalling a structural shift in Persian Gulf crude transit patterns and supply-chain risk reassessment.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks importing sour crude at Hormuz-benchmarked differentials face immediate uncertainty in **WTI-linked cost structures**, as alternative routing through longer sea lanes compresses traditional **Oman/Dubai spread arbitrage**. Domestic gold importers and bullion dealers holding dollar-denominated inventory see rupee-conversion costs fluctuate sharply on safe-haven demand, while **copper cathode consumers on LME-linked contracts** absorb volatility from risk-off positioning in base metals as geopolitical premium widens.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Indian Oil Corporation and Reliance's refinery scheduling teams must recalculate crude nomination timelines and storage-tank utilization across longer voyage durations, immediately stretching working capital cycles and demurrage costs on every cargo. Mid-chain lubricants manufacturers and specialty fuel blenders sourcing feedstock from refinery gate prices face delayed procurement windows, forcing inventory build-up ahead of actual demand — a cash-flow headwind for FMCG-linked fuel distributors and transportation fleets. Retail petrol pump operators and household consumers purchasing auto fuel will see pump prices reset based on lagged crude cost recovery, while urban logistics operators running e-commerce last-mile networks absorb higher fuel surcharges before consumer delivery thresholds reset.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Refinery margins for cracking **Brent crude into diesel and petrol** compress as transportation premiums widen and working-capital deployment extends. **MCX Crude Oil** will trade elevated if Hormuz-contingency risk sustains above **$85/bbl threshold**. Auto-fuel retail consumers will absorb pump-price increases within 7–10 days of crude consolidation above re-routed transport levels.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch for OPEC's next production statement and Chinese shipping insurance premium moves — both will confirm whether this rerouting is tactical or a sustained structural shift in regional logistics.
