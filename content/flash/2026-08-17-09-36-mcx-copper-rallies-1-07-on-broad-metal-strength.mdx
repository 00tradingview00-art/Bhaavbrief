**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper climbed to 1393.05 INR/kg at 09:36 am IST, rising +1.07% from the previous close. The move extends a pattern of renewed strength across the precious metals complex: Gold gained +0.45% to 155200 INR/10g, Silver surged +0.76% to 237712 INR/kg, while Crude declined −0.38% to 7840 INR/bbl. The rupee weakened +0.19% to 95.58 INR, supporting INR-denominated commodity prices.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's sustained rally near 1393 signals renewed risk appetite in the MCX metals space. The broad-based gains in both precious and industrial metals—coupled with rupee weakness—suggest dollar-headwinds are easing and domestic demand appetite is returning. For futures traders, this price action consolidates recent strength and validates the uptrend initiated from lower levels.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1400 INR/kg round number is the immediate resistance. A close above this psychological level at the end of the session would confirm continuation; a rejection would signal profit-taking and potential pullback toward 1380 INR/kg.
