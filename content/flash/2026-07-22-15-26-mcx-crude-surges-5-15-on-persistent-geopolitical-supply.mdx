**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude accelerated to 8570 INR/bbl at 03:26 pm IST, extending a sharp +5.15% gain from the previous close. The rally reflects sustained geopolitical supply concerns supporting global oil prices. Across the complex, gold climbed +1.16% to 144546 INR/10g, silver gained +0.56% to 225033 INR/kg, and natural gas rose +1.27% to 278.8 INR/mmBtu, signaling broad risk-on momentum.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, this break above 8500 INR/bbl signals strengthening conviction in the geopolitical premium. The synchronized gains across gold and natural gas underscore renewed supply anxiety. USD/INR holding flat at 96.56 keeps import parity mechanics stable, allowing crude's move to ride purely on global tensions rather than currency headwinds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The critical level is 8600 INR/bbl. A close above it would confirm the breakout; failure to hold 8500 would signal profit-taking and reversal into the 8300–8400 range established at 12:37 pm IST.
