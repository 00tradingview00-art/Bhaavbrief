**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR moved to 94.78 INR at 01:41 pm IST, marking a +0.23% gain from the previous close. The rupee's directional shift follows sustained foreign portfolio inflows into domestic equities and bonds. MCX precious metals showed mixed response: gold at 152540 INR/10g (-0.18%), silver at 237645 INR/kg (-0.57%), while crude oil at 8932 INR/bbl (+1.96%) and copper at 1399 INR/kg (+0.89%) posted gains.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX commodity traders, rupee appreciation creates a headwind for rupee-priced commodities like gold and silver, which declined in the snapshot. However, oil and copper strength suggests global demand narratives may be offsetting currency headwinds. The divergence signals traders to monitor whether rupee moves will dominate or whether commodity fundamentals reassert control.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 94.5 INR round-number support level remains critical. A break below 94.50 INR would signal sustained rupee strength; a move above 95.0 INR would reverse momentum and likely lift precious metals off current lows.
