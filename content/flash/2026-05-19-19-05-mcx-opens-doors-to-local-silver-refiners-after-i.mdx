MCX has approved domestic silver refiners as eligible suppliers for its futures contracts following the Indian government's tightening of silver import norms, reducing reliance on imported silver for physical delivery obligations.

On MCX, silver futures face a supply-side structural shift. With imports constrained by policy, exchange-approved domestic refiners now become critical to meeting delivery requirements on the platform. This expands the domestic refining ecosystem's role in price discovery and physical settlement. Reduced import dependency tightens the available delivery pool, which historically supports futures premiums during periods of elevated open interest near expiry. Domestic refiner capacity utilisation and LBMA-equivalent certification standards will now directly influence MCX silver contract liquidity.
