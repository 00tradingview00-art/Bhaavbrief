**WHAT HAPPENED**

MCX Silver crashed **₹231367/kg** down **2.00%** in afternoon trade, leading a broadly subdued commodity complex. Gold fell **1.03%** to **₹145406/10g** while Copper edged lower **0.57%** to **₹1280.00/kg**. Natural Gas remained the sole gainer at **+0.74%** to **₹311.40/mmBtu** as the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee meeting unfolded today.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

Silver's industrial component is under pressure as investors price in potential rate guidance from the RBI's MPC decision. When Indian monetary tightening signals emerge, domestic real yields rise, which suppresses safe-haven demand for precious metals — Silver's 2% drop is proportionally larger than Gold's 1.03%, suggesting the industrial solar/semiconductor demand lens (not just safe-haven positioning) is weakening. The rupee holding steady at **₹94.97/USD** removes currency-driven export tailwinds for Indian metals exporters, meaning domestic MCX prices track global weakness directly without rupee depreciation offsetting the decline.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**

A solar panel manufacturer hedging 3-month silver purchases now faces lower procurement costs but may have locked forward contracts at higher levels. A jewellery exporter preparing silver fabrication orders will see margin compression if domestic retail prices haven't yet adjusted downward in sync with MCX. A copper wire-mill operator managing working capital finds industrial input costs declining but signals weakening downstream construction/electrical equipment demand.

**BOTTOM LINE**

Silver's **2.00%** slide versus Gold's **1.03%** drop signals industrial destocking (solar/semiconductor) driving the move, not pure safe-haven flight — the differential matters structurally.

**WHAT TO WATCH**

RBI MPC rate decision announcement timing today. Post-announcement, watch if Silver stabilizes above **₹226000/kg** (support) or breaks lower, confirming demand destruction versus technical selling.
