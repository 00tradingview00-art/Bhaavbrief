**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rallied to 226300 INR/kg at 11:23 am IST, posting a +2.11% gain from the previous close. The advance extends the recovery that began at 21:23 IST yesterday when silver climbed to 222836 INR/kg (+1.58%), reversing earlier profit-taking. Gold mirrored the move, advancing +0.86% to 143814 INR/10g, while the rupee weakened -0.26% to 95.09 INR, supporting precious metals pricing.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's breach above 226000 INR/kg signals renewed safe-haven demand after volatile intraday swings between 218306 INR/kg and this level. For MCX futures traders, the cross-asset backdrop shows crude weakness (-1.07% to 7137 INR/bbl) and gold strength, indicating risk-off sentiment supporting the precious metals complex over energy.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 228000 INR/kg resistance level—a breach would confirm the safe-haven rally has momentum; a close below 224000 INR/kg would signal profit-taking resumption matching the 11:36 am IST pullback pattern.
