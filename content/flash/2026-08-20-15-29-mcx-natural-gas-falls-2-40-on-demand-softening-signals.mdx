**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas dropped to 264.3 INR/mmBtu at 03:29 pm IST, marking a -2.40% decline from the previous close. The pullback follows an intraday reversal after earlier strength at 263.6 INR/mmBtu (+3.13% at 03:14 pm IST). Crude Oil surged +2.09% to 8321 INR/bbl, while Silver gained +0.91% to 238950 INR/kg and Gold climbed +0.08%. USD/INR weakened -0.12% to 95.7 INR, providing limited currency support for energy futures.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The -2.40% reversal signals profit-taking after this morning's 3.13% rally. Gas traders face conflicting signals: crude strength at 8321 INR/bbl typically supports gas, yet natural gas is underperforming, suggesting independent demand concerns. The sharp intraday swings (from 263.6 to 264.3 in 15 minutes) indicate thin liquidity and position squaring ahead of settlement.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 263.5–264.0 support band. Break below 263 INR/mmBtu would confirm weakness; hold above 265 would signal consolidation within the 263.6–273 intraday range established earlier today.
