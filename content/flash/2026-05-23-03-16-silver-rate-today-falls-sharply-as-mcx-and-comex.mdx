**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver prices declined sharply across both COMEX and MCX platforms, with COMEX silver trading at $75.92/oz and MCX silver reflecting the slide at approximately ₹256,935/kg.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver contract (Silver 30kg and Silver Mini 5kg) bears the direct impact of this synchronized sell-off across international and domestic markets. The COMEX price at $75.92/oz translates to the current MCX rate of ₹256,935/kg after applying the USD/INR rate of ₹95.70, basic customs duty of 6%, IGST at 3%, and handling levies — meaning any further COMEX weakness mechanically compresses MCX levels in near-identical proportion. Indian traders holding long positions face margin pressure as the ₹256,935/kg level now acts as an immediate reference point; a break below this level opens the next technical zone closer to ₹250,000/kg. The import parity calculation remains tight, leaving limited buffer for domestic premiums to absorb further international downside.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Non-Farm Payrolls release and Federal Reserve commentary on interest rate trajectory are the primary triggers that will either extend silver's decline or stabilize prices; additionally, a COMEX close below $74.00/oz signals accelerated downside pressure on MCX contracts in the following session.
