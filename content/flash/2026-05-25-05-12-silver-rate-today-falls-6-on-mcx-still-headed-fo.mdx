**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on MCX dropped 6% in a single session, yet the metal remains on track for its best calendar year ever with a 157% surge year-to-date.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver contract (continuous) is directly in focus, with spot pricing currently at approximately ₹268,026/kg derived from COMEX at $79.19/oz and USD/INR at ₹95.70. The 6% intraday correction represents a drawdown of roughly ₹16,000–₹17,000/kg from recent highs, a significant margin event for leveraged MCX positions. Indian traders face an additional layer of import cost pressure — silver attracts a 15% basic customs duty plus GST, meaning the landed cost structure keeps MCX at a structural premium to raw COMEX conversion; any rupee depreciation tightens that spread further. Traders holding near-month contracts should note that the ₹268,026/kg level now acts as an immediate reference — a sustained close below it signals momentum exhaustion despite the annual trend remaining intact.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Non-Farm Payrolls release and Federal Reserve commentary on rate trajectory are the critical catalysts — a stronger dollar print pushes COMEX silver lower, amplifying the MCX correction via both the underlying price and USD/INR simultaneously. Monitor whether MCX Silver holds the ₹255,000/kg support zone on weekly closing basis to confirm trend continuity.
