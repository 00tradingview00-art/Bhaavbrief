**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas retreated to 297.7 INR/mmBtu at 03:57 pm IST, posting a -1.49% decline from the previous close. The slip extends a volatile downtrend that began at 04:18 IST when prices collapsed to 299.5 INR/mmBtu. The weakness mirrors MCX Crude's -2.28% drop to 8775 INR/bbl, while USD/INR strengthened -0.55% to 95.44 INR, intensifying import cost pressures on energy futures.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The natural gas breakdown reflects broader energy sector softness, with crude's sharper decline signaling demand concerns offsetting any seasonal winter strength. For MCX futures traders, the -1.49% move keeps prices within the 24-hour range (288.4–313.1 INR/mmBtu), suggesting consolidation rather than capitulation. Cross-asset weakness in copper (-0.08%) and precious metals indicates risk-off sentiment constraining upside.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 291.2 INR/mmBtu level (hit at 03:06 pm IST) is the pivot — a close below this would confirm extension toward the 288.4 INR/mmBtu 24-hour low and signal further downside pressure.
