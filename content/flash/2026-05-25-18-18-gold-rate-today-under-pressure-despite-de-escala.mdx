**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on COMEX is trading under pressure at $4,602/oz despite de-escalation in US-Iran tensions, as reduced geopolitical risk premium is unwinding safe-haven demand that had been built into prices.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (current reference: ₹162,826/10g) faces direct downside pressure as the geopolitical risk premium compresses on the COMEX front-month contract. The USD/INR at ₹95.70 provides a partial buffer — a stronger rupee would amplify the dollar-price decline in INR terms, while any rupee weakness limits the fall. Indian traders must account for the 15% customs duty plus GST on gold imports, which widens the structural floor for MCX prices relative to raw COMEX conversion — meaning the MCX spread versus COMEX remains elevated even as international prices soften. The import cost floor prevents MCX from collapsing proportionally with COMEX, but directional pressure remains firmly downward in the near term.

**WATCH**
Monitor the next US Federal Reserve commentary and any fresh Middle East developments — a confirmed breakdown below $4,550/oz on COMEX signals further compression of the risk premium and will translate directly into the ₹160,000/10g zone on MCX as the next technical reference level.
