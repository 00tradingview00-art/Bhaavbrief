**TITLE**
Hormuz Resilience Keeps Crude Soft; Gold Bid Intact on Safe-Haven Demand

**WHAT HAPPENED**
Despite escalating attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, crude oil flows from the Middle East have remained near prewar volumes, signalling supply continuity and dampening immediate scarcity premiums across global and Indian energy markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks and fuel importers face no immediate spike in **MCX Crude** spot costs, undercutting the fear premium that typically embeds itself in rupee-denominated barrel prices during geopolitical flare-ups. Simultaneously, the persistence of regional conflict—even with contained oil impact—sustains safe-haven inflows into precious metals; gold and silver jewellery dealers holding **MCX Gold** inventory at ₹146040/10g and **MCX Silver** at ₹209502/kg see dollar-hedging demand from overseas buyers offset any domestic volume weakness from demand fatigue.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Diesel and petrol retailers operating on thin retail margins benefit from crude remaining range-bound, allowing them to hold retail pump prices steady without flash-revision cycles that erode margin predictability. Mid-chain logistics operators and CNG fleet managers enjoy working capital relief as compressed fuel costs delay margin compression on per-kilometre haulage rates. Simultaneously, gold refiners and bullion processors experience sustained offtake from jewellers rebalancing portfolios toward hard assets during uncertain geopolitical periods, tightening physical spot premiums. Households purchasing jewellery ahead of festival season encounter stable retail gold prices, while fuel consumers filling vehicles face no sudden duty or tax adjustment tied to global crude panic.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Diesel retailers operating on sub-₹2/litre margins avoid forced price cuts if **MCX Crude** holds below ₹9500/bbl, preserving retail profit pools. **MCX Gold futures** signal sustained demand on geopolitical uncertainty hedging, with price holding firm above ₹145500/10g. Petrol and diesel pump customers in urban markets face no imminent price hikes tied to this event.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the next **OPEC+ output decision** and any scheduled shipping corridor security announcements; a material disruption report or new sanctions escalation could break the current crude-supply resilience narrative within 7–10 trading sessions.
