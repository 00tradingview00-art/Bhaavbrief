**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver broke above the psychological 240000 INR/kg level, climbing to 240872 INR/kg at 13:34 IST and posting a +0.60% gain from the previous close. The round-number cross arrives after silver recovered from a −1.73% trough at 229803 INR/kg logged at 19:11 IST on the prior session. Gold strength (+0.57% to 153450 INR/10g) and a softer rupee (USD/INR at 95.15, +0.33%) are supporting the recovery momentum across precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 240000 level represents a key psychological barrier that has now been reclaimed. For MCX futures traders, this recovery consolidates the three-session rally off the 229594 INR/kg low and suggests institutional interest is re-entering the market. The broader precious metals complex strength—with gold and silver both in positive territory—indicates a shift toward safe-haven demand amid rupee weakness.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor whether silver can hold above 240000 INR/kg into the close. A sustained break above 242000 INR/kg would signal continuation toward the 245585 INR/kg intraday peak, while a slip back below 240000 invites profit-taking and re-tests the 236150 INR/kg support zone.
