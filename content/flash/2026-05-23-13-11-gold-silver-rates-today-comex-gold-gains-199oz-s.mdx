**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX gold surged $199/oz and silver rallied $5.20/oz as easing inflation fears triggered by US-Iran diplomatic talks drove safe-haven and momentum buying across precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are both tracking sharp upside, with COMEX gold now at $4,523/oz translating to approximately ₹1,60,047/10g and silver at $76.20/oz pricing near ₹2,57,896/kg at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. The $199 COMEX gold move alone adds roughly ₹5,950–₹6,100/10g to MCX landed cost before India's 15% customs duty and 3% GST are layered in, amplifying the rupee-denominated price spike disproportionately versus the dollar move. Silver's $5.20 rally on COMEX translates to approximately ₹15,900/kg incremental import cost pressure on MCX before duties. Indian traders holding short positions face significant mark-to-market stress at current levels, with the MCX gold ₹1,60,047/10g level now acting as immediate support rather than resistance.

**WATCH**
The next US CPI print and any formal statement from US-Iran negotiations will either sustain this inflation-fear-easing narrative or reverse the precious metals rally sharply. A COMEX gold breach above $4,600/oz triggers the next leg of MCX repricing.
