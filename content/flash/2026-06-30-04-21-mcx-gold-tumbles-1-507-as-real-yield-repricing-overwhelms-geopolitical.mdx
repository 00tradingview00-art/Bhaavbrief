**WHAT HAPPENED**

MCX Gold collapsed **₹1,507 intraday** to **₹140,885/10g**, marking a **-1.07% session loss** and leading the commodity complex lower. Silver and crude followed with softer declines of **-0.55%** and **-0.52%** respectively. The selloff arrived on renewed inflation anxiety amid escalating US-Iran geopolitical tension, triggering a rotation out of traditional safe-haven positioning.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

Gold's safe-haven bid is currently losing to real-yield headwinds—rising inflation expectations push nominal yields higher, compressing gold's inflation-adjusted return even as geopolitical risk persists. The **₹94.59 USD/INR** spot rate provides no offset; rupee stability removes the import-parity support that typically cushions MCX gold when global bullion weakens. A **₹140,885** MCX level now prices in competing macro signals: geopolitical premium versus yield-driven repricing.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**

A jewellery exporter with forward export commitments faces margin compression on rupee-hedged gold purchases; locking in today's **₹140,885** level crystallizes losses against export-contract pricing set 2–3 weeks prior. An industrial-gold consumer (semiconductor coating, electronics) delaying procurement can now access lower spot prices but loses the optionality to defend against a sharp geopolitical spike. A gold fund manager holding physical for inflation hedge must now decide whether the real-yield headwind justifies continued exposure despite war premium signals.

**BOTTOM LINE**

MCX gold's **-1.07%** move reflects yield repricing overpowering geopolitical premium—the safe-haven narrative weakens when inflation-adjusted returns fall. Watch whether **₹140,500** holds as support; breach confirms yield dominance over geopolitics.

**WHAT TO WATCH**

US 10-year yield print at 2pm IST / Fed inflation expectations revision; a **25bp yield rise** could extend gold weakness to **₹139,500**.
