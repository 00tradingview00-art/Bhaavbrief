**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper declined to 1355.5 INR/kg at 02:17 pm IST, sliding −0.36% from the previous close. The pullback reflects broader weakness in the base metals complex amid rupee appreciation—USD/INR strengthened to 96 (+0.39%). Precious metals extended losses, with Gold at 150312 INR/10g (−0.61%) and Silver at 230320 INR/kg (−1.02%), while crude remained resilient at 9937 INR/bbl (+2.26%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's retreat follows the +1.72% rally to 1410.55 INR/kg recorded at 10:29 pm IST overnight. A stronger rupee at 96 typically pressures INR-denominated commodity futures by eroding export competitiveness and dampening import demand. Traders should note the intraday volatility pattern—copper has now reversed from 1410 to 1355, signaling consolidation after overnight strength.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1350 INR/kg level and the 1371.5 INR/kg intraday floor set at 12:17 am IST. A break below 1350 would confirm sustained downside; a recovery toward 1380 would signal renewed support-holding strength.
