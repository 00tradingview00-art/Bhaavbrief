**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude declined to 7754 INR/bbl at 17:37 IST, posting a -2.19% loss from the previous close. The sharp pullback reverses the recovery trajectory that had lifted prices to 8001 INR/bbl at 10:39 IST earlier in the session. The broader commodity complex remains under pressure, with MCX Natural Gas sliding -1.01% to 265.4 INR/mmBtu. USD/INR holding steady at 95.43 INR suggests rupee stability is not the primary driver.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The breach below the 7800 INR/bbl support level signals weakening conviction in the supply-side stabilisation narrative that had underpinned the recovery from yesterday's 7134 INR/bbl trough. For MCX futures traders, the 246-point intraday reversal from the 8001 peak indicates profit-taking at psychological resistance and renewed demand destruction concerns dominating near-term positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 7700 INR/bbl support level is now critical. A hold above this zone would suggest consolidation; a break would target the 7500–7600 range and test yesterday's crisis lows.
