Indian traders tracking MCX Gold must monitor this duty-inclusive import parity level, as any sharp INR depreciation beyond ₹95.24 amplifies the premium further. The ₹1,62,302/10g level now functions as near-term support; a sustained hold above it confirms bullish momentum in domestic futures.

**WATCH**
