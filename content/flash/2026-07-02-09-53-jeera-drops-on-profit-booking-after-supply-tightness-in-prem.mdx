**TITLE:** Jeera Futures Face Profit-Taking as Premium Bold Seed Supply Tightens

**WHAT HAPPENED**
**Jeera NCDEX contracts** experienced selling pressure as traders booked profits following a supply squeeze in **premium bold seeds**, according to market advisory data.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The profit-taking reflects a classic inventory correction in **Unjha and Rajkot mandis** — India's primary jeera spot markets — where premium bold variety holders are offloading positions after the rally driven by constrained supply. This repricing mechanism directly impacts **NCDEX jeera futures basis**, as spot dealers in these mandis adjust their forward hedges downward, widening the discount between near-month and far-month contracts. Exporters holding premium seed stocks face immediate realization pressure as the futures-spot spread normalizes.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Jeera farmers in Gujarat and Rajasthan selling through cooperative societies and private merchants now see their harvest valuations reset lower, while mandi commission agents in Unjha holding inventory for export clients face mark-to-market losses on unsold balances. Spice processors sourcing premium bold varieties for grinding and export shipments to Middle Eastern and European buyers will find procurement costs repricing downward, though their forward sales commitments remain locked at higher contracted levels.

**BOTTOM LINE**
**Unjha mandi traders** managing premium seed stocks will accelerate liquidation as the profit-booking cycle tightens their holding margins. **NCDEX jeera near-month contracts** are signaling a consolidation phase after supply-driven strength, with further downside vulnerable if bold seed arrivals increase. **Ground jeera prices** at retail level in spice markets across Maharashtra and Gujarat will ease modestly as mill procurement costs compress.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **NCDEX daily settlement prices** over the next 48 hours and incoming jeera arrivals data from Gujarat's harvest regions to confirm whether this is a temporary correction or the start of a broader liquidation cycle.
