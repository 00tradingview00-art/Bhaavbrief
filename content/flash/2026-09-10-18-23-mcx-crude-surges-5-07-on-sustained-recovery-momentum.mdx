**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 9560 INR/bbl at 18:23 IST, posting a +5.07% gain and extending the multi-leg recovery sequence that began at 21:26 IST yesterday from 7918 INR/bbl. The move builds on successive peaks at 9264 INR/bbl (14:33 IST) and 9083 INR/bbl (19:19 IST). USD/INR strengthened 0.38% to 95.53, while precious metals—gold at 152030 INR/10g and silver at 234487 INR/kg—declined, signalling risk-on crude appetite offsetting rupee headwinds.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude has now retraced 20.7% from the 7918 INR/bbl floor in under 24 hours, establishing a sustained bull structure above the 8946 and 9083 checkpoints. The divergence—crude rallying while gold/silver retreat—suggests traders are rotating into energy on relative value grounds. USD/INR strength typically pressures rupee-denominated crude, yet the move persists, indicating external WTI strength is overriding FX headwinds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break and hold above 9560 INR/bbl or a reversal back toward 9264 support will signal whether this 2-day rally sustains legs toward 9700+ or consolidates the recovery.
