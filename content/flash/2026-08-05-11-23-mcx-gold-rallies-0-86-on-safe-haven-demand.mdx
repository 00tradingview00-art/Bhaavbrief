**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 143814 INR/10g at 11:23 am IST, gaining +0.86% from the previous close. The rally extends the commodity recovery momentum seen at 23:24 IST yesterday when the contract hit 143298 INR/10g (+1.07%). Silver tracked higher at 226300 INR/kg (+2.11%), while USD/INR weakened to 95.09 (-0.26%), supporting rupee-denominated bullion prices and offsetting modest weakness in crude at 7137 INR/bbl (-1.07%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's persistence above 143k marks a consolidation of gains after the overnight pullback to 141250 INR/10g. The rupee's strength against the dollar and silver's outperformance (+2.11% vs gold +0.86%) suggest renewed safe-haven positioning ahead of potential macro events. Copper's modest +0.30% and natural gas's +0.51% gains confirm broad commodity stability, limiting downside risk for bullion.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 144000 INR/10g round level now becomes the key near-term ceiling. A break above would confirm trend continuation; failure to hold 143500 INR/10g would signal a return to consolidation range trading.
