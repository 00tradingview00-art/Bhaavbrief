**WHAT HAPPENED**
Lebanon has announced a partial ceasefire agreement between Israel and Hezbollah, though sporadic attacks continue. The fragile truce signals de-escalation in the region but remains fragile, with military operations ongoing.

**MCX IMPACT**
Crude Oil faces upward pressure—Middle East tensions historically disrupt shipping routes through the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global supply corridor. Natural Gas remains sensitive to supply chain disruptions affecting regional production. Gold strengthens as investors seek safe-haven assets during geopolitical uncertainty, offsetting any risk-off sentiment from ceasefire optimism.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor escalation indicators: renewed cross-border attacks would reignite volatility. Track global crude inventories and shipping delays through the Red Sea and Gulf routes. Watch crude's movement around key technical levels—breaches could confirm supply fear premiums. Gold's holding above psychological support suggests risk appetite remains cautious. A formal ceasefire agreement would ease safe-haven demand; conversely, breakdown signals could trigger sharp reversals across all three commodities.
