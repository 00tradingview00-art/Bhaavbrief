# LEBANON CEASEFIRE TALKS: MCX COMMODITIES IN FOCUS

**WHAT HAPPENED**
Trump has initiated diplomatic calls with Israel and Hezbollah aimed at securing a lasting ceasefire in Lebanon, signaling potential de-escalation in Middle Eastern tensions after weeks of cross-border conflict.

**MCX IMPACT**
Crude Oil remains sensitive to any supply disruption fears from the region—a ceasefire reduces geopolitical premium embedded in prices. Natural Gas responds to broader Middle East stability concerns affecting LNG trade routes and production facilities. Gold typically strengthens during geopolitical uncertainty as a safe-haven asset; ceasefire progress could ease risk-off sentiment, potentially pressuring prices downward.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor official ceasefire agreement announcements—sustained talks signal confidence, while breakdowns could reverse gains. Track crude oil's reaction to regional supply reassurances and watch if gold holdings show profit-booking. Keep tabs on follow-up statements from involved parties; any escalation rhetoric would immediately re-trigger safe-haven demand across all three commodities.
