**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 142000 INR/10g at 11:13 pm IST, marking a -1.45% loss from the previous close. The pullback mirrors weakness across the precious metals complex—MCX Silver fell -1.48% to 184366 INR/kg and MCX Copper dropped -0.85% to 1339.7 INR/kg. USD/INR strengthened 0.40% to 94.15, reducing the rupee hedge benefit that had supported prices through the recent multi-day rally that peaked at 144045 INR/10g earlier.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The reversal signals profit-taking after a +3.8% rally from 03:54 am IST lows. For MCX futures traders, the rupee's weakness (USD/INR now at 94.15) has removed tailwind support, exposing gold to dollar-denominated headwinds. The broad decline across crude (-1.11%) and base metals confirms a general risk-off bias, tightening correlations and reducing safe-haven demand.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Guard the 141000 INR/10g level as the next support test. A hold there maintains the broader uptrend; a break below opens the 138447 INR/10g channel low from 04:55 pm IST.
