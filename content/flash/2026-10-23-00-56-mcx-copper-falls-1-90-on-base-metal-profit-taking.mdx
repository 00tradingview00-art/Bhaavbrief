**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper declined to 1364 INR/kg at 12:56 am IST, marking a −1.90% pullback from the previous close. The retreat extends profit-taking that began after the intraday peak of 1411 INR/kg logged at 01:37 am IST. USD/INR has edged higher to 94.87 (+0.30%), while crude oil rallied +3.10% to 9493 INR/bbl, signaling selective risk appetite in energy offsetting base metals weakness.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sharp reversal from copper's morning highs suggests traders are locking gains ahead of potentially volatile sessions. The −1.90% move sits within the range of prior intraday pullbacks (−0.76% at 02:59 pm IST), indicating a consolidation phase rather than a structural breakdown. Weaker precious metals—Gold −1.90% to 143303 INR/10g—reinforce selective deleveraging across the commodity complex.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1350 INR/kg support level will signal whether this decline stabilizes or extends further. A hold above that zone keeps the prior 01:37 am IST high of 1411 INR/kg in play as an intraday resistance target.
