**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices in India are facing downward pressure driven by a combination of global and domestic factors currently reflected in COMEX gold at $4,520/oz and silver at $76.34/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold and MCX Silver contracts are the primary instruments affected by this pressure. At the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70, MCX Gold translates to approximately ₹1,59,935/10g, and MCX Silver sits near ₹2,58,366/kg — both representing the landed import cost benchmark before customs duty application. India's 15% gold import duty creates a structural premium spread over COMEX-derived prices; any rupee strengthening or COMEX softness compresses this spread and reduces the domestic price floor. For Indian traders, a firming rupee combined with easing COMEX levels simultaneously erodes the customs-duty-inclusive import parity, accelerating the downside in MCX contracts beyond what COMEX movement alone suggests.

**WATCH**
Monitor the USD/INR rate at the ₹95.50 support level — a break below this level amplifies MCX downside independently of COMEX direction. The next RBI intervention signal or U.S. Federal Reserve commentary on rate trajectory will either reinforce or halt the current bearish momentum in domestic precious metals.
