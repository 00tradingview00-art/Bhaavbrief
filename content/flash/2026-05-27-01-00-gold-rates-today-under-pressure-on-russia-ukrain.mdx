**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold prices faced downward pressure as diplomatic signals around a potential Russia-Ukraine peace deal reduced safe-haven demand, pulling COMEX gold toward the $4,535/oz level currently observed in live markets.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold contracts (June/August) face bearish pressure directly tied to the COMEX slide, with the current COMEX price translating to approximately ₹1,60,440/10g on MCX after applying USD/INR at ₹95.69 and standard customs duty of 6% plus GST. Any sustained COMEX correction toward $4,400/oz would reset MCX fair value near ₹1,55,500/10g, creating a meaningful drawdown for positional longs. The INR-USD rate at ₹95.69 provides partial cushion — a weaker rupee compresses the full impact of dollar-denominated price drops on MCX. Indian traders holding near current MCX levels must account for the import cost structure, as the duty-inclusive landed price sets a hard floor below which arbitrage becomes the dominant force.

**WATCH**
Monitor formal statements from the Russia-Ukraine negotiating parties and the next COMEX settlement — a close below $4,480/oz confirms continuation of safe-haven unwinding and validates further MCX downside. Any breakdown in peace talks reverses the pressure immediately.
