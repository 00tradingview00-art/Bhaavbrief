**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX and COMEX simultaneously raised margin requirements on Gold and Silver contracts amid a sharp surge in volatility, with COMEX Gold trading at $4,523/oz and MCX Gold near ₹160,047/10g.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June) and MCX Silver (July) contracts face direct pressure as elevated margins force leveraged traders to either deposit additional capital or reduce open positions, mechanically compressing open interest. At ₹160,047/10g for Gold and ₹257,896/kg for Silver on MCX, any forced liquidation by under-margined accounts creates downside price pressure independent of COMEX direction. Indian traders carry an additional layer of exposure: the USD/INR rate at ₹95.70 inflates the landed import cost and widens the MCX-COMEX spread, meaning rupee-denominated contracts absorb both the dollar move and the currency component simultaneously. Customs duty on gold imports (currently 15% effective rate including AIDC) further locks the MCX price at a structural premium to COMEX, limiting the degree to which domestic prices can correct even during liquidation events.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official margin circular for the revised SPAN and exposure margin percentages on Gold and Silver contracts — a second consecutive margin hike confirms sustained volatility risk and signals further position unwinding. The next COMEX settlement and any RBI intervention signal at the ₹95.70 USD/INR level will either amplify or stabilize the MCX spread.
