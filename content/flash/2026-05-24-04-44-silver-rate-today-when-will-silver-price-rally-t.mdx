**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on COMEX is trading at $76.20/oz, with market participants actively questioning the sustainability of the current rally and identifying potential exhaustion levels.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (near-month contract) is directly exposed to this COMEX price at the current translated rate of ₹257,896/kg, derived from the $76.20/oz COMEX price and USD/INR at ₹95.70. Any COMEX-side correction compounds on the MCX side through two channels: the dollar price drop plus any concurrent rupee strengthening widens the drawdown for Indian holders. At current import parity, Indian silver import costs — inclusive of 10% customs duty and GST — place the landed cost significantly above spot MCX levels, compressing the arbitrage window for physical importers. Traders holding MCX Silver longs must monitor the ₹257,896/kg level as the immediate reference; a COMEX breakdown below $74.00/oz translates to approximately ₹250,000/kg on MCX at current USD/INR, a technically significant support zone.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve commentary and Friday's U.S. Non-Farm Payrolls release will either reinforce dollar pressure — supporting silver — or trigger a dollar rebound that reverses the current COMEX silver trajectory; a COMEX close below $74.50/oz is the technical invalidation level for the current rally structure.
