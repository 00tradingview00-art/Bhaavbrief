**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas jumped to 263.6 INR/mmBtu at 03:14 pm IST, gaining +3.13% from the previous close. The rally mirrors broad-based strength across the MCX complex, with crude climbing +1.39% to 7527 INR/bbl, copper +0.91% to 1377.9 INR/kg, and silver +1.14% to 234100 INR/kg. The rupee held steady at 95.28 INR, removing currency headwinds. Recovery follows the sharp 4.62% collapse at 01:27 am IST, signaling renewed demand appetite after overnight profit-taking.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, this +3.13% move reclaims the 263–264 INR/mmBtu zone as a critical pivot after violent overnight reversals. The synchronized strength in crude and metals suggests broad energy-commodity risk-on sentiment, reducing isolated downside pressure on gas. However, volatility context—the earlier 4.62% dump and 2.65% rally swings—warns of thin liquidity and fast reversals even within single sessions.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 264.5 INR/mmBtu level (earlier 01:27 am IST low) marks immediate overhead resistance. A break above 265 would confirm sustained upside; a slip back below 262 signals the rally is another false recovery.
