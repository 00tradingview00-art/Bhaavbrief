**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver fell to 218306 INR/kg at 11:36 am IST, marking a -0.49% decline from the previous close. The pullback extends profit-taking that began yesterday at 15:25 IST when silver retreated to 222923 INR/kg (-1.80%). Gold weakness (-0.68% to 141847 INR/10g) and broad-based commodity softening—crude at -1.89%, natural gas at -1.34%—have dampened precious metals appetite.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's retreat from yesterday's 227902 INR/kg high confirms profit-taking dominance over industrial demand. The -0.49% move sits within the recent volatility range established between 218306–227902 INR/kg. For MCX futures traders, the weakness tests conviction in the intraday recovery narrative that drove the +2.24% rally to 223300 INR/kg at 12:35 pm IST yesterday.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 215000 INR/kg support level. A close below this threshold would signal deeper profit-taking; hold above it suggests consolidation within the established range.
