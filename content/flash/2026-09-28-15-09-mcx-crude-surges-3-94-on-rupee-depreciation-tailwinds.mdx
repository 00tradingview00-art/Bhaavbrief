**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude accelerated to 9197 INR/bbl at 03:09 pm IST, posting a +3.94% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 95.95 INR (+0.19%). The rupee weakness continues to underpin crude valuations for domestic exporters. This move extends the rally from 9010 INR/bbl logged at 02:55 pm IST (+2.10%), signaling sustained momentum despite precious metals retreating sharply—Gold at 146996 INR/10g (−2.57%) and Silver at 226885 INR/kg (−3.33%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
MCX Crude has broken through 9150 INR/bbl resistance on consistent rupee headwinds. The cross-asset divergence is pronounced: energy rallies while precious metals and Natural Gas (301.3 INR/mmBtu, −2.99%) weaken, suggesting selective risk-on positioning in export-linked commodities. Traders should monitor whether the rupee depreciation continues to anchor crude support or mean-revision dynamics take hold.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9250 INR/bbl round number represents the next resistance checkpoint. A breach would validate the rupee-driven rally; failure to hold 9197 INR/bbl would signal exhaustion and profit-taking risk.
