**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on COMEX hit an all-time high of $98.98/oz on January 23, surpassing the previous record and marking a historic breakout in the white metal.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver contracts — primarily the Silver 1 kg and Silver Mini contracts — face immediate upside pressure tracking this record COMEX move. At the live COMEX rate of $77.00/oz, MCX Silver is already pricing near ₹260,590/kg; a move to $98.98/oz translates to an MCX equivalent of approximately ₹335,000/kg after applying the USD/INR rate of ₹95.69 and India's applicable GST plus customs duty structure on silver imports (currently 15% basic customs duty plus GST). The ₹260,590/kg level now becomes near-term support rather than resistance for Indian traders. Indian importers and industrial consumers — particularly electronics and solar panel manufacturers — face a structurally higher landed cost at every rupee of COMEX advance, widening the cost-of-import spread materially against domestic buyers.

**WATCH**
The $98.98/oz COMEX high is the immediate technical level — a daily close above this figure confirms continuation, while a rejection and close below $95.00/oz signals a false breakout. Track the next US FOMC minutes release and weekly COMEX Commitment of Traders (COT) report for managed money positioning shifts that validate or negate this move.
