**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas crashed to 255.7 INR/mmBtu at 07:17 pm IST, posting a -4.63% loss from the previous close. The sharp decline extends renewed softness across the energy complex, with MCX Crude rallying +2.86% to 9755 INR/bbl in counter-move. Precious metals followed suit: MCX Gold fell -0.56% to 142502 INR/10g and MCX Silver declined -2.13% to 195343 INR/kg. USD/INR steadied marginally at +0.07% to 94.92 INR, limiting currency support.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, this represents the most significant single-session breakdown in natural gas this session. The divergence with crude's strength signals demand destruction concerns in the gas market specifically, rather than broad energy weakness. The price now trades below recent pivot levels, testing trader conviction on oversold positioning and margin compression risk.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 250 INR/mmBtu support level for hold/breach signals. A close above 260 INR/mmBtu would signal stabilization; a break below 250 would confirm fresh downtrend momentum into tomorrow's session.
