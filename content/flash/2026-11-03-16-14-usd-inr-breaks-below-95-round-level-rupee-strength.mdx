**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR crossed below the 95 INR round level, declining to 94.92 INR at 04:14 pm IST, marking a -0.18% pullback from the previous close. The rupee's renewed strength reverses earlier dollar gains seen at 04:13 pm IST when the pair had climbed to 95.55 INR. Broad-based commodity weakness across MCX—Gold at 138185 INR/10g (-1.43%), Silver at 176820 INR/kg (-3.79%), and Copper at 1357.25 INR/kg (-2.49%)—has weighed on dollar demand, supporting rupee appreciation.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The breach of 95 INR signals a technical pivot for rupee bulls. MCX commodity traders typically correlate rupee weakness with import-driven dollar demand and commodity strength; this reversal suggests risk-off sentiment dominating near-term flows. The cross-asset picture shows commodity deflation pressuring the dollar, creating headwinds for bullish crude and natural gas positioning that relies on dollar weakness offset.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 94.90–94.85 INR support band. A hold above 94.90 INR will confirm the round-level break; a retest of 95.20 INR resistance will invalidate this rupee rally and signal renewed dollar momentum.
