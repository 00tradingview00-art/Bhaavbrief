**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver surged to 235358 INR/kg at 19:35 IST, posting a +1.26% gain from the previous close. The advance extends recovery momentum following yesterday's intraday dip to 229594 INR/kg at 09:33 IST. Broad-based commodity strength underpins the move: MCX Gold rallied +1.99% to 157326 INR/10g, while MCX Natgas jumped +2.42% to 271.2 INR/mmBtu. USD/INR stability at 95.74 INR provides currency support for rupee-denominated metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver is reasserting strength after oscillating between 229594–236969 INR/kg over the past session. The +1.26% move signals renewed risk appetite in the precious metals complex, with gold's outperformance (+1.99%) suggesting safe-haven flows remain intact. For MCX futures traders, this price action mirrors multi-commodity upside, indicating macro liquidity rather than silver-specific strength. Positioning appears constructive near 235000–236000 INR/kg.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 240000 round-number level tested at 15:08 IST yesterday remains the key resistance. A decisive breach above 236969 INR/kg (yesterday's 23:10 IST high) would confirm continuation; failure to hold 235000 INR/kg risks reversal toward 234900 support.
