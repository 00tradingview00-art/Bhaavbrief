**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold crossed ₹1,60,000 per 10 grams, with live COMEX pricing at $4,609/oz translating to approximately ₹1,62,302/10g at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.24.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Gold June contract is the primary instrument tracking this move, with spot conversion from COMEX already pricing the contract above the ₹1,60,000 psychological threshold. The ₹1,62,302/10g import parity level — derived from COMEX spot plus India's 15% customs duty and GST — sets the structural floor for MCX pricing; any domestic discount to this level signals suppressed local demand, while a premium indicates physical tightness. Indian bullion importers and jewellers hedging on MCX face elevated carry costs at these levels, as higher rupee-denominated gold prices widen the margin required for inventory hedging. The USD/INR rate at ₹95.24 remains a critical multiplier — every 50-paisa rupee depreciation adds approximately ₹810/10g to import parity, amplifying COMEX moves for domestic traders.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve policy statement and weekly COMEX Commitment of Traders (COT) report are the immediate catalysts — net speculative long positioning above recent highs confirms momentum continuation, while a reversal in positioning signals a correction risk back toward the ₹1,55,000 support zone on MCX.
