**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold surged ₹2,885 on MCX while silver hit a fresh record high of ₹3,39,927/kg, significantly above the live COMEX-derived MCX reference of ₹2,57,896/kg, signalling a sharp domestic premium expansion.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary contracts affected, with both trading well above COMEX-parity levels. Gold's MCX price is running above the live COMEX-derived equivalent of ₹1,60,047/10g, indicating either a widening domestic premium or a USD/INR move beyond the current ₹95.70 rate being priced into forward contracts. Silver's reported ₹3,39,927/kg represents a **32% premium** over the COMEX-derived ₹2,57,896/kg reference — a spread that points to aggressive domestic demand, duty-inclusive import cost pressures (India levies 15% basic customs duty plus GST on silver), and potential supply tightness in the physical market. Indian traders holding long positions in MCX Silver are sitting on contract values materially elevated against international benchmarks, compressing arbitrage windows for importers.

**WATCH**
Monitor the MCX Silver June contract's ability to hold above ₹3,30,000/kg on intraday pullbacks, and track COMEX Silver's reaction to the next U.S. CPI data release, which will either validate or deflate the safe-haven premium currently embedded in domestic prices.
