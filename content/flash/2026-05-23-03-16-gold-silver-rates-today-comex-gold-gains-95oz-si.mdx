**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX Gold surged $95/oz to $4,511 and Silver jumped $3.50/oz to $75.92 as US-Iran conflict escalation triggered acute safe-haven buying across precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary contracts affected, with both facing sharp upside pressure aligned with COMEX gains. At the live COMEX rate of $4,511/oz and USD/INR at ₹95.70, MCX Gold translates to approximately ₹1,59,597/10g — a level traders must track against current MCX spot to assess any premium or discount. Silver at $75.92/oz converts to roughly ₹2,56,935/kg on MCX, reflecting the full magnitude of the $3.50/oz COMEX move amplified by the rupee rate. Indian traders importing physical gold face elevated landed costs as the combined effect of higher COMEX prices and a USD/INR at ₹95.70 widens import cost exposure, with customs duty and GST layered on top, compressing domestic buying appetite even as futures reflect the geopolitical premium.

**WATCH**
Any official statement from the US State Department or Iranian government confirming escalation or de-escalation directly resets the safe-haven bid in both metals. A breach and hold above ₹1,60,000/10g on MCX Gold confirms the geopolitical premium is pricing in sustained conflict risk.
