**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX has launched options contracts on its liquid Gold and Silver futures, introducing a new derivatives layer on top of the existing futures market with defined trading periods, expiry schedules, and calendar structures.

**MCX IMPACT**
The new options contracts directly affect MCX Gold (current reference: ₹160,946/10g) and MCX Silver (₹259,410/kg) markets by expanding hedging instruments available to Indian traders beyond plain vanilla futures. Options pricing on MCX will derive from the underlying futures, meaning implied volatility premiums will now reflect both COMEX spot moves and the USD/INR rate at ₹96.21 — any rupee depreciation widens the INR-denominated premium on calls. For physical importers and jewellers managing customs duty exposure, options now allow precise cost-of-carry hedging without the full margin commitment of futures. The spread between COMEX gold at $4,525/oz and MCX-equivalent pricing will become a key arbitrage reference point for options writers calculating fair value on strikes.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official contract specification circular for lot sizes, strike intervals, and expiry alignment with existing futures — mismatches between COMEX options expiry and MCX expiry dates create basis risk that traders must price in before the first expiry cycle.
