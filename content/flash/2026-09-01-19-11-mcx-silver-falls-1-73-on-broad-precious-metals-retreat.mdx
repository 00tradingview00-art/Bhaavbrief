**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver declined to 229803 INR/kg at 19:11 IST, posting a −1.73% loss from the previous close. The slide extends a reversal from the 245585 INR/kg peak reached at 10:20 IST, unwinding the morning's +0.96% rally. Gold weakness (−1.58% to 152019 INR/10g) and softer rupee momentum (USD/INR at 95.07, −0.18%) underpin the pullback across the precious metals complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver has surrendered intraday gains and tested lower support after a three-session recovery attempt. The −1.73% evening slide signals profit-taking ahead of key resistance, while broad precious metals weakness suggests traders are rotating risk. USD/INR stability limits rupee support, keeping import parity under pressure for MCX futures positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 229000 INR/kg round-number support now becomes critical. Break below this level would confirm the reversal; a hold would set up a retest of the 240000−245000 zone resistance.
