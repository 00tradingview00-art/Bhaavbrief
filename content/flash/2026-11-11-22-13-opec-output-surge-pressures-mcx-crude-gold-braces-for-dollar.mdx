**WHAT HAPPENED**
OPEC announced that global oil production is approaching pre-conflict levels following de-escalation in Iran-related tensions, signalling a potential supply surplus in coming weeks.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Refinery procurement desks and crude importers face downward repricing pressure on **MCX Crude** as anticipated barrel scarcity dissolves into glut expectations, directly eroding the risk premium currently embedded in **₹8,183/bbl**. Simultaneously, a sustained crude oversupply scenario typically strengthens the **US dollar** against emerging-market currencies including the rupee, which mechanically lifts rupee-denominated bullion — making **MCX Gold** at **₹139,730/10g** and **MCX Silver** at **₹184,622/kg** more expensive for domestic importers and jewellery fabricators sourcing dollar-priced metal.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Indian oil refiners and petroleum product wholesalers will see their crude procurement costs ease, but this margin relief reverses if rupee weakness forces them to pay more dollars per barrel even as WTI retreats. Downstream fuel retailers and aviation fuel suppliers benefit from lower feedstock costs, though logistics operators and road-transport fleets still absorb fuel surcharges set contractually before this supply signal materializes. Jewellery manufacturers and silverware exporters holding dollar liabilities face immediate input cost pressure as rupee depreciation compounds gold and silver fabrication costs, compressing margins until retail prices adjust upward — a lag that squeezes working capital for smaller artisans and D2C brands. Household gold buyers and wedding-season purchasers will encounter higher prices across ornaments and coins, while solar-panel installers relying on silver-coated thermal systems face upstream cost creep.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian oil refiners will see crude input costs decline, but rupee depreciation driven by dollar strength may offset procurement gains within the fortnight. **MCX Crude** is signalling a breakdown below current support, while **MCX Gold** is primed to test resistance as safe-haven demand meets currency weakness. Retail gold and silver jewellery prices are positioned to rise despite crude's directional drop.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
OPEC's next production report and any fresh Iran-geopolitical headlines will confirm whether the de-escalation narrative holds; a breach of **₹8,000/bbl** on MCX Crude would validate the surplus thesis.
