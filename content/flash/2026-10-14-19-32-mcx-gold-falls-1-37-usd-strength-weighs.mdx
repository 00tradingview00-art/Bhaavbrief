**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 146418 INR/10g at 07:32 pm IST, posting a −1.37% loss from the previous close. The pullback extends intraday volatility that saw the contract surge to 151400 INR/10g at 10:40 pm IST yesterday, then reverse sharply. USD/INR edged down marginally to 95.25, while silver fell −3.57% to 211400 INR/kg. Crude posted +1.37% strength at 9076 INR/bbl.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's renewed weakness signals persistent rupee headwinds despite USD/INR's slight pullback. The −1.37% slide breaks the brief safe-haven rally seen yesterday and suggests demand destruction at higher levels. Cross-asset weakness in silver (−3.57%) and natural gas (−2.20%) indicates broad liquidation pressure, not gold-specific flows. Traders face mixed signals: rupee resilience vs. commodity risk-off.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support at 145000 INR/10g. A break below would signal deeper correction momentum; hold above confirms consolidation within yesterday's 146418–151400 INR/10g range.
