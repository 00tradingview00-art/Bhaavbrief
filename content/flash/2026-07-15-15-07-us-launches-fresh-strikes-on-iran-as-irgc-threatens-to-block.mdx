**TITLE:** Iran Threat Sends Crude, Gold Spiking on MCX—Energy Supply Risk Peaks

**WHAT HAPPENED**
The US conducted fresh military strikes on Iranian targets as the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) threatened to block energy exports from the Middle East, escalating regional tensions and supply disruption risks.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Supply-side shock mechanics are now active: **IRGC threats to Strait of Hormuz transit chokepoints** directly compress available crude barrels to global refiners, forcing **HPCL, BPCL, and Reliance** to either bid premiums for non-Gulf barrels or accept landed-cost inflation on existing Gulf procurement contracts. Simultaneously, geopolitical risk premium attaches to **gold bullion**, pushing international spot prices higher and benefiting **Indian jewellers and bullion dealers** holding long physical positions, while the dollar-denominated import basket for crude widens the rupee hedge cost for **refineries and fuel distribution networks**.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
HPCL and BPCL crude procurement desks face immediate rupee-denominated import bill expansion on Persian Gulf cargoes, while Air India and IndiGo aviation fuel hedges reprice against widening WTI-to-Brent spreads, and LPG distribution franchises absorb landed-cost increases at retail cylinder levels. Petrol pump operators across western and southern coastal regions transmit pump-price pressures to commuters, while organized and unorganized jewellers see inventory valuations rise in rupee terms but face consumer demand compression from price-sensitive retail buyers.

**BOTTOM LINE**
**BPCL and HPCL refining margins** face potential compression if crude import costs spike faster than petroleum product realization. **MCX Crude Oil futures** should hold above the **$75–76/barrel threshold**; sustained breach above **$80** signals sustained supply-fear premium. **Petrol and diesel retail prices** will track landed crude costs with a 7–10 day lag, while **22-karat gold retail rates** in Bengaluru and Mumbai will move in tandem with international spot volatility.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **OPEC+ emergency communications** within 48 hours and watch for **Brent-WTI spread widening beyond $3/barrel**, which would signal genuine supply-route anxiety versus tactical posturing.
