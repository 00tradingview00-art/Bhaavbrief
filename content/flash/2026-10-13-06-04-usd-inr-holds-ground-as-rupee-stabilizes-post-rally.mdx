**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR remained flat at 95.76 INR at 06:04 am IST, showing +0.00% change from the previous close. The pair's consolidation follows an intraday peak of 97.07 INR at 03:40 pm IST, where foreign portfolio inflows had driven the dollar strength. MCX precious metals remain under pressure: gold at 149705 INR/10g (-1.18%), silver at 223850 INR/kg (-0.84%), while crude holds at 8902 INR/bbl (+0.24%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's pause at current levels reflects exhaustion of the recent FPI-driven rally. A stronger dollar typically pressures precious metals futures, as seen in today's gold and silver declines. Traders monitoring USD/INR strength as a cross-asset macro anchor should note the intraday reversal from 97.07 INR—suggesting profit-taking or RBI intervention support may cap further appreciation.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break below 95.50 INR or fresh strength toward 96.50 INR will signal whether this consolidation is a base or merely pause before renewed directional movement.
