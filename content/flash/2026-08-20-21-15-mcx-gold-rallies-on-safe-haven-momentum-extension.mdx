The persistence of gains across silver and crude suggests risk-off positioning is broadening beyond precious metals alone. Traders holding positions established near 155450 INR/10g are now testing higher resistance; consolidation or rejection at 160000 INR/10g will determine whether this rally commands fresh breakout conviction.
