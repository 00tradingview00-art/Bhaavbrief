**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rallied to 140222 INR/10g at 03:54 am IST, posting a +1.25% gain from the previous close. The overnight surge mirrors broad precious metals strength, with MCX Silver climbing +2.47% to 183555 INR/kg and MCX Copper advancing +1.41% to 1392.95 INR/kg. USD/INR weakened 0.18% to 95.09, removing currency headwinds. The move reverses yesterday's consolidation phase and extends the intraday volatility band established since 01:38 am IST.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, this breakout tests conviction above the 140000 psychological level after yesterday's tight range between 138447 and 141534. The synchronized strength across precious metals signals renewed safe-haven positioning. The softer rupee backdrop provides tactical support, though energy weakness (Crude +0.14%, Natural Gas −1.66%) suggests risk appetite remains fragile.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 141534 INR/10g resistance—yesterday's intraday low. A sustained close above this level would confirm momentum beyond mean reversion and target the 144100 high posted at 01:38 am IST.
