**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged over ₹1,800 in a single session on March 17, reaching ₹1.57 lakh/10g, while MCX Silver climbed more than 2% on the same day.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold April and MCX Silver May contracts are directly affected by this move. The current COMEX Gold price at $4,527/oz translates to approximately ₹1,61,025/10g at the live USD/INR rate of ₹96.21, indicating MCX spot is trading at a notable discount of roughly ₹4,000/10g to the theoretical import parity price — a spread that reflects customs duty absorption and domestic demand-supply dynamics. Silver's 2%-plus move on MCX aligns with COMEX Silver at $76.69/oz, which converts to approximately ₹2,60,924/kg, confirming the domestic contract is tracking international momentum closely. For Indian traders, the elevated USD/INR at ₹96.21 amplifies rupee-denominated import costs, mechanically widening the base support level for both metals on MCX regardless of COMEX direction.

**WATCH**
The next critical trigger is the U.S. Federal Reserve interest rate decision and accompanying statement, which directly reprices COMEX Gold and feeds through to MCX via the USD/INR conversion. A breach and hold above ₹1,61,025/10g on MCX Gold — the current COMEX parity level — confirms sustained bullish momentum; failure to reclaim it signals position unwinding.
