**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 94.47 INR at 05:05 am IST, marking a -0.30% pullback from the previous close. The rupee's renewed strength breached the 94.5 INR round-number level, extending the downtrend from earlier sessions. Commodity cross-currents persist: MCX Gold rallied +0.80% to 144382 INR/10g, MCX Crude surged +2.27% to 8554 INR/bbl, while MCX Copper retreated -1.06% to 1353.5 INR/kg, signalling mixed risk appetite amid rupee appreciation.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Round-level breaches in USD/INR carry structural weight for MCX traders hedging FX exposure. Sub-94.5 territory suggests sustained rupee strength, which typically compresses INR-denominated commodity export margins and can weigh on crude and metals futures pricing. The divergence between Gold's bounce and Copper's weakness signals selective commodity demand, not broad flight-to-safety.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support formation at 94.40 INR will confirm whether this downtrend holds. A bounce back above 94.6 INR would signal mean reversion within the 94.47–95.0 INR range established overnight.
