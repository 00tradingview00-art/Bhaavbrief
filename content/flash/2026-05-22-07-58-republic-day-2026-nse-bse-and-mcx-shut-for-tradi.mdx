**WHAT HAPPENED**
NSE, BSE, and MCX will remain shut for trading on Monday, January 26, 2026, on account of Republic Day.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold, Silver, Copper, and Crude Oil contracts are all affected by the Monday closure. With MCX offline, Indian traders carry full overnight and weekend exposure into Tuesday's open — any sharp moves on COMEX or NYMEX during the holiday gap will be reflected entirely in Tuesday's opening price. At current levels of ₹160,971/10g for Gold and ₹9,598/bbl for Crude Oil, even a 0.5% COMEX move during the closure translates to ₹805 and ₹48 gap risk respectively on those contracts. Indian importers and hedgers with open positions cannot adjust or exit on Monday, leaving unhedged INR exposure active at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹96.21 for the full session.

**WATCH**
Tuesday, January 27 MCX opening prices versus Monday's COMEX settlement will be the critical gap-measure; any significant move in WTI Crude above $99/bbl or COMEX Gold above $4,600/oz during the holiday will set the tone for Tuesday's session direction and volatility.
