**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude eased to 7640 INR/bbl at 01:30 am IST, posting a -0.07% decline from the previous close. The minor retreat continues the consolidation phase after yesterday's +6.67% bounce from 7545 INR/bbl lows at 10:17 pm IST. USD/INR softened 0.37% to 95.33 INR, while natural gas climbed +0.69% to 264.5 INR/mmBtu, reflecting diverging energy narratives.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sub-0.1% move signals equilibrium between yesterday's supply-driven recovery and lingering geopolitical moderation. Crude is trading within a 268 INR/bbl range (7545–8163) established over the past 24 hours. For MCX futures traders, this micro-volatility suggests position-squaring ahead of stronger directional cues, with precious metals (gold –0.21%, silver –0.32%) showing broader risk-off undertones.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Hold above 7620 INR/bbl support to maintain yesterday's recovery thesis. A break below this level targets the 7545 low; a retest of 8163 confirms consolidation integrity.
