Ram Navami 2026 falls on a scheduled exchange holiday, with both MCX and NCDEX remaining closed for the occasion as per their officially declared 2026 holiday calendars.

On the preceding trading session, MCX contracts across key commodities — including crude oil, gold, silver, and base metals — will see compressed volumes as traders square off or reduce positions ahead of the one-day closure. Carry-over risk from overnight global moves in COMEX and Brent remains unhedged during the holiday gap, with positions resuming mark-to-market only on the next trading day.
