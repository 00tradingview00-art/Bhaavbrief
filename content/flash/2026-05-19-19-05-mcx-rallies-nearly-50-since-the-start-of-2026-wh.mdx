MCX has surged nearly 50% since the start of 2026, making it one of the strongest performances recorded by India's largest commodity exchange in recent history.

The rally reflects broad-based strength across MCX-listed commodities, particularly bullion and energy contracts. Gold and silver futures on MCX have tracked sharp global price increases driven by safe-haven demand and dollar weakness, while crude oil contracts have responded to supply-side volatility. Higher traded volumes and increased open interest have amplified exchange revenue expectations, directly lifting MCX stock. Rising commodity price levels mechanically increase transaction-value-based fees, boosting the exchange's top line without proportional cost increases.
