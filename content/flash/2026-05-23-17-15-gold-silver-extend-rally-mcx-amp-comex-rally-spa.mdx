**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices extended their rally on both COMEX and MCX, triggering accelerated ETF inflows as COMEX gold trades at $4,523/oz and silver at $76.20/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) reflects approximately ₹160,047/10g and MCX Silver approximately ₹257,896/kg at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70 — both elevated benchmarks that are directly compressing the landed import cost advantage for Indian bullion traders. The ETF buying frenzy on domestic exchanges tightens the MCX-COMEX spread, as sustained rupee weakness at ₹95.70 amplifies the INR-denominated price, adding roughly ₹2,800–3,200/10g in currency-driven premium versus a ₹83-level baseline. India's 15% customs duty on gold imports remains a structural floor for MCX prices, meaning any COMEX correction does not translate proportionally to MCX downside. Traders holding MCX Gold positions must monitor the ₹160,000/10g level as an immediate technical reference — a close above this level confirms ETF-driven momentum is sustaining domestic demand pressure.

**WATCH**
The U.S. Federal Reserve's next policy statement and the forthcoming U.S. CPI print are the critical triggers — a softer inflation reading or dovish Fed language will either validate this rally continuation or force an unwinding of ETF positions across both COMEX and MCX.
