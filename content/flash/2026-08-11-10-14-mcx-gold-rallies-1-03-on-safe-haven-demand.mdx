**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold extended its recovery trajectory to 154680 INR/10g at 10:14 am IST, posting a +1.03% gain. The metal's advance reflects sustained safe-haven appetite across the commodity complex: MCX Silver climbed +0.62% to 238334 INR/kg, while MCX Copper and Crude both gained +0.49% and +0.60% respectively. USD/INR weakened marginally at 95.4 INR (+0.20%), providing technical support for gold pricing in rupee terms.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's continuation above 154000 INR/10g signals intact bullish momentum from the 11:23 am IST low of 143814 INR/10g established yesterday. The cross-asset strength—silver outpacing gold, copper holding firm—suggests broad risk-off positioning rather than metal-specific weakness. Rupee stability (95.4 INR) removes headwind pressure, leaving gold traders focused on rupee depreciation or geopolitical triggers as next catalysts.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break above 155000 INR/10g would confirm the move beyond the recent swing high near 151935 INR/10g. Failure to hold 154000 will test 153500 support and reset near-term momentum.
