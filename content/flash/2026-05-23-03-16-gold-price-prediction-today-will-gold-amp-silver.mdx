**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on COMEX has reached $4,511/oz while silver trades at $75.92/oz, with both metals holding near multi-year highs amid sustained institutional demand and macro uncertainty.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (current import parity ~₹159,597/10g) and MCX Silver (~₹256,935/kg) are the directly affected contracts. With USD/INR at ₹95.70, the elevated rupee-dollar rate amplifies COMEX gains in domestic terms, widening the landed cost for Indian bullion importers beyond the base metal price. India's 15% basic customs duty on gold imports plus GST creates a structural premium layer on MCX over COMEX-derived spot — at current levels, any further rupee depreciation tightens this spread against importers and raises physical procurement costs for jewellers and refiners. Indian traders tracking the MCX-COMEX spread must account for this compounded currency and duty load, which makes MCX prices mechanically sticky on the upside even if COMEX consolidates.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve policy statement and U.S. Core PCE inflation print are the primary catalysts — a hotter-than-expected PCE reading reinforces the hard-asset bid, while any Fed pivot signal tests support at the $4,400/oz COMEX level, translating to approximately ₹155,700/10g on MCX at current USD/INR.
