**WHAT HAPPENED**
Multiple vessels faced attacks in the Persian Gulf, triggering a rebound in **MCX Crude Oil** from post-election lows as supply-route risk premiums re-entered pricing.

**WHAT MEANS**
When shipping lanes face repeated disruption threats, Indian refinery procurement desks and oil importers must budget for longer transit windows and potential rerouting costs, pushing effective landed prices higher even if spot **MCX Crude at ₹8465/bbl** remains below peak levels. Simultaneously, **gold** — traditionally a geopolitical safe-haven asset — should gain demand from institutional hedgers and retail risk-off flows, yet the post-election risk sentiment collapse is capping upside, keeping **MCX Gold at ₹142000/10g** within consolidation bands despite tail-risk headlines.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Petroleum refiners and fuel-blending units face immediate margin compression on gasoline and diesel exports, as transportation insurance premiums and demurrage costs rise faster than crude input prices adjust downward. Downstream fuel retailers and petrol pump operators holding inventory purchased at lower pre-attack price levels now absorb opportunity loss if crude stabilizes at elevated levels, squeezing their ability to cut retail prices. End consumers filling vehicle tanks and households consuming diesel for power generation — particularly in logistics, agriculture, and SME sectors — will see any margin relief delayed, as retailers typically lag 7–10 days in price transmission.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Shipping-dependent refiners operating on 3–5% net margins will see cost-per-barrel creep upward even as crude spot prices stabilize. **MCX Crude** must hold above ₹8400/bbl for this safe-haven premium to persist; a drop below ₹8200/bbl signals demand destruction outweighing geopolitical risk. Diesel-consuming logistics companies will delay rate cuts, keeping transportation costs sticky for FMCG and e-commerce fulfilment networks.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Next OPEC+ output guidance and any formal statement from Gulf Cooperation Council members on shipping security will determine whether this rebound holds or reverses. A breach of **₹8600/bbl** on MCX Crude would confirm sustained risk-off repositioning.
