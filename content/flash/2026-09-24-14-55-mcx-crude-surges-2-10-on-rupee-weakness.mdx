**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 9010 INR/bbl at 02:55 pm IST, posting a +2.10% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 95.95 INR (+0.24%). The rupee depreciation continues to provide consistent export-linked support for crude prices. This move extends the recovery from profit-taking exhaustion observed near 9000 INR/bbl levels, with Natural Gas also advancing +1.21% to 292.1 INR/mmBtu alongside crude strength.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, the break above 9010 INR/bbl signals renewed momentum as currency weakness sustains upside bias. The broader snapshot shows precious metals retreating sharply—Gold at 150390 INR/10g (−0.60%) and Silver at 233144 INR/kg (−1.17%)—indicating risk-on sentiment favoring energy. This positioning suggests energy complex strength may persist while safe havens face headwinds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 9050 INR/bbl resistance level. If crude sustains above this, expect acceleration toward 9100 INR/bbl. Conversely, a slip below 9000 INR/bbl would confirm range-bound consolidation rather than directional breakout.
