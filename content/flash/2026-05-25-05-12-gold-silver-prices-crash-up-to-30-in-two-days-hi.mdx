**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices on MCX crashed up to 30% intraday on Union Budget Day following the Finance Minister's announcement slashing customs duty on gold from 15% to 6% and silver from 15% to 6%, a 9-percentage-point reduction.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold and MCX Silver contracts bore the direct brunt of this policy shock, with import cost mechanics driving the sell-off mechanically rather than any COMEX-side move. At the current COMEX gold price of $4,581/oz translating to approximately ₹1,62,083/10g on MCX, the 9% customs duty reduction compresses the theoretical MCX fair value by roughly ₹14,500–₹15,000/10g, forcing the domestic spot-futures spread to reprice sharply lower. Silver's MCX contract at approximately ₹2,68,026/kg faces a proportionally larger absolute rupee impact given its higher import sensitivity. Stocks directly linked to precious metals — Hindustan Zinc, Muthoot Finance, and MCX exchange itself — repriced as market participants recalibrated earnings and collateral valuations tied to the new duty structure.

**WATCH**
The next critical marker is the official gazette notification confirming the duty cut's effective date, which will determine whether the full arbitrage between old-duty and new-duty inventory gets realized in spot prices immediately. MCX Gold holding above or below ₹1,47,000/10g post-session will confirm whether the duty reset is fully priced in.
