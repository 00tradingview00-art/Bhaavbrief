**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX and Shanghai Exchange silver inventories have declined to multi-year lows, tightening physical supply across the two largest silver warehousing systems globally.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (30kg contract) faces direct upward pressure as COMEX inventory drawdowns reduce deliverable supply, mechanically widening the basis between spot and futures. At the live COMEX price of $76.20/oz, MCX Silver translates to approximately ₹2,57,896/kg — any further COMEX rally amplifies INR-denominated costs given USD/INR holding at ₹95.70. Indian importers face elevated landed costs as physical tightness commands stronger premiums over COMEX spot, compressing arbitrage windows that domestic refiners and jewellers typically exploit. With India importing the majority of its silver requirements, a sustained inventory deficit at COMEX and Shanghai structurally lifts the import cost floor for MCX participants regardless of short-term price oscillations.

**WATCH**
The weekly COMEX Silver Warehouse Stocks report (published every Friday by CME Group) is the next hard data point — a further drawdown below current levels confirms the supply squeeze narrative, while any inventory rebuild negates the tightness thesis. Monitor MCX Silver's ₹2,60,000/kg level as the immediate technical resistance zone under this supply framework.
