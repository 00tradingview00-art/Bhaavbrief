**WHAT HAPPENED**
Analysts are forecasting a gap-up opening for gold on Monday following US-Iran geopolitical developments, with immediate upside targets cited for both COMEX and MCX contracts.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) is the primary instrument affected, currently referencing a COMEX spot of $4,523/oz translating to approximately ₹1,60,047/10g at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. A gap-up opening on COMEX directly transmits into MCX pricing through the import parity mechanism — any further dollar-denominated rally amplifies rupee-denominated gains when the exchange rate holds above ₹95. Indian traders face an additional layer via customs duty (currently 6% on gold imports plus GST), which widens the landed cost above raw COMEX-to-INR conversion and keeps MCX at a structural premium to COMEX equivalent. Geopolitical risk premiums in crude — WTI already at $96.60/bbl — simultaneously support gold's safe-haven bid, compressing the window for any mean-reversion trade on MCX.

**WATCH**
Monday's MCX opening print relative to Friday's close is the immediate confirmation trigger — a gap above ₹1,60,047/10g sustained in the first 30 minutes signals follow-through buying. Any de-escalation headline from US-Iran diplomatic channels negates the gap premise and shifts focus back to Fed rate trajectory data later in the week.
