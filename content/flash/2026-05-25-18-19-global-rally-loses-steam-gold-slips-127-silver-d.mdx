**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold dropped 1.27% and silver fell 5% on MCX in a single session as the global commodity rally reversed sharply, with silver's decline outpacing gold by a factor of nearly four.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold and MCX Silver are the direct contracts under pressure. Gold on MCX is currently anchored near ₹162,826/10g, derived from COMEX at $4,602/oz and USD/INR at ₹95.70; a 1.27% pullback translates to approximately ₹2,068/10g of erosion from recent highs. Silver's 5% decline is more acute — at the live import-equivalent rate of ₹264,499/kg, a 5% move represents roughly ₹13,225/kg of value destruction in a single session. For Indian traders, the sharp rupee at ₹95.70 provides a partial cushion against COMEX dollar weakness, but it also inflates the base import cost, meaning any further COMEX softness amplifies downside on MCX with limited offset from currency.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the U.S. Federal Reserve's upcoming policy commentary and weekly COMEX options expiry — a close below $4,550/oz on COMEX gold will pressure MCX gold toward the ₹161,000/10g zone, while silver needs to hold $76/oz on COMEX to arrest further MCX deterioration.
