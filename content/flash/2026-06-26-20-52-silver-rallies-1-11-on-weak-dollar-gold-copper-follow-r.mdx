**WHAT HAPPENED**

MCX precious metals posted a broad rally overnight as the US/EU session closed: **Silver +1.11%** to **₹222,100/kg**, **Gold +0.75%** to **₹144,199/10g**, and **Copper +0.70%** to **₹1,249.90/kg**. The moves signal renewed appetite for hard assets amid dollar weakness in offshore markets, with USD/INR holding at **₹94.39**.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

Dollar depreciation mechanically lifts rupee-denominated commodity prices because MCX contracts are priced in INR but globally arbitraged against USD-quoted spot. A weaker dollar reduces the import-parity cost: if global silver trades at ~$32/oz and USD/INR falls from 94.50 to 94.30, the INR price floor compresses, but local demand for physical hedges rises as rupee strength improves purchasing power. Silver's 1.11% gain outpacing gold's 0.75% suggests rotation into the more volatile precious metal on reflation expectations (CRB commodity index strength).

**WHO IS AFFECTED**

Silver refiners and jewellery exporters (GITC members, Rajesh Masrani Group units) benefit from higher INR/kg realizations on forward sales, though their cost-of-goods remains indexed to global dollar spot. Silver-intensive industrial users—photography chemicals, electronics, solar wafer makers—face higher procurement hedges. Physical bullion dealers and retail investors repositioning into fractional allocations will anchor bids higher.

**BOTTOM LINE**

Commodity reflation, not rupee strength alone, is driving the multi-metal rally. Confirmation requires US real yields to weaken further or China demand signals to firm.

**WHAT TO WATCH**

US CPI release (Tuesday 1 July, 12:30 IST) and ECB speakers over the weekend—a softer-than-expected inflation print would extend the dollar downtrend and validate the rally into MCX's Monday open.
