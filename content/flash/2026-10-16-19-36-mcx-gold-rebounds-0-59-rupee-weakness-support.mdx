**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold recovered to 148354 INR/10g at 07:36 pm IST, posting a +0.59% gain from the previous close. The rebound follows a volatile 6-hour session: the contract had declined to 146418 INR/10g at 07:32 pm IST (−1.37%), then reversed sharply. USD/INR weakened marginally to 95.07, providing tailwind. Silver and copper also posted modest gains (+0.53% and +0.27%), while crude and natural gas softened.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold traders are navigating extreme intraday swings anchored to rupee moves and global risk sentiment. The 1,936 INR swing (146418–148354) within minutes signals thin liquidity and stop-hunting. USD/INR weakness at 95.07 reduces import costs and supports local bullion demand, but the session-high 151400 INR/10g (from 10:40 pm IST yesterday) remains the key overhead anchor for positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break and hold above 148500 INR/10g will signal mean reversion toward the 150000 psychological level. Failure below 148000 will confirm continuation of the −1.37% downtrend into the 146000 zone.
