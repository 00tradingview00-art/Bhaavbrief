**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude crossed the psychological 8000 INR/bbl level at 10:39 am IST, trading at 8001 INR/bbl with a +0.77% gain from previous close. The breach extends the recovery trajectory initiated from the 7134 INR/bbl trough logged at 22:29 IST yesterday. Supply-side stabilisation sentiment remains the primary driver. USD/INR at 95.42 INR provides supportive FX backdrop for rupee-denominated crude.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The round-number cross signals sustained bullish momentum within the recovery phase. For MCX futures traders, this level now transitions from resistance to support, suggesting conviction behind the 866 INR rally from yesterday's lows. Concurrent strength in precious metals (MCX_GOLD +0.58%, MCX_SILVER +0.84%) indicates broader risk-on sentiment supporting commodity positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8000 INR/bbl level itself — now a freshly broken resistance turned support. A close above 8000 validates the recovery thesis; a slip below signals consolidation within the 7940–8001 range.
