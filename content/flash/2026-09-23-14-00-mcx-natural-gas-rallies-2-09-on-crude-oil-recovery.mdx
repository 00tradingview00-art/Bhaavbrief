**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas surged to 288.4 INR/mmBtu at 14:00 IST, posting a +2.09% gain from the previous close. The recovery reverses overnight weakness that had dragged prices to 274.5 INR/mmBtu at 22:29 IST yesterday (−2.38%). Crude Oil's broader strength at 8656 INR/bbl (+0.15%) and steady USD/INR at 95.75 (+0.17%) are supporting the rebound from depressed levels established overnight.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, Natural Gas has reclaimed consolidation territory above 285 INR/mmBtu after breaking support overnight. The two-way volatility reflects correlation weakness with crude despite oil's modest gains. Cross-asset drag from gold (−0.35%) and silver (−0.70%) weakness suggests selective energy sector strength rather than broad risk-on sentiment.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Critical resistance sits at 290 INR/mmBtu. A break above this level would signal sustained recovery momentum; a pullback below 282 INR/mmBtu would retest overnight lows and confirm renewed weakness.
