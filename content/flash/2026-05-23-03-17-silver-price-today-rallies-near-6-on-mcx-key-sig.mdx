**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver prices surged approximately 6% on MCX in a single session, with the benchmark MCX Silver contract tracking the COMEX spot price currently at $75.92/oz, translating to a live MCX reference of ₹256,935/kg.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver futures contract (near-month) is the primary instrument affected by this move. The 6% intraday rally places spot-equivalent MCX pricing near ₹256,935/kg, a psychologically significant level that acts as immediate resistance for technical traders. Indian import costs are directly amplified by the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70 — every 25-paisa rupee depreciation adds approximately ₹800/kg to landed silver cost, widening the MCX-COMEX spread. Traders holding short positions on MCX Silver face elevated mark-to-market losses at current levels, while the customs duty structure (15% basic + GST) further cushions domestic prices from any COMEX correction, meaning MCX prices remain structurally elevated relative to international benchmarks.

**WATCH**
The next US Federal Reserve commentary or US CPI print will either sustain dollar weakness — the primary driver of silver's rally — or trigger a sharp reversal toward the ₹242,000/kg support zone on MCX Silver. A breach above ₹260,000/kg on sustained volume confirms momentum continuation.
