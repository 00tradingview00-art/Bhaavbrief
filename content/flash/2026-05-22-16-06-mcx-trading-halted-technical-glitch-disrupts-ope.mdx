**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX (Multi Commodity Exchange) trading was fully halted for over one hour due to a technical glitch disrupting exchange operations.

**MCX IMPACT**
All active MCX contracts — including Gold (currently ~₹160,802/10g), Silver (~₹259,318/kg), and Crude Oil (~₹9,666/bbl) — were rendered non-tradeable during the outage window, freezing price discovery entirely. During a halt, open positions cannot be hedged or exited, exposing traders to gap risk the moment the platform resumes — a mechanical vulnerability unique to single-exchange commodity markets like MCX where no parallel domestic venue exists. Any price movement on COMEX during the downtime period creates an immediate spread dislocation upon resumption, forcing MCX contracts to reprice sharply against live COMEX benchmarks and the USD/INR rate of ₹96.21. Traders holding leveraged intraday positions in Gold or Crude faced compulsory mark-to-market exposure without the ability to act, with import cost parity acting as the floor reference once trading resumed.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange circular for the declared halt timestamp and resumption time, as SEBI's framework mandates post-incident disclosure — this will confirm whether margin calls or contract extensions were triggered. The next MCX session open is the critical price level to watch for gap-up or gap-down against prevailing COMEX prices.
