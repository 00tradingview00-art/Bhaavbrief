**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude climbed to 7954 INR/bbl at 12:10 pm IST, extending the +1.94% advance from the previous close. The move sustains the recovery trajectory from the 7134 INR/bbl trough logged at 22:29 IST yesterday, as supply-side stabilisation sentiment persists. USD/INR stands at 95.43 INR (+0.23%), while MCX Gold holds at 153597 INR/10g (+0.33%) and MCX Silver at 235350 INR/kg (−0.64%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude has now consolidated above the 7900 INR/bbl zone after a 820 point recovery in under 14 hours, signalling sustained bid support. The steady rupee (+0.23%) and firmer precious metals backdrop suggest broad risk appetite. For futures traders, this breakout holds if supply reassurance narratives remain intact across the session.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8000 INR/bbl round number is the immediate resistance. A break and close above it would confirm the recovery's extension; failure below 7900 INR/bbl re-opens downside vulnerability.
