**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold surged 2% on MCX while silver posted a single-session gain of over ₹8,500/kg, tracking a sharp rally in COMEX prices driven by macro risk-off flows.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (near-month contract) and MCX Silver are the directly affected contracts. Gold is now trading in the vicinity of ₹1,60,047/10g on MCX, a level that represents a historically elevated import cost threshold for Indian bullion dealers. At USD/INR of ₹95.70, every dollar move in COMEX gold translates to approximately ₹307/10g on MCX after applying the standard conversion — this amplifies COMEX volatility for Indian participants beyond the raw dollar move. Silver at ₹2,57,896/kg means import parity costs, inclusive of India's 15% customs duty plus GST on silver, are now running at steep premiums, compressing physical dealer margins and widening the COMEX-MCX spread. Indian refiners and jewellers hedging forward purchases face elevated cost-of-carry at these levels.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve communication or CPI data release will either sustain this safe-haven bid or trigger a sharp mean-reversion in COMEX gold toward the $4,400/oz support zone, directly resetting MCX settlement benchmarks. A breach of ₹1,62,000/10g on MCX will confirm continuation; failure to hold ₹1,58,500 signals reversal.
