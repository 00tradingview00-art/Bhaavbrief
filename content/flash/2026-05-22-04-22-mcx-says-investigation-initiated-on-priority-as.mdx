**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX's trading platform suffered a technical outage lasting over four hours, prompting the exchange to state that an "investigation has been initiated on priority."

**MCX IMPACT**
All active MCX contracts were affected — Gold (currently referencing ₹161,599/10g), Silver (₹262,414/kg), Crude Oil (₹9,591/bbl), and Copper were rendered non-tradeable for the duration of the outage. During the halt, COMEX Gold continued moving at $4,543/oz and WTI at $97.73/bbl, meaning Indian traders were unable to hedge or exit positions as international prices evolved in real time. The spread between COMEX spot and MCX prices widened in practice — not due to duty or import cost shifts, but because MCX participants were locked out of price discovery entirely while customs duty-adjusted fair values continued shifting with live USD/INR at ₹96.21. Traders holding open positions in near-month contracts faced unhedged mark-to-market exposure for the entire four-hour window, with no ability to adjust against international price movement.

**WATCH**
MCX's official post-investigation report will confirm whether positions are being reviewed for forced settlement or margin adjustments; the next scheduled trading session open is the immediate trigger to watch for gap risk across all active contracts.
