**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX silver surged over 6% intraday, pushing MCX silver prices up 5% and triggering a sharp rally in silver and gold ETFs domestically.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (near-month contract) bears the direct impact, with spot prices now tracking around ₹257,896/kg as COMEX silver trades at $76.20/oz. The 5% MCX move reflects the COMEX surge amplified by the USD/INR rate at ₹95.70 — a weaker rupee widens the import cost premium and mechanically lifts MCX settlement prices. Indian traders face elevated import parity costs: at current COMEX levels, landed silver cost including 10% customs duty and GST pushes effective import price well above MCX spot, compressing the arbitrage window for bulk importers. MCX Gold (June contract) has also seen sympathy buying, with COMEX gold at $4,523/oz translating to approximately ₹160,047/10g — traders holding gold-silver ratio positions face ratio compression as silver outperforms.

**WATCH**
The next key trigger is the US Federal Reserve speakers scheduled this week and Friday's US non-farm payrolls print — a softer labor market reading sustains the precious metals bid, while a strong number reverses risk-off flows and pressures both COMEX and MCX silver back toward the $72-73/oz support zone.
