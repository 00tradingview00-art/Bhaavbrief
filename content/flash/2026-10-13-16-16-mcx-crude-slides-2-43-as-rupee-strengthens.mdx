**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude fell to 8762 INR/bbl at 04:16 pm IST, posting a -2.43% loss as USD/INR weakened to 95.45 INR (-0.54% vs prev close). The rupee's unexpected strength has eroded export competitiveness for domestic crude, reversing the afternoon rally to 8869 INR/bbl logged at 03:47 pm IST when the rupee had depreciated to 96.51 INR.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sharp rupee rally (95.45 INR) has decisively broken the export-competitiveness tailwind that supported crude above 8869 INR/bbl. MCX traders are now caught between the afternoon reversal pattern and the currency pivot, signaling a pause in the rally momentum established since 03:47 pm IST.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8627 INR/bbl support level—the overnight low from 10:21 pm IST—will signal whether this rupee-driven pullback has structural conviction or merely corrective legs.
