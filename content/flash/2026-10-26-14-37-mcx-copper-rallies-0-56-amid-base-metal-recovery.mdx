**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper advanced to 1384.75 INR/kg at 02:37 pm IST, gaining +0.56% from the previous close. The move reflects a modest recovery in base metals following earlier profit-taking that had driven prices to 1364 INR/kg at 12:56 am IST (−1.90%). USD/INR edged higher to 95.23 (+0.32%), while precious metals strengthened—Gold at 142908 INR/10g (+0.62%) and Silver at 196403 INR/kg (+0.35%)—signaling renewed risk appetite. Crude oil weakness persists at 9615 INR/bbl (−1.89%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's recovery to near 1385 INR/kg suggests stabilisation after the −1.90% dip, though the metal remains below the intraday peak of 1411 INR/kg logged at 01:37 am IST. The stronger rupee (+0.32%) and resilient precious metals provide technical support, but crude's sharp decline and natural gas's −4.64% collapse indicate mixed macro sentiment. Traders face a consolidation zone between 1364–1411 INR/kg.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1400 INR/kg round number and the 1411 INR/kg intraday peak. A close above 1400 would suggest breakout momentum; failure signals renewed profit-taking toward the 1364 support.
