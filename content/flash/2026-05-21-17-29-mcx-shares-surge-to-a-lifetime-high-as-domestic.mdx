MCX shares hit a lifetime high on the bourses as domestic gold and silver futures rallied sharply, with the exchange reporting strong Q4 earnings driven by higher trading volumes across precious metals contracts.

On MCX, gold futures and silver futures both moved upward, reflecting elevated global bullion prices and sustained domestic demand. Higher futures turnover directly boosts MCX's transaction-fee revenue, explaining the stock's record run. Gold and silver remain the exchange's dominant revenue-generating contracts, and any sustained rally in these commodities translates into increased open interest, higher volumes, and stronger top-line numbers for the platform.
