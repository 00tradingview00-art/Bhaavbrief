**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas rallied to 313.2 INR/mmBtu at 03:29 am IST, posting a +3.64% gain from the previous close. The sharp recovery reverses yesterday's multi-phase decline and marks the contract's approach toward the upper end of its 24-hour trading band (288.4–313.1 INR/mmBtu). USD/INR weakened -0.55% to 95.45 INR, reducing hedging costs. MCX Crude remained under pressure at 8958 INR/bbl (-0.24%), while precious metals slipped (Gold -0.85%, Silver -1.81%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The overnight bounce signals potential reversal of the downtrend that dominated the previous session. For MCX futures traders, this move reestablishes the 310+ level as a key psychological anchor after near-flat consolidation at 309.7 INR/mmBtu at 03:46 am IST. The rupee's weakness supports import-parity narratives, though crude's lag suggests the move is gas-specific rather than broad energy demand-driven.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Traders should monitor the 315 INR/mmBtu resistance level and the 310 INR/mmBtu support zone established overnight. A close above 315 would confirm break-out potential; breakdown toward 305 would signal resumption of downside pressure.
