**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude slipped to 7137 INR/bbl at 11:23 am IST, posting a -1.07% decline from the previous close. The retreat extends the pullback from the 8961 INR/bbl peak logged on 24 January as geopolitical supply anxieties continue to moderate. USD/INR softened 0.26% to 95.09 INR, while MCX Gold climbed +0.86% to 143814 INR/10g and MCX Silver surged +2.11% to 226300 INR/kg.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The crude pullback signals renewed caution after the brief 8163 INR/bbl spike on 24 January. For MCX futures traders, this 1025 INR descent from the January peak suggests mean reversion within a broad consolidation band. Rupee weakness against the dollar is being offset by softer commodity demand signals across energy markets, constraining upside.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 7100 INR/bbl support level—a break below locks in the pullback structure. A rebound toward 7400 INR/bbl would signal range-bound consolidation; failure to hold above 7000 INR/bbl would confirm deeper weakness.
