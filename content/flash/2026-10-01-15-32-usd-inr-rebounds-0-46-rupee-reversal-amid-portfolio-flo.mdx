**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR advanced to 96.57 INR at 03:32 pm IST, climbing +0.46% from the previous close. The rupee's intraday reversal extends the rebound that began at 03:27 pm IST when the pair touched 96.39 INR. Foreign portfolio inflows documented since 01:34 pm IST remain the dominant driver, though profit-taking and dollar positioning adjustments are now offsetting earlier rupee strength. MCX gold stands at 146950 INR/10g (+0.57%), silver at 224600 INR/kg (+0.40%), and crude at 8902 INR/bbl (+1.92%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Rupee volatility in the 95.71–96.57 INR band signals competing flows: sustained FPI demand against dollar demand on dips. For MCX traders, rupee weakness directly pressures gold and silver valuations in rupee terms, while crude benefits from dollar depreciation. The oscillating pattern since 02:54 pm IST suggests consolidation rather than directional conviction—positioning remains fluid as intraday traders scale in and out.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 96.50 INR level now acts as immediate resistance; a sustained close above 96.70 INR would signal renewed dollar strength, while a drop below 96.00 INR would confirm renewed rupee appreciation phase.
