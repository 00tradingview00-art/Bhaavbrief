**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR slipped to 94.49 INR at 01:42 pm IST, crossing the round-number support level of 94.5 INR. The rupee's sustained appreciation reflects continued foreign portfolio inflows into domestic equities and bonds. MCX precious metals remain resilient: gold at 152778 INR/10g (+0.01%), silver at 237301 INR/kg (-0.15%), while crude holds gains at 8612 INR/bbl (+0.40%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX traders, rupee strength creates headwinds for commodity exporters but boosts import competitiveness. A weaker USD/INR compresses rupee-denominated commodity prices relative to global benchmarks. The breach of 94.5 signals sustained inflow momentum; gold's price stickiness despite rupee gains suggests underlying demand resilience offsetting currency headwinds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 94.45 support level. A decisive hold above 94.5 would confirm rupee momentum; a break below 94.45 would test the 94.3 psychological floor and signal deeper currency appreciation.
