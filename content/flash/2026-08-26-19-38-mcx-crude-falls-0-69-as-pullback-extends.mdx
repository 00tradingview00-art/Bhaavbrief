**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude declined to 7783 INR/bbl at 19:38 IST, extending the −0.69% pullback from previous close. The move continues downside momentum established at 16:27 IST (−3.13% to 7880 INR/bbl) and reverses the intraday recovery logged earlier. Cross-asset weakness persists: MCX_GOLD at 162155 INR/10g (−0.45%), MCX_SILVER at 242509 INR/kg (−0.66%), and USD/INR at 95.4 (−0.34% weakness supporting rupee strength).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, the 7783 level signals sustained pressure within a −3.13% intraday range. Rupee appreciation (USD/INR declining to 95.4) continues to weigh on INR-denominated crude pricing. The pullback erases recovery attempts and keeps positioning vulnerable to further downside as cross-asset weakness remains broad-based across precious metals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Breach below the 7658 INR/bbl low (09:30 IST) would confirm renewed momentum. Hold above 7780 is critical to establish a floor for the current consolidation phase.
