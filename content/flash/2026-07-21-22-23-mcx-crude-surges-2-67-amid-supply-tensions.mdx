**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 8161 INR/bbl at 10:23 pm IST, extending a +2.67% gain from the previous close. The move reflects sustained geopolitical supply concerns supporting global oil prices. Across the commodity complex, silver climbed +2.49% to 223847 INR/kg and copper gained +2.05% to 1341.1 INR/kg, signaling broad-based strength. The rupee held steady at 96.23 INR against the dollar.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude has now breached the 8160 level twice in the session, with intraday prints near 8164 INR/bbl confirming sustained momentum. For MCX futures traders, the sharp multi-asset rally—spanning precious metals and base metals—suggests risk-on positioning is holding despite macro headwinds. The stability in USD/INR at 96.23 removes currency drag from upside moves.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8200 INR/bbl round number is the next critical resistance. A close above 8165 INR/bbl would signal the supply risk premium is consolidating at higher levels and worth monitoring for continuation signals.
