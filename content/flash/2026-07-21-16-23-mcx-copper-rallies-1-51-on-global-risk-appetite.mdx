**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper surged to 1334.05 INR/kg at 04:23 pm IST, marking a +1.51% gain from the previous close. The move aligns with broad-based strength across the MCX complex: Silver rallied +2.20%, Gold +0.93%, Crude +1.01%, and Natural Gas +1.38%. The rupee held steady at 96.24 INR (−0.05%), allowing base metal gains to flow through unhedged. Risk-on sentiment appears to be driving the copper recovery.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX copper traders, this breakout suggests a shift toward risk appetite after recent consolidation. The synchronized strength in Silver, Crude, and Natural Gas signals broad liquidity flowing into commodities. At 1334.05 INR/kg, copper is testing recovery momentum; positioning leans constructive if this level holds as support into tomorrow's session.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1340 INR/kg round level is the next structural resistance. A close above it would confirm the breakout; a retest of 1325 INR/kg would signal consolidation resumption.
