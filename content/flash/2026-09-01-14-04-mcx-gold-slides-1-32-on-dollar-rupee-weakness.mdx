**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold fell to 152425 INR/10g at 14:04 IST, extending a -1.32% decline from the previous close. The pullback accelerates a reversal from the 164604 INR/10g peak recorded at 18:59 IST yesterday. Silver mirrored weakness at 230438 INR/kg (-1.46%), while crude climbed +1.89% to 8303 INR/bbl. USD/INR dipped -0.19% to 95.06 INR, reducing dollar-hedging demand for rupee bullion.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold traders face sustained downside pressure as the rupee strengthens against the dollar, eroding the hedging appeal of local bullion. The 12179 INR/10g loss since yesterday's peak signals a shift from safe-haven positioning. Cross-asset weakness in silver and stable natural gas hint at broader risk-off sentiment, though crude's strength keeps inflation hedging alive.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support at 150000 INR/10g level. A break below this psychological floor would confirm continuation toward 148000 INR/10g; a hold signals potential consolidation around current levels.
