**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 8275 INR/bbl at 02:23 pm IST, posting a +1.52% gain and extending the recovery initiated from 7754 INR/bbl (−2.19%) at 17:37 IST yesterday. The advance reflects sustained supply-side stabilisation sentiment. Broader commodity support remains: gold +0.16% to 158245 INR/10g, silver +0.91% to 238950 INR/kg. USD/INR eased marginally to 95.69 (−0.13%), providing relative tailwind.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rally has now recovered 520 INR/bbl from the session low, signalling conviction in the stabilisation narrative. For MCX futures traders, the move consolidates the intraday upside after yesterday's volatility. Cross-asset strength in gold and silver supports risk appetite, though natural gas weakness (−2.25%) suggests selective energy demand dynamics.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8300 INR/bbl round number is critical resistance. A break above confirms momentum; a retreat below 8200 INR/bbl would suggest consolidation within the recovery band.
