**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 9264 INR/bbl at 14:33 IST, posting a +1.81% gain and extending the multi-leg recovery sequence that began at 21:26 IST yesterday from 7918 INR/bbl. The move sustains the upside trajectory logged at 9083 INR/bbl at 19:19 IST and 8946 INR/bbl at 13:45 IST. USD/INR weakness at 95.47 (+0.32%) and supportive global crude sentiment underpin the advance.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, the price hold above 9200 INR/bbl signals sustained bullish positioning after the +4.32% surge yesterday. The recovery from the 7918 INR/bbl base now spans 1346 INR/bbl notionally. Cross-asset weakness in silver (−1.15%) and natural gas (−1.15%) against crude strength suggests selective energy inflows and rupee depreciation (USD/INR +0.32%) supporting oil upside.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9300 INR/bbl resistance level becomes critical. A sustained break above 9264 would confirm the recovery trend; failure to hold this level risks mean reversion toward the 8946–9083 INR/bbl consolidation zone.
