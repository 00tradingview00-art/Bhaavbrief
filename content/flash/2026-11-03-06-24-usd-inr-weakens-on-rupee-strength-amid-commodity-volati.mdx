**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 94.89 INR at 06:24 am IST, marking a -0.02% pullback from the previous close. The rupee's renewed strength follows overnight commodity flux: MCX Crude slumped -4.78% to 9462 INR/bbl, while MCX Silver surged +1.13% to 181164 INR/kg and MCX Natural Gas climbed +1.44% to 281.1 INR/mmBtu. The rupee's resilience reflects broad-based commodity repricing and dollar weakness in early morning trade.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's mild strength at 94.89 INR signals a shift in risk sentiment after yesterday's commodity rally-driven dollar gains. Crude's sharp -4.78% drop is the key driver, weakening dollar-proxy demand. For MCX traders, this cross-asset divergence—crude collapsing while silver and natgas rally—suggests fragmented commodity flows and heightened positioning churn ahead of Asian cash open.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 94.85–95.00 INR band will determine whether rupee strength holds. A break below 94.85 INR would confirm broader dollar weakness; a rebound above 95.00 INR would signal commodity volatility reversal.
