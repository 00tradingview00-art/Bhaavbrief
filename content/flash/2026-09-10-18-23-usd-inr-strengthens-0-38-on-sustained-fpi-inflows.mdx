**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR climbed to 95.53 INR at 06:23 pm IST, gaining +0.38% from the previous close. The rupee's continued appreciation reflects sustained foreign portfolio inflows into domestic equities and bonds, building on the directional strength seen since 01:34 pm IST. Cross-asset pressures emerged: MCX gold fell to 152030 INR/10g (–1.13%), silver contracted sharply to 234487 INR/kg (–3.98%), while MCX crude surged to 9560 INR/bbl (+5.07%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's persistent strength past 95.50 INR signals deepening FPI confidence in Indian assets despite global oil volatility. For MCX traders, the inverse correlation between rupee appreciation and precious metals is now acute: gold and silver losses reflect currency headwinds, not fundamental weakness. Crude's +5.07% surge suggests geopolitical or supply-side tension is overriding rupee tailwinds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break above 95.60 INR would test the 95.70 round-number resistance. If USD/INR holds above 95.50 INR, expect further pressure on gold and silver into the evening close.
