**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices rebounded sharply in Indian spot markets, with 24K gold and silver rates rising across major cities, tracking COMEX gold at $4,523/oz and silver at $76.20/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (nearest active contract) and MCX Silver contracts face direct upward pressure from this rebound. At the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70, COMEX gold translates to approximately ₹1,60,047 per 10g on MCX after accounting for customs duty (currently 6% on gold) and GST (3%), making landed import cost the primary floor for domestic prices. MCX Silver at approximately ₹2,57,896/kg reflects the COMEX equivalent with similar duty structures — any compression in the COMEX-MCX spread signals domestic demand absorption or arbitrage activity that traders must monitor. Indian traders note that the rupee holding at ₹95.70 amplifies the imported commodity cost; any rupee depreciation from here adds a structural premium to both MCX Gold and Silver without any movement in COMEX prices.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve commentary or CPI data release serves as the primary confirmation trigger — a hawkish signal reverses this rebound while dollar weakness extends it. Monitor MCX Gold's ability to hold above the ₹1,60,000/10g level as the immediate technical floor derived from current COMEX-INR conversion.
