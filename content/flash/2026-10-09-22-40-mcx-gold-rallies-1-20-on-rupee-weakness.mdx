**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 151400 INR/10g at 10:40 pm IST, posting a +1.20% gain from the previous close. The recovery extends a broader safe-haven bid across commodities: MCX Silver (+1.95%), MCX Copper (+1.36%), and MCX Natural Gas (+2.29%) all rose in tandem. USD/INR edged down to 96.95 INR (−0.19%), providing marginal rupee support for bullion after a volatile session marked by sharp pullbacks earlier at 03:09 pm IST (−2.57%) and 07:20 pm IST (−0.98%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's recovery signals renewed appetite for rupee-denominated hard assets after intraday volatility. The +1.20% move reclaims ground lost during the session's weakness, though the metal remains below the 152950 INR/10g peak hit at 23:47 IST. Cross-asset strength in silver and copper indicates broad-based commodity support, while the softer rupee (96.95 INR) removes headwinds. Traders should monitor whether this rally sustains above 151000 resistance.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
151600 INR/10g—the next resistance barrier. A break above this level would signal continuation toward the 152950 intraday high; a retest of 150924 INR/10g would indicate renewed weakness.
