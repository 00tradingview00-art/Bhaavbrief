**WHAT HAPPENED**
The United States is deploying additional aerial refueling aircraft to Israel in preparation for a potential military escalation in the Middle East region.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Increased U.S. military presence typically precedes heightened geopolitical tension, which historically triggers supply-chain anxiety in energy markets. **Crude oil** imports face upward pressure as traders price in potential disruptions to Suez Canal transit and broader Middle Eastern production flows. Simultaneously, **gold** rallies on safe-haven demand as institutional investors and central banks hedge currency and equity exposure against escalation uncertainty. **Natural gas** remains sensitive to any disruption signals affecting LNG export facilities in Qatar and Australia, which feed into Asian spot markets that India's import contracts reference.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
**HPCL, BPCL, and Reliance Industries'** crude procurement teams face elevated WTI and Brent spreads translating into higher rupee import costs per barrel, while **IOC's** natural gas sourcing operations monitor LNG spot premiums against their contracted Henry Hub linkages. Aviation fuel hedging desks at **IndiGo** and **Air India** reprice jet fuel forwards, petrol pump operators absorb potential landed-cost increases, and household LPG consumers face cylinder pricing adjustments cascading through distributors.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Refinery operators will absorb margin compression if **crude oil** breaches the **$85/bbl threshold**, directly reducing gross refining margins. **MCX Crude Oil** futures should trend toward resistance around **₹6,800–6,900 per barrel** if geopolitical premium persists beyond 72 hours. Retail petrol prices and cylinder LPG rates will likely hold steady in the near term, but logistics operators face immediate hedging cost repricing.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **OPEC+ production statements** scheduled for the coming week and track any statements from Qatar's energy ministry regarding LNG export readiness. Watch for **Brent Crude** breaching **$85/bbl** on physical market reports.
