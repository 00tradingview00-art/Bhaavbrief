**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 8816 INR/bbl at 07:48 pm IST, posting a +2.00% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 95.71 INR (+0.13%), providing consistent export-linked support. The move follows yesterday's profit-taking exhaustion near 9000 INR/bbl at 03:37 pm IST. Precious metals retreated sharply—Gold fell to 151169 INR/10g (−1.01%) and Silver to 236150 INR/kg (−1.56%)—signaling renewed risk appetite in crude relative to safe havens.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The +2.00% rally reflects recovery momentum off the 9000 INR/bbl support level, with rupee weakness easing headwinds for rupee-denominated crude futures. Cross-asset weakness in Gold and Silver suggests capital is rotating back into energy. MCX Crude traders face renewed resistance above the 8840 INR/bbl pivot tested earlier, with conviction required to sustain higher ground.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 8900 INR/bbl round-number resistance level—a break and close above it would signal sustained recovery momentum toward 9000 INR/bbl. Hold of rupee strength (USD/INR above 95.70) remains critical to upside extension.
