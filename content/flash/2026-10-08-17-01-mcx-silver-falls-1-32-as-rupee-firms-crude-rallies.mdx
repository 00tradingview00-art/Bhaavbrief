**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver declined to 220600 INR/kg at 05:01 pm IST, posting a −1.32% loss from the previous close. The pullback extends a session marked by sharp swings—following a −3.05% crash to 227548 INR/kg at 03:27 pm IST. USD/INR edged higher to 97.13 (+0.06%), adding headwinds for rupee-denominated bullion. Meanwhile, MCX Crude surged +4.97% to 8977 INR/bbl, signaling broad-based energy strength.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver remains trapped in a downtrend despite intraday recovery attempts. The rupee's firmness and crude's exceptional rally indicate risk appetite is reasserting, pressuring safe-haven metals. For MCX futures traders, this suggests weakness persists below the 240000 INR/kg psychological level breached earlier at 02:36 pm IST. Gold's modest +0.18% resilience offers limited support.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 220000 INR/kg support zone—now in play at current levels. A close below here signals acceleration toward 215000 INR/kg. Break and hold above 225000 INR/kg would challenge the bearish narrative.
