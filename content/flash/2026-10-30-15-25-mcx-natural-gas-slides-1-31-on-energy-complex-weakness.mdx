**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas fell to 278.1 INR/mmBtu at 15:25 IST, extending a -1.31% decline from the previous close. The pullback reflects broader energy sector softness, with MCX Crude up +1.81% to 9885 INR/bbl offering mixed directional signals. USD/INR weakened -0.24% to 95.28 INR, reducing headwinds for rupee-denominated commodities. The contract remains volatile, oscillating across its recent trading band established between 238.8 and 287.6 INR/mmBtu over the past sessions.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Natural gas traders face consolidation pressure as the contract pulls back from overnight highs near 287.6 INR/mmBtu. Despite the modest rupee strength providing support, the energy complex remains fragmented—crude's unexpected strength suggests diverging demand signals. Price action continues to reflect uncertainty around seasonal demand and supply dynamics. Positioning caution is warranted until a clear directional catalyst emerges.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 270 INR/mmBtu support level and the 285 INR/mmBtu resistance. A sustained break below 270 would signal continuation of downside momentum; a recovery past 285 would test the recent high near 287.6.
