**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper advanced to 1411 INR/kg at 01:37 am IST, gaining +0.57% from the previous close. The move reflects continued modest momentum in the base metals complex despite broader precious metals weakness—Gold at 153069 INR/10g (−0.85%) and Silver at 239455 INR/kg (−0.89%). USD/INR stability at 95.81 (−0.17%) provides support for rupee-denominated commodity prices.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's outperformance versus gold and silver signals divergent risk appetite within the commodity complex. The base metal is consolidating within recent trading ranges (1355.5–1383 INR/kg) as traders balance global demand concerns against supply tightness. Rupee weakness relative to prior flashes limits upside for INR-priced contracts.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1420 INR/kg resistance level will determine if this bounce extends. A break above signals continuation; failure here invites a retest of the 1355.5 support established at 02:17 pm IST.
