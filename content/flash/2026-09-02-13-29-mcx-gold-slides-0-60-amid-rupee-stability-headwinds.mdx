**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold fell to 150824 INR/10g at 01:29 pm IST, extending a -0.60% pullback from the previous close. The decline continues the downtrend established since the 164604 INR/10g peak at 18:59 IST on the prior session. Silver mirrored weakness at 228032 INR/kg (-0.66%), while crude held steady at 8549 INR/bbl (+0.15%). USD/INR remained flat at 95.03 INR, limiting fresh rupee tailwinds for bullion.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's sustained slide below 151000 INR/10g signals a loss of momentum in the bullion complex. With USD/INR offering no fresh depreciation support, rupee-denominated gold traders face reduced hedging value. The parallel 0.66% silver weakness confirms broad precious metals liquidation, not rupee-specific moves. Cross-asset weakness suggests profit-taking after yesterday's 4-figure pullback.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 150000 INR/10g round support level — a critical psychological floor. Break below here would expose the 148000–149000 zone and signal deeper trend reversal momentum.
