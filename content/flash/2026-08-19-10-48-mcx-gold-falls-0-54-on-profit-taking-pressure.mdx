**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold retreated to 153425 INR/10g at 10:48 am IST, declining 0.54% from the previous close. The pullback follows yesterday's breakout above 155000 INR/10g, suggesting profit-taking after the metal's +1.06% recovery. Silver weakness at 228015 INR/kg (−1.89%) and steady USD/INR at 95.73 point to broader safe-haven consolidation across commodities.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's pullback interrupts the recovery momentum that peaked near 155450 INR/10g. For MCX futures traders, this 153425 level tests whether the 155000 breakout holds as a genuine support anchor or merely a cyclical bounce. Cross-asset pressure—silver down 1.89%, copper −0.47%—suggests metal weakness is broad, not gold-specific.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 153000 INR/10g support hold. A break below signals the recovery is exhausted; a rebound to 154500+ would confirm the 155000 threshold remains structurally significant.
