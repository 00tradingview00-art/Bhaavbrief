**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas climbed to 282.6 INR/mmBtu at 10:25 pm IST, gaining 2.65% from the previous close. The rally mirrors broad-based strength across the energy complex: MCX Crude surged +2.33% to 8340 INR/bbl while MCX Gold (+1.96% to 145678 INR/10g) and MCX Silver (+1.53% to 227210 INR/kg) also posted gains. The rupee held steady at 96.56 INR/USD (+0.07%), removing currency headwinds and allowing commodity strength to translate directly into rupee terms.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The synchronized rally across energy, precious metals, and industrial commodities signals risk-on sentiment in MCX markets. For natural gas traders, the 2.65% move at 282.6 suggests renewed demand expectations or supply-side tightness. The broader strength—without rupee depreciation offsetting gains—indicates this is a genuine commodity rally rather than a currency-driven artifact. Positioning likely remains light given the mid-evening timing.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
280 INR/mmBtu (support) and the 285 round level (resistance). A close above 285 would confirm follow-through; a retreat to 280 would test conviction in this morning's energy complex rally.
