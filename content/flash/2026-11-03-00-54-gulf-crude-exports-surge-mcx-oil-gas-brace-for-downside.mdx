**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gulf Arab oil producers have resumed crude exports at pre-war volumes, signalling a sharp reversal in supply constraints that had underpinned elevated global benchmark prices.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks and crude importers face immediate downward repricing pressure on every barrel cleared under long-term contracts, as the Gulf supply overhang erodes the scarcity premium baked into **MCX Crude at ₹9462/bbl**. Refiners holding forward inventory at higher rupee-denominated cost bases now carry mark-to-market losses on working stock, while downstream feedstock costs normalise. Natural gas traders pricing on crude-linkage formulas watch their contract benchmarks soften in parallel.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Indian refiners—both state-owned and private—managing monthly crude import tenders will immediately bid lower absolute prices per barrel, compressing upstream capex recovery and shifting margin calculations on fuel oil and diesel production. Petrochemical processors and fertiliser manufacturers whose feedstock costs track crude and natural gas pricing see input cost deflation, allowing them to either restore dealer margins squeezed over recent months or pass savings selectively to bulk buyers. Retail petrol and diesel pump operators, though insulated by government price controls in many states, face eventual cost-pass-through timing shifts; LPG bottlers and CNG station networks experience softer procurement costs that may eventually feed into household cooking fuel and transport fuel pricing.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Refinery gross margins hinge on the crude-product spread, and a sustained downside move in **MCX Crude** narrows feedstock costs faster than refined product prices adjust, temporarily cushioning refiner profitability. Watch **MCX Crude** breach ₹9200/bbl as a technical confirmation signal for a sustained supply-driven correction. Retail diesel and petrol price revisions will lag by 1–3 weeks, but households refuelling vehicles should anticipate modest relief if the Gulf export surge persists.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Next OPEC+ production bulletin and weekly API crude inventory data will clarify whether the Gulf export surge reflects genuine demand absorption or inventory build; a breach of **₹9000/bbl on MCX Crude** would confirm structural downside.
