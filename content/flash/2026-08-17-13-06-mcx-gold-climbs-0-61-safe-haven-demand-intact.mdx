**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rallied to 155450 INR/10g at 01:06 pm IST, gaining +0.61% from previous close. The advance extends the metal's recovery momentum that pushed it through the 155000 INR/10g threshold earlier. Safe-haven appetite remains broad-based: MCX Silver +0.92% to 238100 INR/kg, MCX Copper +1.04% to 1392.6 INR/kg, while USD/INR at 95.59 provides modest rupee headwind.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's sustained climb above 155000 signals confidence in risk-off positioning. The +0.61% move, coupled with broad-based strength in silver and copper, indicates traders are rotating into defensive metals despite a firmer rupee. MCX Crude's −0.71% weakness and Natural Gas's −3.75% slump reinforce the flight-to-safety narrative for bullion traders holding or considering fresh exposure.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Hold above 155450 INR/10g to confirm breakout legs. A close above 155500 INR/10g would target the 156000 INR/10g resistance; failure to hold 155000 INR/10g would signal weakness back toward 153700 INR/10g support.
