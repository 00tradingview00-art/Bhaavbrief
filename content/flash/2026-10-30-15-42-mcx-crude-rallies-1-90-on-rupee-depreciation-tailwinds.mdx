**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 9893 INR/bbl at 03:42 pm IST, posting a +1.90% gain as USD/INR weakened to 95.27 INR (−0.25% vs prev close). The rupee's depreciation continues to underpin export competitiveness for domestic crude producers, extending the uptrend from overnight levels. Gold slipped −0.74% to 141534 INR/10g, while silver declined −2.72% to 184300 INR/kg. Natural gas fell −1.14% to 278.6 INR/mmBtu.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
MCX Crude has reclaimed ground above 9800 INR/bbl after yesterday's dip to 9525 INR/bbl. The inverse correlation with INR strength is the dominant driver—each rupee depreciation phase restores margins for domestic producers. Cross-asset weakness in precious metals and natural gas suggests selective risk-on positioning in energy, not a broad commodity rally.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9900 INR/bbl round number is now in play. A close above it would signal continuation; a slip below 9850 INR/bbl would test whether the rupee depreciation thesis still holds.
