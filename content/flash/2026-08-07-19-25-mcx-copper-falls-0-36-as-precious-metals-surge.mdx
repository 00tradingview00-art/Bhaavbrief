**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper declined to 1371.1 INR/kg at 07:25 pm IST, falling −0.36% from the previous close. The pullback contrasts sharply with strength elsewhere in the MCX complex: Gold rallied +2.15% to 152065 INR/10g, Silver surged +3.41% to 233548 INR/kg, and Natural Gas gained +0.83% to 255.8 INR/mmBtu. Crude eased −0.42% to 7346 INR/bbl, while USD/INR edged +0.13% higher to 95.2, signalling a modest rupee weakness that typically supports commodity import costs but failed to lift copper.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's isolation amid a broad-based precious metals rally suggests selective profit-taking or risk rotation away from industrial metals into safe-haven assets. The divergence—with gold and silver accelerating sharply—indicates traders are reducing copper exposure despite the rupee headwind, signalling caution on demand signals or macro sentiment around industrial activity relative to inflation hedges.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 1370 INR/kg floor and prior session highs near 1392.2 INR/kg (03:37 pm IST). A hold above 1365 keeps the range intact; a break below signals deeper pullback momentum.
