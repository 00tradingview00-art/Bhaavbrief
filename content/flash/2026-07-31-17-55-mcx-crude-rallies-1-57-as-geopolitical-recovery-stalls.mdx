**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude climbed to 8163 INR/bbl at 05:55 pm IST, posting a +1.57% gain from the previous close. The recovery builds on the +6.67% bounce logged at 10:17 pm IST yesterday from 7545 INR/bbl lows, as supply-side anxiety stabilizes. USD/INR weakened 0.36% to 95.38 INR, while natural gas edged +1.02% to 267.3 INR/mmBtu, supporting energy demand narratives.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude has reclaimed ground toward the 8110 INR/bbl level touched yesterday evening, signaling modest bullish consolidation after a five-day pullback from the 8961 INR/bbl January 24 peak. Rupee softness (USD/INR down) is providing tailwind. Traders should monitor whether this momentum can sustain above 8160 or if geopolitical premium continues to bleed.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Resistance at 8200 INR/bbl and the 8961 INR/bbl January 24 peak. A close above 8200 would test conviction; a drop below 8100 would resume the downtrend toward 7800 support.
