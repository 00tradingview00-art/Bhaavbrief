**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver dropped 2% to ₹2.56 lakh/kg while MCX Gold also declined, triggered by profit booking and a strengthening US dollar.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver December and MCX Gold February contracts face direct selling pressure from this correction. Silver's current MCX level of ₹2.56 lakh/kg sits below the COMEX-derived theoretical price of approximately ₹2,60,924/kg, indicating the domestic contract is already trading at a discount to COMEX parity — a spread that reflects recent rupee movement and import cost adjustments. With USD/INR at ₹96.21, a stronger dollar simultaneously pressures COMEX silver in dollar terms while inflating rupee-denominated import costs, creating a dual squeeze on MCX pricing mechanics. Indian traders tracking the MCX-COMEX spread must account for India's 15% customs duty on silver imports plus GST, which establishes a structural premium floor; any sustained discount to that floor signals aggressive domestic liquidation rather than import-driven price discovery.

**WATCH**
The next US Dollar Index (DXY) print and the upcoming FOMC meeting minutes release serve as the primary confirmation triggers — a DXY hold above 104 sustains pressure on both MCX Silver and Gold, while a reversal below that level removes the dollar headwind. Monitor MCX Silver's ability to hold the ₹2.55 lakh/kg support zone in the next session.
