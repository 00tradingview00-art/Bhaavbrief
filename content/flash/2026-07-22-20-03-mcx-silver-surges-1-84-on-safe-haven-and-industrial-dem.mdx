**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rallied to 227902 INR/kg at 20:03 IST, extending intraday gains with a +1.84% move from the previous close. The advance mirrors broad-based strength across the precious metals complex—MCX Gold climbed +2.02% to 145776 INR/10g—while industrial commodities including MCX Crude (+3.31% to 8420 INR/bbl) and MCX Natural Gas (+2.36% to 281.8 INR/mmBtu) also posted solid gains. The rupee held steady at 96.56 INR versus the dollar, providing a neutral backdrop for INR-priced commodities.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's outperformance versus copper (+0.22%) reinforces a bifurcated commodity narrative: safe-haven demand and industrial-use appeal are both supporting the white metal. For MCX futures traders, this sustained rally above the 227900 level signals conviction in the uptrend and suggests positioning remains constructive ahead of critical macro data or Fed policy signals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 228500 INR/kg resistance zone. A break above that level would confirm the rally's momentum; a slip back below 227500 would test intraday support structure.
