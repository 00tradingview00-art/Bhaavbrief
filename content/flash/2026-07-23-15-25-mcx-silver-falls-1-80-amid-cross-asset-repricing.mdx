**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver slipped to 222923 INR/kg at 15:25 IST, marking a -1.80% decline from the previous close. The pullback reverses earlier intraday strength—silver had rallied to 227902 INR/kg at 20:03 IST yesterday and 223300 INR/kg at 12:35 pm today—as profit-taking emerges. Gold weakness (-0.95% to 144294 INR/10g) and a firmer rupee (USD/INR +0.21% to 96.54) compound downward pressure.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The reversal signals consolidation after a two-leg rally that captured +2.24% at midday. For MCX traders, this marks a critical test of conviction: silver's industrial demand narrative is intact, but the cross-asset backdrop—firm rupee, softer precious metals tone—is now creating headwinds. Position-holding becomes riskier.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support at the 222000 level and the 20-day moving average. A hold above 222500 INR/kg preserves the broader uptrend; a break below signals deeper consolidation toward 220000.
