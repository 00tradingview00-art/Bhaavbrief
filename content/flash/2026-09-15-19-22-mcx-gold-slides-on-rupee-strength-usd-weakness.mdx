**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold fell to 150924 INR/10g at 19:22 IST, posting a -0.20% decline from the previous close. The pullback reverses the recovery momentum that had pushed gold to 155132 INR/10g at 18:17 IST. USD/INR strengthened to 95.94 INR (+0.33%), reducing rupee tailwinds for bullion despite silver (+0.33%) and crude (+1.48%) extending gains across the MCX basket.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's breakdown below the recent 152510 IST recovery high signals profit-taking after the +1.79% rally. The sharp USD/INR rebound to 95.94 has neutralized the rupee-denominated support that fueled yesterday's bounce. Traders face conflicting signals: cross-commodity strength (natgas +1.65%, copper +0.36%) versus gold's isolation, suggesting selective deleveraging in precious metals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 150824 INR/10g level—the intraday low hit at 13:29 IST on the same session—now acts as critical support. A break below will signal deeper profit-taking; a hold confirms consolidation within the 150824–155132 range.
