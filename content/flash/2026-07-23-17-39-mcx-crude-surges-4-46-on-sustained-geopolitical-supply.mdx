**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude accelerated to 8785 INR/bbl at 05:39 pm IST, marking a +4.46% gain from the previous close. The rally extends the day's bullish momentum anchored in geopolitical supply concerns that have driven prices higher since 10:23 pm IST yesterday. Meanwhile, natural gas climbed +0.85% to 285.8 INR/mmBtu, with gold down 1.19% and silver falling 2.07%, signaling selective strength in energy.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, this intraday breakout has now carved a fresh session high, pushing contract valuations into territory last seen during the 05:15% spike at 03:26 pm IST. The cross-asset divergence—crude surging while precious metals retreat—reflects a risk-on energy rally underpinned by supply anxiety rather than broad commodity strength. USD/INR's modest +0.24% move offers limited hedging friction.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8800 INR/bbl round level, now within 15 paise, represents the immediate resistance. A close above that would signal conviction; rejection here could test the 8700–8750 support band established during intraday consolidation.
