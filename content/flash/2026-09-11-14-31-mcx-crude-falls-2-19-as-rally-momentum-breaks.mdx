**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude reversed sharply lower to 9509 INR/bbl at 02:31 pm IST, shedding -2.19% and snapping a multi-leg recovery that had pushed prices to 9722 INR/bbl at 12:10 am IST. The pullback signals exhaustion in the uptrend that began from 7918 INR/bbl on 21:26 IST yesterday. USD/INR strengthened to 95.66 INR (+0.13%), while copper gained +0.51% and natural gas held flat at 270.6 INR/mmBtu, suggesting selective commodity weakness amid rupee headwinds.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 2.19% intraday decline breaks the sequence of higher peaks logged since yesterday's low. For MCX futures traders, this represents profit-taking at resistance after a +6.85% recovery run. The pullback from 9722 tests whether the rally foundation at 9560–9264 INR/bbl (logged at 18:23 IST and 14:33 IST) holds as dynamic support, or if momentum has genuinely shifted.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 9400 INR/bbl level — the next major support below current price. A sustained hold above it keeps the recovery bid alive; a break below signals deeper mean reversion toward 9083 INR/bbl.
