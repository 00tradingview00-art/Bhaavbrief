# Iran Suspends US Talks Over Israel's Lebanon Attacks

**WHAT HAPPENED**
Iran has suspended diplomatic negotiations with the United States, citing Israeli military operations in Lebanon. The suspension marks an escalation in regional tensions, raising concerns about potential broader Middle Eastern instability.

**MCX IMPACT**
**Crude Oil**: Iran is a major OPEC producer. Geopolitical friction typically tightens supply expectations, supporting prices. Traders should monitor potential production disruptions or transit risks through the Strait of Hormuz, which carries ~20% of global oil supply.

**Natural Gas**: Regional energy markets face uncertainty. LNG shipments and pipeline flows from the Middle East could face pricing premiums if tensions intensify.

**Gold**: Safe-haven demand typically surges during geopolitical crises. Investors historically rotate to gold when macro uncertainty peaks, supporting prices independent of fundamentals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor headlines for military escalation or US diplomatic responses—these could trigger sharp intraday moves. Watch if OPEC releases official statements on production. Track crude's technical levels as they often signal risk appetite shifts. Also observe dollar strength, as a weaker rupee could amplify import costs for Indian consumers.
