**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude slipped to 8955 INR/bbl at 11:17 pm IST, posting a -0.28% loss as USD/INR steadied at 95.45 INR (-0.55% vs prev close). The rupee's continued strength has capped upside momentum after the afternoon rally to 8977 INR/bbl logged at 05:01 pm IST. Precious metals and copper also declined—MCX Gold at 148479 INR/10g (-0.71%), MCX Silver at 219629 INR/kg (-1.46%), and MCX Copper at 1417.15 INR/kg (-0.51%)—while Natural Gas outperformed at +3.64%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude traders face a narrow range as rupee stability removes the currency-driven volatility that dominated the afternoon session. The 8955 level reflects equilibrium pricing with limited export-competitiveness tailwind. Risk-off sentiment across precious metals suggests demand caution, though MCX Natural Gas strength (+313.2 INR/mmBtu) signals selective energy demand.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break below 8900 INR/bbl would confirm the consolidation as bearish; hold above 8970 INR/bbl needed to reignite the afternoon rally momentum.
