**TITLE:** Ukraine Energy Strike Risk Lifts MCX Gas, Crude on Supply Threat

**WHAT HAPPENED**
President Zelensky signalled Ukraine's intent to escalate retaliatory strikes against Russian energy infrastructure in response to ongoing Russian attacks on Ukrainian power systems.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Russian energy exports face supply-chain disruption risk, tightening global **natural gas** and **crude oil** availability at a moment when European and Asian importers are already hedging winter demand. Indian refineries and gas procurement desks currently indexing to Urals crude and Russian pipeline flows face the prospect of forced substitution toward costlier non-Russian barrels or spot LNG, repricing their per-unit rupee outlay in real time. **Gold**, historically a geopolitical safe-haven asset, sees fresh bid support as institutional hedgers rotate into bullion amid renewed conflict escalation.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Domestic power utilities and fertiliser manufacturers operating gas-fired plants holding forward purchase agreements see their feedstock cost assumptions destabilised; spot gas procurement will immediately reprice higher if supply tightens. Bitumen producers and petrochemical units consuming crude as feedstock face margin compression as input costs rise faster than their ability to pass through costs to road contractors and plastic film buyers. End consumers purchasing diesel for agricultural operations, heating oil for winter use, and liquefied petroleum gas canisters for household cooking will face retail price adjustments lagged by 2–4 weeks, while gold jewellery retail stores holding rupee-denominated inventory will see working capital gains if bullion sentiment sustains.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian fertiliser producers reliant on piped gas will absorb tighter margins on DAP and urea exports unless domestic gas allocation increases. **MCX Natural Gas** contract will test resistance if Russian supply disruption signals intensify; watch the **₹142070/10g** **Gold** level for breach above ₹145,000 as a confirmation of sustained safe-haven demand. Households refuelling cooking gas cylinders will see pump prices potentially harden within 10–15 trading sessions.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor Zelensky's next operational announcement for scale and timing specificity; watch Brent crude breach of $85–90/bbl as a supply-shock threshold. Track European TTF gas pricing for contagion signals into Asian LNG spot rates.
