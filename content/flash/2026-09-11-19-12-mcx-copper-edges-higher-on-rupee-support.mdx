**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper climbed to 1380.6 INR/kg at 07:12 pm IST, advancing +0.58% from the previous close. The move reflects modest base metals resilience, with precious metals outpacing the base complex—Gold at 153554 INR/10g (+0.80%) and Silver at 236591 INR/kg (+1.06%). USD/INR weakness at 95.63 (+0.10%) provided marginal tailwind, while crude's sharp retreat to 9449 INR/bbl (−2.81%) signalled broader energy volatility.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's steady climb reverses the −1.31% pullback logged at 12:17 am IST, re-establishing support above 1375 INR/kg. The outperformance of precious metals suggests selective risk-on positioning in the commodity basket, though copper's muted +0.58% gain signals trader caution ahead of key macro data. Rupee strength remains the primary near-term tailwind.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
1395.65 INR/kg—the intraday high from 09:36 am IST. A retest and close above this level would confirm momentum and target the 1410.55 INR/kg resistance from the evening rally.
