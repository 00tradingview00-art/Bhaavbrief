**WHAT HAPPENED**
Four oil tankers caught fire after striking mines in the Strait of Hormuz, with Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps confirming the incident.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
**MCX Crude at ₹9686/bbl** now trades against a narrowed Hormuz transit corridor, forcing Indian refinery procurement desks to price in near-term supply friction and potential **WTI-linked premiums**. Simultaneously, **MCX Gold at ₹142864/10g** absorbs safe-haven demand as geopolitical risk premia widen, pushing rupee-denominated bullion inventory valuations upward for dealers holding physical or forwards. Natural gas import economics shift as energy-security concerns elevate LNG spot procurement costs at landed-rupee levels.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Indian oil refiners and import terminals face immediate pressure to either pre-book barrels at elevated levels or hedge against further Hormuz disruption, squeezing working-capital margins on every cargo cleared. Downstream fuel retailers and aviation fueling operators sourcing diesel and jet fuel from refineries will absorb elevated crude-linked input costs within days, narrowing retail pump margins unless fuel price caps adjust. Gold jewellery wholesalers and certified bullion dealers carrying inventory see mark-to-market gains on existing stock but face buyer resistance at higher retail price points, shifting customer acquisition costs. Households planning gold purchases for festive or wedding seasons encounter higher per-gram pricing; diesel-consuming transport operators and agricultural pump users see fuel surcharges accelerate at the point of fill-up.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian refinery margins compress as crude feedstock costs rise on Hormuz supply fears, forcing refiners to either absorb losses or pass costs to fuel retailers within the week. **MCX Crude futures breach ₹9800/bbl** as a critical threshold signaling sustained supply-route anxiety beyond tactical noise. Petrol and diesel pump prices at Indian retail forecourts face upward pressure within 7–10 days unless government intervention freezes excise duties.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Iran or coalition naval responses to the incident and confirmation of tanker damage assessments; any OPEC emergency statement or US strategic reserve release announcement.
