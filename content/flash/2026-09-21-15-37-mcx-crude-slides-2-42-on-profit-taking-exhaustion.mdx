**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude dropped to 9000 INR/bbl at 03:37 pm IST, posting a -2.42% decline as the multi-leg recovery that peaked at 10215 INR/bbl early session lost momentum. Profit-taking accelerated following the exhaustion signal triggered at 10012 INR/bbl at 02:00 pm IST. USD/INR remained flat at 95.86 INR (-0.12%), offering no fresh support, while copper gained +0.81% and natural gas fell -1.22%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The break below 9000 INR/bbl signals weakening upside conviction after a 28% recovery attempt from session lows. For futures traders, this represents a critical inflection point—the pullback structure now risks invalidating the recovery if support fails here. Positioning that extended into the 10200 rally face margin pressure as volatility contracts.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 8900 INR/bbl support level. A close below it would confirm the uptrend exhaustion; a rebound to 9100 INR/bbl would test whether bulls can defend the recovery sequence.
