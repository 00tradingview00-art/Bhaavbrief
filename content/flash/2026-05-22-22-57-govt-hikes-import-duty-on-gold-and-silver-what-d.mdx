**WHAT HAPPENED**
The Indian government has hiked import duty on gold and silver, directly increasing the landed cost of both metals for domestic buyers.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary contracts affected. Higher import duty mechanically widens the premium of MCX prices over COMEX-derived spot rates, as the duty forms a structural component of the import parity price. With COMEX Gold at $4,520/oz translating to approximately ₹1,59,935/10g and COMEX Silver at $76.34/oz translating to approximately ₹2,58,366/kg at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70, any duty hike layered on top of these levels pushes MCX settlement prices higher independent of international movement. Indian bullion importers and jewellers face an immediate increase in procurement costs, which compresses their margins and reduces the arbitrage window between landed import cost and domestic MCX prices — traders holding short positions in either contract face direct cost-push pressure from the duty adjustment.

**WATCH**
Monitor the official gazette notification for the exact revised duty percentage, as the magnitude of the hike determines the new import parity floor for both MCX Gold and MCX Silver contracts. A confirmed duty figure above 15% will reset the cost-of-carry structure across near-month contracts.
