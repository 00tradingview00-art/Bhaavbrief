**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices rebounded on COMEX following the US CPI data release, with gold holding at $4,523/oz and silver at $76.20/oz, as softer inflation data rekindled US Fed rate cut expectations.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) reflects the COMEX rebound at approximately ₹1,60,047/10g, with the USD/INR at ₹95.70 amplifying the landed import cost for Indian bullion traders. A softer US dollar — the direct consequence of Fed rate cut pricing — compresses the USD/INR, which acts as a partial offset to any COMEX-side gains; Indian traders therefore face a narrower rupee-denominated upside compared to dollar-terms movement. MCX Silver tracks at approximately ₹2,57,896/kg, and with silver's higher industrial demand sensitivity, a dovish Fed narrative supports the white metal more aggressively than gold on a percentage basis. Indian importers additionally absorb a 15% basic customs duty plus GST on gold, meaning the effective landed cost sits materially above raw COMEX-to-INR conversion, widening the MCX premium over parity.

**WATCH**
The next US Federal Reserve meeting minutes release and any subsequent Fed speaker commentary on the rate trajectory will either validate or reverse this CPI-driven rebound. A USD/INR breach below ₹95.00 is the key technical level that caps MCX upside independent of COMEX direction.
