**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude reached 8952 INR/bbl at 14:28 IST, posting a +2.19% gain and extending the multi-leg recovery sequence that began at 21:26 IST yesterday from 7918 INR/bbl. The move sustains upside momentum logged at 22:58 IST (8501 INR/bbl, +4.32%) and 14:15 IST (8348 INR/bbl, +2.44%). USD/INR strength at 94.85 INR (+0.30%) provides supporting tailwinds alongside broad commodity recovery; copper +0.82% and natural gas +0.57%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude's breach above 8900 INR/bbl signals sustained conviction in the recovery structure. For MCX futures traders, the sustained three-leg rally from 7918 INR/bbl (yesterday 21:26 IST) now establishes a fresh intraday high, reducing pullback risk. Rupee weakness (94.85) amplifies commodity denominated in INR. Positioning should respect the uptrend until the next inflection point is defined.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9000 INR/bbl round-number resistance is now in play. A decisive breach above this level would confirm trend acceleration; failure to hold would test support at the 8712 INR/bbl high logged at 18:17 IST yesterday.
