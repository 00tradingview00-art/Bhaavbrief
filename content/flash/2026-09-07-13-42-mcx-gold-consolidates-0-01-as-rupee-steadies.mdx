**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold held at 152778 INR/10g at 01:42 pm IST, posting a marginal +0.01% gain. The consolidation follows a recovery bounce from 150824 INR/10g at 13:29 IST earlier. USD/INR remained stable at 94.49 INR (-0.03%), limiting fresh rupee tailwinds. Silver extended weakness at -0.15% to 237301 INR/kg, while crude and copper both advanced +0.40%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold is trapped between the 150824 support and 155132 resistance established earlier. The flat tape suggests traders are awaiting fresh macro cues—either a sharper rupee move or fresh global rate signals—before committing fresh capital. The mild underperformance versus crude and copper hints at sector rotation pressure.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break above 153000 or hold below 152500 will clarify whether consolidation breaks bullish or rolls back toward the 150824 floor. Monitor USD/INR at 94.50 for rupee directional clues.
