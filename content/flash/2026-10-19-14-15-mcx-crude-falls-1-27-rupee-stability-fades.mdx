**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude dropped to 9275 INR/bbl at 02:15 pm IST, posting a -1.27% loss after a three-hour rally that had lifted prices to 9567 INR/bbl at 11:02 pm IST. USD/INR steadied at 95.09 INR (+0.02% vs prev close), eroding the rupee depreciation that had supported export margins. Gold and silver remain bid at 148194 and 211272 INR respectively, signalling selective risk-off in energy.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The pullback reverses morning momentum and signals profit-taking after a +3.04% surge. For MCX crude traders, the rupee's inability to sustain weakness below 95.07 has removed the tailwind that supported domestic export competitiveness. The cross-asset picture—gold +0.19%, natgas -0.64%—hints at fragmented positioning rather than broad commodity rally legs.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 9250 INR support level. A hold above it preserves the 11:22 pm IST close of 9282 INR as a fresh trading floor; breach opens 9200 as the next test.
