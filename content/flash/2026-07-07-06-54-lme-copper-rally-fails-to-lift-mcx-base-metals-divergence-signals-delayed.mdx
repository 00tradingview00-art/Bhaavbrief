**WHAT HAPPENED**

MCX Copper declined **₹1281/kg**, down **0.50%** in this morning session, tracking a broader LME base metals rally driven by aluminum supply concerns. Silver led the commodity selloff with a **−1.35%** loss, while crude rallied **+1.24%**, creating a divergent cross-asset backdrop. Gold slipped **−0.78%** as USD/INR held steady at **₹95.32**, offering no currency tailwind to precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

LME copper strength (reflected in a record-high base metals index) typically transmits to MCX through import-parity pricing: global copper price in USD × USD/INR rate × import duty = MCX settlement level. Today's paradox—LME upside coinciding with MCX copper weakness—signals that INR strength or localized liquidation is overriding the global bullish signal. This divergence implies Indian copper buyers are not yet hedging at higher global levels, suggesting either forward cover already in place or delayed price discovery into domestic markets.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**

Copper rod manufacturers and electrical equipment producers who hedge quarterly LME exposures face a timing mismatch: while global prices support their export competitiveness, MCX contract holders are realizing losses today. A cable producer with long MCX positions accumulated at higher levels this month is experiencing mark-to-market pressure despite fundamentally supportive global aluminum/copper supply dynamics. Domestic scrap-based smelters purchasing copper spot inventory will defer until MCX stabilizes above **₹1285/kg**.

**BOTTOM LINE**

MCX copper's **−0.50%** descent against LME strength reveals a structural disconnect: the **₹95.32** INR/USD rate is insufficient to offset either profit-taking or delayed global price transmission into Indian futures pricing.

**WHAT TO WATCH**

LME copper's close tonight and MCX copper's 3 PM settlement—a move above **₹1285/kg** would confirm LME strength is reaching Indian hedgers. USD/INR breakout below **₹95.20** would clarify whether today is INR revaluation or purely local selling.
