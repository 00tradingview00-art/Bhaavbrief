**WHAT HAPPENED**
The US Federal Reserve's rate decision on 26 October 2026 will determine whether the central bank holds or cuts rates; a hold or smaller-than-expected cut would strengthen the **US Dollar** and pressure **MCX Gold, MCX Silver,** and **MCX Crude**, while a larger cut would ease dollar headwinds and support bullion prices.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A stronger dollar raises the rupee-denominated import cost of bullion for jewellers, refiners, and dealers across Zaveri Bazaar and regional diamond bourses, directly repricing **MCX Gold and MCX Silver** contracts upward in rupee terms despite potential USD-denominated weakness. Conversely, falling US real yields—the transmission channel if the Fed cuts—reduce the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding gold for RBI reserves and overseas ETF funds, supporting **MCX Gold**. Higher dollar strength also compresses margins for **MCX Crude** traders because Indian refiners' dollar-denominated import bills rise relative to domestic rupee pricing, while **MCX Copper** faces demand headwinds if a hawkish Fed signals slower global growth and reduces Chinese construction activity.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Bullion importers and refinery units sourcing dollar-settled metal see their hedging costs and procurement budgets reprice within hours of the Fed announcement, squeezing working capital on fixed-price contracts already signed with jewellery manufacturers and retail chains. Downstream jewellery retailers and D2C wedding-season brands carrying finished-goods inventory at pre-decision price points face immediate margin compression if rupee gold prices spike post-announcement. Household gold buyers deferring festival purchases until early November now face a pricing fork: accelerated purchases ahead of the decision or delayed entry if a cut triggers a reversal.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Gold refiners' dollar-hedged procurement costs will reset immediately post-Fed, compressing margins on existing forward contracts with wholesalers. **MCX Gold** will track USD strength with particular sensitivity if the Fed signals a pause in rate cuts, while **MCX Crude** will weaken if oil demand expectations dim. Retail jewellery prices in Tier 2 cities will follow **MCX Gold** repricing within 2–3 trading sessions.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The Fed's forward guidance on 2027 rate cuts and the ECB's parallel stance signal on 31 October will extend dollar momentum. Watch for China PMI and manufacturing data in early November to confirm whether weaker growth expectations offset dollar strength on **MCX Copper** demand.
