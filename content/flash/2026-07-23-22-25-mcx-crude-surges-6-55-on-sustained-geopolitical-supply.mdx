**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude accelerated to 8961 INR/bbl at 10:25 pm IST, posting a +6.55% gain from the previous close. The rally extends the multi-session bullish momentum anchored in geopolitical supply concerns that have driven the complex higher since 10:23 pm IST yesterday. Meanwhile, precious metals retreated sharply—MCX Gold fell 1.92% to 142888 INR/10g and MCX Silver dropped 3.20% to 219745 INR/kg—signalling a risk-on rotation into energy.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 6.55% move marks a decisive acceleration through intraday resistance, suggesting conviction behind the geopolitical narrative. For MCX crude traders, the divergence against falling gold and silver indicates sector-specific bullish positioning rather than broad commodity strength. With USDINR steady at 96.56, crude's rupee gains reflect pure energy momentum, not currency tailwinds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 9000 INR/bbl psychological level now breached. A sustained close above 8961 will test the next resistance near 9050 INR/bbl; a pullback below 8900 would signal consolidation.
