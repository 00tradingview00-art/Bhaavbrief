**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR climbed to 96 INR at 02:06 pm IST, gaining +0.38% from the previous close. The rupee's continued appreciation reflects sustained foreign portfolio inflows into domestic equities and bonds. MCX precious metals declined sharply: gold fell to 150437 INR/10g (-0.52%), silver to 230444 INR/kg (-0.97%), while crude oil surged to 9927 INR/bbl (+2.16%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Rupee strength is pressuring gold and silver futures as the weaker dollar reduces import costs and dampens hedging demand. Simultaneously, crude's 2.16% jump signals geopolitical or supply-side tension offsetting currency headwinds. MCX traders face cross-asset divergence: precious metals weakness versus energy strength reflects capital flow rotation away from safe-haven metals into oil.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
96 INR level holds as the immediate resistance. A breach toward 96.50 would confirm sustained inflow momentum; a pullback below 95.80 would signal rupee consolidation and potential mean reversion in precious metals.
