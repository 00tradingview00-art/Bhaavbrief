**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on MCX dropped below ₹15,000 per gram while silver traded around ₹2.33 lakh per kg, as domestic prices tracked a pullback in international spot prices.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are directly affected by this correction. The MCX Gold reference rate derived from live COMEX at $4,523/oz and USD/INR at ₹95.70 computes to approximately ₹16,004/gram, suggesting the sub-₹15,000 print reflects either an earlier session low or a basis differential driven by domestic demand softness and dealer discounts. MCX Silver at ₹2.33 lakh/kg trades at a notable discount to the COMEX-derived import parity of approximately ₹2,57,896/kg — a spread of roughly ₹24,896/kg that signals weak domestic physical demand or aggressive short positioning. For Indian traders, this spread compression versus COMEX narrows the typical arbitrage window and raises the question of whether import costs, inclusive of 15% customs duty and GST, are suppressing price discovery on the domestic exchange.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the US Federal Reserve's upcoming interest rate commentary and the weekly COMEX Commitment of Traders (COT) report — a sustained COMEX Gold hold above $4,500/oz will test whether MCX prices reclaim the ₹15,000/gram level.
