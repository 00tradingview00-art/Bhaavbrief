**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver surged to 240090 INR/kg at 15:08 IST, posting a +1.88% gain and piercing the 240000 round-number threshold. The advance extends a multi-session recovery that began at 21:23 IST yesterday, lifting silver from 222836 INR/kg. Broader commodity strength underpins the move: MCX Gold advanced +0.80% to 154991 INR/10g, MCX Copper climbed +0.41% to 1384.8 INR/kg, and MCX Natural Gas rose +0.60% to 267 INR/mmBtu. USD/INR remained stable at 95.32, removing currency headwinds.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Round-number breaks often signal genuine momentum shifts rather than noise. Silver's crossing of 240000 now establishes a fresh psychological support level and suggests bullish sentiment persists across the precious metals complex. The synchronised strength in gold, copper, and energy signals risk-on positioning—traders are rotating into commodities as a hedge. For MCX silver futures positions, this level validates the recovery narrative.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor whether silver consolidates above 240000 INR/kg or retreats toward 235659 (the previous session close). A sustained hold above the round number signals continuation toward 245000; a break below 240000 would retest the recovery origin at 232300 INR/kg marked at 13:19 IST.
