**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on MCX hit a fresh record high alongside continued gold strength, with COMEX silver currently at $76.20/oz translating to approximately ₹257,896/kg on MCX at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹95.70.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver continuous contract and MCX Gold contract are both directly affected by this move. Silver's rupee price is amplified by the USD/INR rate at ₹95.70 — every 50 paise rupee depreciation adds roughly ₹1,300/kg to the MCX silver price independent of COMEX movement. At ₹257,896/kg, Indian traders importing silver face elevated landed costs inclusive of the 15% basic customs duty plus GST, widening the domestic premium over COMEX-derived fair value. For gold, the MCX benchmark near ₹160,047/10g reflects both COMEX's $4,523/oz strength and the weak rupee, making the import cost equation critical for jewellery trade hedgers monitoring the COMEX-MCX spread.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. JOLTS job data release and Fed speakers this week are the primary catalysts that will either extend dollar weakness — sustaining rupee-denominated record highs — or trigger a COMEX pullback that tests MCX silver support near ₹245,000/kg. A USD/INR move beyond ₹96.00 independently lifts both contracts regardless of COMEX direction.
