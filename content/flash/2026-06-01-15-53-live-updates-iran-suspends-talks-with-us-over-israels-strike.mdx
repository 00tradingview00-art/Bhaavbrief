# Iran Suspends US Talks Following Israeli Strikes on Lebanon

**WHAT HAPPENED**

Iran has suspended diplomatic negotiations with the US following Israel's military strikes on Lebanese targets, according to state media reports. The escalation signals renewed regional tensions in the Middle East, raising concerns about broader geopolitical instability.

**MCX IMPACT**

Crude Oil typically rallies on Middle East supply disruption fears—Iran is a significant regional producer. Natural Gas may strengthen if regional infrastructure faces threats. Gold, a safe-haven asset, gains appeal when geopolitical uncertainty increases and investors seek portfolio protection from volatility.

**WHAT TO WATCH**

Monitor crude oil futures for breakout moves above key resistance levels as traders price in supply risk premiums. Track gold's correlation with global risk sentiment—safe-haven inflows often accelerate during extended diplomatic standoffs. Watch for any announcements regarding shipping lane security in the Strait of Hormuz, as this directly impacts global crude supply flows and sentiment toward energy commodities.
