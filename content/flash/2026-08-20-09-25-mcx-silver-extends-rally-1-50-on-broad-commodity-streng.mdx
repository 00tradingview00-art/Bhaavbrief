**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver surged to 240346 INR/kg at 09:25 IST, posting a +1.50% gain from the previous close. The advance extends recovery momentum that lifted silver to 235358 INR/kg at 19:35 IST yesterday. Broad-based commodity strength underpins the move: MCX Gold rallied +0.47% to 158740 INR/10g, while MCX Copper gained +0.35% to 1376.9 INR/kg. USD/INR weakness at 95.64 (−0.18%) provides additional support for rupee-denominated precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver has reclaimed the 240K threshold after intraday weakness to 229594 INR/kg on 09:33 IST yesterday, signaling renewed bullish momentum in the precious metals complex. For MCX futures traders, the breach of the 235–240K consolidation band reduces near-term downside risk and shifts positioning toward higher resistance levels. Cross-asset tailwinds from softer rupee dynamics add conviction to the move.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break and hold above 240346 INR/kg will confirm momentum. Watch the 242000–243000 resistance band and whether gold's +0.47% strength continues to anchor silver's upside trajectory through the morning session.
