**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rose to 141791 INR/10g at 11:35 am IST, gaining +0.12% from the previous close. The modest uptick reflects a continuation of broad-based commodity recovery: Silver climbed +0.90% to 217782 INR/kg, Crude surged +3.70% to 7884 INR/bbl, and Natural Gas declined -0.46% to 259.8 INR/mmBtu. The rupee remained relatively stable at 95.72 INR/USD (-0.06%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's understated gain masks resilience within a multi-commodity rally anchored by crude strength. The 141791 level sits well below yesterday's 145706 high at 20:20 IST, signaling consolidation after a sharp 1.55% pullback to 143424 at 18:24 IST. Cross-asset momentum remains intact, but gold's lag versus silver and crude suggests selective positioning into energy and industrial metals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 142883 INR/10g prior close and the 143424 intraday low at 18:24 IST define the immediate support zone. A break above 144000 would confirm recovery traction toward yesterday's 145706 peak.
