MCX and NCDEX will remain closed on January 1, 2026, as both exchanges observe New Year's Day as a scheduled trading holiday, resulting in zero trading activity across all commodity segments for that session.

With both exchanges shut, traders holding open positions in metals, energy, and agri-commodities on MCX — including gold, silver, crude oil, and natural gas — will be unable to execute fresh trades or exit positions on that date. Participants with expiring contracts or margin obligations must plan settlements around this closure. NCDEX-listed agri contracts, including chana and guar, will similarly see no price discovery on January 1. Trading resumes the following session on January 2, 2026.
