**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude reached 8648 INR/bbl at 02:12 pm IST, posting a +0.06% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 95.74 INR (+0.16%), providing consistent rupee headwind relief. The move arrives as precious metals retreated—Gold fell to 152085 INR/10g (−0.41%) and Silver to 238059 INR/kg (−0.76%)—while Natural Gas surged +2.02% to 288.2 INR/mmBtu, signaling selective energy demand.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, this modest upside reflects rupee depreciation acting as a natural floor rather than fresh fundamental strength. The price sits well below the 9815–10215 INR/bbl recovery range seen at 01:58 am IST and 02:06 pm IST yesterday, suggesting consolidation mode. Cross-asset weakness in gold-silver indicates risk-off sentiment, which typically caps crude rallies.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 8600 INR/bbl support level and whether crude can hold above it through the European close. A break below signals return to profit-taking momentum seen at 03:37 pm IST yesterday (9000 INR/bbl, −2.42%).
