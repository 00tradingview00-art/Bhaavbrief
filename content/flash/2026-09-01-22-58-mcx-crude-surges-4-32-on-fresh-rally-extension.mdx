**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude jumped to 8501 INR/bbl at 22:58 IST, posting a +4.32% gain and extending the recovery sequence that began at 21:26 IST from 7918 INR/bbl. The move builds on earlier intraday strength logged at 14:15 IST (8348 INR/bbl, +2.44%) and signals sustained upside momentum. USD/INR weakness at 95.07 INR (−0.18%) continues to support crude valuations in rupee terms, while precious metals retreat: MCX_GOLD at 151718 INR/10g (−1.78%) and MCX_SILVER at 229540 INR/kg (−1.84%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The +4.32% rally confirms crude has broken decisively above the 8275 INR/bbl intraday resistance recaptured at 14:23 IST on 20 January. For MCX futures traders, this signals a shift from rangebound consolidation into directional upside, with rupee depreciation acting as a tailwind. Cross-asset divergence—crude strength against gold/silver weakness—indicates selective risk-on positioning in energy.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8501 INR/bbl level now stands as immediate support. A close above 8550 INR/bbl would confirm the breakout; a slip below 8400 INR/bbl would test conviction in this rally extension.
