**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver declined to 236404 INR/kg at 01:58 pm IST, posting a −1.22% loss from the previous close. The pullback reflects broad precious metals weakness, with MCX Gold sliding −0.76% to 151924 INR/10g and USD/INR edging higher to 95.85 (+0.02%), pressuring rupee-denominated bullion. MCX Crude's +0.76% gain to 8903 INR/bbl adds competing energy demand narratives, while MCX Copper remains flat at 1412.05 INR/kg.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's dip extends the intraday volatility range established after the 240000 INR/kg psychological breach at 13:34 IST. Traders face renewed pullback risk as rupee strength and gold weakness compress the bullion complex. The move sits within the 229803–242950 INR/kg range logged since the prior session, signaling consolidation rather than trend reversal.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 240000 INR/kg support level for hold. A break below 235000 INR/kg risks retest of the 229803 INR/kg session trough; a recovery above 240000 INR/kg would confirm the rally structure.
