**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas held at 278.3 INR/mmBtu at 14:06 IST, posting a marginal -0.04% decline from the previous close. The consolidation follows a sharp overnight recovery from 274.5 INR/mmBtu (22:29 IST) that had extended a -2.38% loss. Crude Oil's +2.16% surge to 9927 INR/bbl has provided limited support, while USD/INR strengthened 0.38% to 96 INR, adding headwind to denominated commodity pricing.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The near-flat midday print suggests gas has found temporary equilibrium after violent swings between 274.5 and 284.2 INR/mmBtu overnight. Traders are caught between crude's rally strength and rupee weakness, both typically supportive—yet gas remains pinned. This narrow range signals caution; conviction either way remains absent despite cross-asset moves.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break above 283 INR/mmBtu (the 14:34 IST high) or below 274.5 INR/mmBtu (the 22:29 IST low) will confirm directional intent. Current consolidation at 278.3 is a pivot.
