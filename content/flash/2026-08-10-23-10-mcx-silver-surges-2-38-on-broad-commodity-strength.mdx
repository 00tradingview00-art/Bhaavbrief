**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rallied to 236969 INR/kg at 23:10 IST, posting a +2.38% gain from the previous close. The advance extends recovery momentum that began at 21:23 IST yesterday. Broader commodity strength underpins the move: MCX Crude surged +5.11% to 7803 INR/bbl, MCX Natural Gas climbed +4.30% to 266.6 INR/mmBtu, and MCX Gold advanced +0.88% to 153152 INR/10g. USD/INR remained flat at 95.29, offering no headwind to rupee-denominated metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's sustained recovery reflects broad-based commodity risk appetite rather than metal-specific drivers. The 236969 level represents a fresh intraday high and signals that profit-taking has reversed. For MCX futures traders, this marks continuation of the uptrend that began yesterday evening, with energy complex strength (crude, natgas) acting as a spillover catalyst for precious metals positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 237500 round-number resistance level will determine if this rally holds. A break above signals momentum continuation; failure to sustain above 236000 may invite fresh profit-taking into the close.
