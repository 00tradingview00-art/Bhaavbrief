**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver simultaneously hit fresh all-time highs on MCX, with gold at approximately ₹1,60,047/10g and silver at ₹2,57,896/kg, driven by renewed global trade-war concerns pushing safe-haven demand.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (nearest active contract) and MCX Silver are the directly affected contracts, both printing record levels in sync with COMEX — Gold at $4,523/oz and Silver at $76.20/oz on the international benchmark. The USD/INR rate at ₹95.70 is amplifying the rupee-denominated price: every 50-paisa INR depreciation adds roughly ₹750-800/10g to MCX Gold without any change in COMEX price. India's 15% basic customs duty plus GST on gold imports means the landed cost already embeds a structural premium over COMEX conversion, so the current MCX level at ₹1,60,047/10g reflects that full duty-paid import parity. For Indian traders, the spread between COMEX-converted price and MCX spot confirms no meaningful arbitrage dislocation — the rally is orderly and duty-inclusive, not a spread anomaly.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the upcoming US trade-policy statement or any formal tariff announcement from Washington — a de-escalation confirmation sends both metals back below ₹1,55,000/10g on Gold and ₹2,45,000/kg on Silver. Monitor USD/INR at the ₹96.00 resistance level, a breach accelerates MCX upside independent of COMEX movement.
