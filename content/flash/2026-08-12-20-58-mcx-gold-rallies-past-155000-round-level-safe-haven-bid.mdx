**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold crossed the psychologically significant 155000 INR/10g threshold at 20:58 IST, trading at 155400 INR/10g with a +1.06% gain from previous close. The breakout extends the metal's recovery momentum that began earlier at 21:52 IST when the contract touched 151935 INR/10g (+2.07%). Silver tracked the advance at 238694 INR/kg (+1.29%), while USD/INR held steady at 95.32, maintaining rupee stability that supports domestic gold valuations.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The round-number cross at 155000 signals sustained safe-haven accumulation across the commodity complex. For MCX futures traders, this level now functions as a near-term support anchor; breach below it risks unwinding the +1.06% session gain. The broader complex—silver +1.29%, natgas +1.09%—confirms broad-based risk-off positioning, suggesting institutional demand for defensive hedges remains intact.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 154000–154500 INR/10g support band for hold/break dynamics. If gold sustains above 155000 through the next session open, 156000 becomes the next structural resistance test.
