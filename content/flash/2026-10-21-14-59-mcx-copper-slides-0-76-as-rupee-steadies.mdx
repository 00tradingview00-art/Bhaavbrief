**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper retreated to 1388 INR/kg at 02:59 pm IST, declining -0.76% from the previous close. The pullback follows a modest intraday peak at 1411 INR/kg (01:37 am IST) and reflects profit-taking in the base metals complex. USD/INR stabilized at 94.68 (−0.39%), while precious metals extended weakness—Gold at 145936 INR/10g (−0.40%) and Silver at 209000 INR/kg (−1.18%). Natural gas weakness at 276.9 INR/mmBtu (−2.22%) signals broader commodity sector consolidation.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's reversion from morning highs suggests limited conviction among traders. The rupee's relative stability at 94.68 removes the tailwind that supported earlier gains. For MCX futures traders, this signals a range-bound session with base metals struggling to sustain upside momentum against global risk-off sentiment and the absence of fresh bullish catalysts in the macro backdrop.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 1380 INR/kg support level—a breach would signal deeper weakness. Conversely, a recapture of 1400+ would confirm the morning momentum remains intact despite afternoon profit-taking.
