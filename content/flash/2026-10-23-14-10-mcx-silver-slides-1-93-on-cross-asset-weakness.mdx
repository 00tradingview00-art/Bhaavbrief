**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver fell to 195750 INR/kg at 02:10 pm IST, declining 1.93% from the previous close. The sharp pullback extends losses across the precious metals complex—MCX Gold weakened 0.56% to 142500 INR/10g, while MCX Copper added 0.53% to 1369.9 INR/kg. USD/INR edged higher to 94.99 (+0.14%), reinforcing headwinds for rupee-denominated bullion as global risk sentiment softens and energy markets remain mixed.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's break below recent support signals weakness in the precious metals risk narrative. The 1.93% decline erases earlier recovery attempts and suggests traders are rotating away from haven assets amid a stronger dollar backdrop. For MCX futures participants, this positioning reflects broader uncertainty—Gold's relative underperformance (-0.56%) and Crude's modest resilience (+0.70%) indicate fragmented market conviction.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 193000 INR/kg support level. A hold above it keeps the downside contained; breach would target 190000 INR/kg and signal deeper structural weakness in the complex.
