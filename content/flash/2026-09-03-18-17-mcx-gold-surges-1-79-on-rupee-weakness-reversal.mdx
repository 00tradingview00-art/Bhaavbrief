**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rallied to 155132 INR/10g at 18:17 IST, posting a +1.79% gain and reversing the two-session downtrend that had pushed prices to 150824 INR/10g on 13:29 IST. USD/INR weakened -0.43% to 94.63 INR, supporting rupee-denominated bullion. Silver tracked upside at +1.47% to 233333 INR/kg, while crude (+1.29%) and natural gas (+1.51%) extended broad commodity strength across the MCX complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee depreciation tailwind has reversed the prior correction phase, lifting gold back toward the 155000 zone after a 3800 INR drawdown from the 164604 peak at 18:59 IST yesterday. For MCX futures traders, this move resets momentum after capitulation lows, though the instrument remains 9472 INR below that earlier high—leaving structural resistance overhead intact.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 156000 INR level—the next technical hurdle. A hold above 155000 INR/10g with USD/INR remaining soft (below 94.70) will signal conviction in this recovery phase.
