**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX experienced a technical glitch on its trading platform that delayed the opening of gold and silver contracts by over four hours, prompting the exchange to initiate a formal investigation.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract, reference ~₹1,60,971/10g) and MCX Silver (reference ~₹2,60,431/kg) are the directly affected contracts. The four-hour halt created a price discovery vacuum during active COMEX hours, meaning Indian traders were unable to hedge or adjust positions while COMEX gold at $4,525/oz and silver at $76.54/oz continued trading internationally. When MCX reopened, contracts faced compressed time to realign with COMEX-derived fair value — calculated after applying the live USD/INR rate of ₹96.21, customs duty (15% on gold), and SEBI-mandated adjustments — resulting in wider bid-ask spreads and elevated basis risk. Traders holding overnight positions or physical import hedges faced unquantifiable mark-to-market exposure during the outage window.

**WATCH**
MCX's official investigation report and any SEBI directive on exchange business continuity protocols will determine whether circuit-level safeguards are mandated; monitor MCX's system status bulletin at the next session open for confirmation of full platform stability.
