**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rallied to 232300 INR/kg at 01:19 pm IST, posting a +2.86% gain from the previous close. The advance extends the recovery momentum that began at 21:23 IST yesterday when silver climbed to 222836 INR/kg (+1.58%). Broader commodity strength supports the move: MCX Gold advanced +0.90% to 150200 INR/10g, while MCX Copper gained +0.53% to 1383.25 INR/kg. USD/INR weakness at 95.27 (+0.20%) provided tailwind for rupee-denominated precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver has now reclaimed the 230+ level after a sharp intraday pullback to 217339 INR/kg at 12:39 pm IST yesterday. The breakout above 226300 INR/kg signals renewed safe-haven interest amid broader commodity stabilization. For MCX futures traders, this move tests conviction on the recovery narrative—positioning will hinge on whether the +2.86% rally holds above the 230 psychological anchor.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Track the 235000 INR/kg resistance level. A close above this would confirm fresh momentum; failure to sustain gains at 232300 INR/kg risks mean reversion toward 225000 INR/kg support.
