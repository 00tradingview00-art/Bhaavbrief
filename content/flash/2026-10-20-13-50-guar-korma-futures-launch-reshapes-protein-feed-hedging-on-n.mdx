**WHAT HAPPENED**
**NCDEX** introduced **Guar Korma futures contracts** to establish standardized price discovery across India's protein meal value chain, addressing transparency gaps in the traditionally fragmented spot market for guar processing byproducts.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Guar korma — the protein-rich residue left after guar gum extraction — has traded without organized futures benchmarks, forcing **livestock feed millers** and **cattle nutrition companies** to negotiate prices directly with **guar processors in Rajasthan and Gujarat** based on opaque spot rates. The new futures contract now creates a transparent reference price, allowing feed manufacturers in **Madhya Pradesh dairy belts** and **Punjab poultry zones** to hedge forward procurement costs and lock margins against volatile raw material sourcing. **Guar seed traders and crushing units** gain immediate price signals for their byproduct output, reducing working capital risk on unsold inventory.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Guar processing mills across **Jodhpur and Bikaner** — which produce korma as a co-product alongside guar gum exports — now face daily mark-to-market repricing of their inventory against futures settlement, shifting their forward sales strategy from bilateral contracts toward exchange-based hedges. Livestock feed compounders supplying **organized dairy cooperatives and poultry integrators** can now quote fixed protein meal costs to customers, stabilizing their procurement P&L rather than absorbing korma price volatility quarter-on-quarter. Retail dairy farmers purchasing fortified cattle feed through cooperative channels will see more stable input costs reflected in milk procurement pricing, indirectly affecting household milk and dairy product prices in consuming metros.

**BOTTOM LINE**
**Guar korma processors in Rajasthan** can now hedge byproduct sales directly on **NCDEX korma futures** rather than holding unpriced inventory at month-end. Rising open interest in **NCDEX guar korma contracts** signals feed millers are actively pricing protein meal hedges, confirming structural demand for transparent benchmarks. **Dairy farmers purchasing compound feed** will face less quarterly volatility in their feed input costs as formulators lock korma prices through futures positions.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **NCDEX guar seed and korma contract open interest convergence** over the next 60 days to confirm participation by crushing mills; parallel watch **spot korma rates in Bikaner mandis** to measure basis compression versus futures settlement.
