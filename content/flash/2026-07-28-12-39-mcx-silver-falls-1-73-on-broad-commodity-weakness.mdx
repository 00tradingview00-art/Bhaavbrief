**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver dropped to 217339 INR/kg at 12:39 pm IST, posting a -1.73% decline from the previous close. The pullback extends profit-taking momentum and tracks weakness across the broader commodity complex: MCX Crude fell -2.27% to 7776 INR/bbl, MCX Copper slid -0.61% to 1316.35 INR/kg, and MCX Natural Gas declined -1.29% to 261.1 INR/mmBtu. Rupee strength (USD/INR at 95.78, -0.82%) adds downward pressure on rupee-denominated precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver has now retested support near 217000–218000 INR/kg for the third time in 36 hours, following yesterday's -1.80% pullback to 222923 INR/kg at 15:25 IST and this morning's -0.49% slip to 218306 INR/kg at 11:36 IST. The symmetry suggests profit-taking off intraday rallies remains the dominant theme. Risk-off sentiment across energy and base metals indicates macro headwinds are overriding safe-haven demand.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Hold of 217000 INR/kg or breach below 216500 will signal whether this is tactical profit-taking or a deeper trend reversal. A retest of yesterday's 222923 INR/kg high would confirm renewed strength.
