**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver declined to 241543 INR/kg at 13:25 IST, posting a −0.33% loss from the previous close. The pullback follows a volatile intraday session marked by a +0.96% rally to 245585 INR/kg at 10:20 IST and a −1.73% trough to 229803 INR/kg at 19:11 IST on the prior session. Gold weakness (−0.14% to 155553 INR/10g) and broad commodity softness—crude sliding −0.83% to 8571 INR/bbl, natgas −0.40% to 276.7 INR/mmBtu—weigh on sentiment. The rupee remains relatively stable at 94.52 INR (−0.10%), limiting currency-driven upside.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver traders face range-bound consolidation after yesterday's sharp reversals. The 241543 level sits midway between the 245585 intraday peak and 236150 support tested at 16:31 IST. Cross-asset weakness in crude and natgas signals softer risk appetite, constraining precious metals momentum despite rupee stability. Positioning remains fragile after the −1.73% hammering.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 240000–236150 support zone. A close below 236150 INR/kg would confirm continuation toward 229803 INR/kg lows; a bounce above 243000 INR/kg would test morning peak validity.
