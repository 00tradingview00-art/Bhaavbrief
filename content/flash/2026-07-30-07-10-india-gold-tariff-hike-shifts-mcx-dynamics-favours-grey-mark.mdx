**WHAT HAPPENED**
India raised gold tariffs, triggering a documented shift of bullion demand toward unorganised grey market channels at the expense of regulated jewellers and organised dealers, according to the World Gold Council.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Higher import duties on **gold** create an immediate arbitrage window: grey market operators can source offshore at lower all-in costs and undercut domestic MCX-linked spot premiums, directly eroding margin capture for bullion dealers and refiners who price off **MCX Gold futures**. Concurrently, jewellers importing through formal channels face elevated landed costs per gram on **dollar-denominated international gold**, compressing their rupee-equivalent working capital allocation and forcing either inventory write-downs or retail price pass-throughs that demand elasticity may not support.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Bullion dealers and refinery procurement teams holding long inventory positions on the spot market now compete against cheaper grey-sourced material, forcing them to either liquidate positions at discount or absorb carrying costs on slower turnover. Mid-chain jewellery manufacturers and organised retail jewellers sourcing certified hallmarked bars face a two-tier procurement choice — formal import at tariff-inflated prices or informal supply at regulatory risk — effectively squeezing their wholesale-to-retail markup. Retail jewellery buyers and households purchasing gold ahead of weddings and festivals confront bifurcated pricing: formal channel prices remain elevated, while grey market alternatives offer discounts, fragmenting price discovery and shifting consumer behaviour toward informal channels.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Organised bullion dealers and MCX-linked refiners face immediate margin compression as grey market competition undercuts their spot bid-offer spreads on physical delivery. **MCX Gold futures** will likely trade at elevated premiums to international spot as formal traders price in tariff costs, signalling a breach above historical **₹75,000–76,000 per 10g threshold** as a capitulation trigger. Retail jewellery end-consumers purchasing at organised retail outlets will absorb 3–5% price premiums versus grey market alternatives, tilting short-term demand toward informal supply.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **MCX Gold open interest and volume migration** over the next two weeks to confirm whether long liquidation accelerates. Track any formal statement from the All India Gems & Jewellery Trade Federation on retail pricing adjustments as the leading indicator of demand destruction in organised channels.
