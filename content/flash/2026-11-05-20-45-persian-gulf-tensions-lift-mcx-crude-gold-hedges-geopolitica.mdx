**WHAT HAPPENED**
Multiple commercial vessels were attacked in the Persian Gulf, prompting a rebound in oil prices after recent post-election weakness.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks and oil import terminal operators face renewed upside pressure on dollar-denominated crude acquisition costs, with **MCX Crude at ₹8,570/bbl** now anchored to geopolitical supply-risk premiums rather than demand-driven fundamentals alone. This supply-side shock — distinct from consumption signals — forces inventory replenishment decisions to factor in corridor risk, expanding the working capital requirement per barrel for refiners managing pipeline logistics through chokepoints. Simultaneously, safe-haven demand is redirecting speculative and hedging flows into **MCX Gold at ₹143,970/10g**, compressing risk premia across energy and metal complexes as traders calibrate exposure to sustained Persian Gulf friction.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Shipping and maritime logistics operators quoting freight rates for oil tankers departing Indian ports now embed higher insurance premiums and transit-time buffers into per-barrel service costs, immediately widening delivery costs for downstream fuel retailers and petrochemical processors. Fertiliser manufacturers and power stations purchasing crude-derived feedstock or fuel oil see their Q3 procurement budgets reset upward, forcing either margin compression or a pass-through to agricultural input costs and grid tariff submissions. Retail fuel stations absorb the higher landed cost of petrol and diesel within days, while households purchasing cooking gas cylinders and bitumen-dependent road construction projects face delayed price firming as refiners and distributors clear inventory at transitional rates.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Aviation fuel suppliers to domestic carriers are absorbing immediate cost inflation on jet kerosene purchases, narrowing per-flight margin if ticket pricing lags procurement repricing by even one cycle. **MCX Crude's breach above ₹8,500/bbl validates the supply-risk narrative**, signalling refiners to expedite forward hedging. Retail petrol pump pricing will follow within 48–72 hours as state-owned retailers update their cost base.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor OPEC+ production guidance at the next scheduled meeting and track whether U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve release announcements emerge as a counter-signal. Any escalation in reported ship attacks or Strait of Hormuz transit disruptions will test **₹8,700/bbl as the next resistance level** for MCX Crude.
