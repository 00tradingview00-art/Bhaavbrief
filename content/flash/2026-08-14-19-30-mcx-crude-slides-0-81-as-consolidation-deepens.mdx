**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude fell to 7758 INR/bbl at 19:30 IST, posting a -0.81% decline from the previous close. The pullback extends the near-term consolidation phase after the sharp -2.19% drop to 7754 INR/bbl at 17:37 IST. Broader commodity weakness persists: gold up 0.67% to 154500 INR/10g, silver +0.61% to 236881 INR/kg, while natural gas surges +1.73% to 265.1 INR/mmBtu. USD/INR holds steady at 95.42 INR.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude remains trapped between 7754 and 7944 INR/bbl, signalling trader indecision. The intraday volatility—ranging from 8001 INR at 10:39 IST to 7754 at 17:37 IST—reflects conflicting supply-side stabilisation sentiment against broader macro headwinds. For MCX futures traders, positioning risk centers on whether this 247-point range persists or if fresh directional conviction emerges.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
7754 INR/bbl support (today's low at 17:37 IST) and 7903 INR/bbl resistance (13:26 IST peak). A break below 7754 reopens downside; a recapture above 7903 signals recovery resumption.
