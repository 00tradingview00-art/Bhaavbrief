USD/INR edges lower to 95.16, reducing headwinds for rupee-denominated precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 210000 breach signals tactical momentum reversal after intraday volatility. For MCX silver traders, round-number holds carry momentum amplification signals in thin afternoon liquidity. The correlation with gold's +1.38% move and nat gas strength suggests broad commodity rebalancing, though USD/INR weakness is providing direct tailwind to rupee metals pricing.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
