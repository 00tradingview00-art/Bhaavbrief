**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on Indian exchanges crashed ₹21,000/kg within a single hour after printing a lifetime high of ₹2,50,000/kg, marking one of the sharpest intraday reversals in the contract's history.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver continuous contract bears the direct impact of this whipsaw, with the lifetime high now acting as a hard resistance ceiling. At current COMEX silver of $78.15/oz, the theoretical MCX parity sits near ₹2,64,499/kg at USD/INR ₹95.70 — meaning the Indian market was already trading at a significant premium to COMEX-derived fair value before the collapse, a spread that profit-booking unwound violently. Indian traders carrying long positions face compounded risk: any further INR depreciation raises import costs and widens the COMEX-MCX spread mechanically, but a sharp rupee strengthening compresses that premium and accelerates downside. The ₹21,000 crash also reflects the built-in customs duty component (currently 6% on silver imports) getting repriced as leveraged positions exited simultaneously.

**WATCH**
The next directional confirmation comes from COMEX silver's ability to hold the $78/oz support level in the upcoming US session — a break below that level triggers fresh import-parity selling on MCX, while a recovery above $79.50/oz reopens the premium trade.
