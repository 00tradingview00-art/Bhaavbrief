**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX experienced a technical glitch during active trading hours, with Zerodha publicly flagging order execution issues affecting commodity traders across multiple brokers on the exchange platform.

**MCX IMPACT**
All actively traded MCX contracts — Gold (₹161,662/10g), Silver (₹261,989/kg), Crude Oil (₹9,640/bbl), and Copper — face execution risk during the glitch window, as order placement and modification failures directly impair position management. Traders holding open positions in Gold or Crude cannot rely on stop-loss orders triggering at intended levels, creating unhedged exposure during a period when COMEX Gold sits at $4,545/oz and WTI at $98.23/bbl — both at elevated price points where tick movement carries significant rupee-denominated P&L consequence. For import-linked hedgers using MCX Crude as a cost hedge against physical procurement, any execution gap widens basis risk versus the COMEX benchmark. Indian traders with overnight COMEX exposure using MCX as the hedge leg face uncovered risk for the duration of the platform disruption, with USD/INR at ₹96.21 amplifying the currency translation impact on unhedged positions.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange circulars and Zerodha's status page for confirmation of system restoration before re-engaging order flow. The next critical level to watch is whether MCX extends trading hours or provides a price-band adjustment circular to compensate for the disrupted session.
