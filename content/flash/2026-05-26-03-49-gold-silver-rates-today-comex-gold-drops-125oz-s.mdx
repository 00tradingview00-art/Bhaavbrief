**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX Gold dropped $125/oz in today's session while Silver fell $3/oz, as a crude oil rally to $90.63/bbl stoked inflation fears and triggered a risk-off rotation out of precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold and MCX Silver contracts face direct downward pressure from this COMEX selloff, with the USD/INR rate at ₹95.24 acting as a partial buffer against the full decline for Indian traders. At current COMEX levels post-drop, MCX Gold theoretical fair value recalibrates below ₹162,302/10g, and MCX Silver recalibrates below ₹264,558/kg — traders must account for India's 15% customs duty plus GST embedded in MCX pricing, which widens the floor relative to raw COMEX conversion. The crude rally simultaneously elevates import and logistics costs across commodity supply chains, compressing margins for bullion importers. Any further INR depreciation beyond ₹95.24 will mechanically narrow the COMEX-to-MCX decline transmission, keeping domestic spot prices stickier than international moves.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. CPI data release is the critical confirmation event — a print above consensus will validate crude-driven inflation fears and determine whether this precious metals selloff deepens or reverses. Monitor MCX Gold's ₹162,000/10g level as the immediate technical floor.
