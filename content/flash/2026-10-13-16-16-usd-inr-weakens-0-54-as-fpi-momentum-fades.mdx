**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR retreated to 95.45 INR at 04:16 pm IST, declining 0.54% from the previous close. The rupee reversal follows an intraday peak of 97.07 INR at 03:40 pm IST when foreign portfolio inflows had driven dollar strength. The pullback signals profit-taking after the 68 paisa rally, with MCX precious metals stabilizing—gold at 149300 INR/10g (-0.16%), silver at 222427 INR/kg (-0.20%)—as rupee weakness relief eases pressure.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX commodity traders, the dollar's retreat unlocks repricing across rupee-denominated futures. Gold and silver's modest losses narrow as import costs ease. Crude at 8762 INR/bbl (-2.43%) and natural gas at 297.8 INR/mmBtu (-1.46%) benefit from dual headwinds: softer dollar and underlying commodity weakness. Currency volatility remains the key cross-asset lever for positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support holds at 95.45 INR; resistance cluster at 96.39–96.57 INR marks the zone where intraday reversal began at 03:27 pm IST. A close below 95.30 INR signals sustained rupee strength.
