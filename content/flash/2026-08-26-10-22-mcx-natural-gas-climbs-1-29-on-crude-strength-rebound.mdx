**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas rallied to 273.8 INR/mmBtu at 10:22 am IST, gaining +1.29% from the previous close. The move extends volatility seen across the session, with crude oil recovering +2.09% to 8321 INR/bbl after earlier weakness. Copper climbed +0.59% to 1396.6 INR/kg, while silver gained +0.42% to 245152 INR/kg. Rupee weakness (USD/INR down -0.34% to 95.4) supported commodity valuations.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Natural gas continues its volatile consolidation pattern after yesterday's sharp reversal from 273 INR/mmBtu. The +1.29% move reflects broad-based energy strength tied to crude recovery. For MCX futures traders, this level sits near recent session highs, signaling tentative bullish momentum, though the intraday range remains wide—critical for assessing whether momentum traders are stepping in or profit-taking persists.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break above 275 INR/mmBtu resistance or sustained hold above 273 would signal fresh bullish conviction. Watch if crude stability above 7682 INR/bbl sustains the gas rally through the afternoon session.
