**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude slipped to 7878 INR/bbl at 19:25 IST, posting a -0.78% decline from the previous close. The pullback interrupts the recovery momentum that had pushed prices to 7944 INR/bbl at 21:14 IST earlier. Supply-side stabilisation sentiment persists, but near-term consolidation dominates as traders digest the breach above the psychological 8000 INR/bbl level achieved at 10:39 IST. USD/INR remains near 95.32 INR.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The pullback signals profit-taking after a +1.81% rally from the 7134 INR/bbl trough logged at 22:29 IST yesterday. For MCX futures traders, this modest reversal within the uptrend suggests the market is finding resistance before the next leg higher. The recovery trajectory remains intact despite the intraday weakness, but positioning caution is warranted until directional conviction returns.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
7944 INR/bbl—the recent high from 21:14 IST. A rebreak above this level with conviction would signal the recovery remains intact; failure to recapture it could test support near 7800 INR/bbl.
