**WHAT HAPPENED**
**NCDEX** introduced **Guar Korma Futures** contracts to establish standardized price transparency across India's protein feed value chain, replacing fragmented spot trading in guar processing markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Guar korma processors in **Rajasthan and Gujarat** — who currently negotiate prices bilaterally with cattle feed manufacturers — will now reference a centralized futures benchmark, eliminating the wide bid-ask spreads that characterize current mandi trading. Feed millers sourcing korma inventory in bulk will lock in procurement costs against the **NCDEX contract**, reducing the basis risk they currently face when buying physical stock weeks before production. This mechanism directly reprices the margin between **guar seed futures** (already traded on NCDEX) and the korma derivative product, allowing hedgers to manage crush-spread positions systematically.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Guar seed farmers in Jodhpur and Bikaner mandis, along with oil extractors and korma producers in processing hubs, gain a forward pricing mechanism that links their output directly to cattle feed demand signals. Dairy cooperatives stocking protein supplements across northern India and poultry integrators in Tamil Nadu absorb cost changes as feed millers adjust procurement strategies against transparent futures pricing.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Guar korma processors in Rajasthan will shift from spot negotiations to futures-hedged forward contracts, narrowing their effective procurement spreads. The **Guar Seed NCDEX contract** will now trade with embedded korma crush-spread volatility, signaling demand from the cattle feed sector. Livestock farmers relying on supplementary protein feed will see feed costs stabilize as millers reduce hedging costs they previously embedded in retail pricing.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **NCDEX Guar Korma contract open interest** in the first two weeks post-launch to confirm adoption by feed millers; simultaneously track **guar seed spot prices in Jodhpur mandi** for basis convergence signals.
