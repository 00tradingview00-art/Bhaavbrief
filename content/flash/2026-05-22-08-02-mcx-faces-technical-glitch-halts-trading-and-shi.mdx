**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX experienced a technical glitch that forced a complete halt in trading operations, triggering a failover to its backup system during active market hours.

**MCX IMPACT**
All active MCX contracts — Gold (₹160,908/10g), Silver (₹259,546/kg), and Crude Oil (₹9,589/bbl) — faced forced trading interruptions during the outage window. The halt creates execution risk: open positions cannot be squared off, stop-losses do not trigger, and new entries are blocked, leaving traders fully exposed to live COMEX price movement during the gap. With Gold at $4,523/oz on COMEX and USD/INR at ₹96.21, any COMEX move during the MCX downtime directly widens the spread between international price discovery and the Indian exchange price at resumption. Indian traders face compounded risk — import parity pricing and customs duty are already baked into MCX levels, and a resumed session after a halt typically sees sharp gap adjustments to realign with COMEX benchmarks that moved unhedged.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange circulars for the confirmed resumption timestamp and any revised session extension orders — the gap between COMEX closing levels and MCX's reopening price will determine the magnitude of adjustment traders face on existing positions.
