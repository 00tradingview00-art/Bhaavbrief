**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold slid to 147946 INR/10g at 02:06 pm IST, extending a −0.34% decline from the previous close. The pullback follows a volatile 12-hour session: the contract surged +1.20% to 151400 INR/10g at 10:40 pm IST, then fell −1.29% to 149540 INR/10g at 01:30 am IST. USD/INR strengthened to 95.16 INR (−0.30%), pressuring rupee-denominated gold. Silver mirrored weakness at −0.75%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold traders face competing forces: safe-haven demand remains embedded (copper +0.30%, natural gas +0.61%), but rupee depreciation erodes the local price appeal. The current 147946 level sits below the overnight lows, signalling consolidation in a 1500+ INR range. Cross-asset bid remains intact; rupee dynamics will dictate intraday direction.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor 147500 INR/10g support and the 149000 INR/10g resistance zone. A breach below 147500 would confirm fresh weakness; a rebound above 149311 INR/10g (12:50 am IST low) would signal recovery legs intact.
