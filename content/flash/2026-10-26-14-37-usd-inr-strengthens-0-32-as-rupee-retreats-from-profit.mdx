**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR moved to 95.23 INR at 02:37 pm IST, marking a +0.32% gain from the previous close. The rupee's renewed weakness reverses the profit-taking pullback seen at 02:06 pm IST when the pair had declined to 95.16 INR. The dollar's recovery coincides with a broad-based commodity rally: MCX Gold at 142908 INR/10g (+0.62%), MCX Copper at 1384.75 INR/kg (+0.56%), and MCX Silver at 196403 INR/kg (+0.35%), signaling renewed risk appetite.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX traders, the rupee's 7 paisa swing higher in the last 31 minutes signals uncertainty around support levels near 95.16 INR. The simultaneous strength in precious metals and base metals suggests dollar weakness is not broad-based; instead, commodity strength is tracking independent fundamentals. This cross-asset divergence requires caution in correlation-based hedging strategies.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 95.45 INR level (yesterday's 04:16 pm IST close) now acts as near-term resistance. A close above this will confirm the rupee's retreat; a slip back below 95.16 INR would reconfirm profit-taking dominance.
