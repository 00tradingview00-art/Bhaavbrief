**WHAT HAPPENED**
CME Group raised silver futures margin requirements, triggering an 11% intraday crash in COMEX silver and an 8% decline in MCX silver contracts.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (ongoing contract) bears the direct impact of this forced liquidation cascade originating on COMEX. With COMEX silver currently at $78.55/oz, the translated MCX reference price stands at approximately ₹2,64,558/kg — and the 8% intraday drawdown implies an intraday trough near ₹2,43,393/kg. Margin hikes mechanically force leveraged long positions to liquidate regardless of fundamental outlook, amplifying the sell-off beyond what spot price moves alone justify. For Indian traders, the INR/USD rate of ₹95.24 acts as a partial buffer on import cost calculations, but customs duty on silver imports means the MCX-COMEX spread widens during sharp dollar-driven dislocations, reducing arbitrage efficiency and increasing basis risk for hedgers carrying physical-futures positions.

**WATCH**
Monitor CME's official margin schedule for any second-round hike announcement, which historically compounds the liquidation wave within 48–72 hours of the first move. The ₹2,50,000/kg level on MCX Silver now functions as the critical structural reference — a sustained close below it confirms continuation of the margin-driven flush.
