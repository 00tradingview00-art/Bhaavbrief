**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude jumped to 9282 INR/bbl at 11:22 pm IST, posting a +1.82% gain as USD/INR steadied at 95.18 INR (-0.07% vs prev close). The rupee's stabilization after overnight volatility has restored competitiveness for domestic crude exports. Gold climbed to 147486 INR/10g (+1.12%), silver to 210591 INR/kg (+1.08%), while copper slipped to 1399 INR/kg (-0.35%) and natural gas rose to 305.6 INR/mmBtu (+0.56%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
This breakout reverses the sharp -2.43% decline logged at 04:16 pm IST on 8762 INR/bbl when rupee strength capped crude's upside. The recovery to 9282 signals traders are repricing rupee stability as a structural tailwind for export-linked commodity positions. Broad-based gains across gold and silver reinforce risk-on sentiment in the commodity complex.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9300 round level and the previous intraday high of 9090 INR/bbl (01:09 am IST) are critical resistance zones. A sustained hold above 9282 would confirm momentum; a slip back below 9100 would signal the rally lacks conviction.
