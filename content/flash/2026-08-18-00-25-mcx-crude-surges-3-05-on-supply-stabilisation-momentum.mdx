**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude accelerated to 8110 INR/bbl at 12:25 am IST, posting a +3.05% gain and extending the recovery trajectory initiated at 7754 INR/bbl (−2.19%) at 17:37 IST. The rally resumes supply-side stabilisation sentiment. Broader commodity strength mirrors the move: gold +0.92% to 155930 INR/10g, silver +0.92% to 238086 INR/kg, copper +0.39% to 1383.7 INR/kg. USD/INR at 95.6 (+0.21%) provides modest rupee headwind.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
MCX Crude has now decisively reclaimed ground above 8100 INR/bbl, signalling conviction in the supply-stabilisation narrative. For futures traders, this 3%+ swing breaks the consolidation range that held from 7758–7903 INR/bbl across the session. Cross-asset strength in gold and silver suggests risk appetite supporting energy demand expectations. Rupee weakness (95.6) offers import-cost support for domestic crude positioning.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 8150 INR/bbl resistance level—the next structural ceiling. If crude clears this on sustained volume, the recovery extends toward 8200 INR/bbl. Failure below 8050 INR/bbl would signal a reversal of today's momentum.
