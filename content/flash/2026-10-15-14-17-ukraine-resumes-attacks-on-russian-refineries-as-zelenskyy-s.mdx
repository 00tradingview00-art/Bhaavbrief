**TITLE**
Ukraine Refinery Strikes Push MCX Crude Higher; Energy Importers Face Margin Squeeze

**WHAT HAPPENED**
Ukraine has resumed drone and missile attacks on Russian oil refineries as President Zelenskyy signals an escalation in energy infrastructure targeting, directly threatening crude supply stability in Eastern Europe and risk-premium flows to global markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Refinery shutdowns in Russia tighten crude export capacity, forcing Asian importers — including Indian refiners clearing Urals and ESPO barrels — to bid for marginal barrels at elevated risk premiums, pushing rupee-denominated acquisition costs higher per barrel. Simultaneously, **MCX Crude at ₹9164/bbl** is trading into a tighter supply schedule, and any further geopolitical escalation will compress margins for refinery procurement desks reliant on cost-plus feedstock pricing.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Indian petroleum refiners sourcing feedstock on monthly or quarterly contracts face immediate tension between spot market signals and contractual cost floors, forcing accelerated procurement decisions. Downstream fuel retailers and lubricant blenders — who price diesel and petrol at retail pumps tied to crude cost indices — face margin compression if refinery input costs spike faster than government price-adjustment cycles can absorb them. Logistics operators, trucking fleets, and small-scale transporters purchasing retail diesel experience delayed but visible cost pass-through, while households heating with furnace oil or purchasing kerosene in non-metro regions absorb the first-mover cost shock.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian refinery procurement desks are repricing feedstock acquisition budgets against elevated geopolitical risk, immediately shrinking margins on every barrel cleared above **₹9200/bbl threshold levels**. **MCX Crude** is trading directionally north as supply disruption premiums embed into price, signaling refiners to lock hedges. Retail diesel consumers in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities will see pump prices harden within 7–10 days if crude sustains above current levels.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor Russian crude export volumes reported by shipping trackers and any OPEC+ emergency statement; a breach of **₹9300/bbl** on MCX Crude would trigger hedging acceleration among Indian refiners.
