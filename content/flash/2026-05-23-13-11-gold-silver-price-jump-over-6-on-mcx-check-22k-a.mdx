**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices surged over 6% on MCX in a single session, with gold trading around ₹160,047/10g and silver near ₹257,896/kg, tracking sharp gains on COMEX where gold stands at $4,523/oz and silver at $76.20/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary contracts absorbing this move. The 6% single-session rally reflects a combination of COMEX spot strength and rupee depreciation pressure, with USD/INR at ₹95.70 amplifying import costs for Indian bullion traders. At current COMEX gold of $4,523/oz, the landed cost calculation — factoring India's 15% basic customs duty plus GST and handling — places fair-value MCX gold well above the ₹160,047/10g mark, compressing the import arbitrage window for licensed importers. Silver's move from COMEX at $76.20/oz translates directly into elevated hedging costs for industrial users on MCX, particularly jewellers and silverware manufacturers sourcing physical inventory.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve commentary or CPI print is the critical event — a softer inflation reading reinforces the bullion bid and confirms the breakout above ₹160,000/10g on MCX, while a hawkish surprise pressures COMEX gold back below $4,400/oz, unwinding the MCX premium rapidly.
