**WHAT HAPPENED**
The **RBI Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** is scheduled to announce its interest rate decision in **June 2026**, with the outcome expected to influence rupee strength and foreign fund flows into Indian financial markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A rate hold or cut will weaken the **Indian rupee** against the dollar, making imported **crude oil** more expensive for refiners and raising **gold's rupee-denominated prices** despite stable global quotes. Conversely, a rate hike will strengthen the rupee, lowering landed costs for oil importers but pressuring gold demand among retail buyers and jewellers who price purchases in rupees. **MCX futures traders** holding leveraged positions in **gold and crude oil** will face margin pressure if volatility spikes post-announcement, while currency hedging costs for commodity importers will shift based on the RBI's forward guidance.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
**Indian Oil Corporation, Reliance Industries**, and independent refiners in Gujarat buying **Brent crude**; **NTPC and state power distributors** managing fuel costs; **Titan Company, Kalyan Jewellers**, and unorganized jewellers across metropolitan and tier-2 cities; **MCX gold futures traders** and hedging firms; **retail gold buyers** in north and south India ahead of monsoon weddings and festivals.

**BOTTOM LINE**
**Refiners and power utilities**: A rate cut cuts rupee value, pushing **crude oil import costs up by 2-4%** depending on dollar movement. **MCX gold futures (GOLD)**: Rate decisions typically trigger **30-50 rupee swings** in spot gold within 48 hours. **Retail gold buyers**: Cheaper rupee scenarios delay purchases; stronger rupee triggers buying ahead of June festivals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the **RBI MPC announcement date (early June 2026)** and the central bank's inflation and growth commentary. The **USD-INR pair breaking 85.50** on the announcement day will confirm the rupee direction driving commodity repricing.
