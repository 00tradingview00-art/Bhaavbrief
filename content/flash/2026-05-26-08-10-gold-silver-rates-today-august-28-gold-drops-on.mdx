**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on MCX dropped on August 28 amid pressure from Trump tariff developments, while silver held firm, with MCX Gold trading near ₹160,948/10g and MCX Silver around ₹259,506/kg based on COMEX spot levels.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (October contract) and MCX Silver (September contract) are the primary instruments affected by this divergence. Gold faces downward pressure as tariff-related USD strength pushes COMEX spot lower, which transmits directly into MCX pricing after applying the USD/INR rate of ₹95.24 and applicable customs duty of 6% plus GST. At current COMEX Gold of $4,571/oz, the landed import cost for Indian refiners remains elevated, meaning any further COMEX softness compresses domestic premiums and widens the MCX-COMEX spread against bullion importers. Silver's relative outperformance reflects industrial demand resilience — at $77.05/oz COMEX, MCX Silver's ₹259,506/kg level keeps import economics tight for Indian buyers dependent on overseas supply.

**WATCH**
Monitor the next U.S. PCE inflation print and any formal tariff announcement from the Trump administration, as confirmation of broad tariffs strengthens USD further and pressures MCX Gold below the ₹160,000/10g psychological support level.
