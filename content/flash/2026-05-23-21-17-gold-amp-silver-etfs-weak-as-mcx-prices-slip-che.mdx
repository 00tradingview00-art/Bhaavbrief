**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold and Silver prices slipped, dragging Indian Gold and Silver ETFs lower, as COMEX Gold trades at $4,523/oz and Silver at $76.20/oz amid a global risk-off trigger pressuring precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (current reference: ₹1,60,047/10g) and MCX Silver (₹2,57,896/kg) contracts face direct downside pressure as any COMEX correction transmits immediately into INR-denominated prices at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. A 1% decline in COMEX Gold translates to approximately ₹1,600/10g erosion on MCX before customs duty and GST adjustments are applied. Indian Gold ETFs, which hold physical gold priced against MCX spot, track this decline in near real-time, compressing NAVs without any currency buffer since the rupee holds steady at 95.70. Traders holding MCX Gold or Silver futures must note that the import parity cost — inclusive of 15% customs duty and 3% GST — creates a structural floor around ₹1,55,000–₹1,56,000/10g, below which domestic arbitrage typically activates.

**WATCH**
The next directional confirmation comes from the US Federal Reserve's upcoming commentary on rate trajectory and the weekly COMEX Commitment of Traders (COT) report — a sustained move below $4,480/oz on COMEX will pressure MCX Gold toward the ₹1,57,500/10g level.
