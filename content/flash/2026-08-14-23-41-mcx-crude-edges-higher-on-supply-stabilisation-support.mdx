**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude moved to 7878 INR/bbl at 23:41 IST, posting a +0.10% gain from the previous close. The modest advance extends the intraday recovery after sharp volatility that saw prices hit 7754 INR/bbl at 17:37 IST (−2.19% loss). Supply-side stabilisation sentiment continues to underpin modest strength. USD/INR firmed to 95.42 (+0.07%), while precious metals held gains: gold at 154590 INR/10g (+0.05%), silver at 236272 INR/kg (+0.15%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude remains locked in a volatile consolidation band between 7754–7903 INR/bbl. The +0.10% move signals fragile support but lacks conviction. Traders are caught between supply concerns and macro headwinds. Rupee strength at 95.42 provides only marginal tailwind; the real contest remains whether crude can sustain above 7900 INR/bbl or slip back toward the 7750 support zone.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 7900 INR/bbl resistance level. A decisive break above it signals recovery momentum; a retest of 7754 INR/bbl would confirm the near-term downtrend persists.
