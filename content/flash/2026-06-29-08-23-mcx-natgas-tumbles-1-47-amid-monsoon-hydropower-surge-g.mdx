**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas collapsed **1.47%** to **₹308.30/mmBtu** in Monday morning trade, leading the commodity complex lower. Crude Oil posted modest gains of **+0.94%** at **₹6,639/barrel**, while precious metals showed divergence—Gold held steady above **₹1.59 lakh/10g**, but Silver retreated **0.50%** to **₹220,300/kg** amid renewed US-Iran geopolitical tensions.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
NatGas weakness signals demand destruction in the Indian power sector as monsoon-driven hydroelectric generation rises, reducing thermal power draw. At current USD/INR of **₹94.38**, the import parity for US LNG imports approximates $12-13/mmBtu delivered cost, creating a floor. Silver's decline despite gold's resilience reflects industrial demand concerns—renewed conflict rhetoric typically triggers safe-haven gold accumulation while pressuring silver's manufacturing-dependent pricing.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
NTPC and Torrent Power's gas-fired thermal stations face weaker spark spreads as NatGas input costs drop relative to power realisation. Reliance Industries' MTPL (Dahej terminal) hedging positions benefit from lower LNG procurement windows. Rajesh Masrani (silver jewellers) and industrial users face margin compression if silver weakness persists into Q3 demand season.

**BOTTOM LINE**
NatGas's structural downtrend reflects monsoon seasonality overriding geopolitical upside—a bearish signal for summer power procurement hedging. Gold-Silver decoupling indicates risk-off positioning where safe-haven demand drives precious metals divergence.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
US Energy Information Administration's weekly LNG export data (Tuesday, 10:30 IST) and Monsoon Meteorological Department rainfall update (Wednesday) will confirm whether demand destruction thesis holds or reverses.
