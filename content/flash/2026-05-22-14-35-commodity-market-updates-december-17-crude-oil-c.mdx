**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX futures for crude oil, copper, and aluminium posted gains on December 17, tracking positive momentum across global commodity markets.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Crude Oil futures are directly affected, with WTI at $99.00/bbl translating to approximately ₹9,715/bbl at the current USD/INR rate of ₹96.21 — traders watching the ₹9,700 level as immediate support. MCX Copper futures track COMEX copper at $6.3715/lb; after conversion and applying India's 2.5% basic customs duty plus GST, landed import cost runs materially above COMEX spot, compressing the arbitrage window for physical importers. Aluminium futures on MCX respond to both LME pricing and rupee movement — a rupee weakening beyond ₹96.50 widens import costs and provides further floor support to domestic futures prices. Indian traders holding short positions in these three contracts face margin pressure as all three legs move simultaneously against short inventory.

**WATCH**
The U.S. Federal Reserve interest rate decision and accompanying dot-plot projection — due the same week — is the primary catalyst that determines whether the dollar strengthens and pulls WTI below $99.00 or sustains above it, directly resetting MCX crude's rupee-denominated base. A breach of ₹9,700/bbl on MCX Crude signals reversal of today's gains.
