**WHAT HAPPENED**
Multi Commodity Exchange of India (MCX) released its Q4 FY26 financial results, marking the close of the full fiscal year 2025-26.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX's quarterly earnings directly reflect trading volumes across Gold, Silver, Crude Oil, and Copper futures — the exchange's core revenue-generating contracts. Strong volume data in Q4 results signals heightened open interest and liquidity depth in MCX Gold (currently referencing ₹160,971/10g) and MCX Silver (₹260,431/kg), which translates to tighter bid-ask spreads for Indian traders operating at these elevated price levels. With COMEX Copper at $6.3585/lb and WTI Crude at $97.80/bbl (MCX ~₹9,598/bbl), any volume surge reported in energy and base metals contracts confirms robust hedging activity by Indian importers managing rupee-denominated import costs against a USD/INR rate of ₹96.21. Exchange revenue tied to transaction charges rises in direct proportion to notional value traded — at current gold and crude price levels, per-lot revenue for MCX is materially higher than prior fiscal periods.
