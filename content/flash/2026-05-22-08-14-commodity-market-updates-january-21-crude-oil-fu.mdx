**WHAT HAPPENED**
On January 21, MCX crude oil futures declined while copper and zinc contracts advanced, reflecting a split session across the commodity complex.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Crude Oil futures face downward pressure aligned with WTI spot at $97.48/bbl, translating to approximately ₹9,566/bbl on MCX after currency conversion at ₹96.21 — any further WTI softness compresses refiner margins and reduces import cost for Indian buyers. MCX Copper tracked COMEX gains, with COMEX copper at $6.3752/lb; at current USD/INR of ₹96.21, the landed import cost remains elevated, keeping MCX copper premiums tight against international benchmarks. Indian copper consumers importing refined metal absorb both the COMEX price surge and rupee translation costs simultaneously, widening their raw material burden. Zinc's advance on MCX mirrors base metal demand optimism, though Indian traders must track LME zinc separately as MCX zinc pricing derives from LME settlement, not COMEX.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. crude inventory report (EIA Weekly Petroleum Status) will either confirm the bearish crude direction or reverse it — a draw above 3 million barrels flips the WTI narrative at the $97.48 support zone. For copper and zinc, China's January PMI release is the critical confirmation event for sustained base metal demand strength.
