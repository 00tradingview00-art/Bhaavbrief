**WHAT HAPPENED**
The **US struck 5 Iranian oil tankers** in a direct escalation, prompting **Iran to target a Jordanian military base hosting US forces** and mobilise **naval assets near US warships** in contested waters.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Refinery procurement desks and crude importers face immediate upward pressure on **WTI-linked contract valuations** as geopolitical risk premia embed into tanker route insurance and spot **Brent crude** assessments. **Gold bullion dealers and jewellery wholesalers holding dollar inventory** see rupee-denominated acquisition costs rise as safe-haven demand pushes spot gold higher, compressing margins on pre-hedged retail SKUs. **Natural gas traders** monitoring LNG import parity pricing now calculate higher geopolitical risk into forward contract bids, particularly for **US-origin LNG shipments** competing in Asian markets.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Petroleum refineries and fuel blending units sourcing crude on monthly tenders face immediate repricing of outstanding bids, shrinking gross refining margins on petrol and diesel barrels cleared at elevated spot levels. Power generation plants locked into **LNG spot procurement** for summer demand see fuel input costs reprice upward, forcing grid operators to adjust tariff pass-through calculations before peak load hours. Jewellery manufacturers and bullion fabricators holding dollar-denominated gold inventory experience immediate rupee inventory value appreciation, yet retail jewellers carrying finished-goods stocks face working-capital pressure as wholesale acquisition prices spike ahead of summer wedding season demand.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Shipping and logistics operators face higher insurance premiums on crude tanker charters crossing the Persian Gulf, directly reducing transport margin per barrel. **MCX Crude Oil contracts** signal sustained geopolitical risk premium, with breach above **₹7,200 per barrel thresholds** confirming safe-haven repricing. Petrol and diesel retail pump prices absorb upstream cost acceleration within 48–72 hours as Oil Ministry reviews ex-refinery rates.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Next **OPEC production statement** and any **Suez Canal or Strait of Hormuz transit incident reports** will confirm whether supply-side physical disruption materializes beyond price signals.
