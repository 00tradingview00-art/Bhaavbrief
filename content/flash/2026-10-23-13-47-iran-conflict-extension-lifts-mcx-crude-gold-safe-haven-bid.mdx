**WHAT HAPPENED**
A Trump administration envoy stated the Iran conflict could extend another year, triggering fresh geopolitical risk premium into global oil and precious metals markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude oil refiners and import procurement desks operating on **MCX Crude at ₹9755/bbl** face sustained upward pressure on dollar-denominated WTI contracts, translating directly into higher rupee-equivalent landed costs per barrel as **USD/INR holds ₹94.92**. Gold dealers and bullion wholesalers pricing inventory in rupee terms see **MCX Gold at ₹142502/10g** re-anchored higher on safe-haven demand, while silver-consuming industrial units face a parallel input repricing on **MCX Silver at ₹195343/kg** as geopolitical risk widens precious metals bid-ask spreads.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Petroleum retailers and diesel distributors managing margin compression at pump level will lock higher crude cost positions through forward contracts, immediately squeezing gross margins on fuel sales. Fertiliser producers and petrochemical manufacturers using crude as feedstock see production cost cycles reset upward, forcing either margin absorption or downstream price pass-through to agricultural and industrial buyers. Jewellery manufacturers and silver fabricators holding working capital in bullion inventory will see rupee-denominated stock values rise on the gold and silver revaluation, but face frozen or higher selling prices to retail chains and D2C platforms. Households purchasing diesel for irrigation, petrol for transport, and gold ornaments for festivals will encounter price increases at point of transaction within coming weeks.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Oil marketing companies face margin compression on fuel retail as crude landed costs spike on extended geopolitical duration. **MCX Crude** signals a breach above current levels if the conflict timeline holds, while **MCX Gold** acts as a secondary rally beneficiary. Indian households purchasing fuel and jewellery will absorb price increases as supply-chain repricing cascades downward.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor OPEC production statements and any ceasefire negotiations announcements; crude price stability above ₹9755 for three consecutive sessions will confirm the extended-conflict scenario is priced in.
