**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on MCX is trading near ₹1.31 lakh per 10g while silver has also surged, tracking a broad precious metals rally driven by COMEX gold at $4,602/oz and silver at $78.15/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are both under direct upward pressure from COMEX spot levels. At the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70, COMEX gold at $4,602/oz translates to approximately ₹1,62,826 per 10g — meaning MCX Gold near ₹1.31 lakh is trading at a significant discount to the theoretical import parity price, a gap that reflects domestic demand-supply dynamics and applicable customs duty (currently 6% basic + 5% IGST on gold imports). Silver's COMEX-to-MCX conversion at ₹2,64,499/kg sets the technical ceiling for the domestic contract. Indian traders need to monitor the COMEX-MCX spread closely, as any narrowing driven by rupee depreciation or customs duty revision directly compresses arbitrage margins. The ₹95.70 USD/INR level is amplifying import costs, keeping domestic prices structurally elevated.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Non-Farm Payrolls release and any Federal Reserve commentary on rate trajectory will either sustain or reverse COMEX gold's momentum above $4,600/oz, which directly determines whether MCX Gold challenges the ₹1,63,000 import parity ceiling.
