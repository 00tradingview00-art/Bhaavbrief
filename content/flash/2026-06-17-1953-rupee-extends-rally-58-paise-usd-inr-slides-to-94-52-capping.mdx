This flash extends the rupee strengthening trend flagged on 12 Jun at 07:48 pm IST, when USD/INR stood at 95.10. The pair has now fallen a further 58 paise to 94.52, a cumulative 0.61% move over the period. The stronger rupee is acting as a mechanical drag on MCX commodity prices denominated in rupees. Despite this headwind, MCX Gold has risen 0.53% to ₹153,899 per 10g and MCX Silver is up 0.78% to ₹252,046 per kg, suggesting COMEX-side losses — Gold at $4,247.3 per oz (down 1.93%) and Silver at $66.9 per oz (down 4.29%) — are being partially offset by the rupee's continued appreciation compressing import costs. Watch: ₹94.20 as the next support level on USD/INR; a break lower would intensify the rupee tailwind on MCX pricing.
