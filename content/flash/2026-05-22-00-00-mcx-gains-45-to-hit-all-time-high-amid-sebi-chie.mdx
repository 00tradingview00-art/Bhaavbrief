**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX exchange gained 4.5% to hit an all-time high, driven by SEBI chief's supportive comments on commodity derivatives and a concurrent rally in precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
Gold Futures (MCX June contract) and Silver Futures are the primary contracts in focus, with both riding the tailwind of COMEX gold at $4,551/oz translating to approximately ₹1,61,878/10g and COMEX silver at $77.32/oz equating to roughly ₹2,63,074/kg on MCX. The SEBI chief's comments signal regulatory support for deeper commodity derivatives participation, which structurally increases open interest and liquidity in MCX precious metal contracts. With USD/INR at ₹96.21, the rupee conversion is amplifying the COMEX rally — every dollar move in gold at this exchange rate adds approximately ₹3,091/10g to MCX prices, widening the landed cost premium for physical importers. Indian bullion traders and jewellers using MCX as a hedge face elevated import costs given 15% customs duty plus GST layered on top of the already elevated COMEX base, compressing physical-to-futures spreads.

**WATCH**
Monitor the next SEBI circular or commodity derivatives policy announcement for confirmation of structural participation expansion. A sustained COMEX gold hold above $4,500/oz is the critical level that keeps MCX Gold futures anchored above ₹1,60,000/10g.
