**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold breached ₹1.8 lakh per 10 gram and MCX Silver crossed ₹4 lakh per kg, driven by escalating geopolitical tensions and a weakening US dollar.

**MCX IMPACT**
Both MCX Gold (current COMEX-derived reference: ~₹1,60,908/10g) and MCX Silver (~₹2,59,546/kg) are trading at significant premiums to their COMEX-converted benchmarks at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹96.21, with MCX Gold showing a spread of approximately ₹19,000/10g and Silver a spread of roughly ₹1.4 lakh/kg above COMEX parity — a widening that reflects elevated domestic demand and import cost pressures. The rupee-dollar exchange rate at ₹96.21 is a primary amplifier: every 50-paisa INR depreciation adds roughly ₹800/10g to gold's landed cost before the 15% basic customs duty and GST are applied. Indian bullion importers and arbitrageurs face compressed margins as the MCX–COMEX spread widens beyond normal import parity windows. Traders holding short positions on MCX Gold near ₹1,60,908 reference levels face mark-to-market pressure as the domestic premium sustains.

**WATCH**
The next US Federal Reserve commentary or US Dollar Index (DXY) print will either reinforce dollar weakness and extend this rally or trigger a correction toward COMEX-parity levels on MCX. Monitor USD/INR at the ₹96.50 resistance level as a key trigger for further premium expansion.
