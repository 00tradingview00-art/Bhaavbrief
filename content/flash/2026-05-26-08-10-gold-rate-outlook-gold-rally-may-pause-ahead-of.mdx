**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on MCX hit a lifetime high while gold's rally is flagging ahead of the US Federal Reserve policy decision, with COMEX silver at $77.05/oz translating to approximately ₹259,506/kg on MCX.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (near-month contract) is the primary contract under pressure, having breached historical highs — a level that now acts as near-term support for positional traders. MCX Gold (~₹160,948/10g) faces consolidation risk as the Fed decision introduces rate-path uncertainty; a hawkish hold strengthens the dollar and compresses rupee-denominated gold further. At USD/INR of ₹95.24, every 50-paisa rupee depreciation adds approximately ₹800–900/10g to MCX Gold import costs, creating a natural floor even if COMEX softens. Indian traders must account for the 15% customs duty plus GST on gold imports, which widens the MCX premium over COMEX and cushions downside relative to international spot moves.

**WATCH**
The US Federal Reserve policy statement and Jerome Powell's press conference are the definitive triggers — a hawkish tone firms the dollar and pressures both metals on COMEX, while any dovish pivot accelerates silver's momentum and re-energizes gold above ₹161,500/10g on MCX. Track USD/INR simultaneously, as a move beyond ₹95.50 independently lifts MCX prices regardless of COMEX direction.
