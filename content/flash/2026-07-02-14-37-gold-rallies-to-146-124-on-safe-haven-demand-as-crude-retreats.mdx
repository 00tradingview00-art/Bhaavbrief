**WHAT HAPPENED**

MCX Gold surged **₹146,124/10g**, gaining **1.17%** in the afternoon session as safe-haven demand accelerated. Silver followed with a **0.64%** climb to **₹229,690/kg**, while crude oil weakened **0.78%** to **₹6,474/bbl**, signaling a clear risk-off rotation. Natural gas slumped **1.34%** to **₹302.70/mmBtu**, the session's outlier mover.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

The gold-crude divergence reflects real-yield compression: as oil demand expectations recede, investors repriced precious metals higher through the safe-haven lens rather than reflation. USD/INR at **₹95.38** provides the import-parity floor—rupee stability removes a hedging cost, allowing domestic buyers to absorb gold at imported levels without currency drag. Silver's dual nature shows the safe-haven component dominance today: the **0.64%** move is insufficient to signal industrial demand (solar polysilicon, semiconductor paste) revival; instead, it mirrors gold's directional pull.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**

A jewellery manufacturer holding unhedged dollar-denominated gold contracts faces margin relief; lower rupee volatility reduces their import-parity arbitrage friction. An electronics-grade silver consumer (photovoltaic paste supplier) sees no industrial signal yet—the move is purely safe-haven synchronized with bullion. A refiner managing crude-linked fuel hedges benefits from crude weakness, though **₹6,474/bbl** remains above June's 10-day average, limiting working-capital recovery.

**BOTTOM LINE**

Gold's **1.17%** advance paired with crude's **0.78%** decline shows capital rotating into safe-haven tilt, not broad commodity inflation. Real-yield floors, not geopolitical premia, are pricing today's move.

**WHAT TO WATCH**

MCX Gold hold above **₹146,000/10g** into close and tomorrow's RBI policy expectations data to confirm whether this is tactical risk-off or structural yield repricing.
