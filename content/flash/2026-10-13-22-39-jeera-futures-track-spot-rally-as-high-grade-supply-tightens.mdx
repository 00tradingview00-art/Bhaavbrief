**WHAT HAPPENED**
**Indian jeera** rallied on domestic exchanges as high-grade supply tightened, signalling a shift from the prior season's surplus into a constrained inventory phase across producing regions.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Unjha mandi traders — India's largest **jeera** spot market in Gujarat — are repricing their holding inventory upward, causing the NCDEX basis to narrow as spot values chase futures premiums. Exporters sourcing **jeera** for shipment see their procurement costs rise immediately, squeezing margins on forward contracts already booked at lower price assumptions. The supply pinch reflects seasonal depletion of premium grades (typically moisture under 10%, impurity under 2%) in farmer stocks and cooperative godowns, forcing mills and dealers to bid higher for available lots.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Farmers in Rajasthan and Gujarat who held stock beyond immediate post-harvest sales now face a decision to liquidate at firming prices or carry inventory into the next quarter. Spice mills in Rajasthan and Maharashtra processing **jeera** for domestic blending and export face higher raw material acquisition costs, forcing them to either absorb margin compression or reprice their finished masala blends sold to food manufacturers and kirana distributors. FMCG brands blending curries, ready-to-eat meal kits, and restaurant chains buying pre-ground **jeera** from mills will see procurement invoices repriced in the next purchase cycle, likely filtering into retail shelf prices for packaged masalas and restaurant margin structures by quarter-end.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Unjha mandi dealers holding **jeera** inventory stand to recover better realization per quintal, improving their working capital recovery this month. **NCDEX jeera contracts** are tracking the spot rally with open interest shifting toward buyers hedging forward procurement needs. Retail consumers purchasing packaged spices and households buying loose **jeera** at kirana stores should expect steady-to-firmer pricing through the immediate term.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The next NCDEX spot price settlement and weekly mandi arrival data from Unjha and Jodhpur will confirm whether the supply tightness persists or seasonal fresh arrivals moderate the rally.
