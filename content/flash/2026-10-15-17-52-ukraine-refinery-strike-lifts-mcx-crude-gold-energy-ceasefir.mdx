**WHAT HAPPENED**
Ukraine struck a Russian refinery after Moscow abandoned a Trump-brokered energy ceasefire, escalating direct supply-chain disruption in Eastern European refining capacity.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Refinery outages in Russia tighten global crude allocation, forcing Indian refiners and fuel importers to bid higher for replacement barrels at **₹9282/bbl** and above. Procurement desks managing winter fuel stockpiles and diesel inventory now face rupee-denominated cost inflation on every cargo cleared at elevated Brent-linked levels. Simultaneously, geopolitical risk premiums widen safe-haven demand for **MCX Gold at ₹147486/10g**, pulling bullion dealers and accumulation-focused traders into longs as currency and conflict hedges.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Indian diesel retailers and truck-fleet operators sourcing fuel through IOC and BPCL procurement cycles absorb immediate feedstock cost pressures, compressing retail margin on every litre pumped at the forecourt. Mid-chain logistics operators and fertiliser manufacturers — heavy diesel consumers — face input-cost repricing on their forward delivery contracts, forcing them to either hold inventory longer or accelerate sales at locked-in prices. Retail buyers filling fuel tanks and households paying power bills tied to fuel-cost pass-through clauses will encounter upward pressure on transport and electricity tariffs within 4–6 weeks, as refiners and utilities embed crude premiums into their cost recovery mechanisms.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Aviation fuel suppliers to Indian carriers will see their jet-fuel hedging costs spike, eating into airline ancillary margins. **MCX Crude** has signalled directional strength above ₹9300/bbl as a near-term resistance confirmation of supply-shock pricing. Petrol and diesel retail prices at Indian pump stations will trend upward if crude remains elevated through the next fortnight's inventory-release cycle.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor Russian refining capacity assessments and casualty reports within 48 hours; a second strike or escalated facility damage could breach ₹9500/bbl. Watch OPEC+ emergency meeting calls and IEA weekly supply updates for demand-destruction signals that might cap further crude rallies.
