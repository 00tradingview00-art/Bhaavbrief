**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude dropped to 8541 INR/bbl at 09:23 pm IST, posting a -5.35% decline from the previous close. The retreat follows a sharp multi-session rally anchored in geopolitical supply concerns since 10:23 pm IST yesterday, which had driven prices to 8961 INR/bbl at 10:25 pm IST. The pullback reflects profit-taking momentum as traders lock gains. Precious metals remain resilient—gold at 143324 INR/10g (+0.35%) and silver at 222836 INR/kg (+1.58%)—while rupee holds steady at 96.55 INR.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sharp reversal signals fatigue in the geopolitical-driven bull run after a 6.55% peak-to-trough move. MCX crude traders face a critical positioning decision: whether this pullback is a healthy consolidation or a breakdown of the supply-fear narrative. The 420-point drop in hours suggests profit-taking rather than fundamental weakness, but volatility remains elevated across the commodity complex.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 8500 INR/bbl support level—a breach below would signal deeper profit-taking. Watch whether crude can stabilize and recapture the 8700–8800 INR/bbl range that anchored strength earlier at 05:39 pm IST.
