**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX suffered an extended technical glitch that halted trading and delayed futures contracts in gold and silver for an unspecified duration during active market hours.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract, ~₹161,025/10g) and MCX Silver (July contract, ~₹260,924/kg) are the directly affected contracts. A trading halt creates a price discovery vacuum — any movement on COMEX during the downtime ($4,527/oz gold; $76.69/oz silver) does not reflect in MCX prices until systems restore, widening the COMEX-MCX spread artificially. When trading resumes, MCX contracts reprice sharply to close that gap, incorporating the USD/INR rate of ₹96.21 plus applicable customs duty (currently 6% on gold imports), compressing or expanding the landed cost differential in a single session. Indian traders holding open positions during the halt face mark-to-market risk with no ability to hedge or exit, and margin calls can trigger at reopening if COMEX has moved significantly during the blackout period.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official bulletin for the exact resumption timestamp and any circular on settlement price methodology for the halted session. COMEX gold holding above $4,527/oz or silver above $76.69/oz during the halt directly determines the gap-open magnitude on MCX resumption.
