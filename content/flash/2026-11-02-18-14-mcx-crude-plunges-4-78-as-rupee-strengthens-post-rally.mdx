**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude dropped to 9462 INR/bbl at 06:14 pm IST, posting a −4.78% loss as USD/INR weakened to 95.09 INR (−0.18% vs prev close). The rupee's sharp appreciation has compressed export margins for domestic crude producers after the intraday peak of 9893 INR/bbl at 03:42 pm IST. Meanwhile, MCX Silver gained +1.16% to 181201 INR/kg and Natural Gas rose +1.44% to 281.1 INR/mmBtu.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sharp reversal signals profit-taking after the morning-to-afternoon rally. For MCX crude traders, the rupee's intraday strength has erased the earlier export competitiveness narrative. The 431 INR range (9462–9893) now defines the session's volatility band, with positioning vulnerable to further rupee moves.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 9400 INR support level—a breach would expose the 9300 zone and confirm the breakdown momentum. Track USD/INR moves above 95.15 for fresh directional cues.
