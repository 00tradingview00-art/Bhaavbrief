**WHAT HAPPENED**
Crude oil futures rose while copper and aluminium futures slumped on January 8 amid muted industrial demand signals globally.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Crude Oil (January contract) tracks this upward pressure, with WTI currently at $98.32/bbl translating to approximately ₹9,649/bbl at the prevailing USD/INR rate of ₹96.21 — a level Indian refiners and traders are monitoring closely as import costs rise in rupee terms. MCX Copper (January contract) faces downward pressure mirroring COMEX copper at $6.3633/lb; with India being a net copper importer, softer global prices compress the COMEX-MCX spread but customs duty at 2.5% plus GST keeps domestic prices structurally elevated above import parity. Aluminium on MCX similarly reflects weak demand sentiment from China's manufacturing sector, the primary demand driver for base metals. Indian traders in copper and aluminium are watching whether the price dip breaches key support zones or simply reflects thin post-holiday liquidity.

**WATCH**
China's December 2024 Caixin Manufacturing PMI release and the U.S. EIA Weekly Crude Inventory report are the next confirmed data triggers — a inventory draw confirmation above consensus will reinforce crude's upward trajectory, while any further Chinese demand disappointment cements the base metals decline.
