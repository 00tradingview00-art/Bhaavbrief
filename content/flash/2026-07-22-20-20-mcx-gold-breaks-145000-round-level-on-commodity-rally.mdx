**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 145706 INR/10g at 20:20 IST, gaining +1.98% from the previous close of 142883 INR/10g. The rally breached the psychological 145000 round level as broad-based commodity strength persisted: Silver climbed +1.75% to 227686 INR/kg, Crude advanced +3.19% to 8410 INR/bbl, and Natural Gas rose +2.36% to 281.8 INR/mmBtu. Rupee weakness at 96.56 INR/USD (+0.07%) supported precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, the round-number breach signals conviction above a key psychological barrier. The synchronized strength across silver, crude, and natgas confirms this is a risk-on cross-asset move, not a gold-specific play. Rupee depreciation remains the structural tailwind, making INR-denominated commodity hedges increasingly attractive to domestic players.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 146000 round level is the next natural resistance. A daily close above 145700 would confirm momentum has shifted higher; a pullback toward 145000 would test whether this breach holds as support.
