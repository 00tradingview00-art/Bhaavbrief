**WHAT HAPPENED**

MCX crude fell **2.86%** to **₹6,871/bbl** in today's global session, while gold advanced **1.1%** and silver gained **1.31%**, signaling a clear pivot away from energy into precious metals. Natural gas collapsed **6.30%**, the largest single-commodity move, reflecting demand destruction rather than supply shock. Copper bucked the risk-off pattern, rising **1.93%**, creating a divergence within the commodity complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**

Crude's decline strips out geopolitical premium and exposes softer underlying demand—a signal that reaches MCX when converted through USD/INR at **₹95.38**, pushing import parity lower for refineries. Gold's persistence at elevated levels despite rupee strength (which normally pressures INR-denominated gold) suggests real-yield compression is driving safe-haven demand, not currency mechanics alone. Copper's countermovement signals industrial-demand resilience, contradicting the broad risk-off narrative: this divergence is material because it separates cyclical demand from macro hedging flows.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**

A refiner managing FY27 crude purchase contracts faces narrowed hedging spreads as Brent-to-MCX basis tightens with today's move. A jewellery manufacturer holding forward-sale commitments must now assess whether gold's safe-haven floor will hold through Monday's MCX open or retreat if equity markets stabilize. A power utility evaluating natural gas procurement will recalibrate take-or-pay obligations against the **6.30%** intraday collapse.

**BOTTOM LINE**

Today's data shows safe-haven demand (gold +1.1%, crude –2.86%) decoupling from industrial demand (copper +1.93%), indicating macro uncertainty is pricing in without affecting core manufacturing expectations. The **₹288.40/mmBtu** natural gas flush is structural, not cyclical—it tracks supply overhang, not geopolitical risk.

**WHAT TO WATCH**

Monday MCX open settlement in crude below **₹6,850/bbl** and copper holding above **₹1,290/kg** would confirm the divergence. US inflation data (CPI release timing TBD) will determine whether real-yield compression justifies gold's floor.
