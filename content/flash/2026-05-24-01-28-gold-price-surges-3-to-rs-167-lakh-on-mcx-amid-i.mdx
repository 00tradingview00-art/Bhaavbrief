**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged 3% to ₹1,67,000/10g driven by safe-haven demand as the Israel-Iran conflict escalated, while live COMEX spot is currently anchored at $4,523/oz (₹1,60,047/10g MCX equivalent).

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (May contract) are the primary instruments affected, with gold trading at a premium of approximately ₹6,953/10g above the current COMEX-derived INR equivalent — a spread that reflects customs duty (currently 6% basic + AIDC), GST, and import levies baked into domestic pricing. The geopolitical risk premium has widened this basis beyond its typical structural range, signaling that domestic physical demand and duty-adjusted import costs are amplifying the COMEX move rather than simply tracking it. For Indian traders, the critical level to monitor is ₹1,60,047/10g — the raw COMEX-to-INR conversion at USD/INR 95.70 — as any compression of the current premium toward this level indicates basis unwinding. MCX Silver at ₹2,57,896/kg similarly carries an elevated geopolitical bid layered on top of its industrial demand floor.

**WATCH**
Monitor the next U.S. Fed speakers (scheduled this week) and any de-escalation signals from the Israel-Iran theater — a ceasefire development or hawkish Fed commentary will be the primary test of whether gold holds above ₹1,65,000/10g on MCX.
