**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 9719 INR/bbl at 03:03 pm IST, posting a +2.33% gain as USD/INR strengthened to 95.22 INR (+0.22% vs prev close). The rupee's continued depreciation has restored export competitiveness for domestic crude producers, extending the rally seen overnight. Natural gas climbed +1.21% to 291.9 INR/mmBtu, while gold and silver remained subdued at 144078 and 195611 INR respectively.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sustained rupee weakness is driving crude higher despite global price consolidation. For MCX futures traders, this FX-led rally signals strong export margin support for domestic producers. The move keeps crude bid above 9500 INR/bbl and positions the contract within the uptrend established since the 9493 level at 12:51 am IST yesterday.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor 9750–9800 INR/bbl resistance and USD/INR stability above 95.15 INR. A break toward 9850 INR would confirm momentum; a dip below 9650 INR would suggest profit-taking has begun.
