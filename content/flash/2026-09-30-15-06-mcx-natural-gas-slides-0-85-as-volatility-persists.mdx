**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas retreated to 291.2 INR/mmBtu at 03:06 pm IST, extending a -0.85% decline from the previous close. The slip reflects continued weakness in a volatile 24-hour cycle that has seen prices oscillate between 288.4 INR/mmBtu (14:00 IST yesterday) and current levels. MCX Crude weakness at 8637 INR/bbl (-0.47%) and steady USD/INR at 96.05 provide headwinds.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The retreat to 291.2 levels keeps natural gas pinned in a narrow band bounded by yesterday's 288.4 recovery high. For MCX futures traders, this signals continued consolidation rather than directional conviction. Cross-asset weakness in crude and muted rupee movement suggest energy sector malaise is broad-based, limiting upside catalysts.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break below 288.4 INR/mmBtu (yesterday's intraday low) would confirm downside momentum; hold above it signals continued range-bound trading into the overnight session.
