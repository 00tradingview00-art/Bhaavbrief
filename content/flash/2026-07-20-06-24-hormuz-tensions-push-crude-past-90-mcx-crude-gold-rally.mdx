**WHAT HAPPENED**
The US conducted its ninth consecutive night of strikes against Iranian targets as tensions escalate around the Strait of Hormuz, with **Brent crude surging past $90 per barrel** on supply disruption fears.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Geopolitical risk premium is embedding itself into crude valuations as the world's most critical oil chokepoint faces operational uncertainty—**HPCL and BPCL's import procurement teams must now budget rupee costs at elevated WTI/Brent baselines**, while refiners' margin calculations shift downward on costlier feedstock. Simultaneously, **gold bullion dealers and jewelry manufacturers** see dollar-denominated inventory costs rise as safe-haven flows push the precious metal higher, while **natural gas traders** face volatility from energy security reassessments across Asian LNG contracts.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Refinery operators at HPCL's Vizag and Bathinda units, alongside BPCL's Mumbai facility, face immediate crude procurement repricing on fresh cargo nominations, while aviation fuel desks at **IndiGo and Air India** must recalibrate hedging positions against dollar-denominated jet fuel benchmarks. Petrol pump operators across coastal Maharashtra and Gujarat absorb landed-cost increases at the retail forecourt, while households managing LPG cylinder refills and small industries dependent on energy inputs see working capital pressures intensify.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Downstream fuel retailers see gross margin compression as **crude import costs** climb faster than regulated retail prices can adjust. **MCX Crude Oil futures** are signaling sustained support above the **$85-per-barrel floor**, with resistance forming at **$92 levels**. Petrol and diesel pump prices across Tier-2 cities will track this crude repricing within 10-15 trading sessions, creating cost-push headwinds for logistics and transport operators.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **OPEC+ emergency statements** and **US-Iran diplomatic channels** for ceasefire signals; sustained strikes beyond 72 hours without dialogue will test **MCX Crude's $95 ceiling**.
