**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold on MCX rose 0.31% intraday while COMEX spot gold crossed the $4,523/oz level, with the article referencing a $5,000 COMEX milestone being cited in retail investment commentary.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) is the primary instrument affected, with the live COMEX rate of $4,523/oz translating to approximately ₹1,60,047/10g at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. The 0.31% MCX move reflects direct pass-through of dollar-denominated COMEX price action amplified by rupee conversion mechanics — any further INR depreciation widens the spread further in rupee terms without COMEX moving at all. Indian traders carry an additional cost layer: the 15% basic customs duty plus 3% IGST on gold imports means domestic physical and futures prices structurally trade at a premium to raw COMEX-converted values. The MCX-COMEX spread compression or expansion at this price level is the key arbitrage variable traders are monitoring as import economics tighten.

**WATCH**
The next US Federal Reserve meeting minutes release and US CPI print are the critical triggers — a softer inflation reading or dovish Fed tone directly pressures dollar strength, which simultaneously moves both COMEX gold and USD/INR, creating a compounded effect on MCX pricing. A COMEX close above $4,550/oz confirms continuation; a reversal below $4,480/oz negates the current momentum.
