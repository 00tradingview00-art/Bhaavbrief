**WHAT HAPPENED**
Indian equity markets, gold, and silver are moving in correlated upward direction simultaneously, driven by a weakening US dollar and global risk-on sentiment pushing capital into multiple asset classes at once.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver are both directly affected, with spot gold currently at ₹160,047/10g and silver at ₹257,896/kg reflecting the combined effect of elevated COMEX prices and USD/INR at ₹95.70. The rupee at this level adds a significant import cost premium — every 50 paise depreciation in INR adds approximately ₹800-900/10g to MCX gold's landed cost, meaning the COMEX-to-MCX spread remains structurally wide. For Indian traders, this correlation event is mechanically explained by dollar weakness simultaneously lifting rupee-denominated commodity prices and equity valuations — not a fundamental supply-demand shift in metals. Customs duty on gold at 15% (inclusive of GST and agricultural cess) keeps MCX at a persistent premium to raw COMEX conversion, so any dollar reversal compresses MCX prices faster than COMEX declines.

**WATCH**
The next US Federal Reserve policy statement and US CPI print are the critical triggers — a hotter-than-expected inflation reading strengthens the dollar and breaks this three-way correlation immediately. Monitor USD/INR at the ₹95.00 support level; a breach below signals further MCX price elevation across both metals.
