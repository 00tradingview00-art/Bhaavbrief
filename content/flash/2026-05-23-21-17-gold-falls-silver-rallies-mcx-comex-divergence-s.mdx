**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX-COMEX divergence in Gold and Silver triggered fresh ETF rebalancing activity, with Gold declining while Silver rallied — COMEX Silver currently at $76.20/oz translating to approximately ₹257,896/kg on MCX.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (₹160,047/10g) and MCX Silver contracts are both directly affected by this divergence event. The Gold-Silver ratio compression visible in current COMEX pricing — Gold at $4,523/oz versus Silver at $76.20/oz — is driving arbitrage flows that ripple into Indian ETF and futures positioning. For Indian traders, the landed import cost of Silver is further elevated by the 15% customs duty structure, meaning MCX Silver's premium over COMEX is structurally wider than the USD/INR rate of ₹95.70 alone suggests. ETF rebalancing on the domestic side amplifies intraday volatility in MCX Silver specifically, as fund managers adjust Silver exposure to close the MCX-COMEX spread.

**WATCH**
Monitor the MCX Silver contract for a sustained hold above the ₹257,896/kg level — a breakdown below signals ETF selling pressure has overwhelmed the COMEX rally transmission. The next COMEX options expiry date and any RBI intervention near the ₹95.70 USD/INR handle will either widen or compress the MCX-COMEX basis further.
