**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver on COMEX has surged to $76.20/oz, approaching record highs and translating to approximately ₹257,896/kg on MCX at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver continuous contract bears the direct impact of this COMEX rally amplified by rupee conversion mechanics — at ₹95.70 per dollar, every $1 move in COMEX silver adds roughly ₹3,385/kg to MCX settlement. Indian traders face an additional import cost layer: India levies a 10% basic customs duty plus GST on silver imports, meaning the landed cost of physical silver runs materially above the raw COMEX-to-INR conversion of ₹257,896/kg. The COMEX-MCX spread currently reflects this duty structure, and any rupee depreciation beyond ₹95.70 widens that spread further, increasing the premium Indian buyers pay over global benchmarks. Industrial silver demand from India's solar panel and electronics manufacturing sectors reinforces physical buying support at current MCX levels.

**WATCH**
The U.S. Federal Reserve's next policy commentary and the upcoming U.S. CPI data release are the primary triggers — a hotter-than-expected inflation print sustains silver's safe-haven and industrial demand narrative, while a dovish pivot signal pressures the dollar and provides additional rupee-denominated upside. Monitor whether MCX Silver holds the ₹257,000/kg support level on intraday pullbacks.
