**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude dropped to 8692 INR/bbl at 09:39 pm IST, posting a -2.46% decline after a sharp intraday reversal. The rupee strengthened to 96.27 INR (-0.14% vs prev close), unwinding the USD/INR weakness that had underpinned the afternoon rally to 9197 INR/bbl at 03:09 pm IST. Natural gas also weakened -1.70% to 294.9 INR/mmBtu, signaling broad energy sector pullback.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The collapse of export-linked rupee depreciation support has triggered profit-taking across the crude complex. The 505 INR pullback from today's peak exposes the fragility of the USD/INR-driven rally and suggests traders are now pricing in mean reversion toward 8600–8700 INR/bbl. Cross-asset weakness in silver (-0.55%) and natural gas indicates risk-off sentiment.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8650 INR/bbl support level—roughly where the initial profit-taking exhaustion began—will be critical. A break below this zone signals deeper capitulation; a hold suggests consolidation before the next directional print.
