**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas declined to 264.5 INR/mmBtu at 03:06 am IST, posting a -0.08% loss from the previous close. The slip extends renewed softness across the energy complex, mirroring MCX Crude's -1.19% retreat to 9200 INR/bbl. USD/INR strengthened to 94.62 INR (-0.45%), offsetting some domestic currency headwinds. Precious metals also retreated: MCX Gold fell -0.33% to 146041 INR/10g and MCX Silver dropped -1.15% to 209049 INR/kg, signaling broad-based risk-off sentiment.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Natural Gas traders face a critical juncture after yesterday's volatile +3.64% spike to 313.2 INR/mmBtu at 03:29 am IST. The current pullback to 264.5 INR/mmBtu suggests profit-taking from upper band extremes (288.4–313.1 INR/mmBtu range). Concurrent crude weakness and muted rupee strength indicate energy traders are reassessing near-term demand assumptions.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support emerges at the 260 INR/mmBtu psychological level. A break below triggers deeper retest toward 283.2 INR/mmBtu (recent session low at 03:03 am IST). Crude momentum remains the primary cross-commodity anchor.
