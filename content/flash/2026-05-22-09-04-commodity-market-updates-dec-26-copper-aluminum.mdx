**WHAT HAPPENED**
Copper hit a record high on COMEX at $6.3607/lb while aluminum also reached all-time highs, with WTI crude futures advancing to $97.49/bbl amid escalating geopolitical tensions.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Copper and Crude Oil contracts face direct upward pressure from these COMEX and WTI benchmarks. At the current USD/INR rate of ₹96.21, MCX Copper's import cost conversion amplifies the COMEX move — every 1-cent rise in COMEX copper translates to approximately ₹2.12/kg on MCX after applying the standard 2.5% basic customs duty plus GST loading. WTI at $97.49/bbl converts to approximately ₹9,567/bbl on MCX, placing the domestic crude contract at a critical psychological resistance zone near ₹9,600. Indian refiners and industrial copper consumers tracking physical procurement costs face materially higher landed prices at these currency-adjusted levels, widening the effective spread between hedged and unhedged positions.

**WATCH**
The U.S. EIA weekly crude inventory report and any further geopolitical developments in active conflict zones will either sustain the crude rally above the ₹9,600 MCX level or trigger a corrective pullback. For copper, a close above $6.40/lb on COMEX confirms the breakout and sets the next MCX repricing threshold.
