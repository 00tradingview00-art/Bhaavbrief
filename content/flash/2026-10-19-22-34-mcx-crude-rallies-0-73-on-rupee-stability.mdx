**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rose to 9463 INR/bbl at 10:34 pm IST, posting a +0.73% gain as USD/INR steadied at 95.05 INR (-0.02% vs prev close). The rupee's renewed stability has restored export competitiveness for domestic crude after the earlier afternoon weakness that had pushed prices to 9275 INR/bbl at 02:15 pm IST. Gold and silver continue their modest downside at 147207 INR/10g and 209625 INR/kg respectively.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
This recovery caps a volatile session where MCX Crude swung between 9275 and 9567 INR/bbl, tracking rupee oscillations. The current 9463 level represents consolidation within that range. For MCX futures traders, rupee stability is now the primary price driver—each paisa movement in USD/INR carries outsized crude impact on export margins and competitive positioning versus global benchmarks.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9500 INR/bbl resistance level holds the key to momentum continuation. A breach above it signals renewed strength; failure to hold signals a retest of the 9275 support zone established at 02:15 pm IST.
