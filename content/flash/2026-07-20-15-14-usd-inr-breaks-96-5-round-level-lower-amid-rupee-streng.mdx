**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR crossed below the 96.5 round-number threshold, trading at 96.4 INR as of 03:14 pm IST on a -0.26% decline from the previous close. The breach reflects continued rupee appreciation against the dollar. Concurrent MCX moves show gold at 141828 INR/10g (+0.65%), silver at 219240 INR/kg (+1.31%), and copper at 1312.3 INR/kg (+0.76%), signaling broad-based strength in rupee-priced commodities as the currency firms.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A stronger rupee compresses rupee-denominated commodity valuations relative to dollar benchmarks, creating headwinds for export-linked MCX positions. The 96.5 round level breach is a technically significant pivot; sustained weakness below it may signal broader INR strength that could suppress crude (currently 7893 INR/bbl, -0.65%) and natural gas (274.9 INR/mmBtu, -2.41%) despite flat to bullish global prices.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor USD/INR support at 96.25. A hold above that level would stabilize rupee-priced commodity valuations; a breach toward 96.0 would extend rupee appreciation and risk further downside pressure on crude and precious metals futures.
