**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas collapsed to 299.5 INR/mmBtu at 04:18 IST, posting a -3.57% decline from the previous close. The sharp drop extends a volatile downtrend that has seen prices oscillate between 288.4 INR/mmBtu (14:00 IST yesterday) and current levels. USD/INR's +0.28% rally to 96.39 INR amplifies import cost pressures on rupee-denominated futures, while MCX Crude's modest +0.21% hold at 8930 INR/bbl signals broader energy weakness.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
This 3.57% dip signals technical breakdown below key consolidation zones established over the past 24 hours. For MCX futures traders, rupee appreciation is acting as a structural headwind, pushing Natural Gas toward lower support levels even as global energy demand remains contested. The cross-commodity weakness across Silver (-3.11%) and Copper (-1.04%) reinforces risk-off sentiment.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
280 INR/mmBtu level — a psychological floor tested in recent sessions. Break below this support could unlock further weakness toward 274.5 INR/mmBtu, the overnight low from 22:29 IST yesterday.
