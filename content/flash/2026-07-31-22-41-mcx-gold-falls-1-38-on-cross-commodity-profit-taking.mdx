**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold retreated to 141250 INR/10g at 22:41 IST, declining 1.38% from the previous close. The pullback reverses the momentum that had driven the contract to 143298 INR/10g on 23:24 IST yesterday. Silver fell harder at 216193 INR/kg (-1.72%), while Crude climbed +1.08% to 8124 INR/bbl. The rupee weakened to 95.37 INR, offering minor support to gold but insufficient to counter broader profit-taking across precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX traders, this correction signals consolidation after yesterday's sharp rally. The 1.38% decline mirrors silver's steeper 1.72% fall, suggesting precious metals are shedding speculative length. Crude's resilience (+1.08%) and copper's modest gain (+0.27%) indicate selective profit-taking rather than risk-off panic, keeping the broader commodity complex stable.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support at 140500 INR/10g will validate whether this is a healthy pullback or the start of deeper retracement toward 139500. A break below 140500 threatens yesterday's 143000+ high conviction.
