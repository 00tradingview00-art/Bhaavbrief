**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver outpaced gold in the latest global precious metals rally, with COMEX silver at $76.20/oz driving MCX silver to approximately ₹257,896/kg as ETF inflows accelerated on both COMEX and MCX platforms.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (continuous contract) is the primary contract under pressure, with the spot reference sitting at ₹257,896/kg — a level that reflects both the COMEX move and the USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. The gold-silver ratio compression means silver is outperforming MCX Gold, currently anchored near ₹160,047/10g. For Indian traders, silver imports carry a 10% basic customs duty plus GST, meaning the landed cost premium over COMEX remains structurally elevated — any further rupee weakness amplifies this spread automatically. Rising MCX ETF volumes signal institutional participation is increasing, which tightens the basis between MCX futures and spot but also raises margin requirements during high-volatility sessions.

**WATCH**
The next confirmed directional signal comes from the weekly COMEX Commitments of Traders (COT) report — a further increase in managed money net longs above current levels confirms the rally has institutional depth. A breach and close above ₹260,000/kg on MCX Silver is the key technical level that either validates or stalls this momentum.
