**WHAT HAPPENED**
The gold-silver ratio has surged to 65, meaning 65 ounces of silver are required to purchase one ounce of gold, as US-Iran military escalation drives safe-haven flows disproportionately into gold over silver.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are both directly affected, with gold outperforming silver in relative terms. At current COMEX prices — gold at $4,535/oz translating to approximately ₹1,60,440/10g and silver at $77.00/oz translating to approximately ₹2,60,590/kg on MCX — the ratio divergence signals institutional rotation into gold as the primary geopolitical hedge. Indian traders carrying long silver positions face spread compression risk, as the ratio expansion historically reverts only when either a geopolitical resolution lifts risk appetite or industrial silver demand accelerates. With USD/INR at ₹95.69, import cost pressure on both metals remains elevated, keeping MCX premiums structurally firm against COMEX benchmarks even as the gold-silver ratio widens.

**WATCH**
Any formal US-Iran ceasefire announcement or escalation into direct military engagement will be the binary trigger — ceasefire narrows the ratio by lifting silver, further escalation extends gold's outperformance. Track Thursday's US CPI print, which will determine dollar trajectory and recalibrate both MCX contract levels simultaneously.
