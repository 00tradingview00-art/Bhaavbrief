**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 151216 INR/10g at 07:20 pm IST, posting a -0.98% loss from the previous close. The pullback reverses the +1.42% recovery rally seen at 23:47 IST when gold hit 152950 INR/10g. USD/INR edged up to 95.71 INR (+0.13%), eroding rupee support for bullion. Silver tracked weakness at -1.59% to 236074 INR/kg, while crude added +2.14% to 8828 INR/bbl.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's breakdown below the 152950 resistance established overnight signals softening safe-haven demand amid a firmer dollar. At 151216, traders face renewed pressure near the 150924 support tested at 19:22 IST yesterday. Cross-asset weakness in silver (-1.59%) and strength in crude (+2.14%) suggest risk appetite is returning, limiting bullion's upside.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
150924 INR/10g support level — a break below here validates further downside toward 150500. Hold above this point signals consolidation within the overnight 150924–152950 range.
