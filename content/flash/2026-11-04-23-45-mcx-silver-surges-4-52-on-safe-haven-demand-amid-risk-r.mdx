**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver rallied to 187812 INR/kg at 11:45 pm IST, posting a +4.52% gain from the previous close. The bullion complex is bid across the board—MCX Gold climbed +2.69% to 143203 INR/10g while MCX Copper edged +0.25% to 1368.8 INR/kg. Crude's sharp −7.80% collapse to 8394 INR/bbl signals renewed risk-off sentiment, triggering a flight to precious metals. USD/INR weakened −0.16% to 94.79, offering tailwinds for rupee-denominated bullion.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's outperformance versus gold (+2.69%) reflects industrial safe-haven demand alongside monetary easing expectations. The crude crash signals demand destruction and geopolitical de-escalation fears, anchoring real yields lower and boosting nominal precious metal prices. For MCX traders, this cross-asset repricing suggests sustained bullion strength if crude stays under pressure and equities remain volatile.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 190000 INR/kg psychological resistance level. A close above it would confirm the rally's durability; a retreat toward 185000 INR/kg would signal profit-taking into the crude-induced bounce.
