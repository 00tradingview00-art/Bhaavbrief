**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold climbed to ₹1,57,000/10g while MCX Silver surged nearly 2% intraday as Federal Reserve officials signaled internal disagreement over the pace and timing of future rate cuts.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold August and Silver September contracts are directly in focus. Gold's move toward ₹1,57,000/10g sits below the live COMEX-derived fair value of ₹1,60,946/10g, indicating the domestic contract is trading at a discount to COMEX parity — a spread that narrows when rupee weakens or import premiums expand. With USD/INR at ₹96.21, any further rupee depreciation mechanically lifts MCX landed cost without any move in COMEX. Silver's 2% surge aligns with COMEX Silver at $76.24/oz, translating to a theoretical MCX level of ₹2,59,410/kg — traders tracking the domestic contract must account for 15% basic customs duty plus GST, which widens the absolute rupee gap versus COMEX on any dollar move upward.

**WATCH**
The next Federal Reserve meeting minutes release and any scheduled Fed speaker appearances will either confirm the rate-cut split narrative or signal renewed consensus — a hawkish lean compresses gold's safe-haven premium immediately. Monitor MCX Gold's ability to hold ₹1,57,000/10g as near-term structural support on the active contract.
