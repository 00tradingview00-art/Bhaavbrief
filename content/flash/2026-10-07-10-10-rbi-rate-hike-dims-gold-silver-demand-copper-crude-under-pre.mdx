**WHAT HAPPENED**
RBI raises repo rate in 2026, pushing **real yields higher** and strengthening the **rupee**, reducing the attractiveness of rupee-denominated commodity hedges and increasing import costs for domestic end-users.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Higher real yields increase the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets like **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver**, dampening demand from domestic investors, ETF buyers, and RBI's own reserve accumulation. A stronger rupee simultaneously makes **MCX Crude** and **MCX Copper** cheaper for Indian importers in rupee terms, but signals weakening global growth expectations—a risk-off signal that typically pressures energy and industrial metals as Chinese construction activity may soften. Gold and silver face structural headwinds from yield competition; crude and copper face cyclical demand concerns despite rupee tailwinds.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Bullion importers and refineries hedging dollar exposure see reduced cost advantage from rupee strength, but jewellery manufacturers and D2C gold retailers face margin compression as consumer demand for discretionary purchases—weddings, festivals—typically contracts when borrowing costs rise for household EMIs and working capital. Automotive component suppliers, copper wire fabricators, and industrial motor manufacturers sourcing on **MCX Copper** benefit from lower rupee input costs short-term, but reduced construction orders in India and China mean lower orders flow backward through supply chains. Retail petrol and diesel consumers see pump prices stabilise or ease in rupee terms, but this relief masks underlying crude weakness; shipping companies and logistics operators experience margin relief on fuel surcharges.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Bullion jewellers carrying finished-goods inventory face demand erosion as higher EMI costs deter household purchases, directly compressing retail margins. **MCX Gold** will trade pressure from yield competition while **MCX Crude** faces cyclical softness despite rupee cushioning. Consumers purchasing jewellery ahead of year-end celebrations encounter stable or declining prices, while petrol users see modest pump relief from rupee strength masking crude weakness.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
RBI's next monetary policy communication and Q3 India PMI manufacturing data—weakness below **50** would steepen the copper and crude downdraft.
