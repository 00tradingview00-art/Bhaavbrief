**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver jumped to 223300 INR/kg at 12:35 pm IST, marking a +2.24% move from the previous close. The outperformance versus gold (+1.14%) and the broader commodity complex signals a rotation toward silver's industrial-cum-safe-haven appeal. Copper also advanced +1.10% to 1328.6 INR/kg, while crude remained tepid at 7958 INR/bbl (+0.11%). The rupee held relatively steady at 96.23 INR, limiting currency headwinds to the precious metals complex.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's beat relative to gold suggests traders are pricing in stronger real demand alongside inflation hedging. For MCX futures traders, this reflects a tactical rebalancing within the precious metals space—silver's lower absolute price point and dual-use narrative (industrial + monetary) are attracting fresh positioning. The copper co-move reinforces a broader risk-on undertone in commodities despite muted crude action.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 223000 round level is now live support. A close above 224000 INR/kg would confirm conviction; failure to hold 222500 would suggest profit-taking into the move.
