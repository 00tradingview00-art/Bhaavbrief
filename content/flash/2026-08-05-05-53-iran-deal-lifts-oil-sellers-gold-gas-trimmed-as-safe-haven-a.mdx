**WHAT HAPPENED**
The US signalled an imminent agreement with Iran on nuclear restrictions, prompting markets to price in near-term oil supply risk relief and triggering sharp declines across **crude oil, natural gas, and gold futures** on MCX.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks face immediate downward repricing on **Brent-linked crude** as deal completion removes geopolitical premium embedded in current WTI levels, directly compressing per-barrel acquisition costs on fresh tenders. Bullion dealers and jewellery importers holding **dollar-denominated gold inventory** experience rupee-denominated stock values contract as safe-haven demand unwinds, eroding working capital on unsold finished goods. **Natural gas sourcing contracts** tied to global LNG benchmarks reset lower as risk-off positioning reverses, reducing spot premium paid by power utilities on month-to-month purchases.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Refinery margin books widen immediately as input crude costs compress, while downstream diesel and petrol blending margins face fresh pressure as retail pump pricing begins tracking the softer crude reality. FMCG logistics operators—running bulk diesel fleets for distribution networks—see fuel procurement costs ease within 7–10 days, though most have already locked in weekly contracts at higher levels. Gold jewellery retailers and organised bullion platforms clear inventory at firmer rupee valuations before margin compression fully settles, while casual retail buyers deferring festival-season purchases now face lower absolute prices per gram, making discretionary ornament spending more accessible across Tier 2–3 towns.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Refinery operators will see per-barrel acquisition costs fall by **$2–4 range** if the deal closes, translating to margin expansion on crude-to-fuel conversion before retail pricing fully catches up. **MCX Crude Oil** is signalling a breakdown below the **$78–80 level** as the probabilistic risk of supply disruption evaporates. Retail consumers buying petrol, diesel, or gold jewellery will pay lower absolute prices within a **2–3 week window** as dealer inventory resets to new floor valuations.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Confirmation of formal Iran nuclear pact signing and JCPOA re-entry timeline announcement will consolidate these losses; conversely, any US negotiation setback or Israeli regional escalation would immediately reverse crude's downside momentum and restore safe-haven gold demand.
