**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver climbed to 245585 INR/kg at 10:20 IST, posting a +0.96% gain from the previous close. The advance extends an intraday recovery that lifted silver to 240346 INR/kg at 09:25 IST. Broad-based commodity strength underpins the move: MCX Gold rallied +0.48% to 160186 INR/10g, MCX Copper advanced +0.86% to 1383.5 INR/kg, and MCX Natural Gas gained +0.65% to 262.8 INR/mmBtu, while USD/INR strengthened +0.23% to 95.72.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver is holding above the 240346 INR/kg level established at 09:25 IST, suggesting consolidation support in the recovery phase. The +0.96% move sits comfortably below the +1.50% spike registered earlier, signaling a steadier advance. For MCX futures traders, this reflects risk-on positioning across the precious metals complex, with gold and silver moving in sync.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 246000 INR/kg round number looms as the immediate resistance. A breach would confirm momentum; failure to hold would test support at 240346 INR/kg.
