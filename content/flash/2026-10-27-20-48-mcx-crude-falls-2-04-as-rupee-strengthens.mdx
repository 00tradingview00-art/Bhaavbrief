**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude slipped to 9525 INR/bbl at 08:48 pm IST, posting a -2.04% loss as USD/INR strengthened to 95.05 INR (−0.15% vs prev close). The rupee's appreciation has compressed export margins for domestic crude producers after the earlier overnight rally. Natural gas declined 1.51% to 287.6 INR/mmBtu, while gold and copper posted modest gains of +0.67% and +0.79% respectively.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The pullback signals profit-taking after the +3.10% overnight rally to 9493 INR/bbl. A stronger rupee erodes crude's export competitiveness, pressuring near-term momentum. MCX crude traders face crosswinds: rupee strength weighs on margins, but the 246 INR drop from the 9771 high remains contained, suggesting support interest persists around current levels.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9450 INR support level — established during the 01:03 am IST consolidation — will confirm whether this -2.04% move is corrective or signals fresh weakness below the overnight range.
