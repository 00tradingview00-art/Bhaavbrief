**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper fell to 1371.65 INR/kg at 08:18 pm IST, declining −0.84% from the previous close. The pullback follows intraday volatility that saw copper peak at 1393.05 INR/kg at 09:36 am IST (+1.07%). USD/INR strength at 95.67 (+0.23%) weighed on rupee-denominated metals, while silver fell −1.66% to 234100 INR/kg and gold declined −0.52% to 155125 INR/10g. Natural gas gained +1.28% to 261.2 INR/mmBtu, signaling selective sector rotation.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's intraday reversal—from +1.34% at 03:37 pm IST to −0.84% by late evening—signals weakening momentum despite earlier strength. The rupee's climb is pressuring all base metals. For MCX futures traders, this suggests demand-side hesitation; the previous session's multi-leg rally across precious metals complex did not sustain copper participation into close.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 1370–1372 INR/kg support band. A hold above 1370 keeps the 1393 intraday peak in range; breach below 1368 confirms breakdown toward the −1.07% move trough from 07:25 pm IST.
