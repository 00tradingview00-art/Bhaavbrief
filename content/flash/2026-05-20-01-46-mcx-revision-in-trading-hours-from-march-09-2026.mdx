MCX will revise its trading hours effective March 09, 2026, impacting the exchange's session timings across commodity segments.

This structural change affects all actively traded MCX contracts — including crude oil, natural gas, gold, silver, and base metals such as copper and aluminium. Revised session windows directly influence when price discovery occurs, aligning or misaligning Indian market hours with international benchmarks like COMEX and NYMEX. Any shift in evening session closing times is particularly critical for energy and metal traders, as global cues from US markets are absorbed during late-hour Indian trading. Participants will need to recalibrate hedging windows and margin obligations accordingly.
