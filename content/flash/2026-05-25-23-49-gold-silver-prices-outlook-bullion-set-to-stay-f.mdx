**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver prices are holding firm ahead of the Federal Reserve's May meeting minutes release, with COMEX gold at $4,604/oz and silver at $78.37/oz, as markets await policy signals that analysts expect will moderate the pace of bullion's recent rally.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) is directly exposed to this consolidation phase, with the live import parity level sitting at approximately ₹1,62,115/10g — a historically elevated level amplified by USD/INR at ₹95.24. MCX Silver tracks COMEX at roughly ₹2,63,979/kg, meaning Indian traders are carrying full currency-risk premium with no INR depreciation buffer at current spot. India's 15% customs duty on gold imports remains embedded in domestic MCX pricing, keeping the MCX-COMEX spread structurally wide; any INR weakness from a hawkish Fed reading widens import costs further and lifts MCX independently of COMEX direction. Traders holding long positions in either contract face a mechanical headwind if the Fed minutes signal a higher-for-longer rate stance, which historically pressures non-yielding bullion in dollar terms.

**WATCH**
The Federal Reserve May meeting minutes release is the immediate binary trigger — a hawkish tone confirming prolonged elevated rates will test COMEX gold's $4,604 support level and corresponding MCX levels. A dovish or neutral read sustains the current firm bias.
