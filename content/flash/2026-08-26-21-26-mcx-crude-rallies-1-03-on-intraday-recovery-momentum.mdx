**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rebounded to 7918 INR/bbl at 21:26 IST, posting a +1.03% gain from previous close. The move reverses the sharp −2.28% pullback to 7658 INR/bbl logged at 09:30 IST and recaptures ground lost across the morning session. Natural gas strength at +2.92% and rupee weakness (USDINR at 95.4, −0.34%) provide marginal tailwinds to energy complex recovery, though precious metals (gold −0.97%, silver −1.21%) signal persistent cross-asset caution.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rebound from intraday lows suggests exhaustion of the −3.13% downside momentum that prevailed at 16:27 IST. For MCX crude traders, this recovery tests whether the 7900 zone holds as a pivot or represents another false bottom in a volatile session marked by four distinct swings between 7658 and prior highs.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Resistance at 7950 INR/bbl. Break above confirms intraday reversal; failure below 7880 INR/bbl resets bearish momentum established earlier.
