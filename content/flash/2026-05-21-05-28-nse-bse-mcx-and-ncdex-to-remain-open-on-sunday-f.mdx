NSE, BSE, MCX, and NCDEX will hold a special trading session on Sunday, February 1, 2026, to accommodate real-time market reaction to the Union Budget announcement.

On MCX, all actively traded commodities — including gold, silver, crude oil, and base metals — will see live price discovery immediately after Budget proposals are revealed. Traders will be able to respond instantly to any changes in customs duty, import tariffs, or sector-specific allocations that directly affect commodity input costs and demand outlook. Budget-driven duty shifts on gold and silver imports, or energy taxation changes, typically trigger sharp intraday moves on MCX within minutes of announcement.
