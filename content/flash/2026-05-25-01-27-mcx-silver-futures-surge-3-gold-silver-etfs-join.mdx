**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver futures surged 3%, with Gold and Silver ETFs joining the rally as COMEX Silver trades at $76.20/oz, translating to approximately ₹257,896/kg on MCX.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (near-month contract) is the primary instrument affected, with upward price pressure driven by the COMEX rally transmitting directly through the USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. At current COMEX levels, the INR-converted base for MCX Silver sits at ₹257,896/kg — any further USD/INR appreciation amplifies landed cost for Indian importers, widening the import parity spread. India levies a 15% customs duty plus GST on silver imports, meaning physical import costs run materially above COMEX-equivalent levels, supporting domestic futures premiums during rallies. MCX Gold (₹160,047/10g) is also seeing sympathetic buying, with the Gold-Silver ratio compression at current COMEX prices acting as an additional technical catalyst for silver outperformance.

**WATCH**
The next critical trigger is the U.S. Federal Reserve's upcoming interest rate commentary and weekly COMEX Commitment of Traders (COT) report — a sustained move above $78/oz on COMEX Silver confirms bullish momentum, while a reversal below $74/oz negates the current breakout structure and resets MCX Silver toward ₹250,000/kg support.
