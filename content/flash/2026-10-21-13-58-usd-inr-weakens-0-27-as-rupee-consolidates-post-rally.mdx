**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 94.79 INR at 01:58 pm IST, marking a -0.27% pullback from the previous close. The rupee's renewed strength reflects profit-taking after the intraday peak of 97.07 INR at 03:40 pm IST, when foreign portfolio inflows had driven dollar strength. Precious metals—MCX_GOLD at 145999 INR/10g (-0.35%) and MCX_SILVER at 209035 INR/kg (-1.16%)—track the softer dollar, while crude oil remains resilient at 9374 INR/bbl (+0.68%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX traders, rupee consolidation after a 68 paisa rally reduces hedging urgency on dollar-denominated commodities. The inverse correlation between USD/INR weakness and gold/silver strength tightens positioning in precious metals futures. Dollar weakness also undercuts crude's upside despite WTI gains, creating divergence risk for energy traders holding cross-commodity spreads.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 94.75–95.00 INR band. A break below 94.75 signals sustained rupee strength; a rebound toward 95.40 INR would confirm the 97.07 peak remains contested.
