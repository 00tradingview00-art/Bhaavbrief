**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold prices on MCX retreated from an intraday high after opening positively, with silver also trading volatile, tracking international spot prices near the COMEX benchmark of $4,523/oz (~₹160,047/10g on MCX).

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary instruments affected by this intraday reversal. Gold's pullback from early session highs reflects profit-booking pressure at elevated levels, with the MCX reference price of ₹160,047/10g acting as a near-term resistance zone for domestic traders. Indian import costs remain elevated given USD/INR at ₹95.70 — every rupee of currency weakness adds approximately ₹320–₹330 per 10g to landed gold costs after applying the 15% basic customs duty plus GST, keeping the MCX-COMEX spread structurally wide. Silver's volatility at ₹257,896/kg on MCX mirrors COMEX indecision at $76.20/oz, with industrial demand signals from global manufacturing data creating two-way price risk for Indian traders holding positional silver contracts.

**WATCH**
The next directional trigger is the US Federal Reserve's upcoming commentary on interest rate trajectory — a hawkish tone strengthens the dollar and pressures both metals on COMEX, which transmits directly to MCX within the same session. A breach above ₹161,500/10g on MCX Gold or a break below ₹158,000 will confirm whether the intraday reversal extends or reverses.
