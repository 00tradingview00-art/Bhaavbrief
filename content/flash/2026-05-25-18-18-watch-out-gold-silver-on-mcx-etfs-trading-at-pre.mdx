**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold and Silver contracts, along with domestic ETFs, are currently trading at a measurable premium to prevailing international spot rates after conversion at the live USD/INR rate of ₹95.70.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (nearby contract) is benchmarked against the COMEX-derived import parity of approximately ₹162,826/10g — any premium above this level reflects either rupee demand pressure, ETF inflow-driven buying, or a lag in domestic price adjustment. MCX Silver at ₹264,499/kg faces the same dynamic, where domestic premiums above import parity signal stronger physical or financial demand than international markets justify. For Indian traders, the landed cost of gold includes a 15% basic customs duty plus GST and AIDC levies, structurally embedding a floor premium into MCX prices versus COMEX — so the current premium above even that adjusted level indicates additional domestic demand factors at work. Spread traders monitoring the MCX-COMEX basis must account for this elevated premium as a potential mean-reversion risk if rupee strengthens or international prices pull back sharply.

**WATCH**
Monitor the next RBI foreign exchange intervention data and COMEX gold settlement tonight — a sustained COMEX rally above $4,620/oz or rupee depreciation beyond ₹96.00 will validate the premium, while any reversal in either compresses the domestic spread rapidly.
