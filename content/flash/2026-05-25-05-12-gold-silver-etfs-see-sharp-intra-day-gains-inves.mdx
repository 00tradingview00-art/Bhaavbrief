**WHAT HAPPENED**
Gold and silver ETFs recorded sharp intra-day gains as US-Iran geopolitical tensions triggered safe-haven demand, pushing COMEX Gold to $4,581/oz and COMEX Silver to $79.19/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (current contract) and MCX Silver are the directly affected contracts, with both tracking COMEX upside amplified by the USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. At current COMEX levels, MCX Gold translates to approximately ₹1,62,083/10g — a level that represents a significant import cost benchmark for domestic refiners factoring in India's 15% customs duty plus GST on gold imports. MCX Silver at ₹2,68,026/kg similarly reflects the full landed cost burden, meaning any further COMEX rally compounds import parity pressure and widens the premium Indian physical buyers must absorb. Indian traders tracking the MCX-COMEX spread need to monitor rupee movement closely, as any INR depreciation beyond ₹95.70 mechanically lifts MCX prices independent of COMEX direction.

**WATCH**
Any official statement from the US State Department or Iranian government escalating or de-escalating the current tension directly determines whether this safe-haven bid sustains; COMEX Gold holding above $4,581/oz on the next session close confirms momentum, while a breach below $4,550/oz signals position unwinding.
