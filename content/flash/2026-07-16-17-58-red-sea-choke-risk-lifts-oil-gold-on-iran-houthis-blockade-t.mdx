**WHAT HAPPENED**
Iran has reportedly instructed Houthis to block Red Sea shipping lanes if the United States conducts strikes on Iranian energy infrastructure, according to multiple reports on Tuesday.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A Red Sea blockade would restrict crude oil transit through one of the world's most critical chokepoints, forcing refiners like **HPCL, BPCL, and Reliance** to source barrels via longer Cape of Good Hope routes, raising per-barrel transport premiums by $2-4 and extending voyage time by 10-14 days. The supply-side shock mechanism works through rising Brent-WTI spreads and elevated tanker rates, which directly feed into Indian refiner procurement costs and, downstream, fuel retail pricing.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
HPCL and BPCL crude procurement teams face an immediate 8-12% increase in effective landed costs per barrel on African and Middle Eastern crudes, while **Reliance Industries'** export-oriented refinery spreads compress if regional crude availability tightens. Petrol pump operators, transport operators reliant on diesel, and airline fuel managers see hedging costs reprice upward against **Brent crude** and **MCX Crude Oil** contracts, with cascading impact on ticket fares and freight logistics charges for end consumers.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Shipping and logistics operators will absorb 3-5% margin compression on every tonne of goods routed through Asia-Europe corridors as demurrage and insurance costs reset higher. **MCX Crude Oil October contracts** should test the **₹6,800-7,000** band if blockade risks materialize, signaling a 12-15% premium to current levels. Indian households will experience petrol and diesel prices firming by ₹2-4 per litre within two weeks if geopolitical tensions escalate further.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **US military statements or CENTCOM activity updates** scheduled for Wednesday-Thursday for confirmation of imminent strikes. Track **Houthi statements and shipping insurance premiums** for Red Sea routes—a spike above 3% of cargo value signals market-priced blockade risk.
