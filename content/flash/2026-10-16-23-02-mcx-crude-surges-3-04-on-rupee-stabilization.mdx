**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 9567 INR/bbl at 11:02 pm IST, posting a +3.04% gain as USD/INR steadied at 95.07 INR (-0.09% vs prev close). The rupee's renewed stability has bolstered export competitiveness for domestic crude after overnight weakness. Gold and silver climbed modestly to 147859 INR/10g (+0.25%) and 211111 INR/kg (+0.21%) respectively, while natural gas weakened to 299.4 INR/mmBtu (-2.16%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 3% move marks the largest single-session leg higher in this cycle. For MCX futures traders, the rupee floor at 95.07 is critical—each paisa depreciation from here unlocks fresh export-driven demand for domestic crude. The contra-move in natural gas signals energy market divergence; crude's outperformance reflects FX tailwinds rather than broad commodity strength.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9600 INR/bbl round-number resistance is now in play. A close above this level at the next settlement would confirm the rupee floor hold and signal further upside targeting 9700 INR/bbl.
