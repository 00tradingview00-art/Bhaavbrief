**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude dropped to 7880 INR/bbl at 16:27 IST, marking a −3.13% decline from previous close. The sharp pullback reverses the +1.52% rally to 8275 INR/bbl logged at 14:23 IST and erases intraday momentum as supply-side stabilisation sentiment falters. Cross-asset weakness remains contained: gold down 0.18% to 162936 INR/10g, silver −0.83%, natural gas −1.16% to 263.3 INR/mmBtu. Rupee weakness (USDINR −0.30% to 95.41) offers limited support.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude has now retraced through the 8110–8146 INR/bbl support band established overnight, signalling a breakdown of the recovery structure initiated from 7754 INR/bbl. The move below 7900 INR/bbl fractures bullish positioning and reopens downside targets. Broader commodity softness—gold, silver, and natgas all negative—suggests macro headwinds dominating crude-specific fundamentals.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Support holds at 7850 INR/bbl. A close below this level will confirm trend reversal and target 7750 INR/bbl. Hold above 7850 for stabilisation signals.
