**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX gold dropped below $4,800/oz and silver fell 10% intraday as easing US-Iran geopolitical tensions strengthened the US Dollar, reducing safe-haven demand across precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold and MCX Silver are the directly affected contracts. MCX Gold, currently anchored near ₹1,60,047/10g, faces downward pressure as the COMEX slide feeds through to rupee-denominated prices — though USD/INR at ₹95.70 partially cushions the fall by inflating import costs in rupee terms. MCX Silver at ₹2,57,896/kg is more exposed given the 10% COMEX drop; a move of that magnitude on COMEX translates to a raw import cost swing of approximately ₹25,000-₹26,000/kg before applying India's 15% customs duty and GST, compressing the typical COMEX-MCX spread. Indian traders holding long positions in near-month Silver contracts face amplified mark-to-market losses since MCX Silver's higher leverage magnifies the COMEX percentage move on a rupee-adjusted basis.

**WATCH**
The next US Dollar Index (DXY) print and any formal statement from US or Iranian officials confirming or reversing the diplomatic de-escalation will either extend this selloff or trigger a sharp reversal in both MCX Gold and Silver. Key level to monitor: COMEX gold's ability to hold or break $4,500/oz, which maps directly to the ₹1,60,047/10g MCX reference.
