**WHAT HAPPENING**
MCX Crude declined to 8627 INR/bbl at 10:21 pm IST, posting a -0.48% loss after the sharp reversal logged at 09:39 pm IST. USD/INR held steady at 96.58 INR (+0.03% vs prev close), neutralizing the export-competitiveness tailwind that had driven the afternoon rally to 9197 INR/bbl. Natural gas surged +1.76% to 300.3 INR/mmBtu, signaling divergent energy sector momentum.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's stabilization has arrested crude's export-linked recovery narrative. Traders are caught between the 8627–8692 INR/bbl support band and the intraday resistance at 8869 INR/bbl. The late-session retreat suggests profit-taking after the +3.94% intraday peak, with positioning now neutral as USD/INR volatility subsides. Gold and silver remain bid (+0.42%, +0.32%), indicating risk-off undercurrents.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Hold of the 8600 INR/bbl floor. A breach below invalidates the support established at 09:39 pm IST; a recapture of 8869 INR/bbl signals renewed rupee-weakness momentum.
