**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 94.01 INR at 04:52 pm IST, marking a -0.14% pullback from the previous close. The rupee's renewed strength continues the downtrend established since 04:14 pm IST when the pair crossed below 95 INR. Broad-based commodity support persists: MCX Gold at 143729 INR/10g (+0.83%), MCX Silver at 187664 INR/kg (+1.39%), and MCX Copper at 1349.6 INR/kg (+0.77%) offset weakness in MCX Crude at 8238 INR/bbl (-3.69%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Rupee strength below 94.5 INR elevates hedging costs for MCX commodity importers while improving repatriation margins for exporters. The inverse relationship between USD/INR and precious metals—gold and silver both rallying on rupee appreciation—signals sustained carry demand. Crude's 3.69% decline reflects global WTI weakness, not rupee dynamics, creating cross-asset complexity for position holders.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
94.00 INR support holds critical psychological ground. A break below this level would confirm rupee momentum; rejection above 94.25 INR would test the 94.5 round-number resistance that defined earlier session peaks.
