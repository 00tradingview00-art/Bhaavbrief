**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude fell sharply to 8238 INR/bbl at 04:52 pm IST, posting a −3.69% loss after climbing to 9113 INR/bbl at 11:36 pm IST yesterday. The reversal signals profit-taking and weaker global demand signals, despite USD/INR holding steady at 94.01 INR (−0.14% vs prev close). Gold (+0.83%) and Silver (+1.39%) rallied, suggesting risk-off appetite overtook commodity strength.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sharp crude pullback breaks the uptrend that saw the contract gain +0.45% overnight. For MCX futures traders, this signals exhaustion near 9113 INR/bbl resistance and positions this level as a critical barrier for any recovery attempt. Cross-asset weakness in crude against stronger precious metals indicates diverging commodity narratives.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8200 INR/bbl support zone will determine if this decline stabilizes or accelerates lower. A break below this level toward 8100 INR/bbl would confirm the downtrend's strength.
