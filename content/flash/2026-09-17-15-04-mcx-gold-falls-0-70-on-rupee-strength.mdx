**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold declined to 151410 INR/10g at 03:04 pm IST, posting a -0.70% loss from the previous close. The pullback extends weakness that began at 19:22 IST when gold fell to 150924 INR/10g (-0.20%). USD/INR holding steady at 95.96 INR limits rupee support for bullion. Silver tracked lower at -0.64% to 233287 INR/kg, while crude and natgas posted modest declines of -0.39% and +0.14% respectively. Copper bucked the trend with +0.83% gains to 1385.75 INR/kg.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The -0.70% slide reverses recovery momentum from the 23:47 IST rally at 152950 INR/10g (+1.42%). Traders face consolidation pressure between support near 150824 INR/10g and the intraday high. Rupee stability at 95.96 INR removes a traditional tailwind, keeping gold vulnerable to further profit-taking in afternoon trade.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
151000 INR/10g support level. A break below signals continued downside toward 150824 INR/10g. Hold above 151400 INR/10g restores intraday consolidation.
