**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver fell to 207990 INR/kg at 12:02 am IST, extending a −0.17% decline from the previous close. The pullback reflects continued weakness across precious metals—MCX Gold dropped −1.75% to 145850 INR/10g, while USD/INR edged lower to 95.25 (−0.20%), maintaining structural headwinds for rupee-denominated bullion despite modest currency relief.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver remains trapped in a multi-session downtrend that has compressed the contract −12.23% from the 236404 INR/kg level logged at 01:58 pm IST on the prior session. The current price action signals trader caution amid cross-asset volatility—crude strength at +1.53% and natgas weakness at −2.78% highlight sectoral divergence, keeping precious metals on the defensive.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 205000 INR/kg support level. A breach would confirm fresh downside momentum; a hold would test whether the −0.17% move stabilizes into consolidation ahead of the next structural test.
