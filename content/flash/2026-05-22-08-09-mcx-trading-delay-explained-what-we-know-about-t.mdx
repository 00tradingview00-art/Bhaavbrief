**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX experienced a technical outage that disrupted live trading sessions, preventing order execution across commodity contracts during active market hours.

**MCX IMPACT**
All actively traded MCX contracts were affected — Gold (₹160,860/10g), Silver (₹259,988/kg), and Crude Oil (₹9,588/bbl) — with order books frozen during the disruption window. When trading resumes post-outage, price discovery gaps form between the last traded MCX price and the live COMEX/NYMEX benchmarks, creating basis risk for Indian traders holding open positions. At current COMEX Gold of $4,522/oz and USD/INR at ₹96.21, any rupee movement during the downtime directly widens the spread Indian traders must reconcile on re-entry — customs duty and import parity calculations reset against a moved spot. Traders with stop-loss orders pending during the outage face unexecuted risk, as MCX halts do not pause underlying international market movement.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange notice for the confirmed resumption timestamp and any declared "force majeure" session extension — a gap open on Gold above ₹161,500 or below ₹160,200 on restart confirms the size of the dislocation traders must price in.
