**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude surged to 9090 INR/bbl at 01:09 am IST, posting a +1.53% gain as USD/INR weakened to 95.25 INR (-0.20% vs prev close). The rupee's renewed depreciation has restored export competitiveness for domestic crude after overnight losses. Gold and silver remain under pressure, falling -1.75% and -0.17% respectively, while natural gas slumped -2.78%.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
This reversal consolidates crude's recovery from the 8762 INR/bbl trough logged at 04:16 pm IST. The rupee's weakness is the primary tailwind offsetting broader commodity weakness. For MCX traders, the cross-asset snapshot shows selective strength—crude decoupling from gold and silver weakness suggests crude is tracking currency dynamics rather than broad risk-off sentiment.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9100 INR/bbl round number is now critical resistance. A break above that level would signal sustained rupee-led strength; a retreat below 9050 INR/bbl would indicate the move lacks conviction.
