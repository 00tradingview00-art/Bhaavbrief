**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 93.91 INR at 04:17 pm IST, marking a -0.13% pullback from the previous close after crossing the 94 INR round-number level from 94.03 INR. Rupee strength persists amid broad-based commodity rally support: MCX Gold at 141340 INR/10g (+0.58%), MCX Silver at 187095 INR/kg (+2.48%), and MCX Crude at 8181 INR/bbl (+1.05%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 94 INR breach signals renewed rupee demand that pressures dollar positioning across MCX commodity futures. With precious metals rallying alongside rupee strength, traders face cross-currency headwinds—imported commodity hedges become more expensive while domestic gold and silver futures benefit from INR appreciation, creating mixed risk exposure for multi-commodity portfolios.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 93.85–94.00 INR band for support consolidation. A sustained close below 93.85 INR would extend rupee momentum; a reversal back above 94.03 INR would signal dollar recovery and potential commodity pullback.
