**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver jumped to 183555 INR/kg at 02:14 am IST, posting a +2.47% gain from the previous close. The rally extends across the precious metals complex—MCX Gold climbed +1.25% to 140222 INR/10g, while MCX Copper added +1.41% to 1392.95 INR/kg. USD/INR slipped −0.18% to 95.09, providing tailwind for rupee-denominated bullion as global risk sentiment stabilizes.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The move reverses recent weakness in silver after a two-flash downswing that tested 183555 levels. For MCX futures traders, this recovery suggests demand rotation back into precious metals as a hedge. The concurrent strength in gold and copper confirms broad-based risk-on appetite, though the rupee's modest weakness may cap further upside on INR valuations.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 185000 INR/kg resistance level will signal whether this +2.47% rally has structural support or faces profit-taking. Break above confirms momentum; rejection signals continuation of recent consolidation range.
