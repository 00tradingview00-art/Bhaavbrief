**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 164604 INR/10g at 18:59 IST, extending its +1.33% advance as the metal continues a multi-session recovery arc that has now breached and held above 162000 INR/10g. Silver tracked higher at 248183 INR/kg (+0.64%), while crude slipped -1.60% to 8225 INR/bbl and natural gas jumped +1.77% to 269.5 INR/mmBtu. The rupee remained flat at 95.74 INR/USD, leaving gold unhedged against currency headwinds.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's push past 164600 reinforces the ongoing safe-haven bid in a cross-asset environment where crude weakness and energy volatility are driving flight-to-quality flows. For MCX futures traders, this represents sustained momentum above a key technical resistance zone established earlier in the session, with silver's correlated +0.64% move signaling broad precious metals strength.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
164600 INR/10g holds as the immediate resistance ceiling. A sustained break above 165000 would signal fresh conviction; failure to hold 164000 would test the session's recovery structure.
