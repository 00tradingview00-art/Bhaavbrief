Zerodha flagged a technical glitch on the MCX (Multi Commodity Exchange) platform, warning retail and institutional clients of potential disruptions in live commodity trading sessions.

The malfunction directly impacts order execution across MCX-listed contracts, including high-volume commodities such as crude oil, gold, and natural gas. Platform-level disruptions at a major broker like Zerodha — one of India's largest by active client count — reduce effective market participation, widening bid-ask spreads and increasing intraday price volatility. Thinner order books during glitch windows amplify price swings in already sensitive energy and bullion contracts, particularly during peak trading hours when liquidity is critical for accurate price discovery.
