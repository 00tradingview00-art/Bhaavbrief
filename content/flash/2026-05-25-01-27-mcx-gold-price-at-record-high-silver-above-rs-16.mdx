**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold hit a record high while MCX Silver crossed ₹1,61,000/kg, tracking COMEX Gold at $4,523/oz and COMEX Silver at $76.20/oz against a USD/INR rate of ₹95.70.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold June and August futures are the primary contracts affected, with prices anchored at approximately ₹1,60,047/10g based on current COMEX and spot USD/INR levels. MCX Silver similarly reflects the COMEX print of $76.20/oz, translating to approximately ₹2,57,896/kg — the live contract is already trading above the ₹1,61,000 threshold cited in domestic rate boards, indicating a premium over the mechanical conversion. For Indian traders, the landed import cost of gold includes a 15% basic customs duty plus 3% AIDC, meaning any further rupee depreciation from the current ₹95.70 handle directly widens the COMEX-to-MCX spread and elevates floor support for domestic contracts. Domestic physical demand from jewellery hubs typically compresses at these record levels, reducing spot buying pressure against futures.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve commentary or U.S. CPI print will either sustain the bullion bid or trigger a sharp COMEX correction that pulls MCX contracts off record levels. Monitor the USD/INR at the ₹96.00 resistance level — a breach widens import costs further and reinforces MCX upside mechanically.
