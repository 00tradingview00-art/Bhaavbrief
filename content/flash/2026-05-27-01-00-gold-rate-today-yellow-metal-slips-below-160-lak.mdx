**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold dropped below the ₹1,60,000/10g threshold as COMEX spot gold trades at $4,535/oz, with US-Iran geopolitical tension creating cross-current volatility in the yellow metal.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) bears the direct impact of this price action, with the ₹1,60,000/10g level — currently aligned with COMEX at ~₹1,60,440/10g after currency conversion — acting as immediate technical support. The USD/INR rate at ₹95.69 is amplifying rupee-denominated gold prices; any further rupee depreciation compresses the discount between MCX and COMEX-derived import parity. Indian bullion importers face elevated landed costs given 15% basic customs duty plus GST, meaning physical demand at sub-₹1,60,000 levels gets support from domestic jewellers treating this as a value entry zone. The COMEX-MCX spread at current rates leaves limited arbitrage room, keeping MCX tightly anchored to international price direction.

**WATCH**
Monitor the US-Iran diplomatic developments and any formal escalation statements from Washington or Tehran, as a confirmed military engagement triggers a flight-to-safety bid that reclaims ₹1,60,000 immediately. The next US CPI print and Fed commentary remain the macro events that confirm or negate gold's directional bias through the week.
