**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver collapsed nearly 15% to ₹3,42,390/kg on January 30, a sharp divergence from the current live COMEX silver price of $76.20/oz (~₹2,57,896/kg).

**MCX IMPACT**
The January 30 MCX print of ₹3,42,390/kg represents a significant premium over the current COMEX-derived INR equivalent of ₹2,57,896/kg, a spread of approximately ₹84,494/kg that reflects the time gap between that data point and present live pricing. The MCX Silver continuous contract now trades in line with COMEX at ₹2,57,896/kg at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. Indian traders importing physical silver face an import cost structure that includes a 15% basic customs duty plus GST, making the landed cost materially higher than the MCX spot reference. Any further rupee depreciation from the current ₹95.70 level directly inflates MCX silver prices independent of COMEX movement, widening the basis for domestic hedgers.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. Federal Reserve interest rate decision and accompanying commentary will either sustain upward pressure on silver through dollar weakness or compress the COMEX price back toward the $72–$74/oz support band. Monitor USD/INR at the ₹96.00 resistance level, a break above which mechanically lifts MCX silver regardless of COMEX direction.
