**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver fell to 234904 INR/kg at 21:44 IST, posting a −1.23% decline from the previous close. The pullback reverses earlier recovery momentum that had lifted silver to 236969 INR/kg at 23:10 IST yesterday. The retreat mirrors weakness across the broader commodity complex: MCX Gold down 0.93%, MCX Natural Gas sliding 1.79%, and MCX Crude falling 0.61%. USD/INR holds steady at 95.43 (+0.05%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's retreat after yesterday's +2.38% rally suggests profit-taking at resistance rather than fundamental weakness. The −1.23% move remains modest relative to intraday volatility. For MCX futures traders, the key question is whether the recovery from 222836 INR/kg (established 21:23 IST yesterday) holds as support, or if coordinated commodity weakness signals a broader liquidation cycle.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch whether 234904 INR/kg holds as a floor or if silver breaks back toward 232300 INR/kg (yesterday's 01:19 IST level). A close above 235500 INR/kg would suggest the recovery narrative remains intact.
