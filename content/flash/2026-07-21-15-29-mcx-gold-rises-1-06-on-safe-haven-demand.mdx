**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold climbed to 142883 INR/10g at 03:29 pm IST, gaining +1.06% from the previous close. The rally mirrors broad-based strength across MCX commodities—Silver surged +2.31% to 223437 INR/kg, Crude advanced +1.20% to 8044 INR/bbl, and Copper gained +1.42% to 1332.85 INR/kg. The rupee edged 0.05% weaker to 96.24 INR, reducing import friction for domestic bullion demand and underpinning the yellow metal's appeal.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's synchronized rally with silver and industrial metals signals broad risk appetite rather than isolated safe-haven positioning. However, the rupee's marginal weakness is a tailwind for domestic traders holding futures. With most commodities in positive territory, the momentum reflects macro liquidity rather than geopolitical stress, keeping gold within its near-term range.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 143000 INR/10g round level for resistance confirmation. A close above this handle at the day's end would signal sustained momentum into tomorrow's session.
