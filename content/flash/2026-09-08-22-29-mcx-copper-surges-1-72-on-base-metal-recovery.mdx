**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Copper rallied to 1410.55 INR/kg at 10:29 pm IST, gaining +1.72% from the previous close. The advance reverses the earlier pullback to 1371.5 INR/kg at 12:17 am IST and signals renewed strength in the base metals complex. USD/INR weakness at 94.84 (+0.28%) provided tailwind for rupee-denominated metals, while precious metals remain subdued—Gold at 152824 INR/10g (flat) and Silver at 239405 INR/kg (+0.16%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Copper's recovery through 1410 INR/kg suggests stabilization after intraday volatility that saw swings between 1371.5 and 1395.65. For MCX traders, the rupee strength (USD/INR easing) is acting as a demand cushion. The relative outperformance versus Gold and Natural Gas (−2.38%) highlights selective base metal positioning despite broader commodity pressure.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor resistance at 1420 INR/kg and support at 1395 INR/kg. A close above 1415 would confirm fresh momentum; a slip below 1395 risks retest of the 1371 low.
