**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas slipped to 313.1 INR/mmBtu at 23:30 IST, marking a -1.07% decline from the previous close. The retreat follows a volatile 24-hour cycle that saw prices recover to 288.4 INR/mmBtu (+2.09%) at 14:00 IST yesterday before rolling over. MCX Crude held steady at 9159 INR/bbl (-0.09%), signaling weakening co-movement. USD/INR strengthened 0.27% to 95.98 INR, adding headwind to energy imports.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The breakdown below 315 INR/mmBtu reveals exhaustion of crude's recent rally support. Natural Gas traders are now exposed to rupee strength and a lack of fresh bullish triggers from crude. The -1.07% move signals indecision after yesterday's sharp gyrations, with consolidation risk outweighing recovery momentum near current levels.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break below 310 INR/mmBtu would extend weakness toward the 274.5 INR/mmBtu overnight low (22:29 IST). Resistance forms at 315–318 INR/mmBtu; a close above 320 INR/mmBtu would restart the uptrend.
