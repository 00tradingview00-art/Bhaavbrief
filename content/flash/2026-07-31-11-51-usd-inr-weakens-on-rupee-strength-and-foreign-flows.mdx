**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 95.37 INR at 11:51 am IST, registering a -0.38% fall from the previous close. The rupee's appreciation reflects sustained foreign inflow momentum and reduced dollar demand in domestic markets. Concurrent MCX moves show gold at 142645 INR/10g (-0.41%), silver at 219246 INR/kg (-0.33%), and crude at 7843 INR/bbl (-2.41%), signaling broad-based weakness in dollar-denominated commodities.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A stronger rupee compresses import costs for MCX traders but weighs on export-linked commodity valuations. The -0.38% move sustains the rupee's uptrend observed in earlier 11:51 am IST sessions, supporting gold and silver as rupee hedges. Crude's sharper -2.41% loss signals independent weakness beyond currency, requiring separate tracking of global oil sentiment.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 96.0 INR round level—currently 63 paise above spot—acts as the next intermediate resistance. A breach below 95.30 would confirm momentum; a reversal above 95.70 would test the strength of foreign inflows.
