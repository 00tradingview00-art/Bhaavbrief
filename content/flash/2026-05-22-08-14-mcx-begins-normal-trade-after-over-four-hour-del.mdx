**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX resumed normal trading operations after a technical glitch halted all commodity trading for over four hours during active market hours.

**MCX IMPACT**
All active MCX contracts — including Gold (front-month near ₹160,960/10g), Silver (near ₹260,662/kg), Crude Oil (near ₹9,566/bbl), and Copper — were directly affected by the trading suspension. The four-hour halt created a price-discovery gap, meaning MCX prices at resumption had to rapidly close the basis differential versus live COMEX and WTI moves that continued uninterrupted during the outage. For Indian traders, this gap creates spread risk: if COMEX Gold or WTI moved materially during the halt, the MCX reopening price must absorb that delta plus the USD/INR rate at ₹96.21, compressing or widening the import parity spread in a single session. Positions held through the halt faced mark-to-market resets without the ability to hedge or exit, exposing traders to unmanaged overnight-style gap risk intraday.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange circular for confirmation of settlement price methodology applied during the halt period, as any adjustment to daily settlement prices directly impacts margin calls and open interest calculations for all affected contracts.
