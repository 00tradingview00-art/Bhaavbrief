**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 95.25 INR at 04:27 pm IST, marking a -0.27% pullback from the previous close. The rupee's renewed strength reverses the dollar's earlier recovery seen at 04:13 pm IST when the pair had climbed to 95.55 INR. The rupee's strength comes as broad-based commodity weakness grips MCX: MCX_GOLD at 141385 INR/10g (-0.85%), MCX_SILVER at 183997 INR/kg (-2.88%), and MCX_COPPER at 1380.55 INR/kg (-1.00%), signalling cooling demand pressures that typically weaken the dollar in emerging markets.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX traders, the rupee's intraday recovery signals profit-taking after the 04:13 pm spike to 95.55 INR. Precious metals weakness—particularly silver's 2.88% dive—typically correlates with rupee appreciation as FPI flows stabilize. This creates tactical pressure on USD/INR-sensitive commodity futures, especially gold and silver spreads, as importers lock in rupee gains.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 95.16 INR level (seen at 02:37 pm IST) marks the immediate support. A break below 95.00 INR round would signal sustained rupee strength; a retest of 95.55 INR would confirm reversal failure.
