**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Ltd shares crashed 15% and hit the lower circuit as gold and silver prices on the exchange entered a sharp rout, directly pressuring the exchange's transaction-fee-driven revenue model.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary instruments under stress, with gold trading near ₹161,662/10g and silver near ₹261,989/kg basis current COMEX rates and USD/INR at ₹96.21. A sustained sell-off in these contracts compresses MCX's turnover-linked transaction revenues, since the exchange earns fees as a percentage of traded value — lower prices mean lower notional turnover even if volumes hold. For Indian traders, the COMEX-to-MCX spread is mechanically locked to USD/INR; with rupee at 96.21, any further rupee depreciation partially cushions the domestic price fall, but a COMEX-led rout of this magnitude overwhelms that buffer. Copper and crude oil contracts on MCX face secondary pressure as broad commodity risk-off sentiment accelerates margin calls across multi-commodity portfolios.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's daily trading turnover data and the next COMEX settlement for gold holding or breaching the $4,500/oz level — a close below that level accelerates domestic contract value erosion and compounds the exchange's revenue pressure into the next reporting quarter.
