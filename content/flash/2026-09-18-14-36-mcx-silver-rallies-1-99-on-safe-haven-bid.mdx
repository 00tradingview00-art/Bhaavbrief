**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Silver surged to 242950 INR/kg at 02:36 pm IST, posting a +1.99% gain from the previous close. The rally extends the recovery initiated at 13:34 IST when silver breached the 240000 INR/kg psychological level. Gold strength (+1.02% to 154538 INR/10g) and crude weakness (−1.70% to 9592 INR/bbl) signal renewed safe-haven demand, while USD/INR holds flat at 95.94 INR, removing headwind pressure.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Silver's push above 242000 INR/kg signals consolidation within the intraday +0.96% to +1.99% range, with traders anchoring to the 229803 INR/kg trough from 19:11 IST on the prior session as downside support. The cross-asset snapshot reveals a flight-to-safety trade—gold climbing and crude tumbling—creating tailwind for precious metals positioning ahead of any macro event risk.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
242950 INR/kg is now the intraday resistance. Break and hold above 245000 INR/kg (near the 10:20 IST high of 245585) would signal continuation; failure to defend 241000 INR/kg risks retest of 240000 INR/kg support.
