**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude slipped to 9289 INR/bbl at 01:03 am IST, posting a -0.24% loss as USD/INR held steady at 95.05 INR. The rupee's continued stability, despite earlier overnight volatility, has eroded export margins for domestic crude producers. Natural gas fell sharply to 283.2 INR/mmBtu (-2.95%), while gold retreated to 146482 INR/10g (-0.53%) and copper edged higher to 1397.8 INR/kg (+0.37%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The consolidation around 9289 INR/bbl reflects the rupee's anchor at 95.05 INR, removing the currency tailwind that had propelled crude to 9567 INR/bbl earlier. Traders are caught between intraday rally exhaustion and support that held at 9275 INR/bbl during the 02:15 pm IST dip. Cross-asset weakness in natural gas signals broader energy demand caution.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9275–9300 INR/bbl band will determine whether this consolidation is a retracement or the start of a fresh downleg. A break below 9275 INR/bbl would confirm momentum loss; a hold signals potential re-accumulation.
