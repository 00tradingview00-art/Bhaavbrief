**WHAT HAPPENED**
Silver surged ₹7,570 on MCX in a single session while gold also posted gains, tracking strong momentum in global precious metals markets.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Silver (near-month contract) is the primary instrument affected, with the ₹7,570 single-day move reflecting the global COMEX silver price of $76.20/oz, which translates to approximately ₹2,57,896/kg at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. The sharp rupee-adjusted rally is amplified because every $1 move in COMEX silver adds roughly ₹3,380/kg to MCX prices at this exchange rate, meaning Indian traders face outsized nominal swings compared to dollar-denominated positions. MCX Gold (near-month) is also under pressure to the upside, with COMEX gold at $4,523/oz converting to approximately ₹1,60,047/10g — a level that now represents a key short-term reference for domestic spot and futures convergence. Indian traders must note that import duty and GST on silver remain a structural premium layer above COMEX parity, meaning any spread compression signals physical demand easing rather than price weakness.

**WATCH**
The next COMEX settlement and any intraday USD/INR movement beyond ₹96.00 will either extend or cap this MCX rally — a stronger rupee directly compresses the landed import cost and narrows the domestic premium.
