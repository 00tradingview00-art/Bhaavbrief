**WHAT HAPPENED**
A reported Russian geopolitical move has triggered speculation that COMEX gold prices will fall to $3,000/oz, a projected decline of 33.6% from the current live price of $4,523/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold contracts are directly exposed to this narrative, with the benchmark 10g contract currently priced around ₹160,047 based on COMEX at $4,523/oz and USD/INR at ₹95.70. A drop to $3,000/oz on COMEX, held against the current rupee rate, translates mechanically to approximately ₹106,200/10g on MCX — not sub-₹1 lakh as claimed, since that threshold requires either COMEX below ~$2,820/oz or a significant rupee appreciation simultaneously. Indian traders must note that MCX gold pricing embeds a 15% basic customs duty plus GST and other levies, creating a structural floor that keeps MCX above raw COMEX-rupee conversion. The COMEX-MCX spread compression at lower price levels reduces the import cost buffer but does not eliminate it.

**WATCH**
Monitor the next COMEX settlement for any technical break below the $4,200/oz support level, and track any official statement from Russian diplomatic or defense channels that either confirms or withdraws the reported development driving this thesis.
