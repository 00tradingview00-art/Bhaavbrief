**WHAT HAPPENED**
Deutsche Bank has warned that the RBI may hike the repo rate to **6.25%** by H1 2027 as inflation risks persist, signalling tightening monetary policy beyond the current **5.5%** level set at the 2026-10-07 MPC decision.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Higher repo rates increase the cost of capital for **MCX gold and silver futures traders** holding leveraged long positions, as their margin funding and overnight position carry costs rise in lockstep with RBI tightening. Simultaneously, a stronger real interest rate environment makes non-yielding precious metals less attractive relative to fixed-income instruments, pulling speculative demand away from **MCX Gold (₹144,025/10g) and MCX Silver (₹196,987/kg)** in the near term. For **crude oil traders**, rate hikes typically tighten rupee liquidity and dampen domestic consumption demand, placing downward pressure on **MCX Crude (₹9,525/bbl)** as economic activity moderates.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Bullion importers and refiners financing working capital through rupee debt face higher interest expenses on inventory holdings, forcing them to compress forward-purchase volumes or accelerate sales to reduce days payable. Jewellery manufacturers and D2C brands sourcing raw bullion for fabrication face elevated funding costs, which compress margins unless retail catalogue prices adjust upward—a lag that exposes margin vulnerability during the rate cycle. Households purchasing gold ornaments and silver artefacts during festival and wedding seasons encounter higher retail premiums baked into jeweller margins, while industrial users of silver in electronics and solar applications defer procurement decisions.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Bullion importers financing inventory through rupee credit lines absorb margin compression as working capital costs rise with each repo hike. **MCX Gold futures** signal downside risk if rate hikes accelerate toward **6.0%+**, while **MCX Silver and MCX Crude** face similar structural headwinds. Retail gold buyers purchasing ahead of year-end festivals will encounter sticky higher premiums at jeweller counters as financing costs remain elevated.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The next RBI MPC review in December 2026 and quarterly CPI prints will determine if the 6.25% trajectory firms or softens. Any hawkish surprise in that decision will trigger immediate selling in gold and silver futures.
