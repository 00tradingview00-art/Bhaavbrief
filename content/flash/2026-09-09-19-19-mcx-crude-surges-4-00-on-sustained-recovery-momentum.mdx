**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude rallied to 9083 INR/bbl at 19:19 IST, posting a +4.00% gain and extending the multi-leg recovery sequence that began at 21:26 IST yesterday from 7918 INR/bbl. The move builds on intraday peaks of 8952 INR/bbl at 14:28 IST and sustains upside momentum despite USD/INR strength at 95.18 (+0.36%). Gold and silver also advance (+0.87% and +1.36% respectively), signaling broad-based commodity demand.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The +4.00% move marks a decisive break above the 8952 INR/bbl intraday resistance logged earlier. For MCX futures traders, this consolidation above 9000 signals momentum continuation in the recovery sequence. Cross-asset strength in gold and silver confirms risk-on positioning despite rupee weakness, reducing hedging pressure on crude exposure.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 9100 INR/bbl round-number resistance and the 8946 INR/bbl intraday support logged at 13:45 IST will define continuation. A break above 9100 would confirm the recovery legs; a drop below 8946 would signal consolidation risk.
