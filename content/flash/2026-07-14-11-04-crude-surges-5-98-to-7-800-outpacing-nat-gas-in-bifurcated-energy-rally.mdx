**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX crude surged **+5.98%** to ₹7,800/barrel in afternoon trade, leading a broad commodity rally that lifted gold **+0.59%** to ₹141,139/10g and silver **+1.17%** to ₹220,275/kg. Copper extended gains with **+1.43%** to ₹1,314.80/kg, while the rupee weakened to ₹96.19/USD, amplifying INR-denominated commodity prices.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee depreciation acts as a mechanical amplifier: each paisa of INR weakness adds **0.5-0.8%** to the effective rupee-price of dollar-linked commodities before global price moves are applied. Crude's outsized **5.98%** rally appears driven by geopolitical premium rather than demand fundamentals—natural gas, which tracks only supply-demand without geopolitical overlay, rose just **+0.25%** to ₹279.10/mmBtu, suggesting energy markets are pricing risk concentration in oil rather than broad energy scarcity.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
An oil refiner managing quarterly hedges faces immediate margin compression if refined product pricing lags crude's **₹468/barrel** intraday move, forcing accelerated pass-through decisions into fuel retail contracts. A jewellery manufacturer with unhedged silver inventory sees unrealised gains widen but faces forward-pricing risk if the **1.17%** move reflects temporary safe-haven inflows that reverse after geopolitical clarity.

**BOTTOM LINE**
The commodity complex is bifurcated: oil is repricing geopolitical tail risk at **+5.98%** while nat gas stability at **+0.25%** confirms this is not a broad energy-demand recovery but a concentrated premium in crude supply-side anxiety.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Crude closing behaviour relative to ₹7,850 support and whether nat gas holds ₹279 into evening settlement close at 23:30 IST—divergence here clarifies whether tomorrow's move extends as safe-haven rotation or corrects as positioning unwind.
