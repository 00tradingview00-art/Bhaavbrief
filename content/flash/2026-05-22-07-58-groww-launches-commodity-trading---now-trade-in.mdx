**WHAT HAPPENED**
Groww, one of India's largest retail brokerage platforms with over 10 million active users, has launched commodity trading, giving its retail client base direct access to MCX-listed contracts including Gold, Silver, Crude Oil, and Copper.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract, ~₹160,971/10g), MCX Silver (~₹260,431/kg), and MCX Crude Oil (~₹9,598/bbl) are the primary contracts that will see increased retail order flow as Groww's user base onboards onto commodity segments. Higher retail participation historically widens bid-ask spreads during off-peak hours and amplifies momentum moves around key technical levels. For Indian traders, the Groww entry increases competition for liquidity at existing support and resistance zones — Gold at ₹160,000 and Crude at ₹9,500 are now watched by a significantly larger retail pool. Import parity pricing mechanics and customs duty components embedded in MCX contracts remain unchanged, but retail-driven volumes can temporarily distort the MCX-COMEX spread, particularly in Silver and Gold Mini contracts.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX open interest data in Gold and Silver Mini contracts over the next two trading sessions for confirmation of volume uptick attributable to new retail participation. A breach of Gold's ₹160,000 psychological level on elevated volume will signal retail-driven momentum is active.
