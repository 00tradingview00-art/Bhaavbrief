MCX and NCDEX will remain closed on March 26, 2026, on account of Shri Ram Navami, as per the official holiday calendar for commodity exchanges in India.

On this date, all futures contracts across MCX — including bullion (gold, silver), base metals (copper, zinc, lead, aluminium), and energy (crude oil, natural gas) — will see zero session activity. NCDEX agri-commodity contracts, including chana, soybean, and castor seed, will similarly observe a full trading halt. Participants holding open positions should note that no intraday or evening session will operate, requiring margin and risk adjustments to be completed by March 25, 2026.
