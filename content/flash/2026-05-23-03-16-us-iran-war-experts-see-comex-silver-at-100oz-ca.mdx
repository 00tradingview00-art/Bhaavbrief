**WHAT HAPPENED**
Analysts are projecting COMEX silver could reach $100/oz and MCX gold could breach ₹2,00,000/10g if a US-Iran military conflict materialises, representing a 31.8% upside in silver from the current $75.92/oz and a 25.3% premium to gold's live MCX equivalent of ₹1,59,597/10g.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June/August contracts) and MCX Silver (July contract) are the primary instruments in focus. Gold's theoretical ₹2,00,000/10g target implies a COMEX price near approximately $5,647/oz, requiring sustained geopolitical risk premium on top of existing safe-haven positioning already embedded in the current $4,511/oz print. For silver, a move to $100/oz translates to roughly ₹3,38,500/kg on MCX at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70 — a 31.7% jump from the live ₹2,56,935/kg level. Indian traders must account for India's 15% basic customs duty plus GST on silver imports, which structurally widens the MCX-COMEX spread and amplifies rupee-denominated gains when both dollar prices and USD/INR rise simultaneously.

**WATCH**
Any formal US or Iranian military escalation statement, IAEA inspection breakdown, or Strait of Hormuz disruption report will be the immediate trigger to monitor; a COMEX silver close above $80/oz serves as the first technical confirmation level for the $100 projection thesis.
