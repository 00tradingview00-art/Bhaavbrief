**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude jumped to 8164 INR/bbl at 11:54 am IST, marking a +2.76% rally from the previous close. The sharp move reflects renewed supply risk concerns in global oil markets, with geopolitical tensions supporting prices. The rupee weakened marginally to 96.51 INR against the dollar (−0.15%), providing additional tailwind for rupee-denominated crude. Gold traded at 141399 INR/10g (+0.35%), while natural gas eased to 279.1 INR/mmBtu (−0.92%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX crude traders, the move above 8160 signals conviction in the supply-risk narrative, reducing near-term downside vulnerability. The +2.76% breach suggests fund repositioning into energy amid macro uncertainty. With rupee weakness as a secondary support, the risk-reward skews toward consolidation above this level, though positioning extremes need monitoring for pullback triggers.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 8200 INR/bbl round number is critical resistance. A sustained break above it would confirm the uptrend; failure to hold gains into the London afternoon close would signal exhaustion and potential mean reversion.
