**WHAT HAPPENED**
Analysts have flagged a scenario where silver prices breach the $30/oz support level on COMEX, representing a potential drawdown exceeding 60% from the current spot price of $76.20/oz.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Silver continuous contract, currently priced at approximately ₹257,896/kg, faces direct downside transmission from any COMEX weakness given the near-mechanical USD/INR conversion linkage at ₹95.70. A move to $30/oz on COMEX translates to a theoretical MCX level near ₹101,500/kg after applying the standard 10% customs duty and GST on silver imports — a figure that reframes current Indian prices as carrying a substantial premium over any such distressed COMEX scenario. Indian traders must note that the customs duty component acts as a structural floor buffer, meaning MCX declines will be partially cushioned versus the percentage drop seen on COMEX. However, the wide spread between current MCX prices and this theoretical import cost level signals elevated risk for long carry positions if global sentiment deteriorates sharply.

**WATCH**
The next US Non-Farm Payrolls release and Federal Reserve interest rate decision are the critical triggers — a stronger dollar and rising real yields are the primary mechanical drivers behind any sustained COMEX silver breakdown below the $70/oz near-term support zone.
