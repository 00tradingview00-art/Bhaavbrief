**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX crude oil futures surged over 4% intraday, with a Religare analyst projecting a 13% further upside and recommending fresh initiations at current levels.

**MCX IMPACT**
The MCX Crude Oil contract (near-month) is the primary instrument affected, with the 4% jump aligning with WTI's elevated spot at $98.32/bbl, translating to approximately ₹9,649/bbl on MCX at the current USD/INR rate of ₹96.21. The ₹9,649/bbl level now becomes the immediate reference floor; a 13% upside projection from here places a technical target near ₹10,903/bbl on MCX. For Indian traders, elevated crude directly raises import costs for the world's third-largest oil importer, widening the current account pressure and keeping the INR under structural depreciation stress — which itself amplifies MCX contract values beyond the COMEX move alone. The rupee-dollar spread effect means Indian traders are absorbing a compounded impact: every dollar WTI gains adds roughly ₹96 per barrel to MCX settlement, making long carry positions more sensitive to INR volatility than to crude direction alone.

**WATCH**
The next U.S. EIA Weekly Crude Inventory report is the immediate binary trigger — a draw confirms the bullish momentum toward the ₹10,000/bbl psychological resistance, while a build negates the 4% surge. Monitor the ₹9,800/bbl level as the first structural resistance on MCX intraday charts.
