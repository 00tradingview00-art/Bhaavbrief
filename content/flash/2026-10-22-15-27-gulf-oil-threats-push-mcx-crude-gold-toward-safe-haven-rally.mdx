**WHAT HAPPENED**
Iran has threatened strikes on oil production and artificial intelligence infrastructure across seven Gulf nations in response to potential renewed US and Israeli military action.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude oil refining margins compress immediately for Indian refineries importing from the Arabian Sea, as **MCX Crude at ₹9312/bbl** reflects forward premiums for supply-route disruption risk, forcing procurement desks to lock higher rupee-denominated barrels or delay purchases betting on de-escalation. Simultaneously, **MCX Gold at ₹143836/10g** captures geopolitical safe-haven demand, lifting rupee-denominated bullion costs for domestic jewellery workshops and coin dealers holding working inventory valued in local currency.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Downstream petroleum processors—fuel retailers, bitumen suppliers, and heating oil distributors—face immediate pressure on input costs as crude spot repricing feeds into their landed-cost calculations within 7–10 days, while marketing margins tighten if retail pump pricing lags wholesale moves. Mid-chain lubricant blenders and bunker fuel traders sourcing monthly spot contracts see input repricing, forcing them to either absorb margin compression or pass costs to fleet operators and maritime logistics providers. Retail consumers filling petrol tanks and households booking LPG cylinders will experience price adjustments lagged by 1–2 weeks, while families purchasing gold jewellery or investment bars for Diwali gifting encounter elevated per-gram costs at jeweller counters today.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Petroleum product marketers face margin compression on every litre sold if crude stays elevated while retail prices remain locked for regulatory windows. **MCX Crude** has moved into a risk-premium zone where contract rolls above ₹9500/bbl would signal sustained supply-chain anxiety. Retail gold buyers purchasing ahead of festival season absorb immediate price increases at jeweller counters, translating to 2–4% higher wedding and gifting budgets.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
OPEC+ production guidance and any escalation statements from Gulf oil ministers within 48 hours will determine if crude holds the risk premium or reverses. US diplomatic responses and ceasefire signals will be the critical trigger for safe-haven unwind across both crude and gold contracts.
