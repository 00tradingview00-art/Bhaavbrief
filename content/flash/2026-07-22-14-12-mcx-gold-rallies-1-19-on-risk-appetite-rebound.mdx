**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 144582 INR/10g at 02:12 pm IST, gaining +1.19% from the previous close. The rally extends a broad-based commodity recovery: Silver climbed +0.73% to 225417 INR/kg, Crude advanced +3.98% to 8474 INR/bbl, and Natural Gas rose +0.94% to 277.9 INR/mmBtu. The rupee held steady at 96.53 INR/USD (+0.04%), leaving gold unhedged against currency headwinds and allowing the metal's intrinsic strength to drive the move.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
For MCX futures traders, this move signals renewed appetite for commodity risk assets despite persistent inflation concerns. Gold's +1.19% gain mirrors strength across energy and precious metals—a classic risk-on pattern. The rupee's stability near 96.53 INR/USD removes the dampening effect of currency depreciation, allowing physical demand and portfolio rebalancing to dominate price action.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The 144600 INR/10g round level, now just crossed, will act as initial resistance. A sustained close above it signals continuation; a dip back below 144300 INR/10g would suggest profit-taking dominates.
