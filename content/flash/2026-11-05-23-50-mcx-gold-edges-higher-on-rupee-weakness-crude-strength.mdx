**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold rose to 144045 INR/10g at 11:50 pm IST, posting a +0.57% gain from the previous close. The move extends the multi-day rally that began at 138447 INR/10g on 04:55 pm IST. USD/INR weakened 0.31% to 94.53, supporting rupee-denominated precious metals. MCX Crude surged +2.38% to 8563 INR/bbl, signaling broad commodity strength. MCX Silver declined 0.36% to 187065 INR/kg, showing divergence in precious metals momentum.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's +0.57% move consolidates gains within the established 138447–144100 INR/10g range observed since 04:55 pm IST. Rupee weakness (USD/INR down to 94.53) props up INR-priced bullion despite modest momentum. The divergence with silver and copper weakness suggests selective precious metals demand, not broad risk-off positioning. Traders should monitor whether this reflects safe-haven accumulation or purely currency-driven flows.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
144100 INR/10g resistance—the upper band from the established trading range. A break above signals momentum continuation; failure to sustain suggests consolidation at current levels near 144045.
