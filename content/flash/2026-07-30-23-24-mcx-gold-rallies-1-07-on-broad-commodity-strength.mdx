**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 143298 INR/10g at 23:24 IST, gaining +1.07% from the previous close. The rally reflects sustained broad-based commodity recovery: Silver climbed +1.10% to 219872 INR/kg, Copper advanced +1.32% to 1330.7 INR/kg, and Natural Gas rose +0.72% to 264.7 INR/mmBtu. Crude retreated -0.99% to 8034 INR/bbl, while USD/INR remained flat at 95.67 INR, providing support for rupee-denominated precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's +1.07% move signals renewed safe-haven demand amid mixed commodity performance. The synchronized strength in Silver and Copper suggests broad risk appetite, yet Crude's decline indicates selective profit-taking. For MCX traders, this late-session uptick pushes Gold past recent support zones established during the 143424 INR/10g pullback at 18:24 IST, signaling potential consolidation above 143000 before tomorrow's open.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 145000 psychological level—last breached at 20:20 IST yesterday at 145706 INR/10g. A decisive close above 143500 INR/10g would confirm renewed upside momentum and test that resistance barrier.
