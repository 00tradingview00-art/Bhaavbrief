**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold climbed to 143729 INR/10g at 04:52 pm IST, gaining +0.83% from the previous close. The rally mirrors broad-based strength across the precious metals complex—MCX Silver advanced +1.39% to 187664 INR/kg and MCX Copper rose +0.77% to 1349.6 INR/kg. USD/INR edged 0.14% lower to 94.01, supporting rupee-denominated bullion as crude retreated -3.69% to 8238 INR/bbl.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Gold's rebound extends the multi-day recovery arc visible since 04:55 pm IST, with traders finding support amid rupee softness and crude's sharp pullback reducing inflation hedging demand. The move consolidates above the 142600 level established at 04:17 pm IST, signaling resilience in the precious metals bid despite earlier weakness.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 144045 resistance level set at 11:50 pm IST. A break above signals continuation toward the multi-day rally target; a failure below 143500 would indicate profit-taking and renewed consolidation risk.
