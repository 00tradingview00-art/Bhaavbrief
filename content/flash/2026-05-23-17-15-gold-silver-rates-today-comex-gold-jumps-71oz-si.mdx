**WHAT HAPPENED**
COMEX gold surged $71/oz to $4,523/oz and silver gained $3.50/oz to $76.20/oz as US-Iran geopolitical tensions triggered a sharp safe-haven bid in precious metals.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (June contract) faces immediate upward pressure, with the COMEX spike translating to approximately ₹1,60,047/10g at the current USD/INR rate of ₹95.70. MCX Silver (July contract) reflects the parallel move at approximately ₹2,57,896/kg, a level that represents a significant psychological resistance zone for domestic traders. Indian importers face compounding cost pressure — the $71/oz gold move alone adds roughly ₹2,130/10g to landed cost before customs duty (currently 6% basic + 5% IGST), widening the MCX-COMEX spread further. Traders holding short positions on MCX overnight carry elevated mark-to-market risk given the geopolitical premium now embedded in spot prices.

**WATCH**
Any official statement from Washington or Tehran confirming escalation or de-escalation will be the primary directional trigger — a diplomatic breakthrough collapses the geopolitical premium immediately. Monitor MCX Gold's ability to sustain above ₹1,60,000/10g in early session; a failure to hold that level signals fading momentum.
