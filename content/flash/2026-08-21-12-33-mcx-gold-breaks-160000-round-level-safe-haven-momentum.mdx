**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold surged to 160862 INR/10g at 12:33 pm IST, gaining +0.90% as it decisively crossed the 160000 INR/10g round level from 159425. The breach extends a multi-session recovery arc that saw the metal rally from 153425 INR/10g at 10:48 am IST on the prior session through 155000 INR/10g and 157178 INR/10g. Silver tracked synchronized strength at 245520 INR/kg (+0.94%), while USD/INR remained anchored at 95.74 INR (+0.25%), supporting rupee-denominated precious metals.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 160000 round-number breach signals sustained safe-haven accumulation and breaks a key psychological barrier. For MCX futures traders, this level now becomes a pivot: sustained closes above 160862 suggest bulls have reclaimed the initiative after the earlier 153425 dip, repositioning precious metals as primary hedges amid persistent macro uncertainty. Cross-asset correlation with silver's synchronized rally reinforces broad PM demand.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor the 160000–161000 zone for sustained consolidation. A daily close above 160862 with follow-through to 161500+ would confirm breakout conviction; a pullback and retest of 160000 support would signal profit-taking limits on this round-number cross.
