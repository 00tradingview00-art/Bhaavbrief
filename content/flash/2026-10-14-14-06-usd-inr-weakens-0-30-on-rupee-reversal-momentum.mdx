**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR declined to 95.16 INR at 02:06 pm IST, marking a -0.30% pullback from the previous close. The rupee's renewed strength follows the intraday peak of 97.07 INR at 03:40 pm IST when foreign portfolio inflows had lifted the dollar. This retreat signals profit-taking after the sharp rally. MCX precious metals tracked the softer dollar: gold at 147946 INR/10g (-0.34%), silver at 217565 INR/kg (-0.75%). Crude at 8947 INR/bbl and natural gas at 314.9 INR/mmBtu showed marginal moves.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's consolidation below 95.50 INR eases currency headwinds for rupee-hedged commodity positions. Gold and silver traders benefit from weaker dollar dynamics, reducing import cost pressure. However, the shallow -0.30% move suggests two-way flow remains active. Positioning remains vulnerable to renewed foreign inflows if risk sentiment shifts.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break below 95.00 INR would signal deeper rupee strength; hold above 95.50 INR confirms consolidation range. Gold's 148000 level becomes critical resistance if dollar weakness persists.
