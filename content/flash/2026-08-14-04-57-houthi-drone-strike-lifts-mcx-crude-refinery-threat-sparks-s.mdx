**WHAT HAPPENED**
Houthi militants claimed responsibility for a drone attack on an Aramco refinery in southern Saudi Arabia, marking an escalation in attacks on critical energy infrastructure in the world's largest oil exporter.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Indian refinery procurement desks and crude importers face immediate pressure to lock in barrels at elevated **WTI-linked pricing**, as geopolitical risk premiums widen across forward contracts. Refiners hedging exposure through **MCX Crude Oil** futures experience mark-to-market losses on long inventory positions carried at lower levels, while those with unhedged feedstock requirements must absorb rupee-denominated cost inflation on every tonne of Arabian crude cleared in the spot window.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Domestic petroleum product manufacturers — diesel and petrol blenders, aviation fuel suppliers — see their raw material acquisition cost rise, compressing already-thin refining margins and forcing a lag between pump pricing adjustments and input cost realization. Logistics operators and road transport fleets sourcing diesel through fuel cards and bulk contracts face immediate surcharge notifications, while aviation operators burning jet fuel absorb higher per-flight fuel surcharges that ripple into ticket pricing within days. Retail petrol pump operators managing inventory cycles feel margin pressure on every litre sold at fixed government-notified prices, while households refuelling vehicles and farmers purchasing diesel for irrigation and harvest operations encounter price increases at the retail forecourt.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian refineries operating on thin 1-2% refining margins will see feedstock costs rise faster than they can adjust product slate pricing, eroding near-term profitability. **MCX Crude Oil June futures** should trend toward supply-shock premium levels if Aramco damage reports confirm sustained capacity loss. Domestic petrol and diesel pump prices will face upward revision pressure within 7-10 trading days if the risk premium holds.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Next catalyst: official Saudi Aramco damage assessment and repair timeline, expected within 48 hours. Watch whether **OPEC signals emergency production coordination** at next briefing.
