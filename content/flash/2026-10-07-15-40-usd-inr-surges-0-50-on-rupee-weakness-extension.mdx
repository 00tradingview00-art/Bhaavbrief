**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR climbed to 97.07 INR at 03:40 pm IST, extending gains +0.50% from the previous close. The rupee's continued depreciation reflects sustained foreign portfolio inflows documented since 01:34 pm IST. Across MCX commodities, the stronger dollar pressured precious metals—gold at 149379 INR/10g (-0.49%), silver at 224606 INR/kg (-1.16%)—while energy and base metals benefited: crude at 8733 INR/bbl (+1.22%), natgas at 307.5 INR/mmBtu (+2.09%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The rupee's 97+ breakthrough signals persistent FPI strength and USD demand. MCX gold and silver traders face headwinds from import parity compression. Energy and copper positioning tightens as dollar strength typically underpins crude and natgas while raising INR-denominated cost bases for traders hedging dollar exposures.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
97.50 INR becomes the immediate resistance level. Break above would confirm rupee weakness extension; hold below signals consolidation risk before next FPI flow inflection point.
