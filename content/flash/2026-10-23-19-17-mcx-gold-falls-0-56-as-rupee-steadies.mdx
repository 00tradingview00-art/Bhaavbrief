**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Gold slipped to 142502 INR/10g at 07:17 pm IST, posting a −0.56% loss from the previous close. The pullback extends a volatile intraday session that saw sharp reversals between 146418 and 148354 INR/10g. USD/INR remained anchored at 94.92, offering no currency support. Silver fell harder at −2.13%, while crude surged +2.86%, signaling mixed commodity sentiment.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The renewed weakness breaks the recovery momentum that peaked at 148354 INR/10g at 07:36 pm IST yesterday. For MCX traders, this signals indecision around the 145000–148000 range as rupee stability removes a key bullish catalyst. Cross-commodity strength in crude suggests risk appetite is rotating away from safe-haven metals into energy.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 141000–142000 support level. A break below would confirm downside conviction; a rebound toward 144000 would test whether yesterday's recovery structure holds or collapses further.
