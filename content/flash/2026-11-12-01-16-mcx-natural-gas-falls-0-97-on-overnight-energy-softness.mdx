**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Natural Gas declined to 305.8 INR/mmBtu at 01:16 am IST, posting a -0.97% loss from the previous close. The overnight pullback extends recent volatility across the energy complex, with MCX Crude advancing +1.07% to 8183 INR/bbl sending mixed signals. USD/INR weakened -0.19% to 93.85 INR, reducing export competitiveness support for domestic gas contracts.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The continued downward trajectory—from 287.6 at 20:48 IST yesterday through to current levels—signals sustained demand-side caution despite crude's intraday strength. Traders navigating a consolidation zone between recent lows near 248.8 and resistance, with rupee weakness offering only marginal offset to contractual weakness.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
300 INR/mmBtu holds as the critical support floor. Break below this level in the next session would target 290, confirming sustained bearish momentum. Hold above 300 suggests consolidation rather than capitulation.
