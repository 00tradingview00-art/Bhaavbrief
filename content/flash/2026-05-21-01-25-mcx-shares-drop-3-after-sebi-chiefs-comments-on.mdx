MCX shares fell 3% following remarks by SEBI Chairperson Madhabi Puri Buch raising concerns about speculative activity and structural issues in India's commodity derivatives segment.

The sell-off directly impacts the Multi Commodity Exchange, the dominant platform for commodity futures trading in India covering crude oil, gold, silver, base metals, and agri commodities. Investor sentiment turned negative as regulatory scrutiny signals potential curbs on trading volumes, margin structures, or product design — all of which directly affect MCX's transaction-fee revenue. Lower volumes across energy and metals futures contracts on MCX would compress exchange earnings, explaining the sharp single-session equity decline.
