**WHAT HAPPENED**
The Reserve Bank of India is expected to raise the repo rate by **25 basis points** at its scheduled **Monetary Policy Committee decision on 2026-10-07**, moving from the current **5.25%** level.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A repo rate increase tightens domestic liquidity and raises the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets, which directly pressures **MCX gold and silver futures** as carry-trade demand weakens. Gold importers and silver refiners financing inventory through repo windows will see their working capital costs rise, compressing margins on forward sales already contracted at lower rate expectations. Simultaneously, higher domestic rates typically strengthen the **rupee against the USD** — historically pushing **MCX crude oil** higher in rupee terms despite any dollar-denominated weakness, because refineries' import bills reprice immediately on currency moves.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Jewellery manufacturers and silversmiths sourcing bullion on monthly inventory cycles will face tighter financing costs, forcing them to either reduce order sizes or pass costs to retail chains and wedding-season buyers. D2C jewellery platforms and regional gold finance companies offering loan-against-gold products will see margin compression as their funding costs rise in tandem with the repo rate, narrowing the spread between borrowing and lending rates. Downstream, petroleum retailers and aviation fuel suppliers will face pressure on gross refining margins because higher rupee valuations make imported crude more expensive per unit of local currency despite global crude prices remaining stable, directly lifting pump prices for commuters and fleet operators.

**BOTTOM LINE**
**Gold jewellery manufacturers** sourcing bullion inventory will absorb higher financing charges, squeezing their weekly purchase margins by 20–40 basis points. **MCX gold futures** are likely to test support near ₹149500–149000/10g as rate expectations harden into the decision date. **End consumers** filling wedding-season orders and purchasing silver utensils will see retail premiums widen, translating to **2–3% upside** on finished goods pricing within two weeks of the announcement.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The RBI's forward guidance on the terminal rate and inflation outlook at the **2026-10-07 MPC statement** will determine whether this hike is the final move or signals further tightening, directly resetting long-dated bullion hedges. Watch for any liquidity-draining devolvement auctions announced within 48 hours post-decision, which would amplify rupee strength and crude cost pressures for refiners.
