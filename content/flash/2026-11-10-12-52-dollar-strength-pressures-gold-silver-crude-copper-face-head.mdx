**WHAT HAPPENED**
The **US Dollar Index** has steadied above recent lows as markets price in higher Fed rate expectations ahead of upcoming **core inflation data**, signalling potential resistance to near-term rate cuts.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
A firmer dollar raises the rupee cost of dollar-denominated commodities, directly repricing **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver** upward in rupee terms even if dollar-priced bullion moves sideways—this flow passes through to jewellery fabricators and bullion dealers sourcing hedges on MCX. Conversely, a stronger dollar typically weakens global demand for commodities priced in dollars; higher US real yields (from sustained Fed rates) reduce the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets, which pressures **MCX Crude** as energy demand concerns emerge and dampens **MCX Copper** as manufacturing growth expectations soften in response to tighter financial conditions.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Gold and silver refiners importing raw material and those locking forward prices via MCX hedges see immediate margin compression as rupee import costs rise, while jewellery retailers with pre-set catalogue prices absorb the squeeze until repricing cycles reset. Petroleum retailers and aviation fuel operators sourcing crude for blending face margin uncertainty as crude repricing extends procurement timelines, affecting logistics operators' fuel surcharges. Copper fabricators and wiring manufacturers supplying automotive and infrastructure projects see input cost volatility spike, prompting them to defer forward purchases—delaying cash flow for upstream smelters—while household appliance makers (refrigerators, AC units, wire harnesses) face input cost pressure that cascades to retail consumer prices within 4–6 weeks.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Jewellery retailers importing semi-finished bullion will see hedging costs rise sharply if rupee weakness persists against the dollar. **MCX Gold** and **MCX Silver** futures are likely to trade firm on rupee depreciation, while **MCX Crude** and **MCX Copper** face downward repricing pressure from global demand slowdown signals. Household gold ornament purchases and silver tableware will carry higher price tags at retail counters within days.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
US **core PCE inflation data** (next release in two weeks) and the Fed's forward guidance language in the next policy meeting will determine whether rate hold expectations crystallize or shift. Parallel watch on **China PMI manufacturing and services** data for any demand recovery signals that could support copper and crude.
