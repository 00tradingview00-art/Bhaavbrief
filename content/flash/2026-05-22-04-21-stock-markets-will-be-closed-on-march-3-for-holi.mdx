**WHAT HAPPENED**
Indian equity markets will remain shut on March 3 for the Holi public holiday, while MCX will operate its evening session only.

**MCX IMPACT**
MCX Gold (April contract) and MCX Silver (May contract) will trade exclusively in the evening session on March 3, meaning price discovery during Indian market hours will be compressed into a narrower window. With COMEX Gold at $4,543/oz translating to approximately ₹1,61,599/10g on MCX and USD/INR fixed at ₹96.21, any intraday COMEX moves during the Indian daytime gap will feed directly into the evening open without a morning session to absorb volatility. MCX Crude Oil (April contract) at approximately ₹9,591/bbl carries similar gap risk, as WTI moves during the closed window accumulate and reprice at evening session open. Indian traders carrying overnight positions face wider bid-ask spreads at the evening open due to reduced liquidity and no equity market reference for sentiment calibration.

**WATCH**
Monitor COMEX Gold price action and WTI crude movements during the 9:00 AM–5:00 PM IST window on March 3, as any significant directional move in that gap will determine the MCX evening open price dislocation. The MCX evening session open at 5:00 PM IST acts as the critical price level to watch for gap confirmation.
