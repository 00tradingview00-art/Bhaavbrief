**WHAT HAPPENED**
The U.S. Federal Reserve held rates steady amid conflicting signals: core inflation remains sticky above the 2% target, but **non-farm payrolls missed expectations** and jobless claims are rising, creating uncertainty over the timing of rate cuts.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
This policy pause keeps **U.S. real yields elevated in the near term**, raising the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets — yet the weak labour data signals potential Fed pivot later this year, which erodes real yields forward. For **MCX Gold and MCX Silver**, this creates a bifurcated floor: safe-haven demand from domestic investors and central bank buying on geopolitical risk pushes prices higher, while lower U.S. real yields reduce the attractiveness of holding dollar assets relative to rupee-denominated bullion. A softer jobs picture also weighs on the **U.S. dollar index**, making **rupee-denominated MCX Crude and MCX Copper** cheaper for Indian importers but constraining near-term demand from China-exposed fabricators.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Jewellery manufacturers and bullion dealers at Zaveri Bazaar and Jaipur refineries face immediate repricing on monthly procurement contracts, with forward premiums resetting higher on safe-haven flows. Tier-2 fabricators producing industrial copper components and auto wiring harnesses — already margin-compressed from demand softness — now see refined copper futures pricing in demand-destruction scenarios, forcing procurement delays until price discovery stabilises. Households purchasing gold jewellery and households installing copper-based solar thermal systems absorb cost changes at retail, where jewellers lock margins on finished goods inventory and solar installers pass through material cost shifts to end consumers within 2–3 weeks.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Indian petroleum refiners and fuel retailers see crude hedging costs locked in at lower levels today, protecting margins on retail diesel and petrol through the quarter. **MCX Gold** should consolidate above recent support on safe-haven inflows, while **MCX Copper** signals demand caution if China PMI disappoints next week. Retail gold buyers face stable-to-higher purchase prices at jewellery counters over the next fortnight.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
The next U.S. jobs report (first Friday of next month) and the **China Manufacturing PMI release** (within 72 hours) will clarify whether weak U.S. hiring is cyclical softness or structural, and whether China stimulus is offsetting copper demand destruction.
