**WHAT HAPPENED**
Two oil tankers carrying approximately 2 million barrels struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz and exploded, with Iran confirming the incident.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude importers and Indian refinery procurement desks face immediate rupee-cost escalation on every barrel cleared at elevated global benchmarks, as **₹9,917/bbl** levels now embed supply-line risk premiums. Parallel safe-haven demand pushes **MCX Gold** higher from **₹138,419/10g**, forcing bullion dealers holding dollar-invoiced inventory to recalibrate rupee realisations upward against the **₹95.27 USD/INR** anchor. Natural gas contracts face volatility spillover from crude risk, though LNG import dynamics remain structurally softer than oil.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Petroleum refiners and import terminals sourcing crude on weekly tenders immediately reprice procurement budgets upward, compressing operational margins on fuel oil and middle-distillate sales unless retail pump pricing is adjusted in tandem. Cement plants, thermal power stations, and fertiliser manufacturers consuming fuel oil as process heat see input-cost lines widen, forcing either margin compression or downstream price hikes on clinker and urea. Trucking and logistics operators fuelling commercial fleets absorb higher diesel costs at pump, while passenger transport operators face pressure to renegotiate fuel surcharges. Retail fuel buyers at petrol pumps will face upward pressure on litre prices, though transmission depends on government petroleum pricing decisions and rupee-dollar movements.

**BOTTOM LINE**
Petroleum refiners operating on **₹/bbl** cost-plus models see gross refining margins narrow unless crude offtake pricing accelerates proportionally. **MCX Crude** breaching and holding above **₹9,900/bbl** signals sustained geopolitical premium embedding into Indian domestic pricing. Households refuelling vehicles and purchasing kerosene for seasonal heating will encounter upward price pressure within days.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
OPEC statement on supply response and next U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve release announcement; USD/INR trading below **₹95** would partially offset crude cost impact for importers.
