**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude fell to 8082 INR/bbl at 12:16 pm IST, posting a -6.07% decline from the previous close. The retreat extends the pullback from yesterday's 10:25 pm IST peak of 8961 INR/bbl, as initial geopolitical supply concerns show signs of moderating. Across the complex, natural gas dropped -4.47% to 264.9 INR/mmBtu, while precious metals (gold +0.76%, silver +1.18%) and rupee strength (-0.73% USD/INR to 96.17) suggest broad risk-off repositioning.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The sharp reversal from multi-session highs signals traders are unwinding geopolitical risk premium that had anchored the 8961 peak. With crude now 879 INR/bbl below that level, positioning appears vulnerable to further downside if supply headlines fade. The divergence—crude and natgas weakening while gold and silver firm—indicates a flight to safety, pressuring energy complex sentiment.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Watch the 8000 INR/bbl level. A break below this round number would confirm the unwind of the geopolitical rally. Failure to hold here signals 7800–7900 INR/bbl could be next support.
