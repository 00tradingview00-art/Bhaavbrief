**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX (Multi Commodity Exchange) experienced a technical glitch that caused order execution delays, directly disrupting live trading activity on the exchange platform.

**MCX IMPACT**
All active MCX contracts — including Gold (currently ~₹161,662/10g), Silver (~₹261,989/kg), and Crude Oil (~₹9,640/bbl) — face execution risk during the disruption window, as order delays create slippage between placed and filled prices. When exchange infrastructure lags, bid-ask spreads widen artificially, meaning traders cannot reliably track the COMEX-to-MCX conversion spread — currently Gold at ~₹161,662/10g versus the COMEX-derived base inclusive of 15% customs duty and USD/INR at ₹96.21. Pending orders in high-volatility contracts like Silver and Crude are most exposed, as price movement during a delay window can result in fills far from intended levels. Indian traders with open hedges using MCX as the domestic leg face unhedged exposure for the duration of the outage.

**WATCH**
Monitor MCX's official exchange circular for system restoration confirmation and whether SEBI issues a directive on trade cancellations or void order policies for the affected session window. Any unresolved orders at settlement will be the critical trigger for margin and MTM adjustments.
