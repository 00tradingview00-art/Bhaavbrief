**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX Crude reached 8946 INR/bbl at 13:45 IST, posting a +2.43% gain and sustaining the multi-leg recovery sequence that began at 21:26 IST yesterday from 7918 INR/bbl. The move builds on the +2.19% rally to 8952 INR/bbl logged at 14:28 IST and consolidates above the 8712 INR/bbl level touched at 18:17 IST. USD/INR strength at 95.14 (+0.32%) underpins the rupee weakness tailwind.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude traders face a tightening range as the recovery sequence holds above 8700 INR/bbl support. The rally from sub-7918 levels signals sustained upside momentum, though cross-asset headwinds remain modest—gold (+0.51%) and natural gas (+0.69%) show muted gains, signaling selective crude strength rather than broad commodity risk-on.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Break and hold above 8950 INR/bbl will confirm extension toward 9000—the key psychological round number. A close below 8850 INR/bbl would signal exhaustion of the recovery sequence.
