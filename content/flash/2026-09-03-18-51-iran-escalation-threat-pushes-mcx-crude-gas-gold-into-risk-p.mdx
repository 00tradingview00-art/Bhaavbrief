**WHAT HAPPENED**
Israeli Defence Minister Katz stated that if Iran launches an attack, Israel will respond without constraints and target Iranian energy infrastructure, escalating Middle East tensions beyond the current conflict zone.

**WHAT IT MEANS**
Crude oil refinery procurement desks pricing feedstock at **Brent-linked forward curves** face immediate upward pressure as geopolitical risk premiums embed into WTI and MCX Crude Oil futures; a strike on Iranian energy facilities would disrupt regional supply flows and compress global spare capacity. Natural gas importers hedging LNG contracts on **MCX Natural Gas** encounter volatility spikes as Middle Eastern production disruption scenarios gain probability weight. Gold bullion dealers and jewellery wholesalers sourcing dollar-denominated physical see rupee-translated inventory costs rise as safe-haven demand pushes **spot gold and MCX Gold futures** higher.

**WHO IS AFFECTED**
Indian petroleum refiners holding **crude oil inventory** and drawing down strategic reserves face margin compression if spot crude spikes faster than they can pass costs to retail fuel pumps—a lag that directly hits downstream fuel margins. Power generation utilities and fertilizer manufacturers sourcing **imported LNG** on spot tenders see procurement bids escalate, lifting per-unit electricity and ammonia production costs immediately. Jewellery retailers and temple gold-holding institutions see their rupee-denominated inventory revalue upward on MCX spot premiums, but consumer footfall-driven demand may cool as retail gold prices at local dealers spike, reducing household ornament purchases and wedding-season gifting volumes.

**BOTTOM LINE**
**Indian oil refiners** processing imported feedstock absorb margin erosion as crude risk premiums widen faster than regulated fuel pricing adjusts. **MCX Crude Oil** breach above established resistance levels signals continuation of geopolitical risk pricing into forward contracts. **Retail gold jewellery buyers** face catalog price increases within 48–72 hours as dealers reprice inventory to match spot MCX premiums.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Monitor **Iranian official statements** and IRGC communications within 24 hours for de-escalation or confirmation; track **WTI and Brent spreads** for widening geopolitical risk premia—a gap exceeding $5/barrel signals market conviction of supply disruption.
