**WHAT HAPPENED**
USD/INR moved to 95.15 INR at 01:34 pm IST, gaining +0.33% from the previous close. The rupee's directional shift reverses recent weakness and reflects sustained foreign portfolio inflows into domestic equities and bonds. MCX precious metals responded with gains: gold at 153450 INR/10g (+0.57%), silver at 240872 INR/kg (+0.60%), while crude climbed to 8966 INR/bbl (+2.66%).

**WHAT IT MEANS**
The 95.15 level marks a consolidation zone between recent lows near 94.49 INR and resistance above 95.50 INR. For MCX traders, rupee strength typically pressures dollar-denominated commodity prices; however, crude's +2.66% surge suggests external WTI strength is offsetting currency headwinds. Gold and silver's resilience indicates safe-haven demand persists alongside inflow-driven rupee appreciation.

**WHAT TO WATCH**
Track the 95.50 INR resistance level. A decisive break above would signal sustained dollar demand; rejection would confirm rupee strength momentum and potentially extend weakness toward 94.75 INR support.
