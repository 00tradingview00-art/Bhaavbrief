**WHAT HAPPENED**
MCX restricted all commodity trading to the evening session only on January 15 due to Maharashtra state assembly elections, eliminating the morning trading window for that date.

**MCX IMPACT**
All active MCX contracts — Gold (February), Silver (March), Crude Oil (January), and Copper (February) — operated exclusively in the evening session on January 15, compressing the available trading window by approximately 5 hours. With Gold MCX referenced at ₹160,971/10g and Silver at ₹2,60,431/kg, the truncated session created reduced liquidity windows, widening bid-ask spreads against COMEX benchmarks that continued trading uninterrupted. Indian traders faced elevated basis risk during the gap period, as COMEX gold and silver price movements between the morning close and evening open occurred without a corresponding MCX hedge outlet. Crude Oil at ₹9,598/bbl was particularly exposed to intraday WTI volatility given crude's sensitivity to geopolitical headlines during Indian market hours.
