Your gold just got ₹2,200 more expensive overnight

Gold jumped ₹2,194 per 10g to ₹147,880 as US job numbers weakened, signalling rate cuts may come sooner than expected.
Weak US payrolls make the Fed more likely to cut rates, killing the appeal of holding interest-bearing dollars over gold.

Watch: Watch if the Fed's next statement confirms this dovish shift—if it does, gold stays bid; if hawkish, expect a pullback.
