Before your next MCX trade, know when it stops.

Hit a circuit limit, your orders lock. No exits. No entries. Just waiting.
MCX has two stages: ±3% first, then ±6%. Stage 1 halt is 15 minutes.

Watch: Gold and silver almost never hit these limits. Crude can expand to ±9% with SEBI approval.
