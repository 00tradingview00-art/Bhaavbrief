The option chain shows 20 IV% numbers. Only one prices the move: ATM IV. 📈

It's the implied vol at the strike closest to the futures price — and it carries the most vega in the whole chain, so it swings premiums more than any other strike.

Multiply Price × ATM IV × √(days to expiry ÷ 365) and you get the Expected Move — the range the market is pricing before that date.

It runs up into Fed / OPEC+ / RBI dates and crushes right after. Compare it to realized vol (AAV) and you know if options are cheap or expensive — that's BhaavBrief's Vol Premium metric.
